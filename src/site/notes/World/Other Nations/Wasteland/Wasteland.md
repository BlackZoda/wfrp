Home: [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]

This low-lying land at the mouth of the River Reik is home to Marienburg, one of the largest cities and commercial centres in the Old World. This region used to belong to the Empire — when it was referred to as the province of Westerland, but is now fiercely independent.

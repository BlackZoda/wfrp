Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald/Ranaldian Temples\|Ranaldian Temples]], [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Cities/Remas\|Remas]]

In the backstreets of Remas, a, a dusty stairwell leads to an underground colonnade where [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] is celebrated. Followers offer coins to a statue of the god in the shadows of the chamber. Priests are vigilant about keeping the Hall secret from Remas’ [[Solkanites\|Solkanites]], who loathe [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] followers.

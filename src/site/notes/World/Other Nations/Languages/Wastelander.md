Home: [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]]

Language spoken by the rural folk of the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]], a windswept territory to the west of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]. The official language of the Wasteland’s capital, [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]], is [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Reikspiel\|Reikspiel]], a reminder that the realm was once part of the Empire.

Home: [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]]

Language spoken by the folk of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. Reikspiel is the primary language used in most WFRP games. It manifests in uncounted discrete dialects from across the Empire, most of which are mutually intelligible. It is the modern version of the tongue once spoken by the God-King [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] when he still walked as a mortal over two thousand five hundred years ago.

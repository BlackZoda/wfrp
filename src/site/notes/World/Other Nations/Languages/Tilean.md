Home: [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]]

Language spoken by the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tilea]], who are most commonly encountered in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] as [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchants]] or [[Mercenaries\|Mercenaries]]. The [[Tilean Princedoms\|Tilean Princedoms]] to the south are notorious for their deadly politics and their veneration of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]].

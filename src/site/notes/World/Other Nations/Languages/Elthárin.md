A language spoken by the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Athel Loren\|Athel Loren]], the [[Laurelorn\|Laurelorn]], and visiting [[Merchants\|Merchants]] from [[Ulthuan\|Ulthuan]]. Elthárin is the language of the [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]]. The tonal language has many dialects, and is notoriously difficult for non-Elves to learn.

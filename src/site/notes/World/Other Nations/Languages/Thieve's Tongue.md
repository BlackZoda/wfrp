Home: [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]]

Language spoken by the street folk of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. Expressions of this language are highly localised and individual to each realm; e.g. [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tileans]] sport a completely different ‘Thieves Tongue’ to folk of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]].

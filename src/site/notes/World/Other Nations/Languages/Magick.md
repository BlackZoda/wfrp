Language used by [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizards]]; a tonal language used to shape the [[Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] into material effects. The [[Rules/Class/Academic\|Academic]] form of the language taught by the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]] is called the [[Lingua Praestantia\|Lingua Praestantia]], is quite different to the debased form of the language used by [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witches]] and the untrained. It is not a language used for communication.

The [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Wind of Magic]] may blow through all things incessantly, but they are relatively harmless until harnessed by the *Language of Magick*. For reasons not fully understood, when certain sounds are uttered by those attuned to magic, the [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds]] answer.The [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]] teach a complex language called the [[Lingua Praestantia\|Lingua Praestantia]] which form the basis of their spells, originally taught to Humanity by the [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]]. Although extraordinarily difficult to correctly annunciate, it is a significant simplification of the Elven tongue [[Anoqeyån\|Anoqeyån]], the language used by the [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]] to shape their own, more powerful magics. [[Floating/Magister\|Magister]] of the Colleges and the [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]] are not alone in their knowledge of the *Language of Magick*. Its comlex forms are also spoken by many magical creatures found across [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], including [[Spirit\|Spirits]] and [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]]. Many witches seem to instinctively understand the language, almost as if it somehow wormed inside them, begging to be spoken as a spell.

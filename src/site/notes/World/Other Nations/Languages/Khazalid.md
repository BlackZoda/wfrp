Language spoken by the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Karaz Ankor\|Karaz Ankor]], the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]] kingdoms in the [[World/Other Nations/World's Edge Mountains\|World's Edge Mountains]]. This language is also wide-spread through the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]], which venerates the strong relationship [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] had with the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] when he was mortal.

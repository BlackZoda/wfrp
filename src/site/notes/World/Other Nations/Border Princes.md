The **Border Princes** (singular **Border Principality** or **Border Kingdom**) also known as the **Borderlands**, the **Border Princedoms**, **The Princes** and the **Border Principalities**, is a vast region within [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] located between the [[Black Mountains\|Black Mountains]] and [[Black Fire Pass\|Black Fire Pass]] to the north and the shores of the [[Black Gulf\|Black Gulf]] to the south. This region of vast wilderness is home to a multitude of petty kingdoms of Men that were established by highly ambitious adbentureres who sought to create a realm of their own. These adventurers were often political or religious refugees fleeing from other [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] realms of the Old World such as [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]] and [[World/Other Nations/Kislev/Kislev\|Kislev]].

As such, these lands are infamous as the homes of a wide variety of bandits, mercenaries, cut-throats, pirates and other lawless fugitives. Following the [[Battle of Black Fire Pass\|Battle of Black Fire Pass]], [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] colonists and adventurers during the time of [[Sigismund the Conqueror\|Sigismund the Conqueror]] led a campaign of conquest towards [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s southern borders and fought off the [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskin]] tribes that used to inhabit the lands, clearing the way for many nobles and colonists from all across the lands of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] to colonise and claim patches of this territory as their own.

But what they will find is a land even more dangerous than their previous home, where petty princelings war on each other, farmsteads are raided by marauding [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskin]]s and [[pirate\|pirate]]s from [[Sartosa\|Sartosa]] will come looking for plunder and [[slaves\|slaves]].

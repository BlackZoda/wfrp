The Empire is the largest, most populous, most powerful and most important of the nations of Men in [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. It is a patchwork of [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Electoral Province]], each a mighty realm ruled by an Elector. These Electorial Provinces, in turn, are divided into scores of *[[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Prime Estate\|Prime Estate]]s*, a collective name for many *counties*, *duchies*, *marches*, *baronies*, and more ruled by the vassaals of an [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]]. Most Prime Estates are, again, split into many smaller provinces called [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Fief\|Fief]], and thse too may be divided up again into smaller [[Baronies\|Baronies]], [[Protectorates\|Protectorates]], territories, or similar. The [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] is the richest and argubly the most powerful of all the ancient [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Electoral Province]].

This exceedingly complex, overlapping tangle of territories forms a social pyramid of borders, titles, oaths, and responsibilities. It preoccupies the many thousands of [[Noble H[[The Empire\|Noble H[[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]span [[Imperiet\|Imperiet]], from the tiny number of [[Royal Houses\|Royal Houses]] ruling [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Electoral Province]] at the top, to the uncounted [[Minor Houses\|Minor Houses]] ruling the smallest mile-wide protectorates at the bottom. Knowing one’s place in the tangle, and its privileges, is vital to anyone with any pretensions of nobility.

The Empire was forged by the warrior-king and ascended deity [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] from the primitive trives of [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] barbarians who inhabited what became the lands of the Empire more than 2500 years ago. Although not as skilled in craftmanship or engineering as the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]]s or in [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]] as the [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elf]], the people ofthe Empire are not beholden by the limits of tradition to the same extent as the [[Elder Races\|Elder Races]] and continue to progress culturally, technologically and magically.

Having yet to succumb to any threat, external or internal, it is the faith, the sense of righteousness, and the unconquerable spirit of the realm’s citizens which gives the Empire its strength, as well as the ruthless efficiency of its military and religious orders. The greatest and most powerful realm of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], the Empire is ruled by a monarch with the title of the [[Emperor of Man\|Emperor of Man]] or simply *emperor* and is composed of the descendants of the ancient tribes of [[Mankind\|Mankind]] united by the great Human warror [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] Heldenhammer after the [[Battle of Black Fire Pass\|Battle of Black Fire Pass]] over twenty-five hudred years  ago.

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] was deified after his death by his people and his promise of eternal aid from the Men of the Empire for the kingdoms of the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]]s standing today. This action solidified the relationship between Men and Dwarfs and planted the seeds for the burgeoning Empire through trade between the two races.

Today, the Empire is ruled by the Emperor [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] who reigns from his court in the Imperial capital city of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]. There are ten prov[[The Empire]]s in [[Imperiet\|Imperiet]], each ultimately ruled by a feudal lord known as an [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]] who owes his or her power to the unifying figurehead and ultimate authority of the Emperor. It should be noted that the Empire is not truly a single, centralized nation, but instead a confederation of individual, feudal states united only by the common tongue of [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Reikspiel\|Reikspiel]], a shared faith in [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] and a mutual Imperial culture. The Imperial states correspond roughly to the ancient tribal territories brought together by Sigmar when he united the tribes of Man beneath on crown to defeat the [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskin]] of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]].

The southern Imperial states – [[Averland\|Averland]], [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], [[Stirland\|Stirland]], [[Talabecland\|Talabecland]] and [[Wissenland\|Wissenland]] – resemble a broad chalice, partially surrounded by near-impassable mountain ranges from which drain the mighty rivers which are the lifeblood of Imperial trade. To the west of these are [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]], beyond which lies the Kingdom of [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]] and the [[World/Races/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] magical forest [[World/Other Nations/Athel Loren\|Athel Loren]]. To the south are the inhospitable [[Black Mountains\|Black Mountains]] and to the east are the [[World/Other Nations/World's Edge Mountains\|World's Edge Mountains]] that mark theeastern extent of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]].

To the north, the rolling hills and rapid streams of the south and east are gradually transformed into the forested lowlands and deep waterways that define the heart of the Empire. The vast forests that comprise the central and northern Imperial lands stretch almost unbroken across the countless leagues from the state of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] to the northern border with [[World/Other Nations/Kislev/Kislev\|Kislev]] and the shore of the [[Sea of Claws\|Sea of Claws]]. The [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Great Forest\|Great Forest]], [[Darkwald Forest\|Darkwald Forest]] and the [[Forest of Shadows\|Forest of Shadows]] are all essentially part of the same dark untamed wilderness, with green blanket of trees punctured only be the abrupt mountain range known as the [[Middle Mountains\|Middle Mountains]]. These lands are divvided into the northern Imperial states – [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Hochland/Hochland\|Hochland]], [[Ostermark\|Ostermark]], [[Osterland\|Osterland]], [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Middenland/Middenland\|Middenland]] and [[Nordland\|Nordland]]. The inhabitants of these northern statesgenerally have to deal with much greater hardships and military threats than those of the more civilized south, being that much closer to the [[Deamon\|Deamon]]s, [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastman]] and other dangers of th northern [[Chaos Wastes\|Chaos Wastes]].

The life of a citizen of the Empire is a perilous one, for there are foes within every shadow and enemies around every corner.

# Number 23, Backerstrasse
The shrine of Ranald in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Hochland/Bergsburg\|Bergsburg]] is a gathering place for the poor under the auspices of priestess [[Katrin Spiegel\|Katrin Spiegel]] Behind the anonmous residental door on [[Beckerstrasse\|Beckerstrasse]] is a single room with an altar adorned with a fine painting depicting [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]]’s exploits.

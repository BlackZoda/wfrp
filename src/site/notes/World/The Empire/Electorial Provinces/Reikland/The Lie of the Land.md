The [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] lies in the shadow of the borbidding [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]], and its lowlands are entirely forested save for cleared reagoins around the graind province’s mutlitude of prosperous towns and villages. It has no coastline, but the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] – the largest river in [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] that defines most of Reikland’s twisting eastern and northern borders – is so wide and deep an entire navy of warships and merchant vessles plies its length.

Much of the land near the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] is waterlogged and marshy, with long stretches of bogs, swamps, and dangerous mud plains, the largest of which is the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Grootscher Marsh\|Grootscher Marsh]] (also called the *Cursed Marshes*) on the border with [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]] to the west. Farther inland. Farther inland, the Reikland climbs upwards to the craggy [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Skaag Hills\|Skaag Hills]] and the haunted [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Hägercrybs\|Hägercrybs]], two forested uplands hiding islated regions nigh-on untouched by human hand. The forests eventually give way as the land rises towards [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]], the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s natural border with [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]] to the south, and opens up to a wide plain of fertile foothills and open grassland known as the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]].

## The Mounains, Foothills, and Vorbergland
The Reikland is a rugged realm, its undulating forests broken by peaks, crags, and ridges, many topped by ruined castles and watchtowers from previous, war-torn eras. Rising above this mutitude of minor ranges are the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Hägercrybs\|Hägercrybs]] and the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Skaag Hills\|Skaag Hills]], two highland regions in central and northern Reikland, their heavy fortified mines responsible for much of the realm’s recent prosperity. Farther south, the trees thin out to the exposed plains and foothills of the vertile [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]] that lie between the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Reikwald\|Reikwald]] forest and the dangerous [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]], peaks that offer enormous wealth and danger in equal measure.

## The Grim, Dark Forests
The widespread forests of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] are regarded as some of the safest in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], but stray far from the busy rivers and three-lined roads and such impulsive talk is soon forgotten. Uncharted miles of towering oak, pine, elm, and beech hide much more than desperate bandits and wild animals. And the deeper one goes, the more dangerous it is.

## The Rivers, Canals, and Lakes
The many rivers spilling from the glacial lakes and waterfalls of the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]] down to the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] are centra to the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s economic success. Barges brimful with merchandise navigate the largest of these rivers, bringing diverse goods to the capital of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], many of which are then shipped elsewhere in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] or sold down the river to [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]].

To further support the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s wide-ranging mercantile ambitions, the richer noble and merchant houses invest in extensive canals planned and constructed by [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]’s famous [[Enigineer\|Enigineer]]s. The impact of these impressive waterways is undeniable, with trade goods moving faster than ever before, buth they require maintenance and protection, both of which are costly. To ensure the wrecers and river pirates the canals attract have minimal impact, [[Rules/Career/Road Warden\|Road Warden]]s, guards, and [[Rules/Career/Riverwarden\|Riverwarden]]s are employed as required, but frequently thse are little better than the criminals they purportedly thwart.

While the [[Axe-Bite Pass\|Axe-Bite Pass]] and [[Grey Lady Pass\|Grey Lady Pass]] are the best-known routes over the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]], they are not the only ways through the range. The [[Crooked Corridor\|Crooked Corridor]] is a narrow gorge through the mountains not far from the border with the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]]. It is useless to most merchants, being far too tight for wagons or horses, and dangerous to boot as much of the path is perched precariously on the edge of steep cliffs, meaning the slightest slip of a foot could lead to a painfully inglorious end.

Completely impassable in the winter and perilous at all other times, the *Crooked Corridor* was known only to goat herders and smugglers until a spate of [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskin]] raids brought the pass to the attention of the authorities.

Several decades ago, [[Emperor Mattheus II\|Emperor Mattheus II]] ordered the construction of a fortress to watch over the [[Crooked Corridor\|Crooked Corridor]] after repeated invasion. Nine years later, *Blackstone Tower* was completed, drawing it name from the locally quarried dark stone that forms it walls, and the slate that tops it towers.

Its poisition high above the [[Crooked Corridor\|Crooked Corridor]] offered the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]]’s sharpshooters an excellent perch from which to employ their lethal skills; however, its status as a [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reiklander]] bastion was short-lived. An oversight at the planning stage resulted in the tower being build on lands claimed by the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] of [[Karak Ziflin\|Karak Ziflin]]. Not wanting to upset the Dwarfs, the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] ceded the tower, much to the annoyance of the [[Margrave of Geetburg\|Margrave of Geetburg]] who had helped finance it.

Today, the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] “generously” allow a limited garrison to barrack alongside their own troops at Blackstone Tower, a largely rebuilt structure. With the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reiklanders]] chafing under the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]]’ leadership, and the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] constantly belittling the shoddy human stonework they’ve yet to replace, the Tower is a powderkeg of grievances and resentment, generating a tense – and some would say unsustainable – atmsphere.

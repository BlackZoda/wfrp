[[Campaign/NPCs/Mathilda Wolfenbüttel\|Mathilda Wolfenbüttel]] has a good thing going: turn up at a [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|Town]], invent a new tax, collect as much coin as she can, and be on her way before anyone sees through the scam. After a narrow escape from the [[Finsterbad\|Finsterbad]] watch, she’s enjoying her spoils at the [[Campaign/Events/Schaffenfest\|Schaffenfest]]. She’s looking to hire, an unsuspecting group of toughs to watch her back while she conduct her “legitimate services to [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]” in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Bögenhafen/Bögenhafen\|Bögenhafen]].

Southmost of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s major trade centres, the market town of Dunkelberg stretches across several hills along the banks of the [[Grissen\|River Grissen]]. The older, richer portions of the town sit high on the hills, giving the nobles, and the rising merchant class wealthy enough to afford the oldest townhouses, a commanding view of the surrounding countryside. Despite its ever-increasing size, travelers note Dunkelberg’s “rustic” feel, with reguar markets clogging its winding streets. There shoppers can purchase livestock; local produce; several excellent, fuity wines produced by the surrounding villages; and hand-crafted goods from across the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Suden Vorbergland\|Suden Vorbergland]]. Wide-ranging “bleachfields” extend from the town, where local linen is dyed white by the sun around crops.

Dunkelberg’s position near the looming [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]] and the wild [[Graugrissen\|Graugrissen]] forest leads to regular raids by [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Goblin\|Goblin]] tribes. While the richer portions of the town are well fortified and protected by a high, stone wall, the rest lack any significant defences. As such, the poorer citizenry have learned not to grow too attached to their homes, belongings, or loved ones.

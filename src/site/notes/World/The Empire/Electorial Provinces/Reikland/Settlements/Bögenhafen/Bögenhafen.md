The largest and most prosperious of all the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]]’s market towns, Bögenhafen lies in the very heart of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] and serves as the crossroads of the [[Grand Province\|Grand Province]]. Lying equidistant between the capitals of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] and [[Monfort\|Monfort]], it is a crucial stop on the principle trading route between [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] and [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]]. Goods from Bretonnia and the four corners of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] are traded here for local [[Lumber\|Lumber]], [[Wool\|Wool]], and [[Metal\|Metal]]s shipped down from the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]].

Bögenhafen is located in [[Campaign/NPCs/Graf Wilhelm von Saponatheim\|Graf Wilhelm von Saponatheim]]’s [[Duchy\|Duchy]], but he is quite content to let the town be run by a local council – whose membership is dominated by members of the local [[Merchants’ Guild\|Merchants’ Guild]], as well as representatives form [[Stevedores’ Guld\|Stevedores’ Guld]] and [[Teamsters’ Guild\|Teamsters’ Guild]] – so long as they keep the money flowing his way. As gods coming north are transferred from wagon to barge in Bögenhafen, the city is always well stocked with warehouses full to the brom of [[Fine Wines\|Fine Wines]] and expensive, fragrant [[Cheeses\|Cheeses]].

The Reikland prides itself as one of the most populated and civilised of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s [[Grand Province\|Grand Province]]s. Nevertheless, great swathes of the realm are still wild, blanketed in [[Forest\|Forest]] and untouched by [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] hand. Though these woodlands are relatively safe when compared to the extraordinary dangers found in the great forests elsewhere in the Empire, the darker corners of the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Reikwald\|Reikwald]] are still home to all manner of terrible creatures.

So, even after two thousand five hundred years of expansion, most [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] settlements are dound along the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] and its many tributaries, connected by river if not always by road. It is only to the south of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]], in the breadbasket of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], that rural settlements spread freely, connected by well-maintained roads and remarkable canals.

## Altdorf and the Manifold Towns
It is said that all the roads and the rivers lead to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], the largest, richest city of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], but there are many other interesting places to visit beyond that city’s high white walls. Numerous towns, both large and small, can be found across the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], most acting as centres of trade or manufacture. A growing number of these flourishing centres of commerce also holds the status of [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Freistadt\|Freistadt]], meaning they are “free towns”: granted charters to govern themselves, usually via a [[Council of Burgomeisters\|Council of Burgomeisters]] with little or no interference from the local [[World/The Empire/Demography/Noble House\|Noble House]]s.

**Diesdorf** is one of many small towns in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] located between [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Wissenland/Nuln\|Nuln]] on the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|River Reik]]. 

Corn is the basis of the economy of Diesdorf and its surrounding villages. The crop is mainly traded downriver to Altdorf. However, Diesdorf's reliance on corn means that disastrous consequences would follow if a corn blight were to strike the area. However, those who visit **Diesdorf** may conclude that its chief export is, in truth, religious fervour. 

[[Floating/Magnus the Pious\|Magnus the Pious]] once gave a legendary powerful speech in the town after after folk travelled far and wide to hear him. Ever since, Diesdorf became a pilgrimage center for the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]].  Diesdorf has many more shrines to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] than would be expected for a [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|Town]] of its size. Most families in Diesdorf have at least one member in the Sigmarite clergy.

Diesdorf's underworld is contested by two rival crime families, the [[Havilunds\|Havilunds]] and the [[Frankles\|Frankles]], who have a long history of murdering each other.

Known and celebrated by the wine drinkers throughout [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], the vineyards around Eilhart produce the succulent grapes, and thus the wine, that shares its name. Eilhart is widely considered one of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s best white wines, valued not just for its crisp light flavour, but also for its famously mild hangovers that grow no worse no matter how much of it was quaffed the night before, or so Eilharters claim. Recently, Eilhart has recently also become famed in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], and as far afield as [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]], for its sharp, acidic beers making use of fragrant hops and local grain.

Given the excellent quality of its alcoholic bevereges,, Eilhart has become a popular destination for riverboat cruises, wherein epicures travel to the town to sample its wares from the many drinking houses, breweries, and vineyards on offer. Some claim the high number of visiting [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnians]] – drawn by the excellent wine – may account for the locals’ recent enthusiasm for beer.

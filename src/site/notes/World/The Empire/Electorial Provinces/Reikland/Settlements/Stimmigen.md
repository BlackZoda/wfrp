The major bridge over the River [[Ober\|Ober]], coupled with access to the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Vorbergland Canals\|Vorbergland Canals]], ensures a great deal of trade passes through *Stimmigen* on its way to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Ubersreik/Ubersreik\|Ubersreik]], making it one of the busiest market towns in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Suden Vorbergland\|Suden Vorbergland]]. It is widely famed for its lush orchards, and is the rouce of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s best known apple, the sweet, yet tart, [[Ernwald\|Ernwald]]. The Ernwald only flourishes in Stimmingen’s environs, proving resistant to attempts to cultivate it elsewhere in the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]]. In addition to curious horticulturists and jealous farmers, the Ernwald has attracted a significant [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] population to the area, whose delicious apple pies, pastries and crumbles – not to mention crisp cider – can be found in most local hostelries all year round, and especially during [[Pie Week\|Pie Week]] at the start of autumn. This [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] festival is celebrated by all in Stimmingen, an opporunity for rich and poor alike to enjoy tastings and eating competitions, and generally gorge themselves on all manner of sweet, baked goods.

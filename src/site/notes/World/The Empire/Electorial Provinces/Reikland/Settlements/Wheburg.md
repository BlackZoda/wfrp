Wheburg is the first town encountered after leaving the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]] on the road from [[Floating/Helmgart\|Helmgart]] to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Bögenhafen/Bögenhafen\|Bögenhafen]]. Its proximity to the fortress of [[Floating/Helmgart\|Helmgart]] ensures troops pass through regularly, and the town offers a sizeable barracks for visiting soldiers. Having survived the mountains’ extreme weather – to say nothing of ravaging armies of [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Orc\|Orcs]] and [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Goblin\|Goblins]], and potential monsters roosting on the nearby [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Drachenberg\|Drachenberg]] – most visitors arrive to Wheburg with a powerful desire to celebrate life.

T accomodate that need, Wheburg has become renowned for its hospitality, and its locals are widely regarded as the friendliest in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] – for a price. The streets are lined with taverns, inns, and gaming dens, not to mention some less aalubrious establishments where all the pleasures of the flesh are puveyed, and illicit substances may be procured. Though fist-fights and petty crime are common, things rarely get too out of hand given a significant number of soldiers usually on hand to disperse any rowdy revellers (except, of course, when those revellers are, themselves, off-duty soldiers). Spending time in Wheburg prior to marriage has become something of a rite-of-passage for well-heeled [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reiklanders]], and it’s common to hear men and women calling out, ‘what happens in Wheburg, stays in Wheburg!’ from many a street corner.

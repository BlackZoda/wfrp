Originally little more than one of many tiny fishing villages along the river [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Bögen\|Bögen]], Weissbruck has in the past century grown rapidly into a bustling port for goods and travellers moving between [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Bögenhafen/Bögenhafen\|Bögenhafen]] and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]. Weissbruck’s change in fortune is due to its shrewd rulers, the [[Floating/Gruber Family\|Gruber Family]], who decided to capitalise on the rich deposits of coal and iron found in the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Skaag Hills\|Skaag Hills]]. This ready supply of much needed resources enabled the [[Floating/Gruber Family\|Grubers]], with the permission and partnership of the [[Prince of Altdorf\|Prince of Altdorf]], to commission [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]] engineers to construct a canal in 2462 [[World/The Empire/History/Imperial Calendar\|IC]], which rapidly accelerated the town’s growth, bringing all manner of citizenry with it.

It is now a bustling merchant town, and the sound of construction rings everywhere as new buildings expand the outskirts to all sides. Warehouses line the canal and river banks, storing coal and iron ore from the nearby mines, wool and wine from Bögenhafen, and all manner of goods from Altdorf, Carroburg, and the Vorbergland Canals.

While the output of the mines has slowed in recent years, trade has steadily increased. Despite its rulers’ riches, Weissbruck retains something of the character of a typical mining town; burly miners and dockworkers ensure an uneasy peace, and the threat of voilence is never far removed. As the mines’ output has diminished, more and more miners have descended from the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Skaag Hills\|Skaag Hills]], a growing agitated, unemployed workforce with little to do but drink cheap rotgut and scowl at strangers.

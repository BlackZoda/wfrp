An ancient town with a long and convoluted history, the [[Grand Freistadt\|Grand Freistadt]] of *Kemperbad* is known and celebrated for producing the finest brandy in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]. Though part of the Reikland, Kemperbad lies on the eastern bank of the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]]; historically the town has been fought over, and ruled by, nobles from [[Talabecland\|Talabecland]], [[Stirland\|Stirland]], and the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], changing hands time and again through a series of marriages, treaties, and wars. Sonce gaining a charter affording it the right to self-rule in 1066 [[World/The Empire/History/Imperial Calendar\|IC]] from [[Boris Goldgather\|Boris Goldgather]], Kemperbad has been a [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Freistadt\|Freistadt]] led by a local council.

Its prime location on the river, coupled with the ability to impose and retain its own taxes, has made the town very rich indeed. Beacouse of this, the merchants of Kemperbad are legendarily ostentatious, dressing in outfits so gaudy as to shame a peacock, and costly enough to beggar folk of more more modest means. Unsurprisingly, the neighbouring nobility regards Kemperbad’s coffers with envious eyes, and some send agents to destabilise the town.

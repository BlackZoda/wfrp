The *Reikland Estates* are ruled by vassals of the [[Grand Prince of Reikland\|Grand Prince of Reikland]]. Their rulers can do as they will with their lands, parcelling out fiefs to family and friends as they wish – though the creation of new hereditary titles requires the approval of the [[Grand Prince of Reikland\|Grand Prince of Reikland]] and the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Politics/Reikland Diet\|Reikland Diet]].

Each estate has a range of distinct legal and military obligations to the crown, but all are required to build and maintain at least one regiment for the [[Reikland State Army\|Reikland State Army]], soldiers typically used in peacetime as watchmen, road wardens, or guards. Most *Reikland Estates* are feudal, comprised of smaller fiefs ruled by vassals, many of whom hold hereditary titles that have existed for centuries.

 An example of a *Reikland Estate* is the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Estates/Barony of Böhrn\|Barony of Böhrn]].

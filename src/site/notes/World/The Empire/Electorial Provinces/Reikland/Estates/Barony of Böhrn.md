The *Barony of Böhrn* is a [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Estates/Reikland Estates\|Reikland Estate]] of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]] ruled by [[House Böhrn\|House Böhrn]] for over four centuries. [[Baroness Agetta\|Baroness Agetta]] holds court in the town of [[Siedlung\|Siedlung]] and currently has six vassals: three with hereditary titles – [[Baron Markham of Siebbach\|Baron Markham of Siebbach]], [[Countess Osterhild of Kaltenwald\|Countess Osterhild of Kaltenwald]], and [[Baron Baltzer of Ettlingdal\|Baron Baltzer of Ettlingdal]] – and three with non-hereditary titles, all of which were appointed by Agetta personally – [[Warden Fabian of Ort\|Warden Fabian of Ort]], [[Warden Luethold of Koff\|Warden Luethold of Koff]], and [[Castellan Fronika of Neumarkt\|Castellan Fronika of Neumarkt]]. The three hereditary vassals have, in turn, a total of seven vassals of their own.

The Baroness does not attend the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Politics/Reikland Diet\|Reikland Diet]]; instead she relies on her influential younger sister, [[Lector Agatha von Böhrn of Verena\|Lector Agatha von Böhrn of Verena]], to watch over it in her place. Agatha resides in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] serving as a member of the [[Imperial Council of State\|Imperial Council of State]] and the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Politics/Reikland Council\|Reikland Council]], as well as tending to her duties as a [[Lector of Verena\|Lector of Verena]].

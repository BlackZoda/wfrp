Home: [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]

Reikland is the richest and most powerful of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s [[Grand Provinces\|Grand Provinces]]s. It’s known for it’s [[Enigineer\|Enigineer]]s, [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizard]]s, and [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchant]]s, and for being the birthplace of the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]]. It is a realm of soaring mountains snaking rivers, dark forests, and powerful trading centers. From his throne in the city of high-spired [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] rules not only the Reikland, but all the Empire that lies beyond.

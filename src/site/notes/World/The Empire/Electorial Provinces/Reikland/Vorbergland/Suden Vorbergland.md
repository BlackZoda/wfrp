Home: [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]]

The productive south-eastern provinces around [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Estates/Barony of Böhrn\|Böhrn]], [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Ubersreik/Ubersreik\|Ubersreik]], [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Stimmigen\|Stimmigen]], and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Dunkelberg\|Dunkelberg]] are known locally as _**Suden Vorbergland**_, and are the most heavily cultivated regions of the Reikland. They are peppered with many flourishing towns, villages, farms, and vineyards, and are often referred to as **‘Ranald's Garden’** for the vast quantities of [wine](https://warhammerfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Wine "Wine") they produce for export.

The High Lord Ambassador organises the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s foreign relations and runs the [[Altdorf Black Chamber\|Altdorf Black Chamber]]: the infamous spies of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]].

[[Graf Liepmund Holzkrug\|Graf Liepmund Holzkrug]], the current High Lord Ambassador, is an intense, fiercly ambitious man, whose family are strong rivals to [[Floating/House Holswig-Schliestein\|House Holswig-Schliestein]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]. He is known for his vindictive nature, his ruthless politics, and his love of hunting rare and exotic game.

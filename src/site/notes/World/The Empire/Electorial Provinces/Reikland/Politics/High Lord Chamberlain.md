The *High Lord Chamberlain* runs the [[Palace of Altdorf\|Palace of Altdorf]] and the adjoining [[Volkshall\|Volkshall]]. The position is arguably one of the most influential in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], as most high-level imperial politics take place within the chanberlain’s domain.

The current High Lord Chamberlain is rake-thin [[Duchess Elze von Skaag\|Duchess Elze von Skaag]], an astute negotiator and careful planner who publicly supports [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] in all matters. Elze’s husband, [[Duke Alardus von Skaag\|Duke Alardus von Skaag]], is said to be deepl frustrated at his wife residing in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] and not in [[Skaggerdorf\|Skaggerdorf]] with him; by comparison, it seems to suit her perfectly as most evenings she can be found carousing through the city with her bodyguards.

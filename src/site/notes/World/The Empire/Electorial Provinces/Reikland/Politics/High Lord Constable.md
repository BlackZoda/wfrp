The *High Lord Constable* is the ultimate authority on the genealogy and heraldry of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]].

The current High Lord Constable is smiling [[Grafina Matrella von Achern\|Grafina Matrella von Achern]], a woman with no interest in genealogy, but a voracious appetite for political intrigue. She has made the most of this relatively inconsequential position, by chairing several private councils on the [[Grand Prince of Reikland\|Grand Prince]]’s behalf. It is hard to reconcile the bubbly, maternal demeanour she projects, and her reputation amongst her peers as the most ruthless woman in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]].

The High Lord Steward is the onl man legally allowed to judge a crime committed by the lord of a [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Estates/Reikland Estates\|Reikland Estate]]. As a High Lord Stewart’s Court is rarely called – most lords being simplay too powerful to call to task – the position is largely ceremonial. Nevertheless, the High Lord Steward is argubly the most senior High Lord, and often stands in for the [[Grand Prince of Reikland\|Grand Prince]] when he is unable – or unwilling – to attend court.

The current High Lord Steward is [[Archduke Adelbert von Bögenberg\|Archduke Adelbert von Bögenberg]], a quiet man with little experience of the law, but a shrewd understanding of people. As much of the grand prince’s time is taken up with imperial matters, he is argubly the *de facto* ruler of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], much to the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Politics/High Lord of the Chair\|High Lord of the Chair]]’s chagrin.

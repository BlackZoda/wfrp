The *High Lord Chancellor* is the crown’s spiritual advisor, and oversees the [[Reikland Chancery\|Reikland Chancery]] and the [[Silver Seal\|Silver Seal]].

The current *High Lord Chancellor* is [[High Priestess Halma Habermann of Sigmar\|High Priestess Halma Habermann of Sigmar]], a robust woman with pale complexion, rosy cheeks, and thick limbs. Though generally charming and approachable, she is zealously out-spoken in her opposition to the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]], and rumours abound regarding the atrocities she witnessed during her service in the [[Order of the Silver Hammer\|Order of the Silver Hammer]].

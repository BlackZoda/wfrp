The *Reikland Diet*, which meets as required in the [[Holzkrug Chamber\|Holzkrug Chamber]] of the [[Volkshall\|Volkshall]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], is responsible for scrutinising decrees issued by the [[Grand Prince of Reikland\|Grand Prince of Reikland]], either passing them or returning them to the crown with suggested amendments.

As most lords of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Estates/Reikland Estates\|Reikland Estates]] don’t have time to attend the diet themselves, they generally send favoured children, spouses, relatives, or minions in their place, although decrees of significant import will often bring worried lords from across the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] to attend in person. Given the numeber of agents the [[Grand Prince of Reikland\|Grand Prince of Reikland]] tasks to influence the *Reikland Diet*, most votes are a formality.

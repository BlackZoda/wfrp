Home: [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Politics/Politics of the Reikland\|Politics of the Reikland]]

The day-to-day business of ruling the province is hadled by the *Reikland Council*, which cimprises ten “[[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Politics/The High Lords of the Reikland\|High Lords]]”. The council meets weekly in the [[Wilhelm Chamber\|Wilhelm Chamber]] of the [[Volkshalle\|Volkshalle]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] to discuss matters of import. It is rare for more than six of the high lords to attend simultaneously, and almost unheard of for the prince to attend in person.

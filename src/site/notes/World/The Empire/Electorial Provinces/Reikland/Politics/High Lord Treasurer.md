The High Lord Treasurer is responsible for the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s treasury and for raising revenues. The position is extraordinarily important and is almost always held by a close ally of the crown.

The current High Lord Treasurer is the aging genius [[Grafina Elena von Midwald\|Grafina Elena von Midwald]], a dear friend of the previous grand prince, well known for her outspoken appreciation of fine wine and fine men.

The High Lord Judge is the ultimate voice on all [[Reiklander Law\|Reiklander Law]], which is quite different from [[Imperial Law\|Imperial Law]].

The current High Lord Judge of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] also serves as [[Supreme Law Lord\|Supreme Law Lord]] of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]: [[Lector Agatha von Böhrn of Verena\|Lector Agatha von Böhrn of Verena]]. She is experienced in all legal matters, and widely regarded as one of the most intelligent people in *the Empire*, especially by those who have bribed her.

The *High Lord Admiral* is responsible for the [[Admirality of the Reik\|Admirality of the Reik]], thus the entire [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] navy.

The current High Lord Admiral is the venerable [[Sea Lord Adalmann von Hopfberg\|Sea Lord Adalmann von Hopfberg]]. The Sea Lord – who is now quite senile – took residency in the [[The Great Hospice\|The Great Hospice]] some fifteen years ago, and the Admirality has governed the navy without his influence – or interference – since then.

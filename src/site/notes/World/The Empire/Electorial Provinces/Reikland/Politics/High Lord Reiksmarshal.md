The *High Lord Reiksmarshal* is responsible for leading the armies of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], and ensuring each of the [[Reikland State Regiments\|Reikland State Regiments]] is raised, financed, and made available to the crown as required.

The current Reiksmarshal is the veteran general [[Duke Kurt Helborg\|Duke Kurt Helborg]], close friend and tutor to the current grand prince – [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] – and said to be the finest swordsman in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. Helborg’s military commitments ensure that he rarely takes his seat on the council, a circumstance that suits his natural inclination.

Home: [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Politics/The High Lords of the Reikland\|The High Lords of the Reikland]]

A close advisor to the grand prince, the high Lord of the Chair officially leads the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Politics/Reikland Council\|Reikland Council]] in the elector count’s stead.

The current High Lord of the Chair, the stoic [[Graf Archibold von Lilahalle\|Graf Archibold von Lilahalle]], was granted the influential position after being gravely wounded whilst saving the life of [[Emperor Lutipold III\|Emperor Lutipold III]] from an assissin’s blade. Lingering, painful injuries confine him to a steam-driven wheelchair; an irony only the unwise note aloud.

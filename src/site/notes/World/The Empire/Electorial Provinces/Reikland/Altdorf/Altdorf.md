[[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] holds court in Altdorf, the capital of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] and [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] as a shole. It stands proutly at the confluence of the mighty [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] and [[Talabec\|Talabec]] rivers, as well as at the end of many major [[Trade Route\|Trade Route]]s, and is easily the wealthiest [[World/Other Nations/Cities\|Cities]] in the Empire. This ensures it is always expanding and renewing, with fresh, wide-eyed hopefuls arriving daily, and more commissions for new bridges, guildings, and engineering marvels presented weekly to the city’s [[Burgomeister\|Burgomeister]]s than most cities consider in a year.

Today, Altdorf is a city of [[Steam-Powered Bridge\|Steam-Powered Bridge]]s, mismatched islands, enormous [[Tenement\|Tenement]]s, and seemingly endless hordes of people: one cannot traverse its crooked streets without becoming intemately familiar with complete strangers. Because it draws [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchant]]s and [[Diplomat\|Diplomat]]s from across the [[Known World\|Known World]], it is also surprisingly cosmopolitan. Not only do folk of all ranks, species, creeds, and backgrounds rub shoulders freely but even [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizard]]s are a common sight, rarely receiving more hostility than a wary glance or muttered blessing.

Always surprising outsiders, a populous [[World/Races/Elf\|Elf]] quarter thrives near the [[Reiksport\|Reiksport]] – Altdorf’s deep-water harbour and shipyard – founded by [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elf]] merchant princes trading up the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] from [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]] over a century ago. The old alliance between the Empire and [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]]s has stood since [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s time and ensures many [[Dwarf Clan\|Dwarf Clan]]'s also reside there in tight-knit communities, some having toiled on the city’s stonework for untold generations, though naturally their stubborn natures preclude them from ever calling Altdorf home. There is even a healthy [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] population, with most earning their crusts serving fine [[Campaign/Items/Food\|Food]] and better [[Ale\|Ale]]s in Altdorf’s [[Hostelery\|Hostelery]]s, many of which are guarded by the city’s sizeable [[World/Races/Ogre\|Ogre]] population. Even odder creatures also call the city home, with none more fantastic than those found in the [[Imperial Zoo\|Imperial Zoo]], which boast myriad monsters and animals including the [[Abomination of Stirland\|Abomination of Stirland]] and the [[Drakwald Gibberbeast\|Drakwald Gibberbeast]], each caged and displayed for the amusement of all.

The enormous and varied population helps secure on of Altdorf’s more notorious traits: it is infamously smelly, known affectionately, and all to accurately as *“The Great Reek”*. During the hot, summer months the stench rolling in from the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Altdorf Flats\|Altdorf Flats]] grows so potent that many of the city’s wealthiest citiens flee to [[Country Estate\|Country Estate]]s or the sumptuous, sun-kissed palaces of [[Grenstadt\|Grenstadt]] in [[Southern Averland\|Southern Averland]] ([[Averland\|Averland]]).

The ever-present fetor does little to ease Altdorf’s citizenry, a mob known for being unquietly vocal and prone to rioting at any imagined sight, especially if it involves new taxes ([[World/The Empire/Economy/Taxes and Revenue\|Taxes and Revenue]]). Even seemingly trivial actions of the local [[Burgomeister\|Burgomeister]]s and [[Rules/Career/Noble\|Noble]]s can earn their ire – but the fact that man of their protests are entirely fair does not lessen the crown’s fervour in quashing any rebellious rabbles that may take to the streets.

Altdorf is also a renowned centre for learning. The sons and daughters of wealthy households matriculate at the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Locations/University of Altdorf\|University of Altdorf]], often with little regard to academic ability. Those with scolarly potential, but without the financial clout to match it, often find their way to the [[High Temple of Verena\|High Temple of Verena]], for the [[Goddess of Wisdom\|Goddess of Wisdom]] always has space for those blessed with intellect. The famed [[Imperial Engineers’ School\|Imperial Engineers’ School]] – founded by [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tilea]]n genious [[Leonardo da Miragliano\|Leonardo da Miragliano]] – also lies within Altdorf’s walls. Here some of the Empire’s bravest, and most reckless, souls devise new and inventive methods for raining fiery death on the enemies of mankind. Perhaps unsurprisingly the school has been rebuilt over a dozen times since it was founded.

Argubly the most significant educational institution in Altdorf is also its most mysterious: The [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]]. Founded over two centuries ago at the behest of [[Emperor Magnus the Pious\|Emperor Magnus the Pious]], the colleges are guided by the principles and precepts of the elven [[Loremaster Teclis\|Loremaster Teclis]]. The colleges are charged with training magically gifted citizens to channel their dangerous talents in the defence of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. Rumours of the warping influence of the potent magical energies gathered by so many [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizard]]s living in such close proximity persist  allegedly the very streets of the capital have been twisted, and certain college buildings are believed to be hidden from all but those few blessed – or should that be cursed – with [[Witchsight\|Witchsight]], but few belive such talk.

Standing in stark contrast to the arcane mysteries of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]], and as a bulwark against any errant [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witch]]es or rouge [[Magister\|Magister]]s, Altdorf is also the beating heart of the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]]. Over two-thousand five-hundred years ago, [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] was born, raised, and eventually crowned as first emperor witin the coundaries of modern Altdorf, and fittingly the city now boast more Sigmarite temples and holy sites than all the other cities of the Empire combined. Indeed it is often said that one cannot throw a heretic without hitting a [[Campaign/Locations/Buildings of the Reikland/Religious Buildings/Temple of Sigmar\|Temple of Sigmar]] in the city.

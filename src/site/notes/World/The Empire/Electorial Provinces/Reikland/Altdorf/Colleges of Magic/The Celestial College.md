High above [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], the sixteen slender towers of the Celestial College soar into the sky, as if the magisters are trying to reach the stars themselves. Each is surmounted by a glass dome, from which the most exalted astromancers gaze upwards into the night sky. Despite its spectacular presence, few citizens of the Imperial capital ever acknowledge the blue and white stone college. It is protected by enchantments to divert attention and bring innocuous distractions which subtly draw the eye away. Perhaps a flag blows in the line of sight, or a line of laundry billos to block their view. Those determined to reach the college gates ill do so, bu casual onlookers have no clear memories of its presence.

Until 102 years ago, the college was an impressive but mundane five-storey building in the [[Hexxerbezrik\|Hexxerbezrik]] district, reputedly built on [[World/Races/Elf\|Elven]] ruins. When [[Helmut Rozenkranz\|Helmut Rozenkranz]] become [[High Celestial\|High Celestial]], he constructed the first glass-domed tower.This sparked the so-called [[Time of the Towers\|Time of the Towers]] when [[Rules/Career/Wizard Lord (level 4)\|Wizard Lords]] vied to build ever-taller observatories. hen the Celestarium as completed, taller than the rest, the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] decreed an end to the astromancer’s rivalery. This tower is a spectacular sight, with its enchanted glass and great orrery mounted at the summit. Other towers are named for former inhabitants, such as the [[Tower of Casandora\|Tower of Casandora]], [[Urannon’s Observatory\|Urannon’s Observatory]], and [[Gabriel’s Chamber\|Gabriel’s Chamber]] with impressive moondial.

The outside walls are inscribed with comets, stars, planets, and moons. Beyond the main doors, the air is fresh and wall hangings and tapestries move in a gentle breeze with no obvious source. Near the entrance is a suite of receiving rooms where [[Rules/Career/Astromancer\|Astromancers]] host visitors, including those willing to pay hansomely for auguries.

There are living quarters for the acolytes and college servants, several libraries, and the private chambers of resident astromancers. At the core of the college building is the [[Grand Experiment\|Grand Experiment]] – where portents are entered into a large silver astrolabe. The astromancers believe this will eventually predict the entire future with complete accuracy.

Astromancers are notoriously bad at the mundane tasks in life, so the Celestial College has many servants. There are skilled artisans such as lensmakers, horologists, and other artificers, who create new devices for studying the heavens. Countless housekeeping staff keep magisters fed and the halls clean.

A specialist crew of cartwrights keep the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic/Celestial Hurricanum\|Celestial Hurricanum]]s battle-ready at all times.

The current [[High Celestial\|High Celestial]] is [[Campaign/NPCs/Hildegard Niederthaler\|Hildegard Niederthaler]], a sprightly 87-year-old who monopolises three toers in the college for her personal use, including [[The Celestarium\|The Celestarium]]. The portents imply her time in office is coming to an end and other wizard lords are manoevering to take her place. [[Lord Stern Glazend\|Lord Stern Glazend]] is a likely successor.

He believes the college should encourage exploration, after learning that the ancient and mysterious denizens of [[Lustria\|Lustria]] supposedly left gold plaques which describe every event from past to future. One of Glazend’s allies is [[Bartolomi Kereveld\|Bartolomi Kereveld]], who plans an expedition to find lost knowledge of the cosmos in [[Lustria\|Lustria]]’s jungle ruins.

A rival faction favour [[Lord Methuselis\|Lord Methuselis]], ho has seem ominous (albeit contradictory) portents of [[The End Times\|The End Times]]. He wants the [[Celestial Order\|Celestial Order]] to focus on predicting and countering the threat of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Ruinous Powers]]. His supporters extend their influence ith the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]]’s rulers and some take direct action against agents of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]].

Outside [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], the Celestial College has many holdings, typically observatories dedicated to tracing the flow of [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]]. [[The Tower of Vane\|The Tower of Vane]], for example, stands high in the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]] above [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Ubersreik/Ubersreik\|Ubersreik]], overseen by [[Sibylle Hagerdorn\|Sibylle Hagerdorn]], ho is grateful for the distance between herself and her former rival the [[High Celestial\|High Celestial]].

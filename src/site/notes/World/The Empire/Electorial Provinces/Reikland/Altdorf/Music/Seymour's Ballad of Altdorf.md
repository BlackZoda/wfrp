# Seymour's Ballad of Altdorf
Gather 'round, you people of Altdorf town, Hear my song about our city, up and down.
From palace grand to Fleishmark's mire, Seymour's truth will flow, like flames from fire.

Karl-Franz sits on high, with his eagle's stare, but who cleans up the mess, if the griffin dares?
Is it Tybalt sly, in his coat of grey? No, it's servants toiling, for a pittance they say.

On the Street of Taverns, the ale is flowing, Hooks and Fish make merry, their troubles going.
But watch those smiles, for steel lies near, In Altdorf's shadows, death is always here.

Down in Undercity, where shadows creep, Hide monstrous things, best left asleep.
They plot and scheme, for chaos to reign, While Altdorf's folk, oblivious remain.

But have no fear, good people, Seymour's on the scene, With songs and laughter, our spirits convene.
So raise your mugs, to Altdorf let us cheer, May it stand forever, despite all fear.

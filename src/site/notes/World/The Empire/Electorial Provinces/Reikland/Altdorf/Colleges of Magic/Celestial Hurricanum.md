The Hurricanum is a bizarre engine of war built originally for mobile astronomical observation. Each arrige carries a colossal orrery, constructed around one of the [[Celestial Order\|Celestial Order]]’s precious [[Orbs of Sorcery\|Orbs of Sorcery]]. These powerful magic items were bequeathed by [[Teclis\|Teclis]] to help scry the heavens, but they can also focus [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]] as a weapon.

On the rare occations that the [[High Celestial\|High Celestial]] permits a Hurricanum for warfare, they are taken by acolytes to muster ith the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]]’s armies. By channeling [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]] through the [[Orbs of Sorcery\|Orbs of Sorcery]], the acolytes bring powerful storms to strike at their enemies and even grant nearby soldiers ith subtle foresight. Hurricanums and the [[Orbs of Sorcery\|Orbs of Sorcery]] are highly prized by the [[Celestial Order\|Celestial Order]] – their loss would be keenly felt.

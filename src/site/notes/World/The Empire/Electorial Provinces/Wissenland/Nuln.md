Nuln, known as the *Bastion of the South* and the *Jewel of the Empire* is the capital city of the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Electoral Province]] of [[Wissenland\|Wissenland]], the second largest city of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] and the largest manufacturer and exporter of [[Imperial Blackpowder\|Imperial Blackpowder]] munitions and warmachines, earning itself the title of the *Armory of the Empire*. Sitting at the heart of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] southern trade routes, it stands as an imposing bastion, its fortified wals bristling with enough heavy blackpowder artillery emplacements to arm a fleet of war galleons.

In previous times, Nuln was home to the court of the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] and today it remains [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]’s largest rival, both politically and economically. North of Nuln, the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|River Reik]] is too wide to bridge, and the great bridge at Nuln, whose central section can be raised and lowered through an ingenious mechanism, is one of the great marvels of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. Though there are bridges at [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], where the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] divides into a number of lesser channels, it is a point of some pride in Nuln that none of the capital’s bridges can be said to span the entire width of the mighty [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|River Reik]].

Nuln’s reputation as a city of learning precedes that of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], although it has been overshadowed by the new capital the last few centuries. Nuln is most famed for being home of the [[Imperial Gunnery School\|Imperial Gunnery School]], a sprawling network of forges and smelteries where veteran gunsmiths manufacture and maintain the [[Imperial Artillery Trains\|Imperial Artillery Trains]]. Steam-powered air pumps work night and day to vent the acrid fumes of the blazing foundries, and portions of Nuln are forever wreathed in palls of choking soot and black smoke. Indeed, the dark smog clouds hanging over nuln are such a permanent feature that many travellers use them to navigate. Many counts send their artilleryment to the [[Imperial Gunnery School\|Imperial Gunnery School]] to learn the art of ballistics, and crews trained in Nuln are highly valued.

Nuln is also important diplomatically, as much of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s trade with [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]] and northern [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tilea]] passes through the region.

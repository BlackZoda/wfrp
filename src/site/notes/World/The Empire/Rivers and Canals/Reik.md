## Adventure Hooks
* [[Campaign/Hooks/Shrine On\|Shrine On]]

<hr>

The Reik carries more traffic and trade than all the other rivers in [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] combined. Indeed, so massive is the river that almost half the fresh water of the Old World flows down its length, for it not only trains all the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] with its many tributaries, but, but most of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] beyond. By the time the Reik flows into [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], it is already so wide it frequently appears more lake than river, leaving it impossible to bridge sing standard engineering methods. As this immense watercourse approaches [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] to meet the dark sluggish waters of the [[Talabec\|Talabec]] heavily ladden with black soil from the [[Middle Mountains\|Middle Mountains]].

Where the two rivers meet the heavy sediment makes them split into a complex network of channels that spread outwards to form the foggy [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Altdorf Flats\|Altdorf Flats]]. The sediment has formed extensive mud flats and small islands upon which the city of Altdorf stands. Many of the thinner distributaries caused by this are bridgeable, ensuring [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] is a natural center for trade as it is the only place where the Reik can be crossed on foot for many hundred of miles. This single fact has secured Altdorf’s dominance of the area, both militarily and fiscally.

Beyond the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Altdorf Flats\|Altdorf Flats]], the boggy channels reconverge and the Reik begins its long yourney westwards to the sea. By this point, it is so wide the opposite bank sometimes slips into mist, and so deep that even the largest sea-faring vessels can navigate without fear. Resultingly, warships of the [[Imperial Navy\|Imperial Navy]], some so large to have crews out-numbering the populations of smaller towns, not only patrol these waters, but are built there, most launching from the [[Reiksport\|Reiksport]], a deepwater harbour built on the shores of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]. Rocky islands are common in this last stretch, most of which are secured with ancient fortresses, overridden by river pirates, or abandoned completely, boasting nothing more than a handful of old smugglers’ covers. For long stretches, the banks remain heavily forested and provide potential ambush sites for river pirates, although most vessels are usually more than capable of defending themselves.

The Grünberg canal is a recent addition to the waterways of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]. Commissioned by [[Emperor Lutipold III\|Emperor Lutipold III]] as part of an extensive dowry paid to theprevious [[Baron of Grünburg\|Baron of Grünburg]], it was completed in 2506 [[World/The Empire/History/Imperial Calendar\|IC]] and has seen heavy use ever since. The canal bypasses the treacherous shallows  of the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Reiker Marshes\|Reiker Marshes]] overlooked by [[Castle Reikguard\|Castle Reikguard]], and it now takes much of the river [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Teufel\|Teufel]] [[Barge\|Barge]] traffic heading for [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]. [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Grünburg\|Grünburg]]’s walls protect the tollhouse on the south end of the canal, and any barge wishing to pass it must ay a tax based on the length of the vessel entering the canal system. Queues are common at first light and dusk, but otherwise the tollhouse sees infrequent business. At the north end there is a [[Lock-Keeper’s House\|Lock-Keeper’s House]] on the outskirts of [[Prieze\|Prieze]] that doubles as a [[Barracks\|Barracks]] and [[Campaign/Items/Stables\|Stables]] for the tweleve [[Road Wardens\|Road Wardens]] paid to patrol the canal path and help any barges as required. This help typically comes in the form of impromptu protection taxes, whcih if not paid leave the offending barge open to [[Bandit\|Bandit]] attacks. Attacks that inevitably follow for any who refuse to pay.

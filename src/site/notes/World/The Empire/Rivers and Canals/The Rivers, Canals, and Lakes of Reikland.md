The many rivers splling from the glacial lakes and waterfalls of the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]] down to the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] are central to the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s economic success. [[Barge\|Barges]] brimful with merchandise navigate the largest of these rivers, bringing diverse goods to the capital of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], many of which are then shipped elsewhere in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] or sold down the river to [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]].

To further support the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s wide-ranging mercantile ambitions, the richer [[World/The Empire/Demography/Noble House\|Noble]] and [[Merchant Houses\|Merchant Houses]] invest in extensive canals planned and constructed by [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]’s famous [[Rules/Career/Engineer\|Engineers]]. The impact of these impressive waterways is undeniable, with trade goods moving faster than ever before, but they require maintenance and protection, both of which are costly. To ensure the [[Rules/Career/Wrecker\|Wreckers]] and [[Rules/Career/River Pirate (level 3)\|River Pirates]] the canals attract have minimal impact, [[Road Wardens\|Road Wardens]], [[Rules/Career/Guard\|Guards]], and [[Rules/Career/Riverwarden\|Riverwardens]] are employed as required, but frequently these are little better than the criminals they purportedly thwart.

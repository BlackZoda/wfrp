The Teufel flows down from the mountains to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Ubersreik/Ubersreik\|Ubersreik]], then northwards to the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] through [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Auserwald\|Auserwald]] and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Grünburg\|Grünburg]]. Its waters have a distinctly reddish hue caused by iron deposits in the mud and slit, though storytellers suggest it’s the unending wars between the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]] [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] and [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Goblin\|Goblins]] that have permanently stained the river red. A great deal of rain feeds into the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Teufel\|Teufel]] and it regularly overflows, especially in spring, frequently flooding its mud-filled waters deep into the surrounding forest. [[Campaign/Locations/Buildings of the Reikland/Inns and Taverns/Reikland Inns and Taverns\|Inns]] – often built high to avoid floodwater  are common along the Teufel, as are [[Bandit\|Bandits]], since much of the river runs through the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Reikwald\|Reikwald]]. [[Road Wardens\|Road Wardens]] regularly patrol the banks of the Teufel and they are not fond of any that linger without good reason.

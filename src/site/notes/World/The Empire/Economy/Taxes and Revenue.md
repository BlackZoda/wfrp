Taxes may be levied at a variety of levels. The [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] sets taxes to maintain his court and finance his armies. Provincial rules test taxes to maintain their [[Court\|Court]]s and [[Army\|Army]], as well as to build [[Road\|Road]]s, [[Canal\|Canal]]s and other public infrastructure projects. [[World/The Empire/Politics/Institutions/Town Council\|Town Council]]s set taxes to maintain their [[State Army\|State Army]]s in order to post a [[Watch\|Watch]] and train local [[Rules/Career/Road Warden\|Road Warden]] patrols, and cover the costs of things like [[Town Walls\|Town Walls]] [[Campaign/Locations/Buildings of the Reikland/Municipal Buildings/Reikland Municipal Buildings\|Reikland Municipal Buildings]] and services, and the many monuments and statues along [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s [[Road\|Road]]s.

Not surprisingly, the list of things that can be taxed is endless. In more [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Prime Estate\|Prime Estate]]s, households are expected to pay an annual poll tax of at least 10 shillings per [[Rules/Talents/Doomed\|Doomed]] family member. This is anyone who is at least 10 years old and has been Doomed by a [[Doomsayer\|Doomsayer]] of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]], thus granting the right to work as an adult. Tolls are charged for the use of roads and river locks. Gate taxes are charged on all non-residents entering and leaving [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|Town]]s. [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchant]]s are taxed on merchandise sold. This list goes on and on. Simply: whatever the activity, there is almost certainly a tax on it. Since it is reckoned that only the unpropertied, non-guild, working class actually pays all the taxes imposed on them, the burden on the poor is unbelievably great. Nobles pay hardly any taxes at all, and chartered exemptions free many towns and [[World/The Empire/Politics/Institutions/Guild\|Guild]]s from specific taxes. This is generally why the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]] and local [[Liege Lord\|Liege Lord]]s are forced to have new taxes devised so frequently, so that they have some money.

In fact, there are so many [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Province\|Province]]s, [[World/Other Nations/Cities\|Cities]]s, individuals, and offices with exemptions from taxation, and the requirement to supply troops, that there have been long periods when the defence of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] was severely compromised. [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]], like many of his predecessors, has instructed his treasury officials to invent new taxes to raise money, and then been forced to grant exemptions as interest groups united to defy him. As new taxes are devised every month, charlatans and con artists travel from place to place, collecting taxes that the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] has not actually imposed… yet.

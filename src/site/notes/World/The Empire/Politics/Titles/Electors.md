The real power in the Empire is in the hands of the Electors. Just to complicate matters, the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] is an Elector in his own right. The Electors are the rulers of powerful realms or powerful religions within [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. Even so, their most jealously guarded power is their right to choose a successor when an Emperor dies. An election is a cut-throat affair as each Elector puts forward a candidate who will advance their personal interests, in some cases little more than a puppet. Despite this politicking, some truly greate Emperors have been elected. An equal nuber have been compromise candidates, people of modest ability whose main quality was being inoffensive.

As [[History\|History]] shows, there is much rivalery between the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]]s of the grand provinces. Similarly, there is contention between the two reoligious cults with Electoral votes: the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]] and the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ulric\|Cult of Ulric]]. The Cult of Sigmar is based in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] and powerful in the south. The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ulric\|Cult of Ulric]] is based in [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Middenheim\|Middenheim]] and popular in the north. The [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Grand Thegonist Yorri XV\|Grand Thegonist Yorri XV]] of the Cult of Sigmar can be said to have tipped the balance in his favour by getting two of his subordinates appointed as Electors, while the Cult of Ulric is represented only by [[Ar-Ulric\|Ar-Ulric]], a title meaning “Son of Ulric”.

Although the Electors spend much of their time kockeying for position among themselves, they often show a united front to the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]], the [[Independent Town\|Independent Town]]s, and the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Institutions/Guild\|Guild]]s – all of whom they wish to control as much as possible. Since the power of an Elector derives from lordship over a [[Grand Provinces\|Grand Provinces]], the title is hereditary.

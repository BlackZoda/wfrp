Down through the centuries, there have also been other provincial rulers granted a right to vote for the next [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]]. This right has been granted by Emperors in the past for various political or social reasons, but is not linked to royal title or the right to claim a [[Runefang\|Runefang]]. Currently, [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] only has one Electoral Province ruled by an Elector: the Grand County of [[World/The Empire/Provinces/Mootland\|Mootland]]. This means the [[Elder of the Moot\|Elder of the Moot]] has an [[Electoral Vote\|Electoral Vote]], but no attendant royal or noble privileges.

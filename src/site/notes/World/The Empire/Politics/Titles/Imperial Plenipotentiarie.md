From time to time, the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] also appoints special representatives in the provinces and towns. Officially known as Imperial Plenipotentiaries, these are usually loyal servants sent to look after the Emperor’s interests and keep tabs on his so-called servants. They may also be ambitious or dangerous nobles sent to quiet backwaters to keep them away from the capital ([[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]) and the centre of power.

Whatever the reason behind their appointment, Imperial Plenipotentiaries are widely regarded as little more than court favourites, and few accord them much real respect. [[Rules/Career/Noble\|Noble]]s and [[World/The Empire/Politics/Institutions/Town Council\|Town Council]] cooperate with Plenipotentiaries because of their Imperial commison, but are generally sullen and deeply resentful during any encounter with these “jumped-up lap-dogs”.

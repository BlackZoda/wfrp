Home: [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]]

In theory, the Emporor has absolute control over every aspect of [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]]. However, in practice his power is limited by a range of special privileges granted to various individuals and Imperial offices by the edicts of his predecessors. Emperors find it very difficult to revoke these grants, much as they might want to see them cone.

The office of Emperor is maintained out of a strong national sense of necessity and for religious reasons. The last time the Empire lacked a recognised Emperor, it was almost wiped out during the [[World/The Empire/History/Great War Against Chaos\|Great War Against Chaos]]. Seeing that as just another sign that an Emperor is always needed, the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]] believes that “*[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s Heir”* should always lead the Empire. They claim that Sigmar’s will is made manifest through an Emperor sitting on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s throne, and strongly support its continued relevance.

Like all his predecessors, [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] swore an oath that he would “at all times govern and maintain the majesty of the Empire”, but this has been no easy task.

The Emperor govern through the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Institutions/Council of State\|Council of State]], [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Imperial Plenipotentiarie\|Imperial Plenipotentiarie]]s,  [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]]s, and [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector\|Elector]]s.

The internal structure of guilds varies enormously across [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], although most of those in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] follow the same basic pattern, with minor variations. A [[Guild Council\|Guild Council]] madeup of a dozen or so of the guild’s most senior members elects a [[Guildmaster\|Guildmaster]], who chairs regular Council meetings that make all policy decisions from setting membership fees to holding ad hoc trials for those who transgress guild laws. Vacant places on the Council are very rare, and usually only araise following a member’s death. The surviving members meet in closed session to fill the empty seat by invitation.

In small towns and villages, there might not be enough members to justify such a formal structure, and in many such cases a town or city guild will extend its control to cover the nearby vilages.

The guilds’ collective power is subtle, yet potent. Since they are the representatives of skilled labour and moneyed wealth, they have managed to change the will of the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]], provincial rulers, and town authorities on many occasions. Guilds have been known to become involved in riots, revolts, and strikes as more blatant manifistations of their strength, and it is widely recognized that threre is a struggle for power between the rural propertied interests represented by the nobility, and the urban oligarhies who run the towns, and the guilds who control labour and trade. This struggle become especially fierce when the guilds are excluded from offices and privileges granted to the former two groups.

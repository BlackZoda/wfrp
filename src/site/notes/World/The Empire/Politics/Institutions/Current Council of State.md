In the time of [[0_GM/Enemy in Shadows/Fiender i Skyggene\|Fiender i Skyggene]] the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Institutions/Council of State\|Council of State]] has been largel unchanged since [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] first rose to power a decade ago. Its members are old, powerful, and used to wielding absolute authority. Like his father before him, Emperor Karl-Franz grants each of his councillors and broad-reaching “portofolio”, an official title, and allows them to wield the full authority of the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] in their area of control.

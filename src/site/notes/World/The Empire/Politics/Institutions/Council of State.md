* [[World/The Empire/Politics/Institutions/Current Council of State\|Current Council of State]]

Broadly speaking, the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] has the power to set [[Taxes\|Taxes]], rasie [[Armies\|Armies]], and issue [[Edicts\|Edicts]]. The Emperor appoints a Council of State to assist him in the day-do-day running of the [[Imperial Court\|Imperial Court]], and to advice him on matters financial, military, and beyond. The membership of this Council – which always includes the [[World/Religion and Belief/Titles/Grand Theogonist\|Grand Theogonist]], the head of the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]] – is usually selected from the families of the most ancient lineage. As such, it changes very infreqently.

The Council has no constitutional authority, but its members still wield considerable power since they control access to the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]]. Whenever they manage to agree, they can exercise unparalleled influence on him. The [[World/Religion and Belief/Titles/Grand Theogonist\|Grand Theogonist]] who is arguly the most powerful individual in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], is usually beind the Council’s more urgent recommendations.

[[Emperor Mattheus II\|Emperor Mattheus II]], the grandfather of [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] and strong believer in democratic principles attempted to draw up an Imperial Constitution based around the Council. This aroused suspicion of the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]]s and the Coundil of State, and the idea was quitetly abandoned. It takes a strong Emperor indeed to go against a united Council of State.

The Coundil meets (at least) monthly at the [[Matteus-Ferrand Chamber\|Matteus-Ferrand Chamber]] of the [[Volkshall\|Volkshall]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], though it is a rare day when more than three members sit simultaneously. When more are present it’s almost always in response to a momentous event.

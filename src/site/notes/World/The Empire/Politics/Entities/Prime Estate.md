The Prime Estates are ruled by the vassals of the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Electoral Province]] and [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Province\|Province]]. Whether the land is a duchy, march, leage, county, palatinate, barony, or something else, its ruler is considered one of the most important nobles in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], and can vote in the [[Prime Estate Diet\|Prime Estate Diet]]. Most Prime Estates are split into many smaller [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Fief\|Fief]].

The Prime Estates of each *Electorial Province* or *Province* go by different names, and are ruled in different ways. For example, in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] the Prime Estates are known as the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Estates/Reikland Estates\|Reikland Estates]], and they are granted many rights to restrict the authority of the [[Prince of Reikland\|Prince of Reikland]], their Elector Count ([[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]]). However, the [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Middenheim\|Middenheim]] Prime Estates do not have the same powers. Instead, [[Grand Duke Boris Todbringer\|Grand Duke Boris Todbringer]] has absolte authority by law, and cannot be overruled or legally opposed by his vassals, no matter the insult or injury he cases.

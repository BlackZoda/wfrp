As far as many people in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] are concerned, the most important type of government with a direct bearing on their daily lives is the nearest town. This is the place where taxes are collected, military service is rendered, trails held and merchandise sold. The exact governmental structure varies from town to town: some have autocratic governors appointed by the provincial ruler, some have elected councils, some have [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Imperial Plenipotentiarie\|Imperial Plenipotentiarie]], the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]]’s direct representatives, and a few seem to muddle along with no obvious administration at all.

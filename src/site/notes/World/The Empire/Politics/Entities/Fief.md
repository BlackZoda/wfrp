A fief is ruled by [[World/The Empire/Demography/Noble House\|Noble House]] that has sworn loyalty directly to its [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]], but not to a lesser noble, perhaps a ruler of a [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Prime Estate\|Prime Estate]]. However, that doesn’t mean such nobles wield any less power in their personal domain. Indeed, minor fiefdoms are some of the most autocratic regions in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], where peasants are little better than slaves and the crack of the whip is an everyday occurrence.

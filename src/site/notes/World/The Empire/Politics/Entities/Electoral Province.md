The Electoral Provinces are the realms within [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] whose rulers are responsible for electing a new [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] when the incumbent dies or abdicates.

Historically, there is considerable rivalery and tension between Electoral Provinces. Fortunately for the unity of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], these ambitions have been suppressed in recent decades. The memory of centuries of bitter civil war ensures most political machinations are conducted discretly.

The rulers of the Electoral Provinces are effectively sovereigns in their own realms, meaning they are largely able to exercise their authority as they see fit within the bountaries of their domains without needing to ask the Emperor’s permission. Some are more independent than others, thanks to special privileges granted by previous Emperors. For example, the [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Fristadt\|Fristadt]] of [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Middenheim\|Middenheim]] and [[Talabheim\|Talabheim]] send no taxes to the Imperial coffers, and only have to supply troops during times of war when all the other provinces have been exhausted.

The style of government also varies between the Electoral Provinces. [[Talabecland\|Talabecland]], for example, is rigidly autocratic, while [[Sudenland\|Sudenland]] has many democratic institution. However, these variations usually have little meaning for the average citizen of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. No matter where you go, the rich are still rich and the poor will do all the work.

All of the Electoral Provinces are ruled by either [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]] or [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector\|Elector]].

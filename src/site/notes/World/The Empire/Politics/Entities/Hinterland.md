Whatever its form of government, a [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|Town]]’s authority rarely ends at its walls. Usually, all farms and vilages within a radious of some 10–20 miles look to the town for protection. In return, they are subject to its [[Laws\|Laws]] and [[Taxes\|Taxes]], though ancient borders ant rivalries between [[World/The Empire/Demography/Noble House\|Noble House]] frequently interfere with this arrangement.

**Leopold I** of [[House Bildhofen\|House Bildhofen]], [[Grand Duke of Middenland\|Grand Duke of Middenland]], [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]], and [[Prince of Carroburg\|Prince of Carroburg]], is based in [[Carroburg\|Carroburg]]. Aged 46, his named heir is his younger brother, [[Baron Siegfried\|Baron Siegfried]]. He has five legitimate children of his own, but all are disinherited until they can suceed in *”impressing him”*. His eldest child, [[Primus\|Primus]], is never likely to do this (see **[[0_GM/Enemy in Shadows/Fiender i Skyggene\|Fiender i Skyggene]]**, page 62).

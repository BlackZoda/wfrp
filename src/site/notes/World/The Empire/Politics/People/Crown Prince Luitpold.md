Crown Prince Luitpold, 16, is [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]]’s son and air to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]. Luitpold used to be heir to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], but was disinherited from the title for reasons unknown, and then banished to the [[West March\|West March]]. The heir to Reikland is now [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Crown Prince Wolfgang\|Crown Prince Wolfgang]].

**Gustav XI** of [[House Krieglitz\|House Krieglitz]], [[Grand Duke of Talabecland\|Grand Duke of Talabecland]], [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]], [[Count of Krieglitz\|Count of Krieglitz]], and “[[Grátal\|Grátal]]” – *”Beloved of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]]”* – isbased in [[Castle Schloss\|Castle Schloss]], just outside the town of [[Herzig\|Herzig]]. Aged 22, he has no publicly named heir due to a *”dipute of lineage”* concerning his younger brother, [[Gunter\|Gunter]], lodged by their uncle, [[Count Ottlin off Gehrandt\|Count Ottlin off Gehrandt]].

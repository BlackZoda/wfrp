**Hans-Hals I** of [[House Tassenick\|House Tassenick]], aged 62, is [[Grand Prince of Ostland\|Grand Prince of Ostland]], an [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Count]], [[Prince of Wolfenburg\|Prince of Wolfenburg]], and [[Graf of Tassenick\|Graf of Tassenick]]. His named heir is his only son, the wildly charismatic [[Crown Prince Hergard\|Crown Prince Hergard]], 32, a man known for his deep passions.

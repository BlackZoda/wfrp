Home: [[World/The Empire/Infrastructure/Coaching House\|Coaching House]]

Castle Rock Coaches is fighting hard to keep its share of thecapital route as [[World/The Empire/Infrastructure/Four Seasons Coaches of Altdorf\|Four Seasons]] expands. It has made arrangements with many independent coaching inns, and has blocked the efforts of Four Seasons to take control of several inns between [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] and [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Middenheim\|Middenheim]].

# Borgklippen Drosjehus fra Middenheim
Hjem: [[World/The Empire/Infrastructure/Coaching House\|Drosjehus]]

Borgklippen Drosjehus kjemper hardt for å beholde sin andel av hovedstadsruten mens [[World/The Empire/Infrastructure/Four Seasons Coaches of Altdorf\|Fire Sesonger]] ekspanderer. De har inngått avtaler med mange uavhengige vertshus og har blokkert Fire Sesongers forsøk på å ta kontroll over flere vertshus mellom [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] og [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Middenheim\|Middenheim]].

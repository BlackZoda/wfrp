Knownfor its majestic capital and major cities with striking architecture and intricate canals, the variable quality of the Empire’s roads and highways is in stark contrast. A few days of bad weather can turn a firm, smooth highway into an almost impassable morass. While some effort is made to keep [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s great highways open, the smaller roads – some little better than dirt tracks at the best of times – are often merely a collection of puddles and mud.

The busiest overland routes are the roads from [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Middenland/Middenland\|Middenland]], the [[Old Forest Road\|Old Forest Road]] leading from [[Wurtbad\|Wurtbad]] to [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Middenheim\|Middenheim]], and the routes over the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]] into [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]]. Beyond these, countless miles of lesser roads link [[World/Other Nations/Cities\|Cities]]s, [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|Town]]s, and [[Village\|Village]]s together, running through the deep forests and across [[Hill\|Hill]]s and [[Plain\|Plain]]s.

Coaching inns are spred out along all major highways, and in towns along the way. They are often saced about 30 miles apart, which is about half a day’s trabel by coach or horseback, or, for the hardy, a full day’s travel on foot in reasonable weather.

The [[Campaign/Locations/Buildings of the Reikland/Inns and Taverns/Hest & Kjerre\|Coach and Horses]] is a typical Imperial coaching inn. It can be used as a model for any others the Characters may visit on their travels. A similar example, the [[Campaign/Locations/Buildings of the Reikland/Inns and Taverns/Pouncing Pegasus\|Pouncing Pegasus]].

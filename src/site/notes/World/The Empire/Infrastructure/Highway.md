The state of [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]]’s highway system owes much to [[Emperor Wihelm the Wise\|Emperor Wihelm the Wise]]. Having heard of the extraordinary delivery speeds offered by the [[Ostland Checkpoint Service\|Ostland Checkpoint Service]] used along the [[Kislev Verge\|Kislev Verge]] and into the [[Northern Marches\|Northern Marches]] Wilhelm decided to duplicate the system across [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]]. In a decree of 2453, a system of Imperial messengers was created to carry news rapidly between all of [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]]’s great cities, especially in times of war, to complement that [[Imperial Post\|Imperial Post]]. By commandeering fresh horses from coaching inns along the way, a messanger could travel from [[World/Other Nations/Kislev\|Kislev]]’s borders to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] in 6 days, and from the notorious [[Black Fire Pass\|Black Fire Pass]] in just 11 days.

The 2453 decree demanded the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Prime Estate\|Prime Estate]]s set aside cavalery from the [[State Regiment\|State Regiment]]s they were obliged to raise. Naturally, the nobility of [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]] immediately looked for exemptions, and many simply ignored the “demand”. However, as the scheme was sensible and costs were low, many nobles provided patrols for their local area.

The decree also laid down standards for the size and quality of [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]]’s arterial highways. The rulers of each province were made responsible for the maintenance of all important highways that passed through their lands.

According to [[Emperor Wihelm the Wise\|Emperor Wihelm the Wise]]’s decree, an [[Imperial Highway\|Imperial Highway]] must be 20 ft (6 meters) wide (enough to permit two wagons to pass comfortably) and constructed of hard, crushed stone tamped down to provide a firm sirface. It must also be constructed with a camber, allowing rain to wash away to either side, allowing rain to wash away to either side. The roads that run alongside the great rivers, such as the [[Talabheim\|Talabheim]] to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] road, are designated Imperial highways, but because the river takes the bulk of heavy traffic, they tend not to be maintained to a high standard. Often, the road is of "highway quality" for a few miles beyond the city but becomes a [[World/The Empire/Infrastructure/Major Road\|Major Road]] after that.

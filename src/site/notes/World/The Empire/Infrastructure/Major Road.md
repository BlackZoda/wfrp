Home: [[World/The Empire/Infrastructure/Roads and Highways\|Roads and Highways]]

Major roads are maintained by the communities they serve, oftenfunded by tolls or taxes on goods passing particular spots. Those closest to a [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|Town]] or [[World/The Old World/Cities\|Cities]] are the best maintained, 8–12 ft (2,5–3,5 meters) wide wwith occasional wider spots where traffic in opposite direction can pass. Further from the nearest town, their chipped-stone or gravel surfaces turn to dirt and they become narrower, with the vegetation to either side growng inwards.

Mootland is the self-governed [[Grand Province\|Grand Province]] in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] founded and ruled by the [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]]s. The [[Elder of the Moot\|Elder of the Moot]] – currently [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Elder Hisme Stoutheart\|Elder Hisme Stoutheart]] – is the custodian of the [[Haffenlyver\|Haffenlyver]], an ancient embroided scrill detailing the chief boodlines of their primary clans, said to be the greatest treasure of the [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]]s.

After five decades of extraordinary rule, [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] vanishes. To avoid destroying [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s empire, the tribal chiefs eventually agree to vote for a new [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] from their own number, and select [[Siegrich of the Asoborn Tribe\|Siegrich of the Asoborn Tribe]]. Thus, the counts become [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Counts]], and each swears to be a companion and protector of their elected [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]].

[[Emperor Ludwig I\|Emperor Ludwig I]], commonly called *Ludwig the Fat*, grants the [[World/Religion and Belief/Titles/Grand Theogonist\|Grand Theogonist]] a vote to select the next [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]]. Though some [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Counts]] cry foul, and other [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|Cults]] are stounded at the blatant favouritism, [[Emperor Ludwig I\|Emperor Ludwig I]] appears far more interested in the lavish banquetes prepared for him by the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Reikdorf]].

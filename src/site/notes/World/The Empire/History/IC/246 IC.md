A [[High Temple to Sigmar\|High Temple to Sigmar]] is completed in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Reikdorf]], and it acts as the centre of worship for a [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|cult]] that now extends throughout [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. This rapid expansion leads to open conflict with the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ulric\|Cult of Ulric]] and the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Taal\|Cult of Taal]].

[[The Reign of Magnus the Pious\|The Reign of Magnus the Pious]]. [[Floating/Magnus the Pious\|Magnus von Bildhofen]] is elected [[Floating/Magnus the Pious\|Emperor Magnus I]], the first elected [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] representing all [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Grand Provinces]] for almost a thousand years. Riding the wave of popularity he secred by winning [[The Great War Against Chaos\|The Great War Against Chaos]], he immediately initiates sweeping reforms to end the corruption that previously brought the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]] to its knees, creating new laws to limit the [[World/The Empire/Demography/Noble House\|Nobility]], [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|Cults]], [[World/The Empire/Politics/Institutions/Guild\|Guilds]] and much more. He also oversees the foundation of may new institution, including the formal creation of the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Empire State Armies\|Empire State Armies]], [[Imperial Navy\|Imperial Navy]], and, controversially, the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], making magic legal across all the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]] for the first time.

[[Fourth Siege of Altdorf\|Fourth Siege of Altdorf]]. The [[Liche King\|Liche King]], [[Arkhan the Black\|Arkhan the Black]], invades the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] with a seemingly endless swarm of walking corpses that march straight for [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]. Once the siege begins, [[Arkhan the Black\|Arkhan the Black]] slips unnoticed into the [[High Temple to Sigmar\|High Temple to Sigmar]] and steals the dreaded [[Liber Mortis\|Liber Mortis]] kept within. Minutes after he escapes, his army colapses, leaving thousands of caracasses mouldering outside the city walls as state soldiers look on in confusion and relief.

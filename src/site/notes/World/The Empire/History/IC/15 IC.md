[[Battle of the River Reik\|Battle of the River Reik]]. [[Nagash, the Lord of Undeath\|Nagash, the Lord of Undeath]], gathers a shambling horde of unliving monsters, and attempts to claim the [[Crown of Sorcery\|Crown of Sorcery]] from [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]. The [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] almost overwhelm the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reiklanders]] and their [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]] allies, but, after an exhausting battle, [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] eventually strikes [[Nagash\|Nagash]] down, causing the unliving army to crumble to dust.

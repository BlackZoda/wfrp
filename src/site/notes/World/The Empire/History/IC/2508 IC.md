[[Malathrax the Mighty\|Malathrax the Mighty]], an enormous [[Doomfire Dragon\|Doomfire Dragon]], terrorises the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]], razing villages and taking livestock, before its driven north across the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Hägercrybs\|Hägercrybs]]. After several months of pursuit, and the eradication of the entire [[Knightly Order of the Ebon Sword\|Knightly Order of the Ebon Sword]], the [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Dragon\|Dragon]] is slain by the [[Imperial Huntsmarshal\|Imperial Huntsmarshal]], [[Markus Wulfhart\|Markus Wulfhart]], who places three arrows in its heart.

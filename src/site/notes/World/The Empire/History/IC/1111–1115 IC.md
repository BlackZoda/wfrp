[[Rules/Disease and Infection/The Black Plague\|The Black Plague]]. Disease sweeps through the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], killing nine in every ten people, and debilitating half of those left alive. [[Skaven\|Skaven]] then erupt from the [[Under-Empire\|Under-Empire]] and attacks. When [[Emperor Boris I\|Emperor Boris I]] dies of the plague in [[World/The Empire/History/IC/1115 IC\|1115 IC]], no-one replaces him in the ensuing anarchy.

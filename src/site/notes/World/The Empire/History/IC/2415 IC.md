[[The Night of a Thousand Arcane Duels\|The Night of a Thousand Arcane Duels]]. War erupts between the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Eight Colleges of Magic]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], razing large sections of the city and resulting in the death of six of the eight [[Patriarch\|Patriarch]]s. The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]] pressures the [[Prince of Altdorf\|Prince of Altdorf]] and [[Emperor Dieter IV\|Emperor Dieter IV]] to lock the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges]] down, whie they do, resulting in the execution of many [[Floating/Magister\|Magisters]], and effectively brining legal magic use to an end in the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]]. Many of the surviving magisters flee the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges]], never to return.

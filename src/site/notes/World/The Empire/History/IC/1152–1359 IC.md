[[Age of Wars\|Age of Wars]]. [[Emperor Mandred II\|Emperor Mandred II]] is assassinated by the [[Skaven\|Skaven]] in [[1152 IC\|1152 IC]]. The [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Counts]] cannot agree on a successor as they are too concerned about rivals invading their devastated [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Grand Provinces]], so a fraught interregnum ensues for over two centuries. During this time, the [[Princes of the Reikland\|Princes of the Reikland]] rule their [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Grand Province]] unfettered as civil war rages across [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]].

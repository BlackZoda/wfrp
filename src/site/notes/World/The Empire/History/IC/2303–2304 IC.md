[[The Great War Against Chaos\|The Great War Against Chaos]]. [[Asavar Kul\|Asavar Kul]], [[Everchosen of Chaos\|Everchosen of Chaos]] leads a horde of unimaginable size into [[World/Other Nations/Kislev/Kislev\|Kislev]], laying waste to everything in his path. In response [[Floating/Magnus the Pious\|Magnus von Bildhofen]], a young [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Wissenland/Nuln\|Nulner]] [[Rules/Career/Noble\|Noble]] claiming to be inspired by [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]], rallies the fractured and broken [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]] into a mighty army to relieve [[World/Other Nations/Kislev/Kislev\|Kislev]]. He eventually defeats [[Asavar Kul\|Asavar Kul]] at the [[Gates of Kislev\|Gates of Kislev]] alongside [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Gospodarinyi\|Kislevite]], [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]], and [[World/Races/Elf\|Elven]] alies.

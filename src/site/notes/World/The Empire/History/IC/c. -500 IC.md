The [[Unberogen\|Unberogen]] tribe of [[World/Races/Human\|Humans]] settle the land where [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] now lies, and begin fortifying the area. It will be sacked many times by rival tribes [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastmen]], [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Orc\|Orc]]s, [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Goblin\|Goblin]]s, and other vile creatures. Nevertheless, the site is strategically important at the confluence of the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] and [[Talabec\|Talabec]], and a mixture of conquest and trade helps the settlement flourish. Soon, scholars claim, the fortified town is known as the ““rich village”, or Reichsdorf. As centuries pass, this becomes Reikdorf, with the surrounding land called and the Reikland.

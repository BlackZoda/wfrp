[[The Third Parravon War\|The Third Parravon War]]. The [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Bretonnian\|Bretonnian]] duchy of [[Floating/Parravon\|Parravon]] invades the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] across the [[Grey Lady Pass\|Grey Lady Pass]], claiming the military escalation in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Ubersreik/Ubersreik\|Ubersreik]] caused by the new [[State Army\|State Army]] breaces an ancient treaty. They are quickly driven back, and the following year [[Floating/Parravon\|Parravon]] is besieged. Eventually, after a year of occasional skirmishes outside [[Floating/Parravon\|Parravon]]’s extraordinarily high walls, and a great deal of standing around shouting insuts, the [[King of Bretonnia\|King of Bretonnia]] treats on behalf of [[Floating/Parravon\|Parravon]] with [[Floating/Magnus the Pious\|Emperor Magnus I]], ending the war.

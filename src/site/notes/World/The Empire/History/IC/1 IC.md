[[The First War Against Chaos\|The First War Against Chaos]]. Te newly founded [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]] is attacked by theforces of [[Morkar the Uniter\|Morkar the Uniter]], [[Everchosen of Chaos\|Everchosen of Chaos]]. The war is desperate, short, and devastating. Eventually, Morkar is slain by [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] in a day-long battle that was said to have split the earth and rent the sky.

[[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]] buys its independence from the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]] by birthing [[Emperor Dieter IV\|Emperor Dieter IV]], reforming itself as [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|The Wasteland]] with [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]] as its capital. Using anti-corruption laws put in place by [[Floating/Magnus the Pious\|Magnus the Pious]] almost a hundred years earlier, the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Counts]] depose Dieter in the ensuing scandal. He is replaced by [[Grand Prince Wilhelm\|Grand Prince Wilhelm]] of [[Floating/House Holswig-Schliestein\|House Holswig-Schliestein]] of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], who si name [[Emperor Wilhelm III\|Emperor Wilhelm III]], starting the imperial dynasty that rules the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]] to this day.

The [[Battle for Grootscher Marsh\|Battle for Grootscher Marsh]]. [[Emperor Wilhelm III\|Emperor Wilhelm III]], under pressure from the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector Count\|Elector Counts]] to respond to the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]]’s seccession from the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]], gathers the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Empire State Armies\|State Armies]] to invade [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]]. In late Autumn, the opposing forces meet at the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Grootscher Marsh\|Grootscher Marsh]] just outside [[Siert\|Siert]], and the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]] is routed by the advanced [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]] navy, well-trained mercenaries and militias, and the magics of the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]]’s [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elven]] allies. Begrudgingly, Wilhelm verbally recognises the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]]’s independence, but refuses to ratify it with a treaty. [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]] accepts this and draws their new border at [[Siert\|Siert]].

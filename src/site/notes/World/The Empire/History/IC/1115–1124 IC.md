[[The Rat Wars\|The Rat Wars]]. [[Skaven\|Skaven]] move to systematically enslave the remaining population of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], and effectively wipe-out what remains of the [[Drakwald\|Drakwald]] province. In response, [[Elector Count Mandred of Middenland\|Elector Count Mandred of Middenland]] rallies a desperate defence, and with the help of [[World/Races/Elf\|Elven]] allies from the [[Laurelorn Forest\|Laurelorn Forest]], finally drives the [[Skaven\|Skaven]] back below ground. At the end of the war, the victorious Mandred is elected emperor by the three surviving elector bloodlines.

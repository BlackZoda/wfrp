[[Johann Helstrum\|Johann Helstrum]] builds a temple to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Reikdorf]].

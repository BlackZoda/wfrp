Home: [[The Poisoned Feast\|The Poisoned Feast]]

The [[Great Enchanter\|Great Enchanter]], [[Constant Drachenfels\|Constant Drachenfels]], in one of his most famous treacheries, invites the [[Electoral Emperor Carolus II\|Electoral Emperor Carolus II]], and the entire imperial court and its families, to a great feast held in his castle. Drachenfels poisons his guests, paralysing them. He then starves them to death in front of the marvellous banquet he prepared. This wipes out several important noble bloodlines of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], deeply destabilising the grand province and the [[Electoral Emperor\|Electoral Emperor]]s as a whole.

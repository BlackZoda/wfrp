[[The Time of Two Emperors\|The Time of Two Emperors]]. In a desperate bid to end the bloodshed, the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Electors\|Electors]] meet in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] and eventually agree on a choice for emperor, crowning [[Elector Count Wilhelm of Stirland\|Elector Count Wilhelm of Stirland]] as emperor by a majority of one. [[Elector Countess Ottila of Talabecland\|Elector Countess Ottila of Talabecland]] is outraged at this, and claims the vote is rigged. When she returns to [[Talabheim\|Talabheim]] she declares herself [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Empress]] without a vote, and is supported in her claim by the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Taal\|Cult of Taal]] and the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ulric\|Cult of Ulric]]. She then outlaws the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]] in [[Talabecland\|Talabecland]], a decree that stands for almost a thousand years. The civil wars escalate.

[[Rise of the Drakwald\|Rise of the Drakwald]]: [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastmen]] and other vile creatures swarm from beneath the twisted bowers of the [[Drakwald\|Drakwald]], sacking villages, towns, and fortifications accross the [[Drakwald\|Drakwald]] province. When the last heir to the [[Drakwald\|Drakwald]] throne is slain in battle with an enormous [[Bestigor\|Bestigor]], [[Emperor Boris I\|Emperor Boris I]] chooses to place the [[Drakwald Runefang\|Drakwald Runefang]] in his [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] palace. Publicly, when a suitable heir is found, the [[Runefang\|Runefang]] will be passed on. Privately, Boris has no intention of ever giving up the magical blade.

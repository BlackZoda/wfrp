[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] names the twelve tribal chiefs his “counts”, which modern scholars claim can be sourced in the [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Classical\|Classical]] word *comes*, meaning “companion”, for they were his companions on the battlefield against the [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]] and against the [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] tribes from the north.

A merchant-train from [[Karaz-a-Karak\|Karaz-a-Karak]] is ambushed by [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Orc\|Orc]]s, and they capture [[King Kurgan Ironbeard\|King Kurgan Ironbeard]]. [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] rescues the king, and in return is named a [[dawonger\|dawonger]], Dwarf-friend, and given the king’s greatest heirloom, the magical warhammer [[World/Religion and Belief/Holy Items/Ghal-Maraz\|Ghal-Maraz]].

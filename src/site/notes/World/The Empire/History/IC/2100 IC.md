After the inconclusive [[Battle of Four Armies\|Battle of Four Armies]] ended in multiple assassination attempts by supposed allies on [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] side, it is decided that it is time to elect an [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] to pull everyone together. [[Elector Count Helmut\|Elector Count Helmut]] is the most popular candidate for this, until its discovered he is actually a zombie in the thrall of [[Konrad von Carstein\|Konrad von Carstein]], the very [[Vampire Lord\|Vampire Lord]] they are trying to defeat. All plans to elect an emperor are abandoned.

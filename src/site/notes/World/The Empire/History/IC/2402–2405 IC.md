[[The Fourth Parravon War\|The Fourth Parravon War]]. The [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Bretonnian\|Bretonnian]] duchy of [[Floating/Parravon\|Parravon]] again invades the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] across the [[Grey Lady Pass\|Grey Lady Pass]]. [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Ubersreik/Ubersreik\|Ubersreik]] is besieged twice during the war, but doesn’t fall. Peace is eventually brokered by the intervention of [[Emperor Dieter IV\|Emperor Dieter IV]] who agrees to pay the [[Floating/Parravon\|Parravonese]] an extortionate sum of money to retreat, drawing much criticism at court.

Grom!]] The [[Goblin Warboss\|Goblin Warboss]] [[Grom the Paunch\|Grom the Paunch]] leads an enormous horde of [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]] across the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]], sacking many [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] towns and villages, before eventually heading westwards, undefeated, where he then takes to the sea. Te lack of [[Floating/Magister\|Magisters]] from the suspended [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]] is widely blamed for the repeated military defeats suffered by [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s armies.

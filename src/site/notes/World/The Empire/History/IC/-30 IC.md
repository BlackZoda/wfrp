A twin-tailed comet streaks through the sky, heralding the birth of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] to [[Chief Björn\|Chief Björn]] of the [[Unberogen\|Unberogen]] tribe in Reikdorf. The comet leads a crazed warparty of [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Orc\|Orc]]s to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s birthplace, and his mother, [[Griselda\|Griselda]], is killed in the attack, leaving [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] with a life-long hatred of [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]].

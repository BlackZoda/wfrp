[[The Time of Three Emperors\|The Time of Three Emperors]]. After [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Sigmarites]] botch an assassination attempt when [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Electors\|Electors]] refuse to back their choice for the next [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]], the [[Elector Count of Middenland\|Elector Count of Middenland]] denounces the imperial elections as a corrupt sham, and declares *hiself* as the rightful emperor. He has the support of the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ulric\|Cult of Ulric]], which had recently fallen out with the [[Talabecland\|Talabecland]] emperors.

[[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] now has three Emperors: The [[Electoral Emperor\|Electoral Emperor]] supported by the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]], the [[Ottilian Emperor\|Ottilian Emperor]] supported by the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Taal\|Cult of Taal]], and the [[Wolf Emperor\|Wolf Emperor]] supported by the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ulric\|Cult of Ulric]]. The civil wars intensify.

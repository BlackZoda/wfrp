1000 years after [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] was crowned [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]], the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]] completes a new [[High Temple to Sigmar\|High Temple to Sigmar]] in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Reikdorf]]. It is the largest temple in all the [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]], and cements [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] worship as the primary [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|Cult]] in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]. In celebration, [[Emperor Ludwig I\|Emperor Ludwig I]] renames his city [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], demonstrating its age and importance to [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] as a whole.

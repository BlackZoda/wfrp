Taing advantage of the divided [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|Empire]], the [[Orc Warboss Gorbad Ironclaw\|Orc Warboss Gorbad Ironclaw]] invades with a tide of [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]] at his back, destroying the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Electoral Province\|Grand Province]] of [[Solland\|Solland]], sacking [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Wissenland/Nuln\|Nuln]] and much of [[Wissenland\|Wissenland]], and eventually sweeping through the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] razing almost a third of its settlements before his green wave eventually breaks against the walls of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] – but not before the [[Prince of Altdorf\|Prince of Altdorf]], the [[Electoral Emperor\|Electoral Emperor]], [[Sigismund IV\|Sigismund IV]] is killed.

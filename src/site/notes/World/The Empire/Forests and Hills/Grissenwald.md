The south-easternmost end of the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Reikwald\|Reikwald]] branches southwards along the [[Stirland\|Stirland]] border and thins out as it heads uprivers towards the [[City-State\|City-State]] of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Wissenland/Nuln\|Nuln]]. This wide section of the forest is known locally as the Grisenwald, a tight woodland packed with distorted trees and twisted undergrowth, the deapths of which are said to be swarming with [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastman]], [[Whitch\|Whitch]]es, and tribes of feral [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Mutant\|Mutant]]s. Because of this, most local woodsmen travel in groups and seldom stay outdoors come nightfall, and its commonplace to find fluttering bills posted on roadside trees offering rewards for the retrieval of lost family and friends from the bowels of the forest.

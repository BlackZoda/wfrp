South of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] an outcorp of pine trees grows down the southern face of the magical [[Amber Hills\|Amber Hills]] and spills into the depths of the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Reikwald\|Reikwald]]. Called bloodpine for its deep-maroon wood, the lumber here is greatly desired by the [[Rules/Career/Artisan\|Artisan]]s of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], and is most often used to produce exquisite furniture destined for the high-class markets of [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]] and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Wissenland/Nuln\|Nuln]]. Recently, bloodpine has become hard to source, for the Bloodpine Woods are plagued by [[Forest Goblin\|Forest Goblin]]s of the [[Spiderclaw\|Spiderclaw]] tribe who’ve managed to tame a handful of swift-moving [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Giant Spider\|Giant Spider]]s. Few dare to work there now, for those attempting to cut the trees simply disappear, their cries echoing from the mists high in the trees before suddenly falling silent. This has, of course only served to increase the value of bloodpine, which has deepl enraged purchases, several of whom have taken to hiring mercenaries and private parties to clear the [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Goblin\|Goblin]]s, figuring it is cheaper to throw bodies at the problem than pay more for their new filing cabinet.

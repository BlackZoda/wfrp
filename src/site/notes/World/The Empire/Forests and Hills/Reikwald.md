The Reikwald blankets almost all of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] outside the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]], only thinning around the jagged [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Skaag Hills\|Skaag Hills]] or where it is cleared by the towns and villages lining the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] river and its many tributaries. Most travellers through the region prefer the relatively safety of river travel to journying along uncertain roads, and with good reason; the *Reikwald* is a favoured haunt of outcasts, cut-throats, and other lawless folk. While all majour routes are patrolled by [[Rules/Career/Road Warden\|Road Warden]]s, their numbers are too few and the roads too long, so it’s not uncommon to encounter upturned coaches and other signs of banditry. The majority of the open clearings and abandoned ruins from past wars are claimed as camps by outlaws or by one of the herds of [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastman]] roaming the deeper wood, and forays to clear these isolated placs by regiments of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] [[State Army\|State Army]] are not uncommon.

In most parts, the Reikwald’s canopy is not so overgrown as to block sunlight, but its depths are frequently gloomy and heavy fogs are common, especially along the marshier stretches of the river [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]]. Scholars claim before [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] was founded the [[Unberogen\|Unberogen]] tribe of [[World/Races/Human\|Human]]s ranged across this misty forest alongside older trives whose names are now lost to memory. Concentric rings of carved standing stones called [[Ogham\|Ogham]]s still exist from this era, though many are overgrown and almost impossible to find without a guide. Isolated communities still following ancient ways – old beliefs said to predate the coming of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] – are sid to hold such sites sacred. Intellectuals from the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]] postulate terrible battles were once fought for these ancient, megalithic sites, and it’s not uncommon to find richer magisters funding explorations into the Reikwald’s depths to learn more about the magical stones.

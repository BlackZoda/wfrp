Home: [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]

Although [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Middenland/Middenland\|Middenland]] to the north claims many famous marshlands – such as the [[Furdienst\|Furdienst]], [[Midden Marshes\|Midden Marshes]], and [[Shadensumpf\|Shadensumpf]] – the river banks of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] are no less waterlogged, frequently giving way to fens, mires, and dangerous mud plains, especially down the length of mighty [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|River Reik]]. The largest of these is the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Grootscher Marsh\|Grootscher Marsh]] on the border with the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]], but many other examples exist, such as the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Altdorf Flats\|Altdorf Flats]], the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Reiker Marshes\|Reiker Marshes]], and the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Uhland Bogs\|Uhland Bogs]].

The argest wetland of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], the Grootscher Marsh spreads out on both banks of the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]]. It extends some fifty miles into the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Middenland/Middenland\|Middenland]] from the border with the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]], and is considered by many to be a cursed place, for it is the site of one of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s most famous military defeats of the last century: [[The Battle of Grootscher Marsh\|The Battle of Grootscher Marsh]], where the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]] secured its independence from imperial rule.

In modern times, the Grootscher Marsh remains the foul-smelling fenland it always was, filled with ill-sounding birds and infested with [[River Troll\|River Troll]]s. In leaner years, when meat cannot be found for their rotting larders, the [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Troll\|Troll]]s grow hungry and rumour holds they slip into the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] to pluck the unwary from passing ships. Being sent to clear out the Grootscher MArsh is considered one of the worst punichment details imaginable by the [[State Army\|State Armies]] of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]; even the hardiest [[Rules/Career/Soldier\|Soldiers]] hesitate when ordered there.

Travellers on the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] occasionally report the sounds of strange, omnious horns echoing throughthe thick fogs that frequently coalesce across the marsh, and wise locals note [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Troll\|Trolls]] don’t make use of such. Old hands on the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] agree, and then swiftly change the subject…

The river [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] and [[Talabec\|Talabec]] split around the city of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] into hundreds of shifting channels, black-soiled mudflats, and marshes that spread north and south of the great city. Know as the Altdorf Flats, these wide wetlands are dominated by rushes and reeds, and are notorious for their foul stench and slurping, River-Troll-infested bogs. Some twenty miles west of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], the sneaking channels converge again into the wide [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|River Reik]], where the waters flow further westwards to the [[Princedom of Carroburg\|Princedom of Carroburg]].

The flats are crossed by six primary causeways that fan from the fertile farmlands immediately surrounding [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]. Each causeway is punctuated by several stone bridges, some of which are [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]]-wrought and date from the [[Time of Sigmar\|Time of Sigmar]]. Their roads are ever-busy with coaches and merchant trains coming to and from the capital, so are heavily patrolled by [[Road Wardens\|Road Wardens]]. Similarly, the marshy waterways are a haven for smugglers looking to circumvent [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]]’s heavy tolls and taxes, so [[Rules/Career/Riverwarden\|Riverwarden]] and [[Shipsword\|Shipsword]]s are a frequent sight plying the unsure waters, seeking criminals and driving back larger marsh monsters.

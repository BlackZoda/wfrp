* [[World/Races/Halfling Names\|Halfling Names]]

Halflings are ubiquitous across the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], found working in service industries in all [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|towns]], with an entire district of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s capital of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]], known as [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Districts/Haffenstadt\|Haffenstadt]], packed tight with hundreds of extended Halfling families supporting restourants, taverns, pipeweed stores, and an enormous number of street-food hawkers. Halflings are also a common sight in many of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s [[Village\|villages]], where it is not uncommon to find them empoyed to staff an inn or run a farm. They are communal creatures, preferring to live in close-knit family groups, sharing houses, rooms, and even beds with doens of friends and relatives; everyone contributes and everyone shares. This interdependent lifestyle leaves many Halflings struggling with the concept of private ownership and space.

Halflings are notoriously interested in recoding their lineage, and many Halfling clans can trace their ancestry back many centuries to the very founding of the [[World/The Empire/Provinces/Mootland\|Mootland]] (their self-governed [[Grand Province\|Grand Province]] in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]). The [[Elder of the Moot\|Elder of the Moot]] – currently [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Elder Hisme Stoutheart\|Elder Hisme Stoutheart]] – is the custodian of the [[Haffenlyver\|Haffenlyver]], an ancient embroided scrill detailing the chief boodlines of their primary clans, said to be the greatest treasure of the [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]]s.

Another peculiar detail is the Halflings’ strange affinity for [[World/Races/Ogre\|Ogres]]. Despite their ravenous appetites, and penchant for eating whatever is at hand Ogres tend to respect Halflings. Indeed gangs of Ogre labourers are often overseen by Halfling gaffers, and most Ogre mercenary bands have a Halfling cook on staff.

Halflings are short, apple-cheeked and beardless, resembling big-eyed, round-faced (and round bodied) [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] children, and thir sunny disposition and curly locks reinforce this impression. They are known for their enormous appetites in all things and their lack of concern for personal space (they’re huggers), social boundaries (“Well me’ great aunt just shacked up with me best mate, and ou should hear what they’ve been up to!”), andproperty rights (“It’s not like he’s using it!”); this last has landed more than one Halfling in jail for thievery.

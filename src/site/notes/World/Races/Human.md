Humans ([[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reiklander]]) are the most numerous and widespread of the civilised species of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. fromthe plains of the [[World/Other Nations/Estalia\|Estalian Kingdoms]] to the frozen oblasts of [[World/Other Nations/Kislev\|Imperial Kislev]], Humans occupy every corner of the continent, and they thrive. The largest, most powerful Human realm is [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]], a patchwork of powerful [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Province\|Province]] blanketed in seemingly endless [[Forest\|forests]]. Standing proud at the heart of [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]], the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] is its richest, most cosmopolitan region.

Many Reiklanders see it as their divine right to rule, for the patrongod of [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]], was himself a Reiklanderbefore he ascended to godhood many centuries ago. Temples and shrines to the god are found everywhere, and the majority of Reiklanders are devout believers in Sigmar’s message of Empire and unity. Because of this, they are significantly more friendly, open, and optimistic than other folk, since what could possibly go wrong for a land that birthed a god? By comparison, outsiders often see them as arrogant, over-bearing meddlers who stick their unwanted noses into any affair.

Beyond their affluent lifestyles and pushy personalities, [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reiklanders]] are largely the same as other Humans. They may be shorter-lived than other species, but Humans possess more vigour, versitality, and ambition. They also have a seemingly inextricable relationshhip with the insidious horrors of the [[Ruinous Powers\|Ruinous Powers]], with more Humans falling to corruption than any other species. Perhaps it’s no surprise the declining elder species grow incresingly concerned the meteoric rise of Humanity could result in cataclysm.

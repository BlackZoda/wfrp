Dwarfs – or “*Dawi*” as they call themselves – are legendary gruff and stubborn. While the majority reside in vast mountainside fortresses known as [[Hold\|Holds]], most larger towns and capital of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] also have Dwarf populations. Given their clannish nature, they tend to band together, forming enclaves or districts wherever they settle. Many of the Dwarfs living in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] are the descendants of those driven from [[Dwarven Holds\|Dwarven Holds]] that fell many centuries ago, but most still consider themselves as Dwarfs of the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]], although some have never seen a hill, let alone a mountain. Dwarfs who has travelled further can have their ancestoral origins in [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Karaz Ankor\|Karaz Ankor]] in the [[World/Other Nations/World's Edge Mountains\|World's Edge Mountains]] as well.

Dwarf culture respects skill in [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Crafting\|Crafting]] – chiefly stonework, smithing and engineering – and Dwarf Holds are resplendent with impressive feast of artifice. They also covet gold and jewls, mining deep beneath the mountains in the pursuit of precious metals and gemstones. However, more than these material possessions, Dwarfs venerate their elders and ancestors, and have entire religions focussing upon important progenitors. Dwarfs cannot cast [[Spell\|spells]], although their [[Runesmith\|runesmiths]] carve artefacts with intricate runes to harness magical power. However, their prowess as engineers is so impressive that some of their more ingenious clockwork or steam-powered devices are mistaken for magic by simpler folk.

Dwarfs are squat with thick, muscular limbs and stout, broad torsos. Their features are heavy and their hair is thick. Length of hair is a mark of pride and status amongst Dwarfs, with elaborate braids and adornments demonstrating rank: to shave a Dwarf causes terrible shame. Indeed honour is a fundamental aspect of their character. Given their long memories and proud natures, they bear grudges against those who have slighted or dishonoured them, gripping tight to their grievances for many years, even taking on the grudges borne by their ancestors, knowing their forebears will be watching of them, nursing their bitterness long after death. While it might be hard for others to win a Dwarf’s friendship once given it is absolute. While not as ageless as the near-immortal Elves, Dwarfs can live for many centuries. Indeed, some say that as long as a Dwarf has a purpose, they will not die unless stuck down in battle, such is the strength of their conviction.

### On [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reiklander]] Humans…
“*Like my father and my father afore me, I’ve been living in Reikland all me life. As folk go, they know not to mess with my business, and show the respect I deserve, as is right. Yes, they’re unreliable, and as changeable as the wind, but they’re also resourceful and shrewd, so I’d recommend them as risky business partners, as they see solutions I’d not even consider*” – [[Garral Herraksson\|Garral Herraksson]], [[Jeweller\|Jeweller]] from [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Eilhart\|Eilhart]]
### On [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halflings]]..
“*They’re just not my kind of folk. Always smiling. Always fidgeting. Always talking. Always moving in big groups that just won’t shut up! When they come in my store, I like to shoo them off with a broom. Really, what have they got to be so happy about? I just don’t trust them.’*” – [[Helgi Galannasniz\|Helgi Galannasniz]], [[Rules/Class/Burgher\|Burgher]] from [[Schrabwald\|Schrabwald]]

## Elf Forenames
The [[World/Races/Elf\|elves]] use a naming system quite incomprehensible to outisders. [[World/Races/Eltharin\|Eltharin]] is a complicated language, and it’s easiest to use an [[Rules/Tables/Elf Name Generator\|Elf Name Generator]].
## Elf Epithets
When travelling foreign lands, Elves typically present themselves with a forename and an epithet rather than attempt to explain the deeper concepts of Kindreds, Kindbands, or Houses. These epithets are always translated into [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Reikspiel\|Reikspiel]] as they are much easier for other [[Rules/Species/Species\|Species]] to pronounce and understand. Among [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elves]], epithets usually describe character traits and physical appearance, but can be more obscure. [[World/Races/Wood Elf\|Wood Elf]] epithets typically reference the natural world so beloved by the forest kin.

**High Elf examples:** Emberfell, Fireborn, Foamheart, Goldenhair, Silverspray, Spellsign

**Wookd Elf examples:** Fleetriver, Shadowstalker, Treeshaper, Weavewatcher, Willowlimb, Windrunner

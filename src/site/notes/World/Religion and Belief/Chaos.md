The [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Ruinous Powers]] of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] are the foremost existential threat to [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], but much about them remains a mystery. Merely seeking such knowledge is punishable by death without permission from the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]]. And seeking such permission attracts intense scrutiny of your person and motives, and is seldom granted.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Ruinous Powers]] are seen by the common folk as the punishing manifistation of sin, and the reason that all must behave within socially acceptable boundaries as espoused by the cult. Indulging in voilence, lust, slovenliness or unseemly curiosity has direct repercussions for the individual (as they are [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corrupted]] and come to a sticky end) and for their community, as their deviant behaviour attracts the destructive attention of these evil forces.

A small number of the faithful stand apart from their peers, seemingly able to appeal for their deity’s direct intervention in the form of miracles. Those who perform such geats are known by many names in different parts of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] – including: [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/The Blessed\|Living Saints]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/The Blessed\|Gods's Servants]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/The Blessed\|The Hallowed]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/The Blessed\|Divine Wills]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/The Blessed\|Anointed Ones]] – but, in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], they are most commonly referred to as [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/The Blessed\|Blessed]], which often used as a title. So, if Siste Anna were to be [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/The Blessed\|Blessed]] by [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] – i.e. granted [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s grace, able to have her prayers answered – she would be *Blessed Anna*, or, in full *Blessed Anna, Sister of Sigmar*.

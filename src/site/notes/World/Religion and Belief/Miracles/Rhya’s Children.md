Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Rhya\|Miracles of Rhya]]

**Range:**  You
**Target:** Area of Effect
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Rounds

Laying hands on the earth, you chant a prayer to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]] appealing for her aid in understanding her Realm. This [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] may only be invoked outdoors, outside settlements. You sense the presence of all sentient creatures within [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards. Each +2 SL extents the area of effect by +[[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards.

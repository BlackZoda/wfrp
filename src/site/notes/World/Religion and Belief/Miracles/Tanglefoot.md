Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Taal\|Miracles of Taal]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards
**Target:** Area of Effect
**Duration:** Instant

You call on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]], chanting prayers to protect his wild places. Roots, vines, and creepers wrap themselves around your foes. All targets within [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus yards of the target point gain an [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. For every +2 SL you may increase the area of effect by your [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus in yards, or inflict an extra [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. *Tanglefoot* has a [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] for the purposes of breaking free.

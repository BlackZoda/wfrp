Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Manann\|Miracles of Manann]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus Miles
**Target:** 1 sailing vessel within Line of Sight
**Duration:** 1 Hour

You steal the wind from the sails of a ship or boat. It is completely becalmed. Even in stormy weather in area of eerie calm and smooth waters surrounds the vessel while gales, lashing rains an towering crests surge and crash around it. This area of calm extends for [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] yards from the vessel, and if the ship is propelled by some other method, such as oars, the area of calm travels with it.

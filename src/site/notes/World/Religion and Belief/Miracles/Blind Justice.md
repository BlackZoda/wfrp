Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Verena\|Miracles of Verena]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You articulate prayers concerning [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]]’s acute perceptions, able pierce through the truth of all things. You may make a **Simple Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to see through spells and [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]] of involving illusion or misdirection. You may also make a **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to tell whether a character speaking to you is lying. **Note:** this will only tell you if the character believes they speak the truth, it will not alert you if they are mistaken.

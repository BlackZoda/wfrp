Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Sigmar\|Miracles of Sigmar]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** Area of Effect
**Duration:** Instant

You call the power of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] to smite the enemies of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. A holy fire explodes from your body blasting outwards for [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus yards. All targets within range take 1d10 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] ignoring [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus and APs. Targets with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] and [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] also gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. For every +2 SL, you may increase the area of effect by +[[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus yards, or cause an extra +2 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] to any [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]], or servants of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Ruinous Powers]] affected.

**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You call on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]] to instil you with her wisdom and knowledge.

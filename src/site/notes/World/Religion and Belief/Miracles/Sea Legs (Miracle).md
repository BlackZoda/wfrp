Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Manann\|Miracles of Manann]]

**Range:** Fellowship yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

Your target are immediately drenched in saltwater, and reel as if on the rolling deck of a tempest-tossed vessel. Their hair is whipped by spectral winds, and a torrent of spray lashes their skin. They gain one each of the [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded\|Blinded]], [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened\|Deafened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]], and must attempt an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to use their [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]]. If they fail, they also gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

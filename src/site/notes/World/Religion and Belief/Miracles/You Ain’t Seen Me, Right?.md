Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Ranald\|Miracles of Ranald]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Rounds

You spin a complex conundrum concerning the reality of that which is unperceived. Targets affected by this [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] may pass unnoticed and remarked, providing they do nothing to draw attention to themselves, such as touching, attacking, calling out to someone, casting a spell, or making a loud noise. You may only involke this Miracle if no-one is looking directly at you.

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Myrmidia\|Miracles of Myrmidia]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You call on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]] to send a [[World/Religion and Belief/Worshippers/Divine Servants\|Divine Servant]] to grant you knowledge of your enemies. A spectral [[Eagle\|Eagle]] manifests, soaring into the sky above. The eagle looks like and has the capablities of a normal eagle, but cannot physically affect the world, or be harmed in any way. While the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] is in effect, you can see through the eagle’s eyes and control its flight, surveying the battlefield and spying upon your enemies. Your vision is acute, but you do not have access to any of your own sense-enhancing [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talents]] such as [[Rules/Talents/Night Vision\|Night Vision]]. While looking through the eagle’s eyes, you cannot see through your own eyes, leaving you potentially vulnerable.

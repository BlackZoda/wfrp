Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Rhya\|Miracles of Rhya]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus
**Target:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus allies
**Duration:** Instant

You chant [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]]’s song of revitalisation. All affected targets have 1 [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] removed. If this removes all suffered [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]], the targets feel as refreshed as if they had just awoken from a good night’s sleep, and gain a bonus of +10 to any tests on their next [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]].

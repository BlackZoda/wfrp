Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Shallya\|Miracles of Shallya]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

In answer to your heartflet prayers, [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]] draws a poison or disease into you and purge it, completely removing it from your target’s system. For every +2 SL you may purge another disease or poison. For each poison removed or disease cured in this manner, you suffer [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] equal to 1d10 – your [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus, not modified for [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus or [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour\|Armour]] Points.

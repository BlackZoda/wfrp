Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Verena\|Miracles of Verena]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

By caling [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]] as your witness, the truth of your words shines out for all to see. For the duration of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]], providing you speak only the truth, all listeners will believe you speak truly. This does not necessarily mean they will agree with your conclusions, of course.

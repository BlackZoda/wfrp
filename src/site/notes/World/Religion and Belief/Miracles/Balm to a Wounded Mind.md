Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Shallya\|Miracles of Shallya]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Minutes

You call on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]] to calm the troubled mind of your targets. All [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]] traits are removed for the duration, and afterwards the targets enter deep and restful slumbers that last until next sunrise, assuming they are not disturbed. Unwilling targets may make a **Challenging (+0] [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to resist sleeping.

Delberz is a town of Middenland in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] , south west of the [[Howling Hills\|Howling Hills]].

Delberez is situated along the River [[Delb\|Delb]], Delberen is a rich [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchant]] town now well known for its wines made from wild grapes growing at the [[Howling Hills\|Howling Hills]]. Today, it is a chartered [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Freistadt\|Freistadt]] that is taxed by [[Graf Boris von Todbringer\|Graf Boris von Todbringer]] that counts around 2000 inhabitants.

Recently a [[High Priest of Myrmidia\|High Priest of Myrmidia]] belonging to the [[Order of Fury\|Order of Fury]], has brought its teachings to [[Delberz\|Delberz]].
## Locations
* [[The Great Oak Inn\|The Great Oak Inn]]: Owned by the [[World/The Empire/Infrastructure/Four Seasons Coaches of Altdorf\|Four Seasons Coaching House]], it is named after the great tree that stands in the courtyard.
* [[The Wheel and Anchor\|The Wheel and Anchor]]: Owned by the [[Merchant’s Guild of Delbren\|Merchant’s Guild of Delbren]].
* [[Delbren Port\|Delbren Port]]: Theriver port is small but busy as huge amounts of trade flow through it back and forth from [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] to [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Middenheim\|Middenheim]].
* [[Steinplatz Shrine\|Steinplatz Shrine]]: [[Steinplatz\|Steinplatz]] is asmall square in the northern quarter, built around an elaborte shrine that houses a reliquary containing the severed hand of [[Grand Theogonist Helmgart\|Grand Theogonist Helmgart]].
* [[Under-Delberen\|Under-Delberen]]: The warren beneath the town of Delberen is a hub of activity for [[Skaven\|Skaven]] on the move. Even without its prominence as a way station, however, Under-Delberez is a valuable source of food, [[Slaves\|Slaves]], and material, all stolen by the ratmen form the [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] city’s docks above.

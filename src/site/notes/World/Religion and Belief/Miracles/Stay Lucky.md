**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** Special

Crossing your fingers, you pose [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]]’s enigma and ask what, exactly, is luck? Gain +1 [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] point. For every +2 SL you may gain an extra +1 [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] points. For every +2 SL you may gain an extra +1 [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] point, which may take you beyond your normal maximum. YOu may not invoke this [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] again until you reach 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] points.

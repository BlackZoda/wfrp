Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Ranald\|Miracles of Ranald]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

Des anything existing that cannot be seen? You riddle with [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]], who sends a [[World/Religion and Belief/Worshippers/Divine Servants\|Divine Servant]] in the form of a cat as an answer. The cat looks like and has the capabilities of a normal cat, but cannot be harmed in any way. While the Miracle is in effect, you perceive everthing the cat perceives – sight, sound, touch – and control its movement. Your senses are as sharp as a cat’s, but you do not have access to any of your own sense-enhancing [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talents]] such as [[Rules/Talents/Night Vision\|Night Vision]]. while the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] is in effect, you cannot perceive anything through your own senses, leaving you vulnerable.

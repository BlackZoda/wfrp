Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Sigmar\|Miracles of Sigmar]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards
**Target:** Area of Effect
**Duration:** Instant

You invoke litanies to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]], calling on him to smite his foes. A twin-trailed comet, blazing a trail of fire in its wake, plummets from the heavens to strike a point within Line of Sight and range. Everything within [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus yards of the point of impact suffers 1d10 + SL [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]], ignoring [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus and [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour\|Armour]] Points, and gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. The target location must be outdoors, and may only target those [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] would deem an enemy.

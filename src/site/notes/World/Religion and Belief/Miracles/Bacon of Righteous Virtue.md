Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Sigmar\|Miracles of Sigmar]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** Area of Effect
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

As you bellow prayers in [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s name, you become infused with holy fire of righteousness. All allies with Line of Sight to you instantaneously remove all [[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]], and gain the [[Rules/Talents/Fearless\|Fearless]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] while the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] is in effect and they remain in your Line of Sight. Any [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]] with Line of Sight to you are subjected to [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] 1.

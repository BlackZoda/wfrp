Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Shallya\|Miracles of Shallya]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Special

You passionately appeal to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]] to spare a poor, wounded soul as tears flow freely down your cheeks. You pray for 10 – your [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]], at which point you heal the target of 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]]. For every +2 SL you may heal another [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]]. If your prayer is interrupted, the target receives no benefit. This [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] cannot reattach amputated body parts.

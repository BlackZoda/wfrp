Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Ranald\|Miracles of Ranald]]

**Range:** 1
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Minutes

You smile at [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] as you cheekily ask others what, exactly, is wealth? For each target affected, choose one option:
* the target’s purse appears empty
* the target’s purse appears full
* the target’s attire appears cheap and unremarkable
* the target’s attire appears rich and finely crafted
* a single valuable item is impossible to perceive

For every +2 SL you may select an additional effect for one of your targets.

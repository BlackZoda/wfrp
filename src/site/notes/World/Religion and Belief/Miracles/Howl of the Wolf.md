Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Ulric\|Miracles of Ulric]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]]
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rounds\|Rounds]]

You howl for [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]]’s aid, and he sends a minor [[World/Religion and Belief/Worshippers/Divine Servants\|Divine Servant]] in the form of a [[White Wolf\|White Wolf]]. Th wolf fights your enemies for the duration of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]], before vanishing to [[Ulric’s Hunting Grounds\|Ulric’s Hunting Grounds]] with a spectral, blood-chilling howl. The *White Wolves* have the statistics of a [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Wolf\|Wolf]] with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Frenzy (Trait)\|Frenzy (Trait)]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Magical\|Magical]], and [[Rules/Attributes/Size\|Size]] (Large) [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]].

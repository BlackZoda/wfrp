Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Taal\|Miracles of Taal]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Hours

You call on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]] to guide you in the hunt for your quarry, which must be an animal you have seen, or an individual you know (as limited by the GM). While the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] is in effect, you cannot lose your quarry’s trail save by supernatural means. Should your quarry enter a settlement, the trail ends there. You also receive +10 bonus to all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] regarding your quarry while under the influence of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]].

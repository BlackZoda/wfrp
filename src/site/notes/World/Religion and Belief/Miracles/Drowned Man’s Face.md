**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards

**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

Your targets gains a [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigue]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and are subject to the rules for [[Rules/Injuries/Drowning and Suffocation\|Drowning and Suffocation]] while the Miracle is in effect. If a Failure is scored, also inflict a [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] Condition.

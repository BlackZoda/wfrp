Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Morr\|Miracles of Morr]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** Area of Effect
**Duration:** Special

You draw a line up to 8 yards long on the ground while incanting a dirge to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]]. Upon enacting the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]], an indistinct, shadowy portal seems to manifest to the hoarse croaking of ravens. Creatures with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Trait]] must pass a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to cross the line. Creatures with both [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] and [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Construct\|Construct]] simply cannot cross the line. The [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] remains in effect until dawn.

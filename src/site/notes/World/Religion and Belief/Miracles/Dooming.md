Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Morr\|Miracles of Morr]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

Gazing deepy into your target’s eyes while muttering a threnody of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]], you are granted a vision of the target’s [[Rules/Talents/Doomed\|Doom]], a glimpse of what the future holds: This is almost always related to the target’s death. This [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] may only be performed on a character once, after which the [[Rules/Talents/Doomed\|Doomed]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] my be purchased with XP as if it were in the target’s [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]].

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Ulric\|Miracles of Ulric]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** Area of Effect
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You scream angry prayers, and cold [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]] answers. Your eyes gain a steely blue glint and the air around you grows unnaturally cold. You cause [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] (1) i all enemies, and all within your [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards range lose -1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] at the start of each [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], as they are chilled to the bone.

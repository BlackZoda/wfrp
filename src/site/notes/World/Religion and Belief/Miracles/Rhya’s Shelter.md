Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Rhya\|Miracles of Rhya]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** Special

You sing one of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]]’s hymns concerning shelter and safety. You may only invoke this [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] outdoors and outside settlements. You discover a perfect natural shelter. Some combination of earth and trees has formed a perfect location to camp for the night. The spot is protected from all naturally occuring wind and rain, and last as long as you remain camped there. The shelter is large enough for 1 person. For every +2 SL it fits another individual. When you break camp, the shelter cannot be rediscovered, as though it only existed through your goddess’s will.

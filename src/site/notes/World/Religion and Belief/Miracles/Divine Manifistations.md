Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessings\|Blessings]]

Blessings are subtle, completely imperceptible to those without the [[Rules/Talents/Holy Visions\|Holy Visions]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], their manifestation usually indistinguishable to good fortune. In this way, Clerics of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] without the Blessing Talent often appear to be as effective as those with it.

By comparison, [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]] are overtly manifest, always accompanied by holy signs and portents, which should reflect the circumstances and relevant deity. For instance, an [[World/Religion and Belief/Worshippers/Worshippers of Ulric\|Ulricans]] Miracle may be accompanied by a chill wind and the spectral howl of wolves, while those receiving a Miracle of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]] may find themselves drenched in saltwater.

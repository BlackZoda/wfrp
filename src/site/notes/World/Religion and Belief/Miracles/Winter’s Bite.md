Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Ulric\|Miracles of Ulric]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Rounds

You roar prayers concerning [[Blitzbeil\|Blitzbeil]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]]’s ever-thirsty axe. If wielding an axe, it counts as [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Magical\|Magical]], causes an additional + SL [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]], and any living targets struck must make a **Challending (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. Further, stuck targets lose any [[Rules/Conditions/Bleeding\|Bleeding]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] as their flood freezes; similarly, attacks from your axe cannot cause any [[Rules/Conditions/Bleeding\|Bleeding]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]].

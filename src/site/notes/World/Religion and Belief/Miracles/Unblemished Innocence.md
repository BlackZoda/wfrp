Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Shallya\|Miracles of Shallya]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

Laying hands on the afflicted, you beg [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]] to rid them of recently acquired corruption. The target loses 1 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] point, and can lose another per +2 SL scored. However the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Chaos Gods\|Chaos Gods]] do not like to be so directly opposed. Should an attempt to invoke the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]], you and the target both gain 1d10 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] points on top of any other effects. This [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] must be enacted within an hour of the target gaining a [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] point.

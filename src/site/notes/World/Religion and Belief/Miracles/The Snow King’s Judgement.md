Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Ulric\|Miracles of Ulric]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

You call on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]] to make manifest his disdain for the weak, the cowardly and the deceitful. The target suffers 1d10 wounds ignoring [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus and Armour Points. If the GM rules that the target is neigher weak, cowardly, or deceitful, you sufffer the effects instead.

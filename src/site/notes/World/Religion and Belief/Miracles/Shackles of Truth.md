Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Verena\|Miracles of Verena]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

Your appeal to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]], requesting her judgement concerning a suspected criminal. If your target committed a crime and claims they did not, while affected by this miracle they gain an [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] that cannot be removed for the duration. If you have falsely accused the target, [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]] is displeased with your lack of wisdom: you gain +1 [[World/Religion and Belief/Strictures/Sin Points\|Sin Point]] and must immediately roll on the [[Rules/Tables/Wrath of the Gods Table\|Wrath of the Gods Table]].

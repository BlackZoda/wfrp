Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Manann\|Miracles of Manann]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

You implore [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]] to provide you with sustenance. Reaching onto a body of water you catch enough fish to feed 1 person; if you reach into the sea, you provide enough fish for 2 people. For every +2 S, you ma feed another person.

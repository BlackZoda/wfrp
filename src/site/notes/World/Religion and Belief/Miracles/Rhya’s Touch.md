Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Rhya\|Miracles of Rhya]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Special

You lay hands upon an injured or diseased target as you sing your prayers. Choose one of the following effects:
* [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus wounds
* Cure 1 naturally occurring [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Diseases\|Disease]].

For every + 2 SL, you may choose another effect, and may choose the same effect repeatedly. This [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] is slow, with the effects taking at least 10 minutes to manifest. If interrupted, the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] will need to be attempted again.

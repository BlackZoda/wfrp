Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Rhya\|Miracles of Rhya]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** You
**Duration:** 1 Round

You chant to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]], and life springs forth. Edible fruit, vegetables, and fungi grow at the point where you touch. For each round in which the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] is in effect, you cause enough food to feed 1 person to grow. The type of food depends on your location: in a cavern you may grow mushrooms, while outdoors you may cause many different fruits and vegetables to spring forth.

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Shallya\|Miracles of Shallya]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You intone prayers concerning [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]’s need to take on the world’s pain. Any [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] taken by your targets are instead suffered by you. If you suffer any [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] because of this [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]], your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus is doubled for the purposes of calculating the [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] suffered from that [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]].

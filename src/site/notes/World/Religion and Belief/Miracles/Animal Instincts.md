Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Taal\|Miracles of Taal]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Hours

While the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] is in effect, you gain +1 [[Rules/Talents/Acute Sense\|Acute Sense]] (choose one) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] and, if you rest, ou will automatically awaken should any threats come within [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] yards.

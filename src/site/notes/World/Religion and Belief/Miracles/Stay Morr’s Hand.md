Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Morr\|Miracles of Morr]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Hours (Special)

You touch the eyes of someone close to death and request [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]] guide the sould within, but not take it. The target must have 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] and be willing. For the duration of the Miracle, the target gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and will not deteriorate until the [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] ends, staving off disease, ignoring [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]], [[Poison\|Poison]], and similar. This miracle comes to an end should appropriate healing be provided, or should you perform the last rites. If you do this, which takes about a minute, the target’s sould will pass through Morr’s portal upon death, and the resulting corpse may never be targeted by [[Necromancy\|Necromancy]].

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Ulric\|Miracles of Ulric]]

**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Hours

Your bellowed prayers bring [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]]’s attention, allowing your targets to survive the bite of his realm. While targets still feel the pain and discomfort caused by cold and wintry weather, they suffer no mechanical penalties.

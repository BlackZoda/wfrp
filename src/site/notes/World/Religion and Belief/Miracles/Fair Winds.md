Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Manann\|Miracles of Manann]]

**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus Miles
**Target:** 1 sailing vessel within Line of Sight
**Duration:** 1 Hour

The target vessels’ sails fill with favourable winds, speeding them safely towards their destination. While this [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] is active, the sailing vessel moves at top speed, no matter the prevailing wind, tide, or current, and all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] made to steer the vessel gain a bonus of +10.

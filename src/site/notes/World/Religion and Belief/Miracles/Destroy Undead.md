Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles of Morr\|Miracles of Morr]]

**Range:** You
**Target:** Area of Effect
**Duration:** Instant

You call the power of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]] to smite all [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]]. A black fire ripples forth from your body in a perfect circle for [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus yards. All potential targets with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Trait]] lose 1d10 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], ignoring [[Toughness Bonus\|Toughness Bonus]] and AP. Any [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] destroyed by this [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracle]] can never be raised with [[Necromancy\|Necromancy]] again under normal conditions. For every +2 SL, you may increase the area of effects be +[[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus yards.

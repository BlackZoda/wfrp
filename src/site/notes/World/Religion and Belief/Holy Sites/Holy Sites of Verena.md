Temples to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]] can be found in most cities and larger towns, generally situated in the administrative or university quarters. Most libraries and court-houses include a shrine to the goddess, and smaller shrines can be found in the homes of many [[Rules/Career/Scholar\|scholars]] and [[Rules/Career/Lawyer\|lawyers]]. Temples usually have colonnaded facades, with symbols of the goddess and allegorical figures of learning presented in low relief. Inside is a large statue of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]], normally seated with a book in her lap, a pair of scales in her left hand, and her right hand resting on the hilt of a sword. Smaller rooms lead off from the main temple, including a library and chambers for the attendant [[Rules/Career/Priest\|priests]]. Each temple has at least one meeting room where negotiations can take place under the eyes of the goddess.

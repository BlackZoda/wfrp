Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ranald\|Cult of Ranald]]

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] has no formal temple organisation, although the cult maintains seemingly unconnected [[Gambling Den\|Gambling Den]]s in most [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|Towns]] and [[World/Other Nations/Cities\|Cities]]. Small shrines are found in the headquarters of many [[Criminal Gangs\|Criminal Gangs]] and [[Merchant Houses\|Merchant Houses]], and the poorer quarters of many cities have street-corner shrines dedicated to the *God of Luck*. The latter are often maintained by local ‘shrine clubs’ which operate as both social and religious bodies, and are usually led by one of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald/Crooked Fingers\|Crooked Fingers]]. Shrines are almost never elaborate, often just a simple, smiling statue with crossed fingers behind the back, or a crudely depicted cat or magpie, often fashioned as if smiling.

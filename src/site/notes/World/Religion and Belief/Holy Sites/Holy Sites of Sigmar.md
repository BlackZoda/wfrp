Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]]

Every [[World/The Old World/Cities\|Cities]], [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|Town]], and [[Village\|Village]] in [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]] has at least one temple to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]. The [[Grand Cathedral of Altdorf\|Grand Cathedral of Altdorf]] is staffed by hundreds of priests and lay workers, and guarded by at least two orders of [[Templar\|templars]]; ot the other end of the scale, a village chapel may be visited just once every week by a travveling [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]], who serves the needs of several small settlements. [[Shrine\|Shrine]]s can be found in most homes, and wayshrines dot every major [[World/The Empire/Infrastructure/Highway\|Highway]], mostly marked with hammers or comments.

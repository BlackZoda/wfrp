[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]]’s high temple is in [[World/The Empire/Fristadts/Middenheim\|Middenheim]], and the cult’s leader, [[Ar-Ulric\|Ar-Ulric]] (which means the son of Ulric), has enormous temporal and spiritual sway. At the back of the high temple lies the [[Flame of Ulric\|Flame of Ulric]], a huge, ever-burning, argent fire granted by the winter god to guide his people. This miracle is the focus of several pilgrimage routes, and all [[World/Religion and Belief/Worshippers/Worshippers of Ulric\|Ulricans]] of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Middenland/Middenland\|Middenland]] are expected to bathe in its cold light at least once in their lives. Smaller temples are found in every city and town of any size, but are grander and more numerous in the north than in the south. Chapels and shrines can be found in barracks and forts throughout [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]].

Temples resemble fortified keeps and are normally square. The interior of the main hall is lit by small windows high in the walls, and by an ever-stocked fire in a circular hearth tended by the priesthood. Behind the fire, usually against a rear wall, stands a statue of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]] enthroned, often flanked by a pair of enormous wolves. Shrines are similar but smaller, with a lamp in place of a fire and small statues just a foot or two tall.

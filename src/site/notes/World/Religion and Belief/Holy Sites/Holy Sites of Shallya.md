The high temple of all [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]’s cult is in [[Couronne\|Couronne]], [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]], built over a famous healing spring. Locals claim the magical waters were once poured there from the same chalice Shallya used to grant [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] immortality, which they claim is the holy grail of [[The Lady\|The Lady]], the patron Goddess of Bretonnia. Whatever the truth, it is a popular destination for pilgrims, many of whom travel there to be healed from intractable disease. Elsewhere, every town or city of any size has a temple to Shallya, and most smaller settlements have at least a shrine dedicated to her. Temples of Shallya normally consist of a courtyard with a temple on one side and an infirmary on the other, all constructed in southern styles. Larger temples have smaller subsidiary chapels, commonly endowed by local families, and are often connected to hospitals. Shrines are usually simple, often with the dove or heart of Shallya carved into stone, or with small fountains gushing eternal tears from simple statues.

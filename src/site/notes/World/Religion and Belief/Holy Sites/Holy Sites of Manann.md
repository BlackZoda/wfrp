[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]]’s temples are found in all *costal towns and cities*, and in most river ports where seagoing vessels berth. The high temple is in the great port-city of [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]]: a huge, lavishly decorated complex open to the tides. The [[Matriarch of the Sea\|Matriarch of the Sea]], head of the [[Order of the Albatross\|Order of the Albatross]], is based there, a woman who ostensibly leads the entire cult of Manann, although in practice the sea-god’s clerics are as mercurial as their god, and as likely to be stubborn as to serve. The cult also maintains many monastries and abbys on small isolated islands, most dedicated to one of Manann’s many saints.

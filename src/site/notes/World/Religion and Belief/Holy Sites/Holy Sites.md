* [[Campaign/Locations/Buildings of the Reikland/Religious Buildings/Reikland Religious Buildings\|Reikland Religious Buildings]]

[[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|The Cults]] maintain holy sites across [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], man of them associated  with legendary deeds of the gods from mythical times. Most are protected by buildings such as temples, abbeys, chapterhouses, or monasteries. Some older sites, or those not so well known, may only be marked by a shrine or unattended chapel. Cult buildings are ususally decorated beautifully inside and out with scenes from the religion’s mythology. They vary widely in size and layout and often follow regional styles of architecture. Some hold extreme wealth, especially in the towns and cities where a more affluent population provides greater tithes. Larger cult buildings support doens of people, including lay craftspeople, gurads, and servants, while smaller sites are run by a handful of staff aided by volunteers from the community. In villages, a single priest is the norm, supported by the faithful.

Shrines and chapels are more modest affairs, the size of a one-room house or smaller. They do not normally have a full-time pries, although they may come under the charge of a nearby temple or – especially in rural areas – a wandering priest who travels from one village and shrine to another. In a priest’s absence, local villagers pray unsupervised, leave small offerings, and keep the shrine in good repair.

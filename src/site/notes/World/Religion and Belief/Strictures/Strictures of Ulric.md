Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ulric\|Cult of Ulric]]

* Obay your betters.
* Defend your honour in all matters, and never refuse a challange.
* Stand honest and true; outside an ambush, trickery and deception are forbidden.
* Only wear pelts from wolves killed by weapons crafted by your own hands.
* Blackpowder, helmets, crossbows, and technology are not [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]]’s way.

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Morr\|Cult of Morr]]

* Respect and protect the dead.
* Hunt down Necormancers and the Undead wherever they may gather.
* Pay heed to your dreams.
* Never refuse to conduct a funeral service.
* At no time be party to raising the dead, unless allowed by [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]].

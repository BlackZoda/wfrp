The blessed are watched closely b the gods, and risk gaining the disfavour of their deities if they should act contrary to their gods’ will. In game, this is represented with *Sin Points*.

If you voilate and of the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|Cult]]’s Stricutres listed under your [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|God]], the GM will award one or more *Sin Points*. Every time you receive a Sin point, it is added to your running total. There is no maximum to the number of *Sin Points* you can earn. The more points you have, the more upset your god is likely to be should you call upon divine aid.

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Shallya\|Cult of Shallya]]

* Always render assistance without judgement, based only on a person’s need.
* Never kill except in self-defence or when facing followers of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Nurgle\|The Fly Lord]].
* Hunt down servants of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Nurgle\|The Fly Lord]] wherever they may fester.
* [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]’s work is never done, so turn not to self-indulgence.
* Never take up arms; a walking stick and courage will suffice.

There are two [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talents]] that specifically mark out those *Blessed* by the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]]: [[Rules/Talents/Bless\|Bless]] and [[Rules/Talents/Invoke (Divine Lore)\|Invoke (Divine Lore)]]. Characters with the [[Rules/Talents/Bless\|Bless]] Talent may enact [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessings\|Blessings]], which are minor manifestations of divine will, while the [[Rules/Talents/Invoke (Divine Lore)\|Invoke (Divine Lore)]] Talent allows the Blessed to call on their gods for more powerful [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]].

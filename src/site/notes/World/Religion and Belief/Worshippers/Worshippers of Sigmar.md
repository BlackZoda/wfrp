Most folk of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] pay at least lip-service to their patron deity. In the most devout provinces, like [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] worship is an unquestioned part of daily life. Folk attend weekly “throngs” where the lessons of Sigmar are preached. Many also attend temples to train as local militia, confess sins and purify the soul, or receive advice on how to be more like the [[God-King\|God-King]] of old. [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s cult is comprised of an uncounted number of different orders. The largest is the [[Order of the Torch\|Order of the Torch]] – Sigmar priests who lead their local communities. Other orders of importance include:
* [[Order of the Cleansing Flame\|Order of the Cleansing Flame]], comprised of inquisitors and [[Rules/Career/Witch Hunter\|Witch Hunter]]s
* [[Order of the Silver Hammer\|Order of the Silver Hammer]], which includes [[Rules/Career/Warrior Priest\|Warrior Priest]]s and yet more [[Rules/Career/Witch Hunter\|Witch Hunter]]s
* [[Order of the Anvil\|Order of the Anvil]], a monastic order concerned with preserving [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s deeds and laws. The cult also has many templar orders.
* [[Knights of Sigmar’s Blood\|Knights of Sigmar’s Blood]] is the most famous
* The fanatical [[Knights of the Fiery Heart\|Knights of the Fiery Heart]]
* The militaristic [[Knights Griffon\|Knights Griffon]]

Given the cult is so large, there are many different uniforms, vestments, and robes worn, all of which have different colours, cuts, and accessories according to local tradition and requirement.

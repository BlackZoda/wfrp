[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]] is worhsipped along coasts throughout [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], wherever people make their living from the sea or live close enough for storms and floods to threaten their homes. Even those who know little of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]] will throw a coin or other small treasure into the water before beginning a sea voyage in the hope of a smooth crossing.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Manann\|Cult of Manann]] has significant number of orders, mostly monastic, tasked to guard isolated, sacred islands. The [[Order of the Albatross\|Order of the Albatross]] is largest, comprised of priests who maintain temples across [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] and bless merchant or naval vessels with their presence. Often accompanying them, the [[Order of the Mariner\|Order of the Mariner]] is the military arm of Manaan, the cult’s templar-marines, sworn protectors of [[Marienburg\|Marienburg]].

Manann’s clerics usually wear robes of *dark greenish-blue* or *blue-gray*, *trimmed with white wave-pattern*.

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Halfling Gods\|Halfling Gods]]

Halfling don’t really have priests, and they certainly don’t build temples (though [[World/Races/Human\|Humans]] seem happy to do it for them, especially [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Sigmarites]]). There are better things to do than warble on about someone else’s business. Of course, they respect the gods, and have shrines on-hand should they need to have a quick natter, but none make a career exclusively talking to just one god – why make yourself so exclusive? Should a particular god need to be appeased, it is usually left up to a local elder to do what’s required on behalf of the community, often after a great deal of talking with relevant experts and peers.

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ranald\|Cult of Ranald]]

Ranald most commonly stands as a patron to [[Rules/Career/Thief\|Thieves]] and [[Rules/Class/Rogue\|Rogues]], but the cult also attracts gamblers, liars, [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchants]], tricksters, and the poor and downtrodden.

The cult of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] is, by general perception, a disorganised rabble of [[Rules/Career/Charlatan\|Charlatans]], [[Rules/Career/Thief\|Thieves]], and ne’er-do-wells. However, it is more coordinated than it appears, and split into three primary orders. [[The Crosses\|The Crosses]] are the most accepted — a priesthood overseeing the cult’s gambling-dens, typically using the proceeds to administer to the poor. [[The Brotherhood\|The Brotherhood]] is less open, and is somewhat akin to a secret society of [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchants]] — they use business to bring the pompous and greedy to their knees. Lastly, and most widespread, is the publicly disavowed [[Campaign/Organizations/Crooked Fingers\|Crooked Fingers]], the [[Rules/Career/Thief\|Thieves]], [[Rules/Class/Rogue\|Rogues]], and liars of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]], a group that is roundly distrusted.

Cultists of Ranald have no conventional garb to identify them, but always work cross symbols into their clothing somewhere, perhaps as a repeating pattern.

Rural folk across [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] venerate [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]], and any who make their living in wild places take care not to offend him. [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]] is the patron deity of [[Talabecland\|Talabecland]] in [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]], where his cult holds significant sway, influencing all levels of society.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Taal\|Cult of Taal]] has a variety of smaller orders dedicated to holy sites and groves throughout the Old World, but two orders drive the cult forwards. The [[Order of the Antler\|Order of the Antler]] are the priests of the cult, who are tasked to teach Taal’s ways and protect the wild places from intrusion.

These Taalites are particularly widespread in [[Talabecland\|Talabecland]], and their forest temples are hubs of activity for rural folk.  [[The Longshanks\|The Longshanks]] are a mix of warrior-priest and templar who typically wander as individuals, cleansing [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]]’s wild places of [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] and ensuring rural communities do nothing to upset Father Taal.

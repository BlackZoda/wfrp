Both [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elves]] and [[World/Races/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] have priesthoods for the gods, but they do not have [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessings\|Blessings]] and [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]]. The [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]] don’t believe the gods manifest in this fashion, and instead see magic as a gift from the gods. Because of this, if you wish to be an Elven priest, use the [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizard]] [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]], and chose from an appropriate [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] to represent the magic your god has gifted. So, a “priest” of [[Kurnous\|Kurnous]] would use the [[Lore of Beast\|Lore of Beast]], a sorhipper of [[Isha\|Isha]], the [[Lore of Life\|Lore of Life]], and devoteers of [[Asuryan\|Asuryan]] use the [[Lore of Light\|Lore of Light]] or [[Lore of Fire\|Lore of Fire]].

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Rhya\|Cult of Rhya]]

Rural folk across [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] venerate [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]], relying on her to provide the crops upon which their lives depends. Womanfolk comprise the main body of the cult, and most [[Midwife\|midwives]] pay at least lip-service to [[Rhya’s Wisdom\|Rhya’s Wisdom]], a set of traditions surrounding childbirth. Athough she is not openly worshipped in the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|towns]] and [[World/The Old World/Cities\|cities]] – townsfolk often turning to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]] in her stead – her name is frequently tied with [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]]’s, so she is still well known amongst such people.

Because the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Rhya\|cult]] has no great temples and protects no holy books or relics of significance, many scholars believe her worship to be declining, and possibly already dead. Her many cultists do nothing to contradict such talk.

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]]’s cultists have no fixed vestment or preferred garb, though greens are very common, as is using plants, flowers, or herbs to accessorise any clothing. They often dress in a fashion considered far too revealing by Sigmarite doctrine, which can cause friction as the Rhyans believe giving into such prudishness is tentamount to encouraging the [[Prince of Excess\|Prince of Excess]] into your lives, as it builds forbidden desires. Devotees of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] invariably disagree, believing abstinence and restricting temptation is a better response to such dangers than indulgence.

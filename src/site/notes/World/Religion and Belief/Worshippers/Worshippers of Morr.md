Outside [[Ostermark\|Ostermark]], where [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]] has special importance, few wish to attract the God of Death’s attention, so normally only the bereaved pray to him. However, those desperate or brave enough may pray for dreams of what the future may bring, though it is said he rarely divulges anything not associated with dying.

The [[Order of the Shroud\|Order of the Shroud]] dominates the cult, directly controlling all other orders and the [[Mournes’ Guild\|Mournes’ Guild]], those responsible for overseeing burials and burial grounds. Supporting them, the [[Order of the Black Guard\|Order of the Black Guard]] are the cult’s largest templar order, tasked to guard temples and hunt down the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]]. The [[Order of the Augurs\|Order of the Augurs]] may be small, but it guides the leadership with its foretellings, andorganises the [[Order of Doomsayers\|Order of Doomsayers]]: these wandering priests of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]] tour the land performing [[Doomings\|Doomings]] for all [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] children or their tenth year. Bring them all together, every decate a grand convocation of the priesthood of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]] is held at [[Luccini\|Luccini]] in [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tilea]], where the future for the cult is discussed around city-wide festivities.

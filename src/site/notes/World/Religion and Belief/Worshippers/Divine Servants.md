Divine servants are supernatural minions of the gods in the material realm, in the same way that [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]]s serve the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Chaos Gods\|Dark Gods]]. They tend to take the form either of a favoured animal – such as a [[White Wolf of Ulric\|White Wolf of Ulric]] or a [[Golden Eagle of Myrmidia\|Golden Eagle of Myrmidia]] – or a departed devotee of the[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|God]], such as a legendary [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]] or [[Templar\|Templar]]. To construct a *Divine Servant* appropriate to the god at hand, use the rules in the [[World/Bestiary/Bestiary\|Bestiary]], modifying an animal, [[World/Races/Human\|Human]], or [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]] as you see fit.

Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Shallya\|Cult of Shallya]]

Most Old Worlders think of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]’s cult as composed of healers and [[Rules/Career/Physician\|Physicians]], but her worshippers also include many who work to help alleviate suffering of other kinds: charitable souls who help the poor; workers in orphanages, asylums, and refuges; and even brave folk who go in search of lost and missing people on behalf of their loved ones. The cult is ruled by the far-reaching [[Order of the Bleeding Heart\|Order of the Bleeding Heart]], which maintains all the temples, hospices, mercyhouses, and other [[World/Religion and Belief/Holy Sites/Holy Sites of Shallya\|holy sites]]. The significantly smaller Order of the Chalice tasks its mendicants to cleanse the[[ Nurgle\|Fly Lord]]’s influence, tackling the worst diseases and plagues wherever they may fester.

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]’s cultists all wear white robes, often hooded, with a bleeding heart symbol embroidered on the left breast.

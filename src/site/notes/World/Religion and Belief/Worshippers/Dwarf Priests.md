Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Dwarven Ancestor Gods\|Dwarven Ancestor Gods]]

Those dedicating themselves to the cults of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Dwarven Ancestor Gods\|Ancestor Gods]] do not use the [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]], [[Rules/Career/Nun\|Nun]], or [[Rules/Career/Warrior Priest\|Warrior Priest]] [[Rules/Career/Career\|Careers]], as [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] have a very different relationship with their gods, seeking to emulate them rather than worship or appease them.

Because of this, if ou wish to play a [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]] dedicated to one of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Dwarven Ancestor Gods\|Ancestor Gods]], simply choose an appropriate career to best act like that [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|God]]. So, if you wanted to emulate [[Grimnir\|Grimnir]], effectively becoming a priest of [[Grimnir\|Grimnir]], perhaps play a [[Rules/Career/Slayer\|Slayer]] and join the [[Cult of Slayer\|Cult of Slayer]], or maybe become a [[Rules/Career/Soldier\|Soldier]].

The Classical Gods spread from the southern lands of [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tilea]], [[World/Other Nations/Estalia/Estalia\|Estalia]], and [[World/Other Nations/Border Princes\|Border Princes]] through trade and diplomatic contact. Today their worship is pipular in the cosmopotalian towns and cities, and some nobles and townsfolk secretly regards them as more sophisticated than [[World/Religion and Belief/Pantheons/The Old Gods\|The Old Gods]] – though few would risk voicing such opinions aloud!

The most widespread cults of the Classical Gods in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] are dedicated to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]], and [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]],patrons of wisdom, strategy, mercy, and trickery. Hiding behind these, there is also [[Khaine\|Khaine]], the God of Murder, though his cult is outlawed in most places.

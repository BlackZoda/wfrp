The Old Gods refer to the pantheon of deities worshipped when [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] was unborken forset populated by wandering tribes of barbarians. Many Old Gods stood as patrons to one of the tribes, and to this day some are still associated with the old geographical hunting frounds of those ancient peoples. Although few say so out loud, many citizens of the Empire regads the Old Gods as the true dieties of the Empire, and [[The Classical Gods\|The Classical Gods]] as relative newcomers.

As time passed, five gods rose to prominence amongst the Od Gods, worshipped by dominant cults spread from one end of the Empire to the other: [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]], [[Manaan\|Manaan]], and [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]], representing the primal spheres of war, nature, fertility, seas, and death.

The different countries and species of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] all have deities of their own. Some, according to theologians, are aspects of other deities worshipped under different names. Others are particularly revered by a particular species: examples include [[Grungni\|Grungni]], the Dwarfen God of Mining and Dwarf Pride, [[Isha\|Isha]], a Goddess of Fertility and Nature who is seen as the mother of all Elves; and [[Esmerelda\|Esmerelda]], the Halfling Goddess of Hearth, Home, and Family.

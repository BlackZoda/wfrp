In [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], the pantheon of gods is plit into three broad categories: [[World/Religion and Belief/Pantheons/The Old Gods\|The Old Gods]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Pantheons/The Cassical Gods\|The Cassical Gods]], and [[World/Religion and Belief/Pantheons/The Provincial Gods\|The Provincial Gods]]. Standing apart from these is [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]], the first Emperor, and patron deity of the Empire as whole.

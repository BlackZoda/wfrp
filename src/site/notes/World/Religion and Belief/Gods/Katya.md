Katya is a Provintial God of disarming beauty, and is worshipped by bawds, lovers, and the lonely. Common offerings include coins, jewellery, and clothing. Temples to Katya double as brothels in the towns of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Vorbergland/Vorbergland\|Vorbergland]].

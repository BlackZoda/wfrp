A Provintial God of the river [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]], who is worshipped by merchants, bargees, and fisherment. Common offerings includes, beer, eels, and silver. Shrines dot the length of the river [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]], but there is no formal cult.

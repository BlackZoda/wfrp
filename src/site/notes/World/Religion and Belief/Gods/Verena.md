Wise Verena, the Goddess of Learning and Justice, is the wife of dark [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]] and the mother of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]] and [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]. She is generally depicted as a tall, classically beautiful woman, and usually carries a sword and a set of scales. As the patron of justice she is concerned with fairness rather than the letter of the law: she opposes tyranny and oppression as much as crime.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Verena\|Cult of Verena]] is one of the ten cults represented at [[World/Religion and Belief/The Grand Conclave\|The Grand Conclave]].

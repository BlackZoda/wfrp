A Provincial God of Rethoric, who’s worshipped by Agitators, politicians, and lawyers. Normal offerings include written speeches, acorns, and quills. He got several shrines and two temples found in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]].

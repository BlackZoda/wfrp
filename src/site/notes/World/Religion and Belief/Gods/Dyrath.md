A Provincial God for women, who’s worshipped by womenfolk. Common offerings include fruit, honey, and menses. Dyrath has no temples instead, a secretive cult is spread through the villages and hamlets of the [[World/The Empire/Forests and Hills/Hägercrybs\|Hägercrybs]].

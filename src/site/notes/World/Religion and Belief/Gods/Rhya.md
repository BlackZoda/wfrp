Bountiful Rhya is the Goddess of Fertility and Summer, widely known as the *Earth Mother* and *She Who Sustains Life*. Though typically depicted as the wife of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]], myths connect her to many gods, and she has children from many of those relationships. Most commonly portrayed as a tall, beautiful women wreathed in leaves and bedecked in fruit, Rhya’s statues are normally nude, pregnant, and surrounded by her children. Many theologians tie Rhya to the [[Old Faith\|Old Faith]], a prehistoric cult comprised of ancient farmers and hunters who wrested a living from the land before [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]] was born, and one still found in secluded communities to this day.

Taal is the God of Wild Places and Animals, and the King of Nature. He is the husband to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]], and father to [[Manaan\|Manaan]], and is perceived by [[World/Religion and Belief/Worshippers/Worshippers of Taal\|Taalites]] to be the king of the gods, though other cults dispute this. All nature is under his purview, from the snaking rivers to the tallest mountains, from the smallest insect to the greatest beast. He is normally portrayed as a powerfully built, virile man with long, wild hair and great spreading antlers, and is known for his volatile moods and his need to hunt.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Taal\|Cult of Taal]] is one of the ten cults represented at [[World/Religion and Belief/The Grand Conclave\|The Grand Conclave]].

# The Great Joke
Authored by the pseudonymous [[Ernst Kritzeln\|Ernst Kritzeln]]. This book contains an elaborate prayer-joke that takes a long time to read and reveals cynical truths about the lies that sustain society and the world. It has no punchline – the final joke is on the reader.

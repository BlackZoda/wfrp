# Magister Arctovian Wyssan (2289 to 2365 IC)
[[Grey Wizard\|Grey Wizard]] from the early days of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]], Wyssan was an avid follower of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald/Ranald the Gamster\|Ranald the Gamster]]. He turned his studies to the manipulation of chance and made several items that could help a gambler tip the odds in his favour, most notably [[Wyssan’s Weighted Dice\|Wyssan’s Weighted Dice]]. Nobody now how many he created, but [[World/Religion and Belief/Worshippers/Worshippers of Ranald\|Ranaldans]] always looto get their hands on a set

# Den Hvite Ravn (c2475)
A famous highway robber, feared by the nobility and road wardens alike for her adacious robberies on the roads between [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Middenland/Middenland\|Middenland]]. White Raven wears a feathered hite mask to hide her identity. She is a crack shot with a pistol and seems completely unflappable (though an unverified rumour has it that she demonstrates an extreme and merciless loathing for squirrels). Songs about White Raven are popular in taverns throughout the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Middenland/Middenland\|Middenland]].

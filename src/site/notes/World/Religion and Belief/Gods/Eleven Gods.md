The [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]] worshipped their own gods long before [[World/Races/Human\|Humans]] and most other species existed in their current forms. Their pantheon is extensive, with different groups of deities being held as important by [[World/Races/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] and [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elves]].

Loosly, there are two groups of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]], the [[Cadai\|Cadai]] and the [[Cytharai\|Cytharai]]. The [[Cadai\|Cadai]] rule the heavens, and have strong connection to their Elven worshippers, directl helping whre they can. The selfish [[Cytharai\|Cytharai]] rule the underworld, and care little for the [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]]. Stainding apart from both these courts are a loose group of unaligned gods, the most prominent being [[Morai-Heg\|Morai-Heg]] theCrone, Goddess of Fate and Death.

The [[World/Races/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] take a much more balanced approach and have temples and chrines to all the gods impacting their lives, be they [[Cadai\|Cadai]] or [[Cytharai\|Cytharai]]. Because of their close association with the woods and forests, they revere [[Isha the Mother\|Isha the Mother]] and [[Kurnous the Hunter\|Kurnous the Hunter]] above all others, and rumours persist that both gods take a direct hand in [[World/Races/Wood Elf\|Wood Elf]] affairs.

According to some theologians – most Elven – the deities of this pantheon are the true gods, and those worshipped by other species are simply different aspects of their Elven originals.

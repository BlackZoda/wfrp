The Classical God of History, who is worshipped by Scholars. Normal offerings include ancient artifacts, peaches, and carvings. A significant temple to Clio is attached to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Locations/University of Altdorf\|University of Altdorf]].

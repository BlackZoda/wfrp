Ubane [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]], God of Death and King of the Underworld, is husband to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]], brother to murderous [[Khaine\|Khaine]], and father of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]] and [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]. He sends divine ravens to guide dead souls to the Portal, the pillared gateway between the mortal realms and the realm of the gods. He then leads each soul from there to its final resting place: either Morr’s underworld, or the afterlife of another god. He is commonly protrayed as a tall, dark-haired man of aristocratic bearing, with brooding, intense air.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Morr\|Cult of Morr]] is one of the ten represented at [[World/Religion and Belief/The Grand Conclave\|The Grand Conclave]].

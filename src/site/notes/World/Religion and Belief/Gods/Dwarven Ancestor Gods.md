The [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] venerate their ancient ancestors, valuing tradition above all else. Though the [[Time of the Ancestor Gods\|Time of the Ancestor Gods]] was many thousands of years ago, the Dwarfs still hold records of that time in their oldest [[Dwarven Holds\|Holds]]. Many names echo from that era, creating a broad, inter-related pantheon, but three *Ancestor Gods* are of especial importance and are known by all [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]]: [[Grimnir\|Grimnir]], [[Grungni\|Grungni]], and [[Valaya\|Valaya]], each a progenitor of thespecies as a whole. In addition to the oldest *Ancestor Gods*, [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] also rever the founders of local clans as guardian deities.

A Provincial God of the river Bögen. Worshipped by [[Rules/Career/Boatman\|Boatman]], [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchant]]s, and Bögenhafeners. Regular offerings include coins, sheaves of reeds, and rolled stones. He got a single temple in [[0_GM/Maps/Kart – Bögenhafen\|Kart – Bögenhafen]] with no full-time clerics; shrines line the river.

Shallya is the Goddess of Healing, Mercy, and Compassion. She is the daughter of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]] and [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]], and the sister of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]]. Shallya is normally portrayed as a young, beautiful maiden whose eyes are perpetually welling with tears as she weeps for the world’s pain. It is said Shallya’s compassion knows no bounds, and in some myths — such as the stories of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] tricking her into granting him immortality, or [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]] trapping her at the bottom of the sea — she seems trusting to the point of foolishness. However, her cultists maintain her mercy is available to all, without judgment. True foolishness consists of presuming to judge who is worthy of Shallya’s grace and who is not.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Shallya\|Cult of Shallya]] is one of the ten represented at [[World/Religion and Belief/The Grand Conclave\|The Grand Conclave]].

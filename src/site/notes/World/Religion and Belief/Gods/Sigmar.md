[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] is [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]]’s patron, and his cult dominates the realm. Because Sigmar was once the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]], his worship is inextricably interwoven with politics.Three of the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|cult’s]] highest-ranking members are directly involved with [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Current Electors\|electing new emperors]], and its leader, [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Grand Thegonist Yorri XV\|Grand Thegonist Yorri XV]], is arguably more powerful than the [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]] himself.

According to legend 2500 years ago [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s birth was heralded by a [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Twin-tailed Comet\|Twin-tailed Comet]], and he was born the first son of the chief of the [[Unberogen\|Unberogen]] tribe.

When older, he received the magical warhammer [[World/Religion and Belief/Holy Items/Ghal-Maraz\|Ghal-Maraz]] as a gift from the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]] king [[Kurgan Ironbeard\|Kurgan Ironbeard]] for saving his life from [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskin]]s. [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] later allied with the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]]s and their combined forces defeated the [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskin]]s.

He was then crowned as the first [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Emperor\|Emperor]] of the [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] tribes he’d united. After fifty years of extraordinary rule, Sigmar abdicated and mysteriously vanished after turning east to return [[World/Religion and Belief/Holy Items/Ghal-Maraz\|Ghal-Maraz]] to its forgers – his old allies, the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]]. He was never seen again. Not long after, oracles and prophets caimed Sigmar had ascended to goodhood – crowned as a god by [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]], Sigmar’s patron in life before the entire pantheon of [[World/Religion and Belief/Pantheons/The Old Gods\|The Old Gods]] and new ones.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]] is one of the ten cults represented at [[World/Religion and Belief/The Grand Conclave\|The Grand Conclave]].

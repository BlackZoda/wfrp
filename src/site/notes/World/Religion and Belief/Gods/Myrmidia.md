In [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]], bronzed Myrmidia, daughter of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]] and [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]], sister of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]], is the Goddess of Strategy and Scientific Warfare. However, in the sun-drenched south, Myrmidia is much more than this: she act as the patron diety of both the [[Estalian Kingdoms\|Estalian Kingdoms]] and [[Tilean City States\|Tilean City States]], and is fanatically worshipped in both realms. Becase of this, her cult is the largest in [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], for all it has a limited presence in the Empire. She is commonly portrayed as a tall, muscular, young woman armed and equipped in archaic, southern stylings. She is known for her calm, honourable approach to all matters, and her clerics do what they can to emulate this.

The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Myrmidia\|Cult of Myrmidia]] is one of the ten represented at [[World/Religion and Belief/The Grand Conclave\|The Grand Conclave]].

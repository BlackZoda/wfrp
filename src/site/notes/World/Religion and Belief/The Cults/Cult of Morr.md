* [[World/Religion and Belief/Penances/Penances from Morr\|Penances from Morr]] involves hunting Necromancers and destroying Undead, or finding and restoring burial places and holy sites. Occationally also require servants of [[Khaine\|Khaine]] to be stopped.
* [[World/Religion and Belief/Strictures/Strictures of Morr\|Strictures of Morr]] regards respecting and protecting the dead, paying heed to your dreams, and hunting down Necromancers and Undead.

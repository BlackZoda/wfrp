It seems there are competing factions dedicated to different aspects of ruin, and they are as much enemies of each other as to the rest of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. On the rare occastions when they co-operate, much like they did during [[The Great War Against Chaos\|The Great War Against Chaos]] over two-hundred years ago, the world trembles. Some claim cults dedicated to the *Chaos Gods* have infiltrated [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. Most dismiss this as nonsense, for no right-thining individual would be so foolish asto worship one of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|The Ruinous Powers]].

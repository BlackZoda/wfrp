Most Old World cults are split into orders, with each order focussing on different aspects of their god’s concerns. Orders come in many different forms, including monastic orders of monks or nuns, templar orders of knights, priestly orders that attend to holy needs in the community and mendicant order of friars that wander the Old World, usually subsisting from alms. Each order is organised dfferently according to individual cult law and tradition, but in all cases they swear allegiance to the head of their cult, not the local nobility.

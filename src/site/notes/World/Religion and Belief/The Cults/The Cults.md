[[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] has many cults dedicated to appeasing or appealing to deities and promoting the ideals they embody. Leaders of these cults are frequently influential in local politics. They not only stand as representatives of their gods, but also command hundreds or perhaps thousands of clerics and templars: temporal power that cannot be ignored.

To manage their numerous members, most of the larger cults are split into orders, many of which operate from significant holy sites or temples.

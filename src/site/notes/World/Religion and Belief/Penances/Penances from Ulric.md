Penances set by Ulric are almost always tests of strength, courage, and martial skill. Slaying a powerful monster, or clearing out a nest of [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastmen]] or [[Rules/Career/Outlaw\|Outlaws]], are typical tasks.

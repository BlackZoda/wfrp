Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/Strictures/Sin Points\|Sin Points]]

Appealing to your deity when you have been acting contrary to the god’s wishes is risky. Whenever ou make a [[Rules/Skills/Pray\|Pray]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], if the units die of the result is equal  or less than your current [[World/Religion and Belief/Strictures/Sin Points\|Sin Point]] total, then you will suffer the [[Rules/Tables/Wrath of the Gods Table\|Wrath of the Gods]], even if the [[Rules/Skills/Pray\|Pray]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is successful.

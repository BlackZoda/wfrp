[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]]’s penances usually involve stealing into locked and guarded locations to recover precious items or leave a token of their presence. Humiliating oppressors of the poor is also common — perhaps by framing a brutal [[Rules/Career/Watch Captain (level 4)\|Watch Captain]] for a ludicrous crime, for example, or locking him in his own cells. [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] often sends favoured and disfavoured cultists alike on a ‘Pilgrimage of Fingers’, a set of tasks proving capability and loyalty.

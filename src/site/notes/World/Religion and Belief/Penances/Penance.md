When you have sinned and broken your [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|God]]’s [[World/Religion and Belief/Strictures/Strictures\|Strictures]], and are in need for absolution. Some [[Rules/Tables/Wrath of the Gods Table\|Wrath of the Gods]] results require penance. The GM can decide upon a suitable penance depending on your misdeed, or they may prefer you to choose your own penance, with further punishment awaiting if you are insufficiently penitent. Examples of typical penances are listed in each cult description. Penances may come in the form of a vision, divine inspiration or, very rarely, by direct communication from your deity. If you are not worthy of such contact, a penance may be conveyed through another member of your [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|Cult]].

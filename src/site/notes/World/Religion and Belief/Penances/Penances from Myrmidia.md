Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Myrmidia\|Cult of Myrmidia]]

Penances from Myrmidia are usually military in nature. A cultist may be ordered to defeat an enemy champion in single combat or train a group of peasants and lead them in the defence of their village. Protecting pilgrimage routes to sites of importance to Myrmidia are also not uncommon.

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhyan]] penances may involve replanting devastated areas, helping broken households, and maintaining sacred groves. It is also common to find Rhyan tasked to protect helpless families, which can often put them at odds with local bailiffs and law enforcement.

Sigmarite cultists may be ordered to destroy a cell of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] worshippers, or expose a corrupt official who is secretly in leage with the [[Ruinous Powers\|Ruinous Powers]] or a foreign power. It is also common to be tasked with building, or rebuilding, local communities to better promote unity and strength in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]].

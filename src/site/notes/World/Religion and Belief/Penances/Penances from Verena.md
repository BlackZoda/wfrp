Home: [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Verena\|Cult of Verena]]

Penances set by [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]] normally involves the recovery or preservation of knowledge, the righting of an injustice, or the resolution of a dispute. Cultists may also be sent to recover a long-forgotten book of lore, or to mediate in a difficult quarrel. This could be anthing from a farmers’ boundary dispute to unpicking the complicated politics of two realms on the brink of war.

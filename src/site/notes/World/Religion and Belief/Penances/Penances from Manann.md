Penances form [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]] often involve hazardous, maritime pilgrimages, tests of sailing skills, or expeditions against the sea-god’s enemies; especially followers of the heretical [[Cult of Stromfels\|Cult of Stromfels]], God of Pirates, Wreckers, and Sharks.

[[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessings\|Blessings]] and [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]] are prayers spoken by one of [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/The Blessed\|The Blessed]], and then enpowered by a [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|God]]. To enact a [[Blessing\|Blessing]] or [[Miracle\|Miracle]] make a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Pray\|Pray]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you score a Success, your Blessing or Miracle manifests according to its rules, and a high SL will give you bonus effects. If you score a Failure, your words are spoken, but your god, for whatever reason, refuses to listen. If you [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]] the [[Rules/Skills/Pray\|Pray]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], you have offended your god and must roll on the [[Rules/Tables/Wrath of the Gods Table\|Wrath of the Gods Table]].

## Limitations
You must be able to speak to intone the required prayer, rite, chant, or song to enact a Blessing or Miracle. Each of your [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessings\|Blessings]] or [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]] can only be in effect once, meaning you have to wait for an existing one to come to an end before using the same prayer again.

Multiple invocations of the same prayer by different individuals do not offer cumulative bonuses. This, intoning two [[Blessings of Finesse\|Blessings of Finesse]] will only provide a bonus of +10 to [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]].

When Emperor [[Magnus the Pious\|Magnus the Pious]] rebuilt [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]] after [[World/The Empire/History/Great War Against Chaos\|Great War Against Chaos]], he realised precious shisms and civil wars besetting his realm were often sourced in religious unrest.

To help prevent this happening again, Magnus created the Grand Conclave, a meeting of the primary cults in the Empire held every five years to air frievances and discuss resoltuions with the Emperor sitting as chair.

Though enormously controversial at the time, the Grand Conclave is now fully accepted, and is held alongside great celebraitons and festivities, with the representative of the following gods required to attend: [[Manaan\|Manaan]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Rhya\|Rhya]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Taal\|Taal]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ulric\|Ulric]], and [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Verena\|Verena]]. Although other influential cults exist – such as [[The Cult of Handrich\|The Cult of Handrich]], wich is currently lobbying for a seat on the Conclave – the ten currently sitting are widely percieved as the most important in the Empire.

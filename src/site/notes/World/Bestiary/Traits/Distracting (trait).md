# Distracting
The creature distracts or confuse foes, possibly exuding a soporific musk or nauseating reek, or maybe its appearance is bizarrely horrifying. All living targets within a number of yards equal to its [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] suffer a penalty of -20 to all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. A target can only suffer this penalty once, no matter how many **Distracting** foes there are.

# Horns Rating (Feature)
The creature has horns or some other sharp appendage (if its Horns Trait represents a different feature it will be noted in brackets). When the creature gains an [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] for [[Rules/Combat/Charging\|Charging]], it may make a [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Free Attack]] with its [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Horns\|Horns]], performed as normal, using Rating to calculate [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] (its [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] is already included).

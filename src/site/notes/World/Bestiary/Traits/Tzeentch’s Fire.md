Home: [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]]

The creature is wreathed with [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]]’s fire that frequently flies forth from fingertips, mouths, or burning eyes. All creatures of the same type (e.g. all [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Pink Horror\|Pink Horror]]s, or all [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Blue Horror\|Blue Horror]]s) in the vicinity pool their power. One Horror is chosen to be the focus (caster), and may cast 1 [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Tzeentch Spell]] during their round. [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Pink Horror\|Pink Horror]]s cast the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Pink Fire of Tzeentch\|Pink Fire of Tzeentch]] Spell; other creatures cast the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Blue Fire of Tzeentch\|Blue Fire of Tzeentch]] Spell. Each additional creature of the same type with Tzeentch's Fire adds +1 SL, to a maximum of +9 SL.

# Stride
The creature has a long stride, perhaps because it is a quadruped or has especially long legs. Multiply [[Rules/Combat/Running\|Run]] [[Rules/Attributes/Movement (M)\|Movement]] by 1.5 when [[Rules/Combat/Running\|Running]].

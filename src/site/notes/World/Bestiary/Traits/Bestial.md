# Bestial
The creature has no rational thought or language It shies away from fire and gains a [[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] if struck by it. In defence, it only uses the [[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]. If it loses more than half its [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], it will attempt to [[Rules/Combat/Fleeing\|Flee]] unless protecting its young or cornered, or unless it has the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Territorial\|Territorial]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]]. If so, it enters [[Rules/Psychology/Frenzy\|Frenzy]]. Bestial creatures have no [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristic]].

# Flight (Rating)
As the creature’s [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]], it can fly up to Rating yards. When flying, it ignores all intervening terrain, obstacles, or characters. At the end of the move, it decides whether it has landed or is still flying. It can use this move to [[Rules/Combat/Charging\|Charge]]. If it starts its turn flying, it must choose to [[Fly\|Fly]] for its [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]]. If it cannot do this, the GM decides how far the creature [[Rules/Combat/Falling\|Falls]].

When targeting it, measure horizontal distance as normal, then increase range by 1 step. So, a [[Long Range\|Long Range]] shot would become [[Extreme Range\|Extreme Range]], and if it was [[Extreme Range\|Extreme Range]] it could not be shot at all.

When flying, it suffers a penalty of -20 too all ranged combat attempts as it swoops and sheels in the sky.

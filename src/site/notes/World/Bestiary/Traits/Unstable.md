# Unstable
The creature’s corpus is maintained by foul magics that are inherently unstable in the material realm. Whenever it ends a round engaged with an opponent with higher [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], the creature loses as a many [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] as the difference. So, if the creature had 1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], and its opponent had 3, the creature would lose 2 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]]. If the creature ever reach 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], the magics holding it in place collapse, and it “dies”.

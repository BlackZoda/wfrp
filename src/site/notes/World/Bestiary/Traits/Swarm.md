# Swarm
Swarms are large numbers of the same creature acting as one. The swarm counts as a single Creature that ignores the [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]] rules, and can ignore the [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] rules when using its [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]]. If the Swarm successfully strikes an opponent it ativates the [[Rules/Combat/Deathblow\|Deathblow]] rule (even if it has not kille dits opponent). All opponents [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] with a [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Swarm\|Swarm]] automatically lose 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]] at the end of every [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] as the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Swarm\|Swarm]] overwhelms anything close. The [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Swarm\|Swarm]] has five times the [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] of a normal example of the creature and gains +10 [[Rules/Attributes/Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]]. Any attempt to shoot the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Swarm\|Swarm]] gain a bonus of +40 to hit.

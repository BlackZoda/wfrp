# Painless
The creature feels no pain or can ignore it. All non-amputation penalties suffered from [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]] are ignored, although [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] are suffered as normal.

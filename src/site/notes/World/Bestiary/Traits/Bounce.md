# Bounce
The creature can bounce high, perhaps with powerful limbs, magic, or stubby wings. When [[Rules/Combat/Charging\|Charging]] or [[Rules/Combat/Running\|Running]], it doubles its [[Rules/Attributes/Movement (M)\|Movement]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristic]], and can ignore all intervening terrain and characters as it leaps over them.

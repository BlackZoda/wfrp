# Tracker
Trackers are adept at following their prey, generally through scent or hearing. They add [[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]] equal to their [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] to all *Track* [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

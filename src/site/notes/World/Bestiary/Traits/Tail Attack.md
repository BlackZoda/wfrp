# Tail Attack (Rating)
The creature’s tail is capable of sweeping foes from their feet. On its turn, it may make a [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Free Attack]] by spending 1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]. The Tail does Rating [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]], *which includes its [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] already*. Opponents with a smaller [[Rules/Attributes/Size\|Size]] than the creature, that suffers any [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] from the attack, also gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

# Amphibious
The creature is at home in water. It can add its [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] Bonus the [[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]] of all [[Rules/Skills/Swim\|Swim]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] and move at full [[Rules/Attributes/Movement (M)\|Movement]] through water.

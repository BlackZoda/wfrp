# Ghostly Howl
The creature ca emit a chilling howl, capable of killing those who hear it. On its turn the creature can spend all its [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] (minimum of 2), to unleash a hideous scream as a [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Free Attack]].

All living targets within a number of yards equal to the creature’s [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] immediately gain 3 [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened\|Deafened]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] and suffer 1d10 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] ignoring [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] and [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Armour Point\|Armour Points]]. Those affected must also pass a **Challenging (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

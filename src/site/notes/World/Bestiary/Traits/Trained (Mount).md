# Mount
The animal will accept a rider. Some creatures are especially belligerent, and will not accept a rider without the correct skill. So, to ride a [[Griffon\|Griffon]], you need the [[Rules/Skills/Ride\|Ride]] ([[Griffon\|Griffon]]) [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]].

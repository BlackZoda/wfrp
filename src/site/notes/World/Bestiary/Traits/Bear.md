Wanderers of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s wild places, Bears are solitary and usually shy, only turning aggressive when interlopers threaten their young or when wounded. When food is scarce, they will approach unprotected settlements and travellers, especially those careless with provisions.

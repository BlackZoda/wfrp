# Construct
The creature is a construct of [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]], quite mindless, bound together with magical sinews. It has no [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]], [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]], or [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]], and need never [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] them. If it has no [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizard]] controlling it, or does not possess the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Territorial\|Territorial]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]], it meanders mindlessly, following flows of ambient magic.

For the purposes of calculating its [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], it uses its [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] whenever [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] is required. All its attacks are [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Magical\|Magical]].

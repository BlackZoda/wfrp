# Fear and Terror
If the creature is perceived to be aggressive, it causes [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] in any creature smaller than it, and [[Rules/Psychology/Terror\|Terror]] in any creature two or more steps smaller. The rating of the [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] or [[Rules/Psychology/Terror\|Terror]] equals the [[Rules/Attributes/Size\|Size]] step difference. So if the creature is [[Rules/Attributes/Large\|Large]], and its opponent is [[Rules/Attributes/Small\|Small]], it will cause [[Rules/Psychology/Terror\|Terror]] 2.

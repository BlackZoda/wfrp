# Regenerate
The creature is capable of healing at an extraordinary rate, even regrowing severed parts. At the start of each round, if it has more than 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] remaining, it will automatically regenerate 1d10 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]]. If it has 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] remaining, it will regenerate a single [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]] on a d10 roll of 8+. If it ever rolls a 10 for regenerating, it also fully regenerate a [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]], losing all penalties and [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] associated with it. Any [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]] or [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] caused by [[Fire\|Fire]] may not be regenerated and should be recorded separately.

# Fury
The creature can work itself into an al-consuming range. It can spend all of its [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] (minimum of 1) to become subject to [[Rules/Psychology/Hatred\|Hatred]] to close combat opponents. If the creature has at least 3 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], it may instead expend all of its [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], it may instead spend all of its [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] to become subject to [[Rules/Psychology/Frenzy\|Frenzy]].

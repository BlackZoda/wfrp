# Bite (Rating)
On its turn, the creature may make a [[Free Attack\|Free Attack]] by spending 1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]. The [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] of the attack equals Rating and *includes the creature’s [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] Bonus already*.

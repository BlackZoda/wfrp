# Stomp
Creatures that are larger than their opponent may make one **Stomp** as a [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Free Attack]], by spending 1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], as they kcik downwards or otherwise bash smaller opponents out of the way. This attac has a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to their [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] +0.

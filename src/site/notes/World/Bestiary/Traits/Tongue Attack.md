# Tongue Attacking Rating (Range)
The creature’s prehensile tongue can wrap itself around prey, dragging it to a grisly end. On its turn, it may make a [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Free Attack]] by spending 1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]. This is a ranged attack that does [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to its *Rating* (the range is in brackets). If the attack hits, the target receives 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and, if a smaller [[Rules/Attributes/Size\|Size]], is dragged towards the creature, and is [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] in melee combat. The creature can then choose whether to release the target, perform a [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Free Attack]] using its [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Weapon (trait)\|Weapon (trait)]], or to keep the target wrapped in its tongue, initiating a [[Rules/Combat/Grappeling\|Grapple]].

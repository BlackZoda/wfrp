# Die Hard
No matter how hard the creature is hit, it gets back up. All [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]] not resulting in death can be healed, just attach the requisite body parts to the correct place, and it’s good to go. Even “death” my be “healed” if the appropiate parts, such as a lost head, are attached to the body. If death occurs and all parts are in place, it may attempt a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] requiring an **[[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]] of 6** at the start of every round for [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]] after death. If a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is successful, the creature chokes back to life with 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]].

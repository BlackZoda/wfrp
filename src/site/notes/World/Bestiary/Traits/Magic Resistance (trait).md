# Magic Resistance (Rating)
[[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]] has reduced effect on the creature. The [[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]] of any spell affecting it is reduced by the Rating given. So, *Magic Resistance* 2 would reduce the [[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]] by 2.

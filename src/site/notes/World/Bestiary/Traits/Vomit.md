# Vomit
The creature can spew a stream of corrosive corruption, dowsing its opponents in foul, semi-digested filth. On its turn, by spending 3 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], the creature can activate its *Vomit* as a [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Free Attack]]. The creature chooses 1 target it can see within [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] yards and lets loose; all targets within two yards are also hit.

The creature performs an **[[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] [[Rules/Attributes/Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]]/[[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] against all affected targets (its single roll opposed by each individual target). The [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is typically **Easy (+40)** for the vomiting creature, due to the close range, and **Challenging (+0)** for opponents. All losing targets suffer a hit with a [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Damage\|Weapon Damage]] of the creature’s [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] +4 and recive a [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] condition.

All [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour\|Armour]] and [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon\|Weapons]] carried by affected targets suffer 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] as the acidic vomit corrodes it away.

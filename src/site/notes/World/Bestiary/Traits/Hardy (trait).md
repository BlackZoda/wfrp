# Hardy
The creature can sustain more damage than most. Increase its [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] by a number equal to its [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] (applied before any [[Rules/Attributes/Size\|Size]] modifiers).

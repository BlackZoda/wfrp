# Defending Against Big Creatures
You suffer a penalty of -2 [[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]] for each step larger your opponent is when using [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] to defent an [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]]. It is recommended to dodge a [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Giant\|Giant]] swinging a tree, not parry it!

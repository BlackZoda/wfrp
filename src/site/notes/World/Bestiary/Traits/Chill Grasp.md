The creature’s touch chills its enemy’s souls. For the cost of 2 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] and its [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]], it can attempt an **[[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] [[Rules/Attributes/Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]]/[[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] or [[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. if it wins, its target loses 1d10 + SL [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] with no modification for [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] or [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Armour Point\|Armour Points]]. This attack is [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Magical\|Magical]].

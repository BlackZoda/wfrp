# Vampiric
The creature feeds on blood and draws great physical strength from this act. Whenever it performs a successful [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Bite\|Bite]] attack against an appropriate opponent, it heals as many [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] as its opponent loses. Drinking blood in this way is the *only* way it can heal.

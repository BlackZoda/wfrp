# Breath (Rating) (Type)
The creature’s breath is a powerful weapon. On its turn, for the cost of 2 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], it can activate its *Breath* as a [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Free Attack]]. Choose 1 target it can see within 20+ [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] yards. All characters within [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] yards of the target are truck, as are all characters between the creature and the target. Perform an **[[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] [[Rules/Attributes/Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]]/[[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] against all affected targets (the creature’s single roll opposed by each individual target). All targets that fail take [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to the attacks Rating. Further, if the [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]] is marked with any of the following types in brackets, apply the associated rules:
* **Cold:** Targests gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] for every full 5 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] suffered (minimum of 1).
* **Corrosion:** All [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour\|Armour]] and [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon\|Weapons]] carried by the affected target suffer 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]].
* **Fire:** Any [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] caused ignore [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Armour Point\|Armour Points]]. Targets gain an [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].
* **Electricity:** Any [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] caused ignore [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Armour Point\|Armour Points]]. Target gains a [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].
* **Poison:** Any [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] cased ignore [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Armour Point\|Armour Points]]. Target gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].
* **Smoke:** The area fills with smoke, blocking [[Line of Sight\|Line of Sight]] for [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]].

The creature is immune to its own *Breath*. This [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Breath (trait)\|Breath (trait)]]

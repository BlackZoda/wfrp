# Wallcrawler
The creature can effortlessly scale vertical surfaces and even traverse ceilings, ready to drop on unwary prey. It moves at full [[Rules/Attributes/Movement (M)\|Movement]] across any appropriate surface and automatically passes all [[Rules/Skills/Climb\|Climb]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

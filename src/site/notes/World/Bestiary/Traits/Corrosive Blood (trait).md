Home: [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]]

The creature’s blood is corrosive. Every time its [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Wounded\|Wounded]], blood splashes free, and all targets [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] with it takes 1d10 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] modified by [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] and [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Armour Point\|Armour Points]], to a minimum of 1.

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|The Lord of Change]], has marked his creature ith a physical brand, proclaiming it as an important piece in the great game. This creature gains 1d10/3 [[Rules/Mutations/Mutation\|Mutations]] (rounding up), alternating between [[Rules/Tables/Mental Corruption Table\|Mental Mutations]] and [[Rules/Tables/Physical Corruption Table\|Physical Mutations]]. Use the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] column on page 66 (EiSC). This creature gains [[Rules/Talents/Etiquette\|Etiquette]] (Followers of Tzeentch) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], and is subject to [[Rules/Psychology/Animosity\|Animosity]] towards overt folloers of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Nurgle\|Nurgle]], so long as the mark of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] is visible. Additionally, they may purchase the following [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talents]] as if they were [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]]: [[Rules/Talents/Aethyric Attunement\|Aethyric Attunement]], [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] ([[Rules/Keywords/Any\|Any]]), [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]] ([[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]]), [[Rules/Talents/Fast Hands\|Fast Hands]], [[Rules/Talents/Instinctive Diction\|Instinctive Diction]], [[Rules/Talents/Magical Sense\|Magical Sense]], [[Rules/Talents/Petty Magic\|Petty Magic]], [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight\|Second Sight]], [[Rules/Talents/War Wizard\|War Wizard]], and [[Rules/Talents/Witch!\|Witch!]] for the normal XP costs.

The *Mark of Tzeentch* is a blessing granted to certain especially favoured servants of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|The Changer of Ways]]. Very rarely one may be marked who does not yet serve [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]], but who by bearing the Mark will surely come to. [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] fabours those who reject tradition and stability and display a fascination with magic and manipulation. Such folk may dream of a terrible raven, twisted and knowing its gaze, offering them power. Should they accept the raven’s offer three times they gain the *Mark of Tzeentch*, and thus begin a journey that can end only in ruin.

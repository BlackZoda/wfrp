# Petrifying Gaze
The creature’s gaze can turn flesh to stone. For its [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]], it can spend all its [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] to unleash its gaze (minimum 1). The creature performs an **[[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] [[Rules/Attributes/Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]]/[[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], adding 1 [[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]] per [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] spent. Its opponent gain 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] status per 2 [[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]] by which it wins. If it wins by at least 6 [[Rules/Tests/Success Levels\|SL]], its target is permanently turned to stone.

If the target is a spellcaster, the test can be [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] with [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) instead of [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] as counter spells are cast.

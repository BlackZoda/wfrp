# Weapon (Rating)
The creature carries a melee weapon, or uses theeth, claws, or similar in combat.

The weapon causes [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to its *Rating* which *already includes the creature’s [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]]*. Typically it will be 4+ its [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]] (representing a [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Hand Weapon\|Hand Weapon]]).

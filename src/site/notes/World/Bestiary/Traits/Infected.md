# Infected
The creature, or its weapon, carries a nasty infection. If it causes a living opponent to lose [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], it must pass an **Easy (+40) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or contract a [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Festering Wound\|Festering Wound]].

# Leader
The creature is a practiced leader. It receives a bonus of +10 to [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] and [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]]. **Note:** This [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]] cannot be taken by creatures with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Bestial\|Bestial]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]].

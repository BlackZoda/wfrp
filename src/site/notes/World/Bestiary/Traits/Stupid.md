# Stupid
While not entirely devoid of self-awareness (and so lacking the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Bestial\|Bestial]] trait), the creature is stupid. If it is near any allies without the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Stupid\|Stupid]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]], they guide it and  nothing happens. Otherwise, it must pass an **Easy (+40) [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] at the start of each round or become very confused. Should this occur it will drool, perhaps sitting down or picking its nose, doing little of use, losing both its [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]] and [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] for that [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]].

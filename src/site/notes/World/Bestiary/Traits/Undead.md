# Undead
The *Undead* are neither living, nor dead, meaning they are not reliant on the usual prerequisites for life: air, food, water… This [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]] is most commonly come into use when spells, miracles, or other abilities affect *Undead* only.

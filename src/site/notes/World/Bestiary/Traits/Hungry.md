# Hungry
The creature is always hungry for the fresh meat. If it kills or incapacitates a living opponent (or encounters a fresh body), it must pass a **Challenging (+20) [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower (WP)]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or feast, losing its next [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] and [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]].

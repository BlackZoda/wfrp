# Immunity (Type)
The creature is completely immune to certain type of [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]], such as [[Poison\|Poison]], [[Magic (damage)\|Magic (damage)]], or [[Electricity\|Electricity]]. All [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] of that type, including from [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]], is ignored.

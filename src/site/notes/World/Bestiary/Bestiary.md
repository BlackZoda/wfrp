The creatures presented in the Bestiary are generic, typical starter examples of their ilk. You are encouraged to customize them and create your own, using the statistics found here and adding [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skills]] and [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] as you feel are necessary, perhaps even usingthe full [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] system to create terrifying opponents. The quickest way to create fast [[Templates/NPC\|NPC]] adversaries is to use [[Creature Trait\|Creature Trait]]s as standard, but additional ones can be added as required, and they can be mixed with [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s and [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s as you see fit.

The **Optional** traits listed alongside each creature presents some of the most commonly found variants of the species. However, you may choose to apply any trait to any creature if it fits what you want to represent in your game.

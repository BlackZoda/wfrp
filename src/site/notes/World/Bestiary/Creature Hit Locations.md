Home: [[World/Bestiary/Bestiary\|Bestiary]]

At some point you need to determine a [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location]] for a non-[[World/Races/Human\|Human]]-shaped creature. Normally, this is simple. For quadrupeds, simply make arm hits foreleg hits, and leg hits rear-leg hits. Or for birds, make arm hits equate to wing hits. However, some require special attention, such as [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Snake\|Snake]]s and [[Spider\|Spider]]s. For those, use [[Alternative Hit Locations\|Alternative Hit Locations]].

For any creature 2 step larger than you (see [[Rules/Attributes/Size\|Size]]), choose a location to hit according to what’s closest to you (or in Line-of-Sight for shooting).

If an animal has a [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location]] without a [[Rules/Tables/Critical Tables\|Critical Table]], such as a tentacle, tail, or wing, roll on the [[Arm\|Arm]] table and describe the results in an appropriate way.

# Pigeon
Pigeons are bred across the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] to carry messages of all kinds. Recently they have become renowned for carrying deadlier cargo, as the easily accessible bird has become a favourite amongst engineers, used to deploy their “pigeon bombs” to varying degrees of success.

# Horse
Horses are bred for many jobs, including speedy mounts for messagers, sturdy destriers for knights, and mighty drays for farmers. They are so useful, horse trading is almost a competative sport in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]. Unscrupulous “Horse Copers” (horse salesmen) are keen to make money at the expense of the ignorant buyer, painting and filing teeth of elderly horses or stuffing rags up noses to soak mucous, so the unknowledeable buyer should beware.

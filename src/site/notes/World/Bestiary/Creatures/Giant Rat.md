# Giant Rat
Rats get everywhere and are especially prevalent in the cities and towns. The more densly people are packed in the more densely Rats are packed in with them, which is especially bad when the creatures carry disease. Though typically small, but can grow to monstrous proportions, with reports of rats beneath [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] the size of a [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] and larger. [[Rules/Career/Rat Catcher\|Rat Catcher]]s are employed to keep them in check.

Home: [[World/Bestiary/The People of the Reikland\|The People of the Reikland]]

Big, loud, brutish, and extremely voilent, Ogres are driven by the needs to fill their prodigious, muscled bellies. They lack the wit or intelligence to do this cleverly, defaulting to might is right in most situations. Hailing from lands far to the east, Ogres are a common sight in [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] for they love to wander, always on the hunt for new meats over the horizon. As they pass through on decades-long food excursions, they work hard to integrate, wearing local clothing and following the local customs they understand, as that’s more likely to attact the next meal.

In [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], they frequently serve in the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Empire State Armies\|State Army]], and are an everyday sight in larger towns, contracted as muscle for hire. The [[Halfling Gaffers Guild\|Halfling Gaffers Guild]] (building contractors) has effectively cornered the market in cheap Ogre labour in many towns and cities of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], a source of great annoyance to their rivals.

Home: [[World/Bestiary/The Beasts of the Reikland\|The Beasts of the Reikland]]

Giant Spiders lurk deep within the forests and caves of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], but can live anywhere, including dusty attics and dark cellars. Most trap their pary with strong webs before injecting venom. Though most are the size of a large [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Rat\|Rat]], some Giant Spider species are terrifyingy large. [[Forest Goblin\|Forest Goblin]]s often capture larger examples for mounts.

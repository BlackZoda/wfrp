# Wolf
Wolves usually hunt in packs and have a reputation for being tenacioius hunters, pursuing their prey over dozens of miles without rest or respite. Several species prowl the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]], including the fierce *Giant Wolves*, which are captured and bred by [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Goblin\|Goblin]]s to use as guards and mounts.

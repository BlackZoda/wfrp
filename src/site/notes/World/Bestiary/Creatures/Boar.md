Home: [[World/Bestiary/The Beasts of the Reikland\|The Beasts of the Reikland]]

Scavenging in the forest, Boars are reclusive creatures, but when cornered will fight using their sharp tusks with a tenacity that shocks the unprepared. Though most are 5–6 feet long, some grow to remarkable proportions, with the largest prized by [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Orc\|Orc]]s, who use them as ferocious war mounts.

While difficult to master, [[Qhaysh\|Qhaysh]] is the safe blending of multiple [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]]. [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]], known commonly as *Dark Magic*, is much more dangerous method of casting [[Spell\|Spell]]s using multiple [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds]]. It is usually only practiced by evil [[Sorcerer\|Sorcerer]]s, [[Necromancer\|Necromancer]]s, and powerful [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witches]], offering them a seductive source of raw power, yet one laced with terrible side-effects. Few can channel [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]] for long, without succumbing to the corrupting inflluence of the malevolent [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Wind]], their bodies and minds being warped into unnatural states.

[[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]] resembles a stagnant mire to those with [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight\|The Sight]], pooling in places saturated with evil, or corruption: the herdstones of [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastmen]], the dark idols of [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Chaos Cults\|Chaos Cult]]ists, and places where great workings of several of the [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] took place. [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]] is so dense and potent that it can independently coalesce or crystalize into physical matter, eventally forming the widely feared substance called [[World/Magic/Warpstone\|Warpstone]].

Petty spells, or cantrips as the Colleges often prefer to call them, use miniscule amounts of whatever winds are around, and are often learned instinctively by spellcasters as their talent first manifests. As such, once their training within the Colleges has begun, Magisters rarely learn new Petty spells. Technically, they are classed as Witchcraft, and are thus illegal in the Empire.

While the Colleges of Magic generally turn a blind eye to their use given they are so minor, Witch Hunters are not always so understanding.

The *Lore of Metal* relates to [[Chamon\|Chamon]], the gold wind. [[Chamon\|Chamon]] appears dense and heavy to [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight\|Second Sight]], sinking into the earth and coalescing within dense metals such as lead and gold. The [[Alchemist\|Alchemist]]s  of the [[Gold Order\|Gold Order]] have a reputation for eing unusually prosaic in their attitudes for [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizards]], and many are as interested in learning the facts of physics and chemistry as they are in the working of magic.

[[Spell\|Spell]]s from the *Lore of Metal* often involve the transmutation or alteration of metal. On the battlefield, alchemists have been known to cause fine steel armour and weaponry to corrode or melt, to weigh their foes down with suddenly dense armour, or to enchant their allies’ weaponry with uncanny power.

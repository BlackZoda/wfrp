The *Lore of Shadows* relates to [[Ulgu\|Ulgu]], the grey [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Wind]]. To [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight\|The Sight]] it appears a thick fog, gathering in pools wherever intrigue and deceit are practiced, rising into great storms and tempests when conflict breaks out. [[Magister\|Magister]]s of the [[Grey Order\|Grey Order]], known as [[Grey Guardians\|Grey Guardians]], are secretive in their ways and givien to uncertain loyalties. Despite this, the *Grey Order* is renowned for its wisdom and skill in negotiations, and is ofte called upon for diplomatic missions.

[[Spell\|Spell]]s from the *Lore of Shadows* can be used to mask or obfuscate, confusing and disorienting their foes. In battle, the shadowy, insubstantial tendrils of [[Ulgu\|Ulgu]] they wield can pierce to the heart of their foes, eviscerating the most well-protected troops, yet leaving armour eerily intact.

Practitioners of *Hedgecraft* generally live quiet lives on the fringes of smaller [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] settlements in service to local communities. Just as they live their lives in the ‘hedge’ between civilisation and the wilds of nature, so much of their magic concerning itself with the liminal space between the material world and the realm of the [[Spirit\|Spirit]]s. Their magic tends to focus on [[Folklore\|Folklore]], [[Spirit\|Spirit]]s, and the natural world, as well as means to aid the rural communities which shelter them. Once a relatively common sight in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], over two centuries of persecution since the founding of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]] has all but wiped them out.

The Lore of Light relates to [[Hysh\|Hysh]], the white wind. [[Hysh\|Hysh]] is considered the most difficult [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Wind]] to perceive and manipulate, appearing diffuse even to those skilled with the [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight\|Sight]]. This makes it tricky to manipulate but less unpredictable than other [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds]]. [[Hysh\|Hysh]] is associated with patience, intelligence, and purity. The [[Hierophant\|Hierophant]]s of the [[Light Order\|Light Order]] are acclaimed for their disipline, knowledge, and devoted opposition to [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]].

Spells from the *Lore of Light* are some of the most powerful, including piercing rays of blinding light, and those that banish [[Deamon\|Deamon]]s and [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] creatures from the mortal plane. There are also more gentle applications of [[Hysh\|Hysh]] used to heal comrades or clarify thought.

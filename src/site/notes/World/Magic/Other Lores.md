In less frequented corners of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], in villages and rural communities far from the influence of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]], older forms of magic are still practiced even though they are outlawed and punishable by death. There are uncounted varieties of these, but two ofthe more common [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]]s still practiced by [[World/Races/Human\|Humans]] are Hedgecraft and Witchcraft.

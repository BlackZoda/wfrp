Home: [[World/Magic/Dark Magic\|Dark Magic]]

Warpstone is a lump of pure magic in the material plane. Its unnatural provenance is immediately obvious to all who see it, as it hurts the eyes and mutates anything drawing close for too long. Although its from varies, it often holds hard facets like flint, and radietes a queasy green glow.

Close examination of the substance is not to be undertaken lightly. Warpstone is the stuff of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] made manifest and its presence is deeply [[Rules/Corruption/Corrupting\|Corrupting]]. Those who have direct contact with warpstone risk illness, madness and mutation, and anyone who ingests so much as a pinch of the stuff dooms themselves to catastrophic warping of body and mind. Nonetheless, the world is full of reckless, ambitious fools who know that the olatile and dangerous substance is a tremendous source of energy for [[Spell\|Spell]]s and [[Ritual\|Ritual]]s.

The followers of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] and the [[World/Races/Skaven\|Skaven]] do not hesitate to use it. To them, warpstone is a literal figt from the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]] to be valued above gold and jewels, and to be used against their enemies.

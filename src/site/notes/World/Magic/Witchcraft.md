Home: [[World/Magic/Other Lores\|Other Lores]]

While the *Lore of Witchcraft* is not inherently malicious, or tied to the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]] of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]], it has justly earned reputation for evil and unpleasentness. Often self-taught and using multiple [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]], those practising the *Lore of Witchcraft* lack the discipline or knowledge of College Magisters and are at significant risk of [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]]. The combination of the corrosive effects of [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]] and being shunned by right-minded folk of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] often makes these [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witches]] bitter, spiteful souls, their hearts hard and flinty, their gaze baleful and ill-omened.

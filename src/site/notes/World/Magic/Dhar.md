Home: [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|The Winds of Magic]]

Dhar - foul, twisting Dark Magic - gathers anywhere the winds of magic grow stagnant and are incapable of flowing freely. It also gathers where dark and chaos magic is used frequently, where the cultists of the Ruinous Powers gather, and around sources of corruption, such as [[World/Magic/Warpstone\|Warpstone]].

The following is a brief list of example sources of Dhar, which typically provide a bonus of +1–3 SL (as determined by the GM) to all Channelling (Dhar) Tests in the vicinity, depending upon the source’s power.

* [[World/Magic/Warpstone\|Warpstone]]
* Dark, Skaven or [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]] [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spells\|Spells]]
* [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] Temple, Monolith or Holy Site
* Broken [[Waystone\|Waystone]] (or [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastman]] [[Herdstone\|Herdstone]])
* Creatures or artefacts that are suffused with *Dhar*
* [[Skaven\|Skaven]], [[Necromancer\|Necromancer]]s, or [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Vampire\|Vampire]] Lair.

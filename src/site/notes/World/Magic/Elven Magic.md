[[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]] are long lived beings whose minds are more attuned to the workings of magic than those of humanity. [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elven]] mages usually train in several, sometimes all, of the eight [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds]] as part of their apprenticeships, before the most promising move on to study [[High Magic\|High Magic]]: [[Qhaysh\|Qhaysh]]. This is the blending of multiple winds of magic together into a blinding, coruscating energy. This magic is impressive and difficult, and [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]] claim it is beyond the capacity of humankind.

The [[World/Races/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]], too, make use of the eight [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]], though their [[Spellsinger\|Spellsinger]]s usually focus upon the *Jade* and *Amber* [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds]]. The most powerful usually go on to study either [[High Magic\|High Magic]] like the [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elves]], or dread [[World/Magic/Dark Magic\|Dark Magic]] – a foul mixing of the eight winds that can create tremendously destructive effects.

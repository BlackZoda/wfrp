The *Lore of Death* relates to [[Shysh\|Shysh]], the purple [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Wind]]. It is attracted to places of death, such as battlefields, [[Gardens of Morr\|Gardens of Morr]], and sites of execution. The wind is said to blow strongest during times of transition, so wizards of the [[Amethyst Order\|Amethyst Order]] tend to work their greatest rites during the twilight before dawn, or during sunset. While [[Shyish\|Shyish]] is related to time and mortality, it is distinct from [[Necromancy\|Necromancy]], the illegal practice of raising and binding the dead, which uses the dark magic of [[Dhar\|Dhar]] in lieu of [[Shyish\|Shyish]]. Indeed, the [[Amethyst Order\|Amethyst Order]], much like the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Morr\|Cult of Morr]], works tirelessly to combat the threat of [[Necromancy\|Necromancy]].

Nevertheless, [[Spell\|Spell]]s from the *Lore of Death* resemble [[Necromancy\|Necromancy]] to the untrained eye. They can train their targets of life force, spread fear among their enemies, and contact the [[Spirit\|Spirit]]s of the departed.

The *Lore of Beasts* relates to [[Ghur\|Ghur]], the amber [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Wind]], a cold, primal force associated with the savage wilds and the beasts living there. To those with [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight\|The Sight]] [[Ghur\|Ghur]] seems to blow weakly in areas where the wilderness has been tamed and settlements constructed. This may explain shy the [[Shaman\|Shaman]]s of the [[Amber Order\|Amber Order]] often take up a hermetic existence and shun their fellow men.

The [[Spell\|Spell]]s of the *Lore of Beasts* allow a [[Shaman\|Shaman]] to communicate with animals, request their aid, and even summon them to battle. Shapeshifting magic may also be used by the wizard to adopt animal forms.

The *Lore of Life* relates to [[Ghyran\|Ghyran]], the jade wind. Free flowing [[Ghyran\|Ghyran]] is assoicated with growth fertility and nourishment. To those with the [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight (Talent)\|The Sight]], [[Ghyran\|Ghyran]] appears much like a light rain, falling to the ground and pooling in eddying swirls. It sinks into the soil and is drawn up into the roots of plants from where it goes on to nourish all living things. The [[Druid\|Druid]]s of the [[Jade Order\|Jade Order]] often prefer life away from the [[World/The Old World/Cities\|cities]], attuned to the seasons and the natural world.

[[Spell\|Spell]]s from the *Lore of Life* commonly involve healing and rejuvenation, from healing wounds to causing a barren field to burst with life. They can have offensive applications, too. The enemies of a [[Druid\|Druid]] may find themselves suddenly tangled up in wickedly sharp brambles and vines.

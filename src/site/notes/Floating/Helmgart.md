Set high in the [[World/The Empire/Mountains and Peaks/Grey Mountains\|Grey Mountains]], the fortress town of Helmgart guards the border with [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]] from the easter side of [[Axe-Bite Pass\|Axe-Bite Pass]]. [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s relations with Bretonnia have not always been civil, and while the ramparts of Helmgart are no longer lined with aging skulls in elaborate helms, they mostly likely remain in the castle’s cellars. These days, the soldiers of the Helmgart are more often called upon to patrol the pass, protecting travellers and merchants from [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskin]], [[Bandit\|Bandit]]s, and other menaces.

Helmgart’s keep was carved directly out of the mountainside by [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] long ago. Three tires of stout stone walls buttress a great granite mountain, dominating the surrounding terrain and offering an excellent view of the road below. Adjoining Helmgart proper, is a huge, imposing wall broken only be a single, long tunnel standing between the two, sheer mountainsides; the only rout through to [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]]. amongst the troops garrisoning the fortress are a number of storied regiments. Most famously, the gunners of [[Mackensen’s Marauders\|Mackensen’s Marauders]] – a [[Reiklander\|Reiklander]] [[State Regiment\|State Regiment]] – are well known for their deadly accuracy, a fearsome reputation perhaps aided by the lack of cover on the road below the high walls. The fortress sits at one end of the great road that leads directly to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Bögenhafen/Bögenhafen\|Bögenhafen]], and from there onto [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]].

As guardian of the principle trade route into [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] from [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]], Helmgart is equal parts imposing citadel and bustling trade town. Here, [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Bretonnian\|Bretonnian]] merchants barter brandy, wine, fine fabrics, arms, and armour, while their [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reiklander]] counterparts in turn funnel their province’s goods south. The local [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]] clans, too, trade ingots of silver, lead, and iron, along with precious pieces of Dwarf-smithed metalwork. With so many merchants and traders, the [[Marketplatz of Helmgarth\|Marketplatz of Helmgarth]] is a bustling place, rife with endless opportunities and uncounted thieves.

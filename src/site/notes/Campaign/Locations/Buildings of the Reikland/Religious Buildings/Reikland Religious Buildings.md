Religion is everywhere in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]; all villages have a temple to [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]], and most sizeable towns have more than one. While other faiths are less dominant, in large [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Town\|towns]] and [[World/Other Nations/Cities\|cities]], you may well find temples to these other deities too. Smaller shrines – to all manner of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|gods]], well known and obscure – pepper theroadsides of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]’s heartland.

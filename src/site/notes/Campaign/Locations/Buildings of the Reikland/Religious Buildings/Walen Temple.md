Dominating the Marktplatz of the village of [[Walen\|Walen]], near [[Auerswald\|Auerswald]], the templeof Sigmar is the settlement’s heart in more ways than one. The relatively small building is still the largest in the surrounding area by some measure, facing its nearest rival – [[The Crooked Crook\|The Crooked Crook]] tavern – across the market square.

Like many [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmarite]] temples, the building is loosly laid out in the shape of a hammer, although in this caseit is reverse to the norm, with the “hammer” and “claw” positioned by the entrance rather than in the rear.

It has high, thick walls, making it source of physical as well as spiritual sancity, and it is the first point of retreat should the village be attacked. In addition to the main temple – in which the faithful stand to hear the priest’s sermons – a number of smaller buildings cling to the temple’s rear, including chambers forthe local priest, [[Father Stihlman\|Father Stihlman]], and his beadle, [[Klaus Bahlk\|Klaus Bahlk]]. Stihlmanalso keeps a small, cosy office – which has a small fire burning all year through – where he offers spiritual counsel and confessions to the villagers outwith his services. Above the officeis the “new” bell tower, a somewhat rickety construction that poorly fits with the rest ofthe stone temple. While [[Father Stihlman\|Father Stihlman]] is a placid, genial man, his beadle [[Klaus Bahlk\|Klaus]] is taciturn and dour, if not actively hostile. However heis readily appeased by generous donations to the temple’s upkeep.

Though the tempe is always open [[Festag\|Festag]] is the main day of worship and all locals from miles around are expected to turn up for the weekly “Festag Throng”. Though the templeonce held all villagers and nearby framers easily, as [[Walen\|Walen]] has grown, space has become tighter. These days, latecomers have to linger outside the temple’s main doors, and [[Father Stihlman\|Father Stihlman]] does his best to project his voice.

Beyond its spiritual responsibilites, the temple of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] is also central in the training of the village’s militia – all men and women of age must attend a number of training sessions over the course of the year, to ensure they stand ready to defend the village, and one another in the face of attack. While [[Father Stihlman\|Father Stihlman]] offers words of encouragement, any actual training or advice during these sessions tend to come from [[Else Frink\|Else Frink]], a retired [[Rules/Career/Road Warden\|Road Warden]] and [[State Soldier\|State Soldier]], who now calls [[Walen\|Walen]] home.

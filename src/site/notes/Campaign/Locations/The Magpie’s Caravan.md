# The Magpie’s Caravan
A shrine of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] which travels in the back of a covered wagon between [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]] and the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]. The presiding [[Rules/Career/Thief-Priest\|Thief-Priest]] is [[Campaign/NPCs/Valasta Malden\|Valasta Malden]], a woman who never stay in one place for longer than a night.

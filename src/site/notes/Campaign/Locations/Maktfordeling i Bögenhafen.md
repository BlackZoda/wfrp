[[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Bögenhafen/Bögenhafen\|Bögenhafen]] ligger innenfor grensene til [[Hertugdømmet Saponatheim\|Hertugdømmet Saponatheim]] og faller under jurisdiksjonen til [[Campaign/NPCs/Graf Wilhelm von Saponatheim\|Graf Wilhelm von Saponatheim]] [1](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/wfrp-fragile-alliances-didz/a/the-duchy-of-saponathiem-location)[2](https://whfb.lexicanum.com/wiki/B%C3%B6genhafen)[6](https://periloustales.wordpress.com/2016/03/06/recap-the-journey-so-far/). Likevel viser greven liten interesse for byens anliggender, så lenge hans andel av byens skatteinntekter fortsetter å komme inn [6](https://periloustales.wordpress.com/2016/03/06/recap-the-journey-so-far/)[8](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/wfrp-fragile-alliances-didz/a/graf-wilhelm-von-saponatheim-person). Han holder hoff på [[Castle Grauenberg\|Castle Grauenberg]], omtrent 60 miles nord for byen, og besøker sjelden [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Bögenhafen/Bögenhafen\|Bögenhafen]] – han eier ikke engang noen eiendom innenfor bymurene [6](https://periloustales.wordpress.com/2016/03/06/recap-the-journey-so-far/)[8](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/wfrp-fragile-alliances-didz/a/graf-wilhelm-von-saponatheim-person). I praksis fungerer derfor [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Settlements/Bögenhafen/Bögenhafen\|Bögenhafen]] som en fristad, der byrådet utgjør den øverste myndighet [2](https://whfb.lexicanum.com/wiki/B%C3%B6genhafen)[6](https://periloustales.wordpress.com/2016/03/06/recap-the-journey-so-far/).

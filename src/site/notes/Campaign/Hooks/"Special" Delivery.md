A merchant is offering a ludicrously large fee to whomever will deliver a package. The catch is the package is bound for one of the Colleges of Magic; the merchant claims to be terrified of wizards. To complicate matters, no-one actually knows where the college in question — the Light College — can be found, with some claiming that it exists ‘outside of normal space’ – whatever that means. The package is warm to the touch and seems to vibrate faintly when pressed; just how desperate for cash are the would-be messengers?

* [[Berthold Becker\|Berthold Becker]] is concerned that some of his patrons seem to be disappearing, leaving via the back entrance and never returning. Concerned that they may be fallingafoul of footpads, he needs to hire some brave souls to flush them out. Little does he know that a gang of graverobbers, providing cadavers for a necromancer, have been waylaying his clientele, and that some of them may soon be returning after all.
* [[Della Becker\|Della Becker]]’s husband is not, in fact, dead. He was in a debtors’ prison in Atdorf, but has recently escaped. He want [[Berthold Becker\|Berthold Becker]]to pay him off to leave his sister and son, who he despises and blames for all his misfortune, alone. Becker is happy to do so, but concerned he will extort him for futher funds, so needs a little muscle to watch his back.
* As many of the Drum’s patrons are tourists, it’s a prime location for gangs of [[Rules/Career/Thief\|thieves]], [[Rules/Career/Bawd\|bawds]], and [[Rules/Career/Charlatan\|charlatans]] looking to fleece naive visitors and separate them from their purses. When the party’s purses get lifted, it’son them to get them back.

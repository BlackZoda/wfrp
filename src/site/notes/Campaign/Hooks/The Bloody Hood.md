Theinfamously tight-fisted [[Baron Gottfried of Ranachfels\|Baron Gottfried of Ranachfels]], has placed an unheard-of bounty of 40 gold crowns on the head of an infamous local outlaw known only as the [[Bloody Hood\|Bloody Hood]]. In response – and much to the amusement of the populace of the barony – the Bloody Hood has posted their own bounty of 100 crowns for Gottfried’s head!

Unknown to most, the [[Bloody Hood\|Bloody Hood]] is in actuality the [[Baroness Melusine Hedwig Stockbliz\|Baroness Melusine Hedwig Stockbliz]], and merely wants to collect the taes she believes she is owed, as Gottfried continously claims taxes from the town of [[Moerfort\|Moerfort]] on the border between their two baronies. Neither noble is above using any underhand tactics to get their way, including hiring a band of out-of-town Characters used to getting blood on their hands.

Judge Nussmeg is a notorious harsh magistrate who cares little for justice. He is known for exaggerating the charges against the accused and then sentecing them to arbitrary, random-selected punishments. Followers of the Protector believe they can humiliate and bring down Nussmeg just through the power of spreading tales of Ranald throughout the town. However, Viktor Vogt a Gambler-Priest, wonders whether the “Wheel of Justice” Nussmeg uses to select punishments suggests he is a secret follower of Ranald the Gamster.

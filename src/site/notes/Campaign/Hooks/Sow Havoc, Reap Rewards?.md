[[Duke Lumpo Hohenheim\|Duke Lumpo Hohenheim]] “The Sloppy” succumbed to [[Rules/Disease and Infection/The Black Plague\|The Black Plague]] only four months ago, but already his 18-year-old heir [[Duke Roricus Hohenheim\|Duke Roricus Hohenheim]] tires of leigedom. The young [[Duke\|Duke]] despises the town of [[Beckol\|Beckol]] in particular, full as it is with shiny merchants and making daily requests of their liege-lord  any distraction from hunting with his squires catches Roricus’ ire, but his obligaion to listen to the middle classes has proved the only responsibility that is impossible to dodge.

On hearing of the Characters, the Duke hatches a plan: hire these ne’er-do-wells to raise havoc in Beckol until the overly dependent merchants cough up for a charter to become a [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Freistadt\|Freistadt]]. He issues orders to his guards to let the Characters do as they please, expecting a real crime spree to spur the petulant merchants. Of course, Roricus has no intention of paying the Characters, and cares little about any cult activity they may discover during their rampage through the merchant houses.

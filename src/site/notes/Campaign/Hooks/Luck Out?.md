# Luck Out?
‘Herr Doktor’ Henrich Steinhauer is apparentl an elderly academic, but under his dusty robes is the young charlatan [[Udo Wentz\|Udo Wentz]], a man who thanks [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] for the good fortune he granted him to keep sending gullible ‘patients’ his way. He’s now worried his luck has run out – a dodgy batch of ‘cure-all’ has put the watch on his trail. He bursts into the Characters room and their inn and asks them to cover for him. In return, hell introduce them to a powerful priest of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] who resides in the town.

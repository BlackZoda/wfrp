Home: [[Campaign/Hooks/Adventure Hooks\|Adventure Hooks]], [[Campaign/NPCs/Raphael Julevno\|Raphael Julevno]]

Travellers tell that the mages from the far east have mastered the art of [[Rules/Career/Astromancer\|Astromancy]] beyond the greatest imaginings of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. Julevno wants to learn more, bu [[Weijin\|Weijin]]’s [[Rules/Career/Ambassador (level 4)\|Ambassador]] to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] offers no help. Julevno proposes a trip to [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]], whose [[Zijdemarkt\|Zijdemarkt]] district is home to expatriots who may reveal more. If that is not successful, he is willing to find a journey east along the [[Silk Road\|Silk Road]], where the Characters may discover the truth in person.

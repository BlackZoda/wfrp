If a [[Runefang\|Runefang]] were to be lost, such a scandal would be kept secret. The [[World/The Empire/Politics/Titles/Elector\|Elector]] who owned it would pay handsomely for its return. At least, that’s what [[Campaign/NPCs/Roberta P. Wildschwein\|Roberta P. Wildschwein]] believes. After buying [[Beast Slayer\|Beast Slayer]] from a disgraced former member of the [[Reiksguard\|Reiksguard]], Roberta plans to return it and retire on the fortune she’s sure to be owed.

The “Runefang” has a short, strangely undulating blade, and a faintly glowing, almost eye-like stone set in the crossguard. The chrlatan who sold Roberta the blade felt he should offload it quickly after pilfering it from a quiet fellow wering purple pantaloons, and rightly so: [[Campaign/Organizations/Den Lilla Hånden\|Den Lilla Hånden]] are already searching for the blade, and mean to dispatch anone who knows of it. Roberta might need some help.

# Jakob and Wilhelmina Kauter (c2475)
Twin [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priests]] of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]], Wilhelmina is a [[Trickster-Priest\|Trickster-Priest]] and Jakob a [[Liberator-Priest\|Liberator-Priest]]. It is their life’s work to break into the homes of [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchants]] and [[Rules/Career/Noble\|Nobles]], steal precious artworks, and replace them with a near-perfect forgery. On close inspection, subtle changes can be seen – a wealthy burgomeister is now notably porcine and his hat is a saucepan, a heroic general has the tail and ears of an ass, and so on. The Kauters are also responsible for hoaxes. They claim to have created the celebrated [[Obernarn Stone\|Obernarn Stone]], which tells a tale of the gods (including [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] and obscure entities like [[Sotek\|Sotek]] and [[Margileo\|Margileo]]) fighting the [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Ruinous Powers]].

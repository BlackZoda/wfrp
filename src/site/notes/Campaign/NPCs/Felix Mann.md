# Felix Mann (c2020 to 2061 IC)
Felix Mann was the greatest thief of his age, with a dozen aliases and as many wealthy widows and merchants fooled into trusting him. His greatest exploit occured at [[The Siege of Altdorf\|The Siege of Altdorf]] during [[The First Vampire War\|The First Vampire War]]. The [[World/Religion and Belief/Titles/Grand Theogonist\|Grand Theogonist]] offered him a pardon and riches if he would steal the famed [[Carstein Ring\|Carstein Ring]] from the hand of the [[Vampire Count\|Vampire Count]] [[Vlad von Carstein\|Vlad von Carstein]]. In 2051 IC robbed Vlad of his immortality, allowing the [[World/Religion and Belief/Titles/Grand Theogonist\|Grand Theogonist]] to defeat the vampire. He is remembered as a Hero of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]]], and [[World/Religion and Belief/Worshippers/Worshippers of Ranald\|Ranaldans]] claim he was one of their own – the lack of concrete evidence being sure proof of his cunning.

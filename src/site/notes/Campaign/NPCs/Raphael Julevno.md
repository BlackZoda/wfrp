Julevno is a rising prodigy in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic/The Celestial College\|The Celestial College]], apparently destined to become [[High Celestial\|High Celestial]]. However, he is not ambitious and would rather spend his time researching his own talents than intriguing his way to the top of the order. Julevno looks younger than his 47 years, clean-shaven and attired in the lavish robes typical of the college. He has already quite a reputation in the order. He was the youngest accepted magister in history and his mastery of [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]] is instinctive and effortless. Julevno was born with an innate ability to see blue wind and experience brief intimations of possible futures.

He is distant during conversation and struggles to look others in the eye, preferring gaze upwards in contemplation. In truth, Julevno is constantly sifting through unbidden visions to remain focussed on the present.

He is a keen scholar with a special interest in how [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]] moves against the flow of time. Julevno has a theory that the heavenly bodies and [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]] move in secret harmonies, creating an abstract music which resonates perpetually without regards for temporal constraints. This theory arose hen he found that music helps him control his visions, so Julevno is often found seeking peace at [[Altdorf’s Grand Opera House\|Altdorf’s Grand Opera House]].

Julevano was born to an actress in [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Wissenland/Nuln\|Nuln]], who resented his strange behaviour and tried to put him into a [[Workhouse\|Workhouse]]. The boy fled to become a [[Rules/Career/Beggar\|Beggar]], but visions of a massive building with sizteen towers disturbed his dreams. He as drawn to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] and [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic/The Celestial College\|The Celestial College]].

hen he arrived at the great entrance, almost dead from hunger and exhaustion, [[Lord Stern Glazend\|Lord Stern Glazend]] greeted him – the magister had forseen his coming and immediately took him as an apprentice. After his swift ascent to magister, Julevno established himself in [[Glazend’s Tower\|Glazend’s Tower]] in the college.

The political wrangling within the colege is taking its toll on Julevno. He support [[Lord Stern Glazend\|Lord Stern Glazend]], but would rather the two factions looked outwards to the threats to [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s magisters – the [[Rules/Career/Witch Hunter\|Witch Hunters]], the forces of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]], and the whims of the [[World/The Empire/Demography/Noble House\|Nobility]]. When he expresses these opinions, other [[Rules/Career/Astromancer\|Astromancers]] claim that he’s getting ahead of himself and that he’ll get to the top of the collge when it’s forseen and not before. Julevno is a solitary man and although the rest of the order respect himg greatly, there are few who are close enough to be his friends.

Now Julevno is looking for resourceful people outside the college, who can help with his research, advance the cause of [[The Celestial Order\|The Celestial Order]] and thwart those who he has forseen will cause ruin to [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]].

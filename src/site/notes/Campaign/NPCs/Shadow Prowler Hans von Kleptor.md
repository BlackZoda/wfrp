# Nattsnikeren Hans von Kleptor (2471 IC)
The most prominent priest of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] in [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|The Old World]] today, von Kleptor leads the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Ranald\|Ranaldian Cult]] in [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]] from the [[Gilt House\|Gilt House]], the closest thing the port city has to a temple of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]]. Also knon as *Kurt von Shent*, a notorious charlatan, von Kleptor seeks to unite the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald/Aspects of Ranald\|Aspects of Ranald]] and wishes to bring his cult out of the shadows. Few of his fellow cultists agree though, as the shadows suit them just fine.

# Count Jäger
A strange character roams [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], from the poorest slum to the highest palaces. Although he is apparently insane, rumour has it he is an avatar of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]], and to meet him brings great luck. See page 173 of **Altdorf: Crown of Empire**for more on Count Jager.

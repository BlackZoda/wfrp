The sheer scale of the Empire, and the world beyond, means that travel is a necessary part of life for transient Characters. One of the main differences between the Characters and run-of-the-mill citizens of the Empire is that they lack roots that force them to stay in one place. Thes freedom comes with dire complications: [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s roads, whilst being the veins of commerce, are infested with [[Bandit\|Bandit]]s; the forests look dark, full of the braying of [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastman]] and the cries of [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Goblin\|Goblin]]s; the looming mountains are crawling with [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Orc\|Orc]]s and worse.

Travel often fills the gaps between adventures, and can provide an opportunity to engage in other activities similar to the [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Between Adventures\|Between Adventures]] chapter on page 192 of **WFRP**. Other times, travelling is the adventure itself. Regardless of when it occurs, this chapter provides tools to tell dramatic tales while on the road.

To begin, the Characters must determine their destination or direction. With a destination in mind, the GM can determine how long the jouney will take, dividing the travel time into Stages. During each [[Stage\|Stage]] of a journey, the GM rolls for [[Weather\|Weather]], the Characters may engage in [[Travel Endeavour\|Travel Endeavour]]s and any [[Rules/Tables/Travel Event\|Travel Event]]s that take place are resolved. The [[Arrival Phase\|Arrival Phase]] occurs as the characters reach their destination.

Attributes describe your character’s innate capabilities – how fast or strong you are, how much damage you can sustain, or how well you cope with stressful situations. Attributes include [[Rules/Attributes/Movement (M)\|Movement (M)]], ten [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]], [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds (W)]], [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]], and [[Rules/Attributes/Resilience\|Resilience]]. Closely connected to Fate and Resilience is also [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]], [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]], and [[Rules/Attributes/Ambition\|Ambition]] (and [[Rules/Attributes/Party Ambition\|Party Ambition]]).

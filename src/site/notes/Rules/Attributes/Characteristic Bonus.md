Home: [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]]

The first or “tens” digit of each Characteristic is its bonus value. Characteristic Bonuses are used in a variety of different ways throughout the rules, particularly for limiting [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s and defining [[Spell\|Spell]]s.

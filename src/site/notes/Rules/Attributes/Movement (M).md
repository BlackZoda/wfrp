Movement is used to determine your [[Walk\|Walk]] and [[Run\|Run]] speed. Standard [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] Movement is 4. If using a grid for movement this indicates how many squares you can move in a [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], or inches across the table top. Over the longer term it indicates how many miles per hour you can comfortably travel on foot.

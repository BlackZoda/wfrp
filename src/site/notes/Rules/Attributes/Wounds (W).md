* [[Rules/Attributes/Determining Wounds\|Determining Wounds]]

Your Wounds show how much [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] you can endure based on your physical power and your force of will to shrug off injury. See **Chapter 5: Rules** for how Wounds are used to track [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]].

Your ability to get on with people and come across as generally pleasant and acceptable. It will help you with [[Rules/Skills/Gossip\|chatting to locals]] or [[Rules/Skills/Leadership\|commanding people in battle]], [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|charming guards]] or [[Rules/Skills/Bribery\|attempt to bribe them]], and, for pious characters, [[Rules/Skills/Pray\|communicate with your deity]].

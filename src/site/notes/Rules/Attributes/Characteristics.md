Your ten Charactersistics are used in [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]s, to determine if you perform an action successfully. The [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] average for these Attributes is 30. Those who are figter or well-practiced, may achieve scores of over 40; only the most dedicated and esperienced will achieve scores over 60. Of course other Species have their own strengths and weaknesses, so their averages are different, sometimes significantly so.

## Effect
* *Move* and *Action* must be used to run away and hide
* *-10* to all Tests not involving running and hiding
## Remove
* Can't be [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] with an enemy
* [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]] Test at end of each Round
* Each *SL* removes an extra *Broken Condition*
* Adjust for cicumstances
* Spend a full Round in hiding out of line-of-sight of any enemy to remove 1 *Broken Condition*
## Consequences
* Gain 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] Condition once all *Broken* Conditions are removed

## Effect
* No Action
* Half movement
* Can't defend with Language (Magick) in opposed Tests
* -10 penalty to all Tests
* Opponents trying to strike you in [[Melee Combat\|Melee Combat]] gains +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] before rolling the attack
## Remove
* **Challenging (+0) Endurance** Test to remove a *Stunned* Condition.
* Each **SL** removes an extra *Stunned* Condition.
## Consequences
* Gain 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] Condition if you don't already have one once all *Stunned* Conditions are removed.

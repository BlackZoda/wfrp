## Effect
* Lose 1 Wound at the end of every Round
* -10 penalty to all Tests to resist [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Festering Wound\|Festering Wound]], [[Minsor Infection\|Minsor Infection]], or [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Blood Rot\|Blood Rot]]
## Falling Unconscious
* At 0 Wounds, you fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]]
* At the end of Round, you have 10% chance of dying per *Bleeding Condition*
* Can't regain consciousness until all *Bleeding* Conditions are removed (see [[Rules/Injuries/Injury\|Injury]])
## Remove
A *Bleeding* Condition can be removed with:
* A successful [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] Test – each *SL* removes an extra *Bleeding* condition
* Any spell or prayer that heals Wounds – one Condition per Wound healed
## Consequences
* Gain one [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] Condition when all *Bleeding* Conditions are removed.

## Effect
* You can't Move
* -10 to all actions involving any kind of movement (including [[Rules/Combat/Grappeling\|Grappeling]])
## Remove
* Remove one *Entagled* Condition with an **Opposed Strength** Test against the source of the entanglement
* Remove an extra *Entangled* Condition per **SL**

## Effect
* Lose 1 Wound at the end of each Round
* -10 penalty to all Tests
## Falling Unconscious
* At 0 Wounds you cannot heal any Wounds until all *Poisoned* conditions are removed
* If you fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] when *Poisoned* make an **Endurance** Test after a number of Rounds equal to your **TB** or die (see [[Rules/Injuries/Injury\|Injury]])
## Remove
* Difficulty determined by the poison or venom suffered
* **Endurance** Test at end of Round
* Each **SL** removes an extra *Poisoned* Condition
* A **Heal** Test provides the same results
## Consequences
* Gain 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] Condition when all *Poisoned* Conditions are removed.

## Effect
* Move can only be used to crawl at half of Movement or to stand up (if you're above 0 Wounds)
* -20 penalty to all Test invoving movement of any kind
* +20 bonus to Opponents who tries to strike you in Melee Combat
* Does not stack like most other conditions
## Remove
* When you stand up

## Effect
* [[Melee Attack\|Melee Attack]] targeting you automatically hits on the location of the attacker's choice with the maximum possible SL
* [[Melee Attacks\|Melee Attacks]] inflicts a [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]]
* Same apply for [[Point Blank\|Point Blank]] range for ranged attacks
* Does not stack
## Remove
* Depends on the cricumstances you fell unconscious. Refer [[Rules/Injuries/Injury\|Injury]]
* If you spend a [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]] point to remove it, but haven't resolved the cause of the incapacitation, you gain another *Unconscious* Condition at the end of the round
## Consequences
* You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] conditions when you lose the *Unconscious* Condition

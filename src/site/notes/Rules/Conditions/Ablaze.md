## Effect
* Roll **1d10** Wounds at the end of every Round
* Modified by *TB*, but not *Armor Bonus*
* Minimum 1 Wound
* Each *Ablaze Condition* adds +1
## Remove
* One *Ablaze Condition* removed by *Athletics Test*
* Each *SL* removes an extra *Ablaze Condition*
* Adjust difficulty by circumstances

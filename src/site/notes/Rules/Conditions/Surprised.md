## Effect
* Can take no Action or Move on your turn
* Cannot defend yourself in opposed Tests
* Opponents gets +20 to hit you in [[Melee Combat\|Melee Combat]]
* Does not stack
## Remove
* Remove the *Suprised* Condition at the end of each round, after the first attempt to attack you

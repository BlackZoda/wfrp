The Wrath of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]] table is referenced whenever you [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]] a [[Rules/Skills/Pray\|Pray]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], or when the unit die of a [[Rules/Skills/Pray\|Pray]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is equal or lower than our current [[World/Religion and Belief/Strictures/Sin Points\|Sin Points]]. The GM may also use it, or pluck results from it, whenever you foolishly insult any of the gods. When rolling on the *Wrath of the Gods Table*, add +10 to the roll for each [[World/Religion and Belief/Strictures/Sin Points\|Sin Point]] you have accrued. After rolling and applying the result, reduce your [[World/Religion and Belief/Strictures/Sin Points\|Sin Point]] by 1, to a minimum of 0.

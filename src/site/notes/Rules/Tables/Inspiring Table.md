> **Example for [[Rules/Talents/Inspiring (Talent)\|Inspiring (Talent)]]:**
> Abbess Birgitte van der Hoogenband’s monastery is under attack by [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]], and things are going badly. So, she decides to bolster her soldiers’ spirits with a [[Rules/Skills/Leadership\|Leadership]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], granting them *+10 to all Psychology Tests*. Her Leadership Test scores *3 SL*. Given she has a [*[Fellowship]] Bonus of 6*, and she can influence her *Fellowship Bonus + SL* of her soldiers using Leadership, she bolsters 9 soldiers. However, as she has Inspiring 3, that number is multiplied by 20, meaning 180 of her soldiers take heart from her screamed encouragement to, ‘HOLD THE LINE!’

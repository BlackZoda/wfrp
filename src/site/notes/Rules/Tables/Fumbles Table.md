| Roll  | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01–20 | You catch a part of your anatomy (we recommend you play this for laughs) — lose 1 Wound, ignoring [[Toughness Bonus\|Toughness Bonus]] or [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]].                                                                                                   |
| 21–40 | Your melee weapon jars badly, or ranged weapon malfunctions or slightly breaks – your weapon suffers 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]]. Next round, you will act last regardless of Initiative order, [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s, or special rules as you recover (see page 156). |
| 41–60 | Your manoeuvre was misjudged, leaving you out of position, or you lose grip of a ranged weapon. Next round, your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] suffers a *penalty of –10*.                                                                                       |
| 61–70 | You stumble badly, finding it hard to right yourself. Lose your next [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]].                                                                                                                                                                |
| 71–80 | You mishandle your weapon, or you drop your ammunition. Miss your next [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]].                                                                                                                                                            |
| 81–90 | You overextend yourself or stumble and twist your ankle. Suffer a [[Rules/Injuries/Torn Muscle\|Torn Muscle]] (Minor) injury (see page 179). This counts as a [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]].                                                                                         |
| 91–00 | You completely mess up, *hitting 1 random ally in range* using your *rolled units die to determine the SL of the hit*. If that’s not possible, you somehow hit yourself in the face and gain a [[Stunned Condition\|Stunned Condition]] (see page 169).          |

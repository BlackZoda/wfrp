Here is a list of the primary languages of the Old World, and who most commonly speaks them. There are an enormous number of other tongues spoken, but these are the most commonly encountered in the Reikland.

| Language              | Spoken by…                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Albion (Language)\|Albion (Language)]] | …the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Albion (Language)\|Albion (Language)]], who hail from a far-off mist-shrouded island-realm and are rarely encountered.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| [[Rules/Skills/Battle Tongue\|Battle Tongue]]     | …warriors of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. It is very common amongst soldiers and mercenaries and is used for giving orders swiftly during battle. It was supposedly developed by the goddess [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]] when she walked [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] as a mortal.                                                                                                                |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Bretonnian\|Bretonnian]]        | …the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Bretonnia/Bretonnia\|Bretonnia]], who are commonly encountered in the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]]. Bretonnia is a chivalric realm to the south-west of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]].                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Classical\|Classical]]         | …[[World/Races/Human\|Human]] [[Rules/Class/Academic\|Academics]] of all kinds. It is the first recorded Human language of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] – over 3000 years old – used in most scholarly texts of the Empire including wizard grimoires and many [[Holy Book\|Holy Book]]s.                                                                                                                          |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Elthárin\|Elthárin]]          | …the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Athel Loren\|Athel Loren]], the [[Laurelorn\|Laurelorn]], and visiting merchants from [[Ulthuan\|Ulthuan]]. Elthárin is the language of the [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]]. The tonal language has many dialects, and is notoriously difficult for non-Elves to learn.                                                                                                                          |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Estalian\|Estalian]]          | …the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Estalia/Estalia\|Estalia]], people of the hot, southern-western kingdoms known for their bitter politics and worship of the goddess [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]].                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Gospodarinyi\|Gospodarinyi]]      | …the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Kislev/Kislev\|Kislev]], a hardy people from the frozen oblast to the east of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. The languageis often called *Kislevarin* or *Kislevite* by outsiders.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Grumbarth\|Grumbarth]]         | …[[World/Races/Ogre\|Ogres]]; few others learn this simple tongue, though some mercenary commanders and [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] gaffers find it useful for communicating with any Ogres they may hire.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Khazalid\|Khazalid]]          | …the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Karaz Ankor\|Karaz Ankor]], the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]] kingdoms in the mountains. This language is also wide-spread through the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Cult of Sigmar]], which venerates the strong relationship [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] had with the [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]] when he was mortal.                                                                                                                |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]            | …wizards; a tonal language used to shape the [[Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] into material effects. The academic form of the language taught by the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Colleges of Magic\|Colleges of Magic]] is called the [[Lingua Praestantia\|Lingua Praestantia]], which is quite different to the debased form of the language used by [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witch]] and the untrained. It is not a language used for communication.             |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Mootish\|Mootish]]           | …the folk of the [[World/The Empire/Provinces/Mootland\|Mootland]]; i.e.: the [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] grand province in the Empire. This language is little known outside the [[Moot\|Moot]].                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Norse\|Norse]]             | …the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Norsca/Norsca\|Norsca]]. Norse is commonly spoken across the north of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] in [[Nordland\|Nordland]] and [[Ostland\|Ostland]], and is well-known in the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]] and [[World/Other Nations/Kislev/Kislev\|Kislev]], too.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Reikspiel\|Reikspiel]]         | …the folk of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. Reikspiel is the primary language used in most WFRP games. It manifests in uncounted discrete dialects from across the Empire, most of which are mutually intelligible. It is the modern version of the tongue once spoken by the God-King [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]] when he still walked as a mortal over two thousand five hundred years ago. |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Queekish\|Queekish]]          | …the folk of the [[Under-Empire\|Under-Empire]]. Used by the [[Skaven\|Skaven]] and their many slaves. Few admit to speaking it, or even being aware of its existence.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Tilean\|Tilean]]            | …the folk of [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tilea]], who are most commonly encountered in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] as [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchants]] or [[Mercenaries\|Mercenaries]]. The [[Tilean Princedoms\|Tilean Princedoms]] to the south are notorious for their deadly politics and their veneration of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]].                                                                                                               |
| [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Thieve's Tongue\|Thieve's Tongue]]    | …the street folk of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. Expressions of this language are highly localised and individual to each realm – e.g. [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tilea]]ns sport a completely different ‘Thieves Tongue’ to folk of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]].                                                                                                                                             |

1. Roll 1d100 on the table. If you don’t like the result, move to step 2. If you keep the result gain +50 XP.
2. Roll twice more on the table, bringing your total to 3 choices. If one of the three now suits you, select one and gain +25 XP. If not, movet to step 3.
3. Choose our [[Rules/Class/Class\|Class]] and and [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]], or keep rerolling on the table until you get something you like. There is no XP bonus for this.

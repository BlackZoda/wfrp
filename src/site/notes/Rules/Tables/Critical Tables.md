On a *Critical Wound* roll a **d100** instead of reversing the roll on the [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location Table]]. Then roll on the appropriate *Critical Wounds Table* and sufer the wounds indicated, not modified by *TB* or *Armor Points*:
* [[Rules/Tables/Head Critical Wounds\|Head Critical Wounds]]
* [[Rules/Tables/Arm Critical Wounds\|Arm Critical Wounds]]
* [[Rules/Tables/Body Critical Wounds\|Body Critical Wounds]]
* [[Rules/Tables/Leg Critical Wounds\|Leg Critical Wounds]]

I the character suffers the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition, you remain so until the end of the encounter, or until you receive [[Rules/Healing and Death/Medical Attention\|Medical Attention]]. For injury types, refer to [[Rules/Injuries/Broken Bone\|Broken Bone]], [[Rules/Injuries/Torn Muscle\|Torn Muscle]], or [[Rules/Injuries/Amputated Parts\|Amputated Parts]].

Before selecting your [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]]s, you first roll *1d100* on the Events table. Some Events will only affect you, others may affect the whole party.

## 01–03: Accused Ally
One of you rallies (GM’s choice) is implicated in a terrible crime. A character with a [[Law Guild Licence\|Law Guild Licence]] can spend an [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] to free the ally with a successful **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (Law)** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. Otherwise, 3 [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]]s spent by the party will exonerate their ally (or, if guilty, manufacture an alibi). If the ally is freed, gain a Major [[Favour\|Favour]] for future use. If not, their ally hangs!
## 04–06: Arcane Auction
The belongings of a recently ceceased neighbour are auctioned including a dusty, old book of unknown origin. If you are able to pay the **10 GC** asking price, the book is yours! Beyond any other advantage the book may bring as determined by the GM any Academic characters attempting the [[Rules/Between Adventures/Class Endeavours/Research Lore\|Research Lore]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] gains +20 on the [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].
## 07–10: Betrayed!
A friend, family member, or ally turns on you, the ramifications of which will impact your next adventure. If you have no friends, family, or allies, your time between adventrures are blissfully uneventful though somewhat dull.
## 11–14: Bolt from the Blue
A lazy ostler didn’t lock the stable door, and your mount bolted! If you succeed at an **Average (+20) Animal Training (Horse)** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], your well-trained beast returns to the hand that feeds it. If you fail, your steed is gone, unlikely to ever be seen again. If you don’t havea mount, you have developed a painful new blister on your foot.
## 15–18: Oi! You spilled my pint!
A petty argument in the local area has developed into a feud – the GM decides who you offended and how. This person will not pass up an opportunity for petty revenge, probably during the next adventure…
## 19–21: Crime Crackdown
The Watch is cracking down on crime hard. [[Rouges\|Rouges]] can get no funds from the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]], and if Rouges undertake a [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Banking\|Banking]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]], they may only select a *High-risk* deposit, as legitimate banking houses won’t handle dirty money.
## 22–25: The Exciseman Cometh
An unexpected visit by the tax collector (with several soldiers in tow) sweeps through your local settlement. All characters lose 30% of their funds before any can be spent on [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]]s.
## 26–29: False Silver
To the horror of the merchants, a run of conterfeit coins plagues the characters’ locale. A fifth of all coins are affected. Characters who undertake the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Banking\|Banking]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] will lose 20% of meny banked, and characters undertaking the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] similarly reduce any money earned by 20%.
## 30–33: Flowing Profit
Business is particularly good for folk involved in river trade. All [[Rules/Class/Riverfolk\|Riverfolk]] make an additional 50% from any [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] pursued during the next batch of [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]].
## 37–40: Festivities
A celebration is called! Decide the nature of the event with the GM. Possibilities includes a local wedding, a bountiful harvest, or a public execution! You are cought up in the event (and its aftermath) and lose an [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]].
## 41–44: Inclement Weather
Particularly bad weather arrives. For the next adventure, all social Skill [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]s suffer a penalty of -10 (everyone is in a foul mood) and food prices increase by 20% (due to spoiling of stored provender).
## 45–48: Glorious Weather
Beautiful conditions inspire you and give you cheer. You can add a new Short-term [[Rules/Attributes/Ambition\|Ambition]]. When it’s concluded, you do not replace it with a new one.
## 49–52: Local Crop Failure
Food becomes exceedingly scarce and many folk suffer terribly. Characters from the [[Rules/Class/Peasant\|Peasant]] class cannot undertake the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] Endeavour, and food prices are doubled in the area for the duration of the next adventure.
## 53–56: Malicious Malady
[[Rules/Disease and Infection/The Bloody Flux\|The Bloody Flux]] sweeps through town. Make an **Easy (+40) Toughness** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. On a sucess, the Flux passes by. On a failure, you and the [[Washers Guild\|Washers Guild]] are soon to know each other very well. Contract [[Rules/Disease and Infection/The Bloody Flux\|The Bloody Flux]].
## 57–60: Monstrous Complications
A monster (chosen by the GM from [[Beastiary\|Beastiary]]) is causing panic among the locals. The [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] provides no funds until the beast is dealt with. The characters can choose to each sacrifice one [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] to deal with the menace (and this encounter should be roleplayed). If you succeed, you gain a free [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] to represent their individual rewards, and a feast is held in their honour. If not, or if the party chooses to ignore the creature, it will move on or be killed by a local rival.
## 61–63: Morr’s Embrace
One of the character’s relatives, friends, or allies dies. It could be of natural causes, an accident, or the beginnings of something sinister…
## 64–65: New Moon
The nights are particularly dark. All [[Rouge\|Rouge]] characters undertaking the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] receive a +20% bonus to their monies earned.
## 66–67: Old Debts
You have a *Major* or *Significant* [[Favour\|Favour]] called in. Dealing with the favour will be part of your next adventure and you lose one [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] in preparation for what is to come.
## 68–69: Opportunity Saunters Through
Passing soldiers, well-heeled merchants or vacationing nobles pass through the area, anc characters willing to suck up reap the benefits. [[Rules/Class/Burgher\|Burgher]] and [[Rules/Class/Peasant\|Peasant]] Class characters gain +50% money from the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]].
## 71–71: PEace and Quiet
It’s time like these that remind you what life is all about: good sleep and fine cheese. Be smugly content at the start of your next adventure.
## 72–73: Pedlar
A wizened, well-travelled pedlar who likes a gossip passes through. For the price of a flagon of ale – 3d – you receive a +10 bonus to any [[Latest News\|Latest News]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]]s you attempt.
## 74–76: Pestilential Pet
One of your animals fall ill; make a **Challenging (+0) Animal Care** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If successful, your beast pulls through. If not, the unfortunate creature dies. If you have no animals, you are troubled by ill omens of the GM’s creation.
## 77–79: Ransacked
Before you can undertake a [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Banking\|Banking]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]], your stash is raided, and all your money is taken. If your money amounts to less than *1 GC*, the thieves also steal your most highly valued trapping.
## 80–82: Riots
The common folk are furious at the high and mighty! Characters of the [[Rules/Class/Courtier\|Courtier]] [[Rules/Class/Class\|Class]] cannot take the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] as, no matter how many bodyguards they hire, it isn’t even vaguely safe for them to be abroad on business. Further, any deposits at reputable banks ([[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Banking\|Banking]]) must immediately check to see if the voilence and disruption has caused their bank to go out of business. However, players attempting a [[Rules/Between Adventures/Class Endeavours/Foment Dissent\|Foment Dissent]] Endeavour receive +10 bonus to all related Tests.
## 83–85: Sticky Fingers
Your purse is cut! You lose half of the money you ended last adventure with.
## 86–88: Suspicion of Heresy
You fall foul of a [[Which Hunter\|Which Hunter]] who suspects you have been consorting with [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Mutant\|Mutant]]s, [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Cultist\|Cultist]]s, or worse on your so-called “adventures”. It takes a **Very Hard (-30) Charm** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to convince the hunter you are innocent. Failure to do this means you have earned an implacable nemesis who is certain to cause trouble in the future…
## 89–91: Under Suspicion
The party’s irregular movements and sudden wealth has drawn suspicion. All characters must forfeit one [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] laying low or proving their innocence. Characters in the [[Rouge\|Rouge]] class cannot use the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] until after their next adventure.
## 92–94: Uneventful
Little of interest occurs, perhaps leaving you with a boredome-inspired appetite for taking risks!
## 95–97: Unexpected Esteem
Someone you helped in the past has a chance to repay your efforts. The precise nature of the reward should be appropriate to your past deeds, and the NPCs you have assisted during play or in your back story. The tokens of gratitude may vary from a single high-quality item, to a purse of silver (which will be available at the start of the next adventure). Of course, all that glitters is not gold, and not all gifts are what they appear…
## 98–00: Unusual Mercenaries
One or more rare mercenaries turn up in a nearby settlement looking for work: A Tilean Duellist of great repute, the so called Birdmen of Catrazza, unemployed Ogres under the command of a Hlafling captain, or other unlikely bands. The mercenaries will gladly train any character in any martial [[Skills\|Skills]] or [[Talents\|Talents]], at a 20% reduction in costs, should the characters undertake a [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Training\|Training]] or [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Unusual Learning\|Unusual Learning]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]]. Additionally, any characters undertaking a [[Rules/Between Adventures/Class Endeavours/Combat Training\|Combat Training]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] gain a +20 bonus to any relevant [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

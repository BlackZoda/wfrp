When determining [[Rules/Combat/Apply Damage\|Damage]] use your opponent’s [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus as your [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] Bonus if it is higher; always determine this before any other rules modify your [[Strenght\|Strenght]] or Strength Bonus. Further, if your target is larger than you, and you score a [[Rules/Tables/Critical Tables\|Critical]], multiply all [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] Damage you cause by the number of steps larger than your target (so, 2 steps = x2, r steps = 3x, and so on); this multiplication is calculated after all modifiers are applied. See [[Rules/Attributes/Size\|Size]].

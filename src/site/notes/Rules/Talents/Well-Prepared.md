**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus

You are used to anticipating the needs of others, and yourself. A number of times per session equal to your level of *Well-Prepared*, you may pull the trapping required for the current situation from your backpack (or similar) as long as it is [[Rules/Trappings/Encumberance/Encumberance\|Encumberance]] 0, could feasibly been bought recently, and doesn’t stretch credibility too far. This could be anything from a [[Flask of Spirits\|Flask of Spirits]] to fortify a wounded comrade to a pfenning-whistle needed by a passing entertaineer. Whenever you do this, you must deduct the cost for the prepared item from your purse, representing the coin you spent earlier.

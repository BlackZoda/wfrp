**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] when in-fighting, or to enter in-fighting

You are skilled at drawing in close to an opponent. You suffer no penalties for fighting an opponent with a longer weapon than you. Further, if you use the optional rules for [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/In-fighting\|In-fighting]], gain a bonus of +10 to hit your opponent.

**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Ranged\|Ranged]] when making a Fast Shot

If you have a loaded ranged weapon, you can fire it outside the normal [[Rules/Combat/Initiative Order\|Initiative Order]] before any other combatants reacts in the following [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]]. ou roll to hit using all the normal modifiers. Emplying *Fast Shot* requires both your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] and [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]] for your upcomint turn, and these will count as having been spent when your next turn arrives. If two or more characters use *Fast Shot*, the character who has taken this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] most goes first. If any characters have taken Fast Shot an equal number of times, both shots are fired simultaneously, and should both be handled at the same time.

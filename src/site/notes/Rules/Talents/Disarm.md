**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] concerning this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]

You are able to disarm an opponent with a careful flick of the wrist or a well-aimed blow to the hand. For your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]], you may attempt an **Opposed Melee/Melee** test. If you win, your opponent loses a held weapon, which flies 1d10 feet in a random direction (with further effects as determined by the GM). If you win by 2 SL, you can determine how far the weapon is flung instead of rolling randomly; if you win by 4 SL, you can also choose the direction the weapon goes in; if you win by 6 SL or more, you can take your opponent’s weapon if you have a free hand, plucking it from the air with a flourish. This [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] is of no use if your opponent has no weapon, or is a larger [[Rules/Tables/Size Difficulty Table\|Size]] than you.

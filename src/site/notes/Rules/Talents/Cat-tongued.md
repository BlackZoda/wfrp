Like [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] the [[Trickster God\|Trickster God]], you blend truth and lies as if there were no difference. When using [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] to lie, listeners do not get to oppose your Charm with their [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] to detect if there is something fishy in what you say.

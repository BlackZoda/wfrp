**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] ([[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Fencing Weapon\|Fencing]]) for Feint

You have trained how to make false attacks in close combat to fool your opponent. You may now make a Feint for your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] against any opponent using a weapon. This is resolved with an **[[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] ([[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Fencing Weapon\|Fencing]])/[[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]]** Test. If you win, and you attack the same opponent before the end of the next [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], yo may add the SL of our Feint to our attack roll.

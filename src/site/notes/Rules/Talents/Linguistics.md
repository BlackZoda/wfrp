You have a natural affinity for languages. Given a month’s exposure to any [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]], you count the associated [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] as a [[Basic Skills\|Basic Skill]] with a successful [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] (which can be attempted once per month). **Note:** Linguistics only works for *languages used to frequently communicate with others* , do does not work with [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]).

**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] to Distract

You are trained in somple movements to distract or startle your opponent, drawing eyes from your true intent. You may use your [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]] to perform a Distraction. This is resolved by an **Opposed [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]]/[[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you win, your opponent can gain no [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] until the end of the next [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]].

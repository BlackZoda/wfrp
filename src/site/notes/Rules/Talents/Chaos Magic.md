**Max:** Number of [[Spell\|Spell]]s available inchosen Chaos Magic [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]]. By accident or design you have lost a portion of your soul to one of the [[Dark Gods\|Dark Gods]], and can now practice the foul magics of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]]. Your ruinous patron immediately grants you access to a single spell from the cholsen [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (most commonly [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Nurgle\|Nurgle]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Slaanesh\|Slaanesh]], or [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] Lores) and you gain a [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] point as the spell infiltrates your mind, never to be forgotten.

Each time you take this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], which always costs 100 XP per time instead of the normal cost, you learn another spell from your chosen [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] and gain a [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] point. For more about the available spells, see [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]]. Under normal curcumstances, you may ever know one [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] of Chaos Magic.

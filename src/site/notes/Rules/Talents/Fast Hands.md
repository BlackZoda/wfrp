**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Sleight of Hand\|Sleight of Hand]], [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] ([[Brawling\|Brawling]] to touch an opponent)

You can move your hands with surprising dexterity. Bystanders get no passive [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to spot your use of the [[Rules/Skills/Sleight of Hand\|Sleight of Hand]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] instead they only get to [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Oppose]] your [[Rules/Skills/Sleight of Hand\|Sleight of Hand]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] if they actively suspect and are looking for your movements. Further, attempts to use [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] ([[Brawling\|Brawling]]) to simply touch an opponent gain a bonus of +10 x your level in [[Rules/Talents/Fast Hands\|Fast Hands]].

**Max:** [[Fellowship\|Fellowship]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] Tests when arguing and debating

You are used to arguing your points and winning. If you roll a successful [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] Test to debate with an opponent, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die. So, a successful roll of 24 could be used for +4 SL.

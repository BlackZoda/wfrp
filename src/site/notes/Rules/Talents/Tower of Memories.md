A recollection technique first instigated by the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Verena\|Cult of Verena]], reputedly from [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]] practices thought by [[Loremaster of Hoeth\|Loremaster of Hoeth]], *Tower of Memories* allows you to perfectly recall a sequence of facts by storing them in an imaginary spire. You can recall a sequence as long as your [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] without having to make a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. For every 10 more items you attempt to memorise, you must make an increasingly difficutl [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to recall the list correctly, starting at **Very Easy (+60)** for +10, **Easy (+40)** for +20, **Average (+0)** for +30, and so on. Beyond it’s obvious utility for [[Rules/Skills/Gamble\|Gamble]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], where having this talen adds a bonus of +20 to +60 depending upon how useful recaling sequences is to the game at hand, the GM can apply bonuses to other [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] as appropriate. Each time you take this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] you may recall an extra sequence without having to forget a previously stored one.

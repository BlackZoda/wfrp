Home: [[Rules/Talents/Talent List\|Talent List]]
**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus

You are a self-taught spellcaster who has figured out how to cast magic through trial and error. Add [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) to any [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] you enter; if it is already in your [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]], you may instead purchase the [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] for 5 XP fewer per [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advance]]. Further, for the cost of a [[Rules/Attributes/Resilience\|Resilience]] point, you may manifest (and permanently memorise) a spell from any [[Arcane Lore\|Arcane Lore]]. You can do this a number of times equal to your level in this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]].

You are used to desperate combats, able to turn even the direst circumstances to your [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]. If you win an **[[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], insted of gaining +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], you may take all your opponet’s Current Advantage. If you do this, you do not cause any [[Rules/Combat/Apply Damage\|Damage]], even if it is your [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]] in the [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]].

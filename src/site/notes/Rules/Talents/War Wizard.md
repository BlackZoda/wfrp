You are trained to cast [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]] while in the thick of combat. On your [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]], you may cast one [[Spell\|Spell]] with a [[Casting Number\|Casting Number]] of 5 or less for free without using your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]]. However, if you do this, you may not cast another [[Spell\|Spell]] this [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]].

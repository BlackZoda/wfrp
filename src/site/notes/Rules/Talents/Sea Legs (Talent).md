**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus
**Tests:** All those taken to resist [[Sea Sickness\|Sea Sickness]]

You are used to the rolling motion of the oceans, and are very unlikely to get sea sick, even in the worst storms. Under normal conditions at sea, you need never [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to see if you become [[Sea Sickness\|Sea Sick]]. At other times (such as a strom, or a magically unduced bout of Sea Sickness), you can ignore any penalties to [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to avoid Sea Sickness.

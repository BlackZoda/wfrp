You are either born into the nobility, or otherwise elevated to it by in-game events. Assuming you are dressed appropriately, you are always considered of higher [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] than others unless they also have the *Noble Blood* Talent, where [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] is compared as normal.

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]], the Chaos God of Magic, has gifted you with a worming parasite within your mind. When you purchase this Talent, a single [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]] wriggles free into your head where it takes residence, occasionally whispering to you as you sleep. The Spell can be chosen from any Colour Magic [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lore]].

After this, you may purchase one more Colour Magic spell for 100 XP for every spell you have memorised from the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch]].

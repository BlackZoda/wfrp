Once per [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], if you hit an opponent in close combat, you may immediately spend an [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] or your [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]] to make an extra attack (assuming you have your Move remaining).

You have the spark to cast magic within you and have mastered techniqes to control it at a basic level. When you take this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], ou manifest, and permanently memorise, a number of spells equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus. You can learn extra [[Rules/Talents/Petty Magic\|Petty Magic]] [[Spell\|Spell]]s for the following cost in XP.

* [[Rules/Tables/Petty Magic Spell Cost Table\|Petty Magic Spell Cost Table]]

So, if your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus is 3, it will cost you 50 XP for the first 3 spells, then 100 XP for the next 3, and so on. Full rules for new spells are provided in [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]].

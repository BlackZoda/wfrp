**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus

You are as tough as old boots and just soak up damage. You reduce all incoming [[Rules/Combat/Apply Damage\|Damage]] by an extra +1 per time you have taken the *Robust* [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], even if the [[Rules/Combat/Apply Damage\|Damage]] cannot normally be reduced, but still suffer a minimum of 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]] from any Damage source.

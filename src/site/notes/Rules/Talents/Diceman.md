**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Gamble\|Gamble]] and [[Rules/Skills/Sleight of Hand\|Sleight of Hand]] when playing dice games

You are a dicing master, and all claims you cheat are clearly wrong. When you successfully use [[Rules/Skills/Gamble\|Gamble]] or [[Rules/Skills/Sleight of Hand\|Sleight of Hand]] when playing with dice, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die. So, a successfull roll of 06 could be used for +6 SL. If you play any real-life dice games to represent in-game dice games, always roll extra dice equal to your Diceman level and choose the best results.

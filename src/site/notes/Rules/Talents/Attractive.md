**Max:** [[Fellowship\|Fellowship]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] Tests to influence those attracted to you

Whether it’s your piercing eyes, your strong frame, or maybe the way you flash your perfect teeth, you know how to make the best use of what the gods gave you. When you successsfully use [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] to influence those attracted to you, you can choose to either use your rolled SL or the number rolled on your units die. So, a successful roll of 38 could be used for +8 SL.

**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus
**Tests:** Any [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to spot Troublemakers

You are used to getting into, and preferably out of, troublt. You may attempt in [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to spot those seeking to cause trouble or seeking to cause you harm, even if normally you would not be allowed a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] (because of [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talents]] or a [[Spell\|Spell]] for example). This [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] will likely be [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] if others are hiding, and the GM may prefer to take this [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] on your behalf in secret so you do not know the results should you fail. If ny troublemakers you spot start combat, yo may ignore any [[Rules/Conditions/Surprised\|Surprised]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] they normally inflict.

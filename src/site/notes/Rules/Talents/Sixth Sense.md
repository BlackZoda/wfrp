**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] involving your *Sixth Sense*

You get a strange feeling when you are threatened, and can react accordingly. The GM may warn you if you are walking into danger; this will normally come after a secret [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] on your behalf. Further, you may ignore [[Rules/Combat/Surprise\|Surprise]] if you pass an [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

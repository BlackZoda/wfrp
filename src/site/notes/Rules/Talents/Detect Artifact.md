**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to detect [[Magical Artefacts\|Magical Artefacts]]

You are able to sense when magic lies within an artefact. You may attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] for any magical artefact touched. If successful, you sense the item is magical; further, each SL also provides a specific special rule the item uses, if it has any. Normally, you may only attempt this [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] once per artefact touched.

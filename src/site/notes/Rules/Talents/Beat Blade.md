**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] for a *Beat Blade*

You are trained to make sharp controlled blows to your opponent’s weapon, creating an opening for an attack or simply impeding an incoming attack. For your Action, you can choose to Beat Blade before rolling. Perform a [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]; if successful, your opponent loses -1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], and loses a further -1 per SL you score. This Test is not [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]]. This Talent is of no use if your opponent has no weapon, or has a larger [[Rules/Tables/Size Difficulty Table\|Size]] than you.

**Max:** 1

You are blessed by one of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]] and can empower one of your [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|Cult]]’s [[Miracle\|Miracle]]s. Further, you may purchas extra miracles for 100 XP per miracle you currently know. So, if you already know 3 miracles, your next miracle costs 300 XP ro purchase. Full rules for learning new miracles are provided in [[World/Religion and Belief/Religion and Belief\|Religion and Belief]]. Under normal circumstances, you may not learn more than one *Invoke ([[Divine Lore\|Divine Lore]])* [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]. Further, you may not learn the [[Rules/Talents/Petty Magic\|Petty Magic]] or [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talents]] when you have the *Invloke* [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]. YOu can unlearn this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] for 100 XP, but will loas all of your miracles if you do so, and will also garner the extreme disfavour of your [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|God]], with effects determined by your GM.

**Tests:** Any [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] concerning river matters

You know all the tricks for navigating dangerous rivers. You don’t need to [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] for passing through dangerous stretches of water until the [[Rules/Tests/Difficulty\|Difficulty]] for doing so is -10 or lower – you automatically pass all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] easier than this. Further, if you have the appropriate [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] ([[Rules/Keywords/Local\|Local]]) [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]], you need never [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] for navigating dangerous waters – you are assumed to know the route through.

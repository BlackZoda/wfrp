Home: [[Rules/Talents/Talent List\|Talent List]]
**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus
**Tests:** Any for resisting the [[Ruinous Powers\|Ruinous Powers]]

You have dedicated your pain to the service of your [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|God]]. Each day, you must spend half a bell (half an hour) praying as you maintain a number of [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] suffered equal to your level in [[Rules/Career/Flagellant\|Flagellant]]. Until you next sleep, if you have the [[Rules/Talents/Frenzy (Talent)\|Frenzy (Talent)]] you may enter [[Rules/Psychology/Frenzy\|Frenzy]] immediately without testing.

The [[Rules/Talents/Frenzy (Talent)\|Frenzy (Talent)]] is added to the [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] list of any career you are in. Should you fail to flagellate yourself on any given day, or allow your castigated flesh to be healed, you may not spend any [[Rules/Attributes/Resilience\|Resilience]] or [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]] until you flagellate yourself again.

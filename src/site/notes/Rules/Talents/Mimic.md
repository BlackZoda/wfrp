**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Entertain\|Entertain]] ([[Acting\|Acting]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] where accents are important

You have a good ear for accents and dialects, and can reproduce them accurately. You may replicate an accent you are exposed to for at least a day with an [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]; this [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] may be attempted once per day. Once passed, you may always mimic the accent, and locals will believe you to be oneof their own.

* [[Rules/Talents/Talent List\|Talent List]]

Talents describe special abilities your [[Character\|Character]] can access. The Talents you have available to you at character generation are determined by your [[Rules/Species/Species\|Species]] and [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]].

Where [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]] represent your raw potential, and [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skills]] are your training and experience, **Talents** represent all the *tricks*, *quirks*, and '*special abilities*' you have learned throughout life. 

If you learn a Talent, record it in your Talent list on your [[Templates/Character Sheet\|Character Sheet]].

Some Talents can be learned multiple times. If you have an opportunity to learn a Talent a second time, check the Talent entery to see if it can be learned multiple times, and mark how many times you’ve learned it on your Character Sheet.

If you can take a Talent multiple times, it will either have a special rule for what this means written into its description, or it will have an associated Skill marked under ‘Tests’ (see Talent Format). For each time you have the Talent, you gain +1 SL on any successful use of a Skill tied to the Talent.

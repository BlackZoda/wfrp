You are hard to keep down. You may perform a **Challenging (+0 [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]])** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to immediately regain your feed whenever you gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. This [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is often modified by the [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] behind the blow that knocks you down: for every +10 [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] the blow has over your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]], you suffer a -10 to the [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], and *vica versa*.

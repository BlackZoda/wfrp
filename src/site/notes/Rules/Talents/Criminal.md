You are an active criminal making money from ilegal sources, and you’re not always quiet about it. For the purposes of securing money, either when Earning during play or performing an [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavour]], refer to the following table:

* [[Rules/Talents/Criminal Talent Income Table\|Criminal Talent Income Table]]

Because of your obvious criminal nature, others consider you lower [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] than them unless they also have the [[Rules/Talents/Criminal\|Criminal]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], where [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] is compared as normal – perhaps you have gang tatoos, look shifty, or are just rough around the edges, it’s your choice. Because of this, local law enforcers are always suspicious of you and suspect your motivations, which only gets worse the more times you have this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], with the exact implications determined by the GM. Lawbreakers without the [[Rules/Talents/Criminal\|Criminal]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] earn significantly less coin but are not obviously the sort to be breaking the law, so maintain their [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]]. With GM consent, you may spend XP to remove levels of the [[Rules/Talents/Criminal\|Criminal]]/[[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] for the same XP it cost to buy.

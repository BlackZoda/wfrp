**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus
**Tests:** Social [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to influence a superior

You know what to say and when to make the most impact upon your superiors. When you successfully use a social [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] to influence those with a higher [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] tier, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die. So, a successful roll of 46 could be used for +6 SL.

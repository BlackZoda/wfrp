**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]] in enclosed environments

You have learned to make the most benefit out of fighting in enclosed spaces. You ignore penalties to [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] caused by confined spaces such as tunnels, the frontline, small fighting pits, and similar and can use the [[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]], even if it would normally be disallowed due to lack of space.

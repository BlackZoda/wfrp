**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] ([[Embezzilng\|Embezzilng]])

You are skilled at skimming money from your employers without being detected. Whenever you secure money when [[Earning\|Earning]] (during play or performing an [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavour]]), you may attempt on **[[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] Intelligence** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] with your employer (assuming you have one). If you win, you skim 2d10 + SL brass pennies, 1d10 + SL silver shillings, or 1 + SL gold crowns (depending upon the size of the business in question, as determined by the GM) without being detected. If your employer wins by 6+ SL, you gain the money, but your embezzling is detected; what then happens is left to the GM. Any other result means you have failed to embezzle any money.

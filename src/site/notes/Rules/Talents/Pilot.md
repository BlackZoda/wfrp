**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Row\|Row]] or [[Rules/Skills/Sail\|Sail]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] while navigating unsure waters

You are skilled at leading vessels through dangerous waters. If you fail a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to pass through dangerous waters, you may reverse the result if it will score a success; however, if you do so, you may not score more than +1 SL as you catch the incoming danger at the last moment.

**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Evaluate\|Evaluate]] to establish the worth of coins

You are well versed with the different coinage of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], and are adept at determining their value. You can judge the true value of a coin by experience alone, not even requiring a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. Further, you can identify forged coins with a [[Rules/Tests/Simple Test\|Simple Evaluate Test]]; it is never [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] by the SL of the Forger.

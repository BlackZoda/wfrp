**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus
**Tests:** All those to resist the associated Threat

Your strong constitution allows you to more readily survive a specific threat. You may automatically pass the first [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to resist the specified threat, such as [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]], [[Poison\|Poison]], [[Disease\|Disease]], [[Rules/Mutations/Mutation\|Mutation]], every session. If SL is important, use your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus as SL for the [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

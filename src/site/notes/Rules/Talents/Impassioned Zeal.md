When you talk about your cause, case, or religion, your words fill with passion and fervent zeal. You may double your [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] for the purposes of determining the number of people influenced by your [[Rules/Talents/Public Speaker\|Public Speaking]] when talking about your cause.

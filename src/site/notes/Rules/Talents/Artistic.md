Home: [[Rules/Talents/Talent List\|Talent List]]
**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] ([[Rules/Career/Artist\|Artist]])

You have a natural talen for art, able to produce precise sketches with nothing but time and appropriate media. This ability has several in-game uses, ranging from creating [[Wanted Poster\|Wanted Poster]]s to sketching accurate [[Journal\|Journal]]s, and has spot benefits determined by the GM. Futher to this, add [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] ([[Rules/Career/Artist\|Artist]]) to any [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] you enter; if it is already in-Career, you may instead purchase the [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] for 5 XP fewer per Advance.

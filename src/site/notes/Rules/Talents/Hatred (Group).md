You are consumed with hatred for something in [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]], as described under [[Rules/Psychology/Hatred\|Hatred]]. Each time you take this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] you develop hatred for a new group. Eamples you could take include: [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastmen]], [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]], [[Monster\|Monster]]s, [[Rules/Career/Outlaw\|Outlaws]], [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Sigmar\|Sigmarites]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]], and [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witches]].

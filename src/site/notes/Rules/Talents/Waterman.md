**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Sail\|Sail]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] for river-going vessels

You are an experienced freshwater sailor and are well-versed with river vessels. You can ignore all negatives to your [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] when onboard a [[Barge\|Barge]] derived from rolling waters, swaying vessels, unsure footing, and similar. Further, you count as two boatmen towards the minimum number of crew to pilot a river vessel.

Home: [[Rules/Talents/Talent List\|Talent List]]
**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus
**Tests:** Cool to oppose your Enemy’s [[Rules/Skills/Intimidate\|Intimidate]], [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]], and [[Rules/Psychology/Terror\|Terror]]

You are either brave enough or crazy enough that fear of certain enemies has become a distant memory. With a single **Average (+20%) [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** Test, you may ignore any [[Rules/Skills/Intimidate\|Intimidate]], [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]], or [[Rules/Psychology/Terror\|Terror]] effects from the specified enemy when encountered. Typical enemies include [[World/Races/Beastman\|Beastmen]], [[World/Races/Greenskin\|Greenskins]], [[Rules/Career/Outlaw\|Outlaws]], [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Vampire\|Vampires]], [[Rules/Career/Watchman\|Watchmen]], and [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witches]].

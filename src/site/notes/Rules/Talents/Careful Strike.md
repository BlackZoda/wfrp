Home: [[Rules/Talents/Talent List\|Talent List]]
**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus

You are skilled at hitting your enemy exactly where you want to, either at range or in melee. You may modify your [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location]] result by up to +/- 10 per time you have this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]. So, if you had this Talent twice and hit location 34, the [[Right Arm\|Right Arm]], you could modify this down to 14, the [[Left Arm\|Left Arm]], or up to 54, the [[Body\|Body]].

**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to detect [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizards]]

You are able to sense the [[Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] in others. You may attempt an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] whenever you encounter a spellcaster. If you pass, you sense the target is a witch. Further, if you score an [[Astounding Success\|Astounding Success]] (+6), can also determine the target’s highest [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] [[Rules/Skills/Grouped Skills and Specialisation\|Specialisation]].

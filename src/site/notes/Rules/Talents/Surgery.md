**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] outside combat rounds; i.e. when you have time to do it “properly”

You are a surgeon, able to open and close the flesh in order to heal others. You can treat any [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]] marked as needing [[Rules/Talents/Surgery\|Surgery]]. You can also perform surgery to resolve internal issues with an **[[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Challenging (+0) Heal]]** Test with a target SL determined by the GM (usually 5–10) depending upon the difficuty of the procedure at hand. This will cause 1d10 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Bleeding\|Bleeding]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] per [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], meaning surgery has a high chance of killing a patient if the surgeon is not careful. After surgery, the patient must pass an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or gain a [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Minor Infection\|Minor Infection]].

**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Research\|Research]] and [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] whre speed of reading is important

You read books at voracious pace. You may reverse a failed [[Rules/Skills/Research\|Research]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] (make singular if previous suggestion is taken) if this will grant success. If the speed at which you read is important during combat, a successful [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] lets you read and fully comprehend a number of pages per [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] equal to your SL plus *Speedreader* level (minimum of 1, even if you fail the [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]).

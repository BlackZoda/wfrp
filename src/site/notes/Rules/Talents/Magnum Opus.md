You are an undisputed mater in your field, able to create work of such incredible complexity others can but sit back and marvel at your genius. Each time you take this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] you may create a single extraordinary work of art with one of your [[Rules/Skills/Art\|Art]] or [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skills]]. This work is unrivalled in your field, a unique piece that will always impress, giving bonuses as determined by the GM, most commonly to [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] from those who have witnessed your astounding work. Selling the piece will net you at least ten times its normal value, and sometimes significantly more than this.

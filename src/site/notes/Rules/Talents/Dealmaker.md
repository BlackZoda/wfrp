**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Haggle\|Haggle]]

You are a skilled businessman who knows how to close a deal. When using the [[Rules/Skills/Haggle\|Haggle]] skill, you reduce or increase the price of the products by an extra 10%.

**Note:** The GM may put a lower limit on price here to show a seller refusing to sell below cost.

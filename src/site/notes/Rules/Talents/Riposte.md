**Max:** [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] Bonus
**Tests:** [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] when defending

Conforming to “the best defence is offence”, ou respond to an incoming attack with a lightning-fast counterstrike of your own. If your weapon has the [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Quaity/Fast\|Fast]] [[Quality\|Quality]], you may cause [[Rules/Combat/Apply Damage\|Damage]] when you are attacked, just as if it was your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]]. You can *Riposte* a number of attacks per round equal to your *Riposte* level.

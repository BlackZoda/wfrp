You are nimble and balanced like a cat, and are ableto fall much greater distances unharmed than others might. When you fall, you attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] Test. If successfull, reduce the distance fallen by 1 yard +1 extra yard per 1 SL scored, for the puposes of calculating [[Rules/Combat/Falling\|Falling]] Damage.

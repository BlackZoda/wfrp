Your fast reactions have allowed you to fell many opponents before they have even swing their blades. When you are [[Rules/Combat/Charging\|Charged]], you may attempt a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]]** Test to gain an immediate [[Free Attack\|Free Attack]] outside the normal turn sequence. This attack is resolved with whatever weapon you are carrying in your primary hand. You may make as many *Reaction Strikes* in a [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] as you have levels in this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], but can only attack each individual charger once each.

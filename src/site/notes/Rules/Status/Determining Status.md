Home: [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]]

Your status is determined by your [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]]. Beside the name of your [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]] is your [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]], marked as “Brass 3”, “Silver 1”, or similar. This is the [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]] and [[Standing\|Standing]]. Should you change [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]], check the new [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] by the name of your new Career Level, and mark it on your [[Templates/Character Sheet\|Character Sheet]].

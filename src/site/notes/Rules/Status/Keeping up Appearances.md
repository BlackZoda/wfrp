Home: [[Rules/Status/Maintaining Status\|Maintaining Status]]

Characters need to spend a certain amount of money to maintain their lifestyle. As explained in [[Rules/Economy/The Cost of Living\|The Cost of Living]] (WFRP page 289) there are minimum expectations of expenditure for each [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]].

Should a character fail to live up to these levels of expenditure they will begin to be perceived as being of lower [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]], lsing 1 point of [[Standing\|Standing]] per week.

To stop this decline, the character must resume spending the money expected of them, whereupon they regain 1 point of [[Standing\|Standing]] a week until they recover the lost points.

Should [[Standing\|Standing]] reach 0, your [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]] drops by one and your standing is set to 5. if you are alread at the lowest [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]], then your [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] sticks at [[Brass\|Brass]] 0.

**Example:** *Pietr is a Noble and has been slumming it in the East End of Altdorf for the last two weeks, letting his Standing of Gold 3 drop to Gold 1 as he eats poor food, drinks cheap beer, and hostels in flop houses. Should another week pass and Pietr does not change his ways, his Standing will drop to Gold 0, which immediately becomes Silver 5. Should this occur, Pietr’s father will almost certainly step in to curtail the family shame…*

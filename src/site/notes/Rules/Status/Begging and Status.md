Home: [[Rules/Status/Effects of Status\|Effects of Status]]

Begging is often most effective when aimed at those who are just above you in the social order.

For example: Most Beggars are [[Rules/Status/Brass Tier\|Brass Tier]] and therefore benefit from a +10 bonus when begging from people who are [[Rules/Status/Silver Tier\|Silver Tier]], instead of suffering a –10 penalty.

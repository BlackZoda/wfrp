Home: [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]]

Reserved for the rulers of society; those who directly serve them as [[Rules/Career/Advisor\|Advisor]]s, protectors, and maintainers of the law; and the independently wealthy. To reach this position requires hard work, success, and not a little bit of luck. All the Gold tier are rich, respected, and of higher [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] than those in the [[Rules/Status/Silver Tier\|Silver Tier]] and [[Rules/Status/Brass Tier\|Brass Tier]].

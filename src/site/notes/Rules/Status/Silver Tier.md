Home: [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]]

Populated by well-to-do townsfolk, and those following prefessions requiring skill and expertise. Tradesmen, craftsmen, and merchants make up most of the upper ranks of the tier, whilst the bottom is filled by those providing services. These folk lead relatively humble lives, but they are still respectable. People of the Silver Tier are of higher [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] than those of the [[Rules/Status/Brass Tier\|Brass Tier]].

Home: [[Rules/Status/Effects of Status\|Effects of Status]]

[[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] plays a very important role in [[Rules/Skills/Leadership\|Leadership]]. Indeed, the [[State Army\|State Army]] normally only grants higher ranks to those of noble birth.

If you are a higher [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]] than the target of your [[Rules/Skills/Leadership\|Leadership]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], you gain a bonus of +10. If you are two tiers higher, the bonus increses to +20.

Home: [[Rules/Status/Effects of Status\|Effects of Status]]

Status impacts [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] significantly, with those of higher [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] more easily able to get what they want, and those of lower status finding it harder to secure influence. In most circumstances, those of a higher [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]] gain a bonus of +10 to Charm [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]s influencing those with a lower tier. Similarly, those of lower [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]] suffer a penalty of -10 to influence those of a higher [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]]. The GM may also apply these modifiers to those of different [[Standing\|Standing]] in the same [[Rules/Status/Tier\|Tier]], but this is rarer.

If the target of the [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] attempt is atypical, this might not work as usual however. An individual who “Defies Status” would mean no modifiers apply, and one with “Extreme Views” might mean that the opposite modifiers apply (-10 rather than +10, for exampe).

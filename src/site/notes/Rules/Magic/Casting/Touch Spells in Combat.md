Certain spells require you to touch the target. If in combat, or if the target is unwilling to be touched, you must make an **Opposed [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] (Brawling)** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] (generally opposed by the [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] or [[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]) after completing the [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Test]]. If you do this and your spell was a *magic missile*, the [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] ([[Brawling\|Brawling]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is used to determine [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location]] struck, not the [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

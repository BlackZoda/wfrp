Home: [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]]

In areas where the influence of the Chaos gods is strong- such as in their temples, where their Daemons are manifest, where their spells are in effect, or where rituals in their honour are performed - the Ruinous Powers’ influence may be made manifest. Whenever a Test performed in such areas rolls the sacred number of the god at hand, the tide of corruption rises and the Chaos god draws close.

* [[Rules/Talents/Suffused with Magic\|Suffused with Magic]]

If you roll a 9 on any Casting or Channelling Test taken in an area influenced by [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] (such as 09, 19, 29, 90, 92, and so on), you suffer the following: 1 [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]], and gain the [[Rules/Talents/Suffused with Magic\|Suffused with Magic]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] until the start of your next Turn. If you roll 99, you suffer a [[Rules/Tables/Major Miscast Table\|Major Miscast]], and a [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Pink Horror\|Pink Horror]] of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] manifests within 1d10 yards of your position (see page 85 EiSC).

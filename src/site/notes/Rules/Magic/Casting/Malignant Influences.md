Casting close to a sources of [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] makes controlling the [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] much more difficult. When attempting a [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) or [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] in the vicinity of a [[Rules/Corruption/Corrupting Influences\|Corrupting Influence]] any roll of an 8 (representing the eight-pointed symbol of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]]) on the units die result in a [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]] as the [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]] goes wild. If you already have [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]] on the [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] for another reason, the [[Miscast\|Miscast]] escalates to a [[Rules/Tables/Major Miscast Table\|Major Miscast Table]].

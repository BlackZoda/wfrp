Some spellcasters own a spellbook, or a grimoire, in which they transcribe their spells. [[Rules/Career/Wizard’s Apprentice (level 1)\|Apprentices]] copy spells from their master, while experienced [[Floating/Magister\|Magisters]] will actively seek out new spells from other [[Floating/Magister\|Magisters]], often trading spells for favours. A spellcaster may cast a spell directly from a grimoire if the spell belongs to a [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] they possess. Doing so doubles the [[Casting Number\|Casting Number]].

If you start play with a Grimoire, it is up to the GM to determine how many spells your master has recorded within it. Some Masters are more generous than others, but most provide at least four new spells to study, and few provide more than eight. If you have a spell Memorised, you do not need access to your Grimoire to cast it. If you have not yet memorised a spell, you may use your Grimoire to cast it using both your hands. This doubles the CN of the spell and exposes your Grimoire to possible damage or theft

Spellcasters may channel their magic through an appropriate ingredient before unleashing their spells. Doing this offers protection agains [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Critical Casting\|Miscasts]] as their attuned ingredient absorbs the worst of any magical backlash.

If you use an ingredient when casting, any suffered [[Rules/Tables/Major Miscast Table\|Major Miscast]] become a [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]], and any [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]] has no effect. If used in this way the ingredient is consumed or destroyed in the process, even if no Miscast was rolled.

Ingredients cost the CN in silver shillings for [[Arcane Lore\|Arcane]] and [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] spells. Whenever you purchase a new ingredient, mark on your character sheet which spell it supports; ingredients only work for specific [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spells\|Spells]], not for all spells in your [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]]. For those looking to add character to their ingredients, sample ingredients for each of the primary [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lores of Magic]] are provided by the individual spell lists.

[[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] in combat applies to [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Tests]], not [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. When casting magic, you can also gain [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] in the following way: If the target has already had a spell from the same [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lores of Magic]] cast upon it iin that [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], gain +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] as you cast, as the upswelling of your [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Wind]] aids channeling magic.

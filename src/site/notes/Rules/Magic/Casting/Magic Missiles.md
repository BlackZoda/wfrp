Some spells are marked as *magic missiles*; these are damage-causing spells that all follow the same rules. When a *magic missile* is successfully cast and targets another character, the [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location]] struck is determined by reversing the dice rolled on the [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] and referring to the [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location Table]]. The SL of the [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is added to the spell’s listed [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] and your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus to determine the total inflicted [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]]. This [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] is reduced by the target’s [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] and [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour\|Armour]] Points as normal.

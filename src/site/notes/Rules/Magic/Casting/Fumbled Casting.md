Home: [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Test]], [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumbles]]

If you lose control of the magical energy you are channelling, things invariably go awry. If you [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]] your [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Test]], you suffer a *Miscast*. Roll 1d100 and consolt the [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast Table]].

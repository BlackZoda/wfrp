Home: [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]]

When casting spells with Dhar, [[Rules/Magic/Other Rules/Overcasting\|Overcasting]] happen for every +1 SL scored above the [[Casting Number\|Casting Number]] of the Spell, not every +2 SL. However, rolling a double on any associated [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] or [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] also counts as a [[Rules/Corruption/Minor Corrupting Exposure\|Minor Corrupting Exposure]] as the foul energies course through the spellcaster’s body.

Further, any [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] or [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]] results containing an 8 (such as 08, 18, 81, or 82) and therefore symbolising the eight-pointed star of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] are especially bad. This causes the roiling [[World/Magic/Dark Magic\|Dark Magic]] to move massively beyond the spellcaster’s control. All living souls within [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards suffer a [[Rules/Corruption/Minor Corrupting Exposure\|Minor Corrupting Exposure]] and a [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]]; a single result applies to all those within the area affected. Should an 88 be rolled, the effect increases to a [[Rules/Corruption/Major Corrupting Exposure\|Major Corrupting Exposure]] and a [[Rules/Tables/Major Miscast Table\|Major Miscast]].

To cast a spell, make a **[[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]])** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you succeed, match your SL to the *Casting Number (CN)* of the spell (listed in the individual spell description). If your SL is equal to or higher than the CN of the spell, it is cast as explained in the spell’s description. If failed, the spell attempt fails, and nothing else happens.

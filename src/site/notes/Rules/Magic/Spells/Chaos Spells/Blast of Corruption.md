**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] bonus yards)
**Duration:** Instant

You channel profane magic into an explosive blast of corruption. This is a magic missile ith [[Rules/Combat/Apply Damage\|Damage]] +5 that targets everyone in the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]]. Everyone affected by the spell must pass a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** Test, or gain 1 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]].

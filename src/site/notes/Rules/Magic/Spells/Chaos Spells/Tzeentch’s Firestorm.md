**CN:** 11
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

A sirling storm of purple arcane fire bursts forth, summoned by your profane cackling. *Tzeentch’s Firestorm* is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]]. Those stuck suffer a +9 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] hit, and gains +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

Should a [[0_GM/Fleeting/Character Creation\|Character]] of size small or larger be reduced to 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] by this [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]], or while suffering from an [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] caused by *Tzeentch’s Firestorm*, roll a d10. On a roll of 9, to [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Pink Horror\|Pink Horror]]s (see page 85 EiSC) claw their way out of the target’s magically burning corpse, killing the poor unfortunate.

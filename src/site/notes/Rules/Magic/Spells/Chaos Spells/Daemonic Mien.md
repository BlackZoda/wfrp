Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Chaos Arcane Spells\|Chaos Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You channel the transformative power of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] into your own flesh, temporarily sacrificing your mortal form on the alter of your god’s will. Your body is warped, assuming aspects of a [[Daemon\|Daemon]] loyal to your patron god. Roll d10 on the table on page 78 (EiSC), and apply the relevant [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]] for the duration of the spell. For every +2 SL, you may both extend the duration and roll again on the table. Should you roll the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Daemonic\|Daemonic]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Trait]] and be reduced to 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], your soul is sucked into [[The Realms of Chaos\|The Realms of Chaos]] as described in the Creature Traits rules.

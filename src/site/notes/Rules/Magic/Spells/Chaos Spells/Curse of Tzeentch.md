Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]]

**CN:** 9
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Varies

[[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Tzeentchian Lore]] has it that all spells ultimately derive from the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Chaos Gods\|Chaos God]]’s will, and so belong to him. Using this spell, a [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] [[Sorcerer\|Sorcerer]] can cause another wizard to lose the knowledge and ability to cast a spell they previously had mastery of.

Should the curse of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] be successfully cast, the caster must then make an opposed [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Test against the target. If the caster wins, the target loses access to a randomly selected spell. The effects of the spell lasts for one day for every level of success scored by the caster during the opposed test.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]]

**CN:** 10
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

At your command, bonds aethyric fire coil from the earth, enshrouding your target in a cocoon of magical energy. Targets affected gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], and are considered [[Rules/Combat/Helpless Target\|Helpless]] for the duration of the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]]. When *Transformation of Tzeentch* ends, the target must make a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]], opposed by your [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]. If they lose, they gain +1 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption Point]], +1 extra Point for every SL by which they lost.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus Days

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] sorcerers often bargain with their master to provide them ith insights about their potential fate. Scrying in this manner is dangerous, for [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] is a capricious god, even in the case of his own favoured servants. Once the spell is cast it may not be cast again until its duration has run out.

Determin the result of the [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]] needed to cast the spell. For every positive SL the caster benefits from a point of [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] which they can use as normal for the duration of the spell. However, if the spell fails then for every negative level of success the caster receives a point of [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] as they receive a dizzying array of disturbing future echoes.

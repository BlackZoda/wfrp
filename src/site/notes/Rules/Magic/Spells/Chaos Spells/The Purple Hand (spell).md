**CN:** 3
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Varies

This spell is a petty curse used by [[Campaign/Organizations/Den Lilla Hånden\|Purple Hand]] sorcerers to intimidate and cajole truculent co-conspirators. Of all the non-verbal codes used by [[Campaign/Organizations/Den Lilla Hånden\|The Purple Hand]] the meaning of this curse is clear: mind your ways immediately or face punishment.

In order to cast the spell the sorcerer must first acquire a small amount of matter from their target: a loose hair or toenail clipping will serve. Should this item be acquired and the spell successfuly cast, the palms of the target’s hands turn a deep purple. The spell’s effect last for one hour for every level of success scored by the caster while casting the spell.

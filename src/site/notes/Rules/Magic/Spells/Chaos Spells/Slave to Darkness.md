Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Chaos Arcane Spells\|Chaos Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Special
You implore your patron to take the soul of your target, replacing it with something terrible. Mage an **Opposed Willpower** Test, wich you must win by at least 2+ SL. Should you do this, the victim’s soul is sent howling into [[World/Religion and Belief/Realms of Chaos/The Realms of Chaos\|The Realms of Chaos]], and the remaining body is possessed by a [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]]. Unless the target spends a [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] point, contro of the Character is passed to the GM. Should you not win the **Opposed** Test by at least 2+ SL, you instead suffer a [[Rules/Tables/Major Miscast Table\|Major Miscast]], and the spell fails. Should you [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]] the test, you are punished by your god for your arrogance. Your soul is tornfrom your body, never to return unless you spend a [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] point to resist this, although your patron is unikely to be pleased if you flagrantly reject their authority in such a fashion.

Given the risk and limitations inherent to this spell, it is generally cast on victims that have been ‘prepared’ for their sacrifice: they have been bound, drugget, and/or otherwise pacified.

**CN:** 7
**Range:** [[Strenght\|Strenght]] Bonus yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

As your tongue forms the twisted sound-patterns of this blasphemous [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]], two voices seem to utter from your throat, in unholy and unnatural harmony with each other. Your words carry extra meaning for those affected by the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]], thoug targets can never remember what was said. They are left with a haunting sense of foreboding, as if they have forgotten something very important about their own future.

Living targets affected by the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]] must make an **Opposed [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]] with you. If the target loses, they gain 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], +1 additional [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] for each SL by which they lost. If they [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]], they gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and +1 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption Point]].

Once all [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] have been removed, targets affected must pass an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]], or gain +1 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]]. Shuld they [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]], they immediately gain 1 [[Rules/Tables/Mental Corruption Table\|Mental Mutation]], and may not take a Short-term Ambition for the next 1d10 weeks.

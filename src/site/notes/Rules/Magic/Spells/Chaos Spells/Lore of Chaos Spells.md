Characters with a [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] may select any combination of [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]], and spells from their own [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Lore]]. A character with [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]] ([[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]]) can select spells from the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]] List , the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Chaos Arcane Spells\|Chaos Arcane Spells]] list and the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]] list. A character ith the [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]] (Undivided) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] can select spells from the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]] and [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Chaos Arcane Spells\|Chaos Arcane Spells]] lists.

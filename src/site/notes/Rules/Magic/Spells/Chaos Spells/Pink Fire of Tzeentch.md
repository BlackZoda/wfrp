Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

Swirling bolts of pink fire arc from your outstretched fingertips, causing reality to scream in anguish. *Pink Fire of Tzeentch* is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]]. Targets affected suffer a +6 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] hit, and gain +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

Should a [[0_GM/Fleeting/Character Creation\|Character]] of size small or larger be reduced to 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] by this spell, r while suffering from an [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] caused by *Pink Fire of Tzeentch*, roll a d10. On a roll of 9, a [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Pink Horror\|Pink Horror]] (see page 85 EiSC) claws its way out of the target’s rithing corpse, killing the individual.

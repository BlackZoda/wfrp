Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]]

**CN:** 9
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

Your words, imbued with the labyrinthine prescience of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|The Changer of Ways]], tist your mind, granting you access to new arcane knowledge. Choose one [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]] from any [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lore]] (to ensure swift play, the GM may impose a nine second time limit). While *Boon of Tzeentch* is in effect, you may cast your new [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]] as if you had memorised it, but may not record it in a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Grimoire\|Grimoire]], [[Scroll\|Scroll]], or similar. If you channel to cast the new [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]], you may use either [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] ([[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]]) or the [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] appropriate to the [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lore]] the spell normally requires, should you possess it.

If you suffer a miscast when channeling or casting this new [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]], the *Boon of Tzeentch* [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]] quits your mind in disgust, and also erases itself from your grimoire should it have allowed itself to be recorded there.

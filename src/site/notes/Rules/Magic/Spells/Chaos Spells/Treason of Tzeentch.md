You [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] fine strands of traitorous mergings of the [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] directly into your target’s mind, twisting motivations, undermining desires, and fanning fires of discontent. The target can no longer use [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talents]], and doesn’t add [[0_GM/Fleeting/Skill Advances\|Skill Advances]] when making [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], meaning the target only [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] against unmodified [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]].

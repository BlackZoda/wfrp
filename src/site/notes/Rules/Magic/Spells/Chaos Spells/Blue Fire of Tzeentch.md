Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]]
**Duration:** Instant

Your words conjure a rithling ball of coruscating blue fire that engulf your foes. *Blue Fire of Tzeentch* is a *magic missile*. Anyone within your [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus yards of the target suffers a +3 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] hit, and gains +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

Should  a [[Character\|Character]] of size small or larger be reduced to 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] by this spell, or hile suffering from an [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] from *Blue Fire of Tzeentch*, roll 1d10. On a roll of 9, two [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Blue Horror\|Blue Horror]]s (see page EiSC) claw their way out of the [[Character\|Character]]’s screaming flesh, killing them in the process.

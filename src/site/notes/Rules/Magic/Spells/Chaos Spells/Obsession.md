Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Chaos Arcane Spells\|Chaos Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus miles
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** SL days

You must possess a beloved or precious object belonging to the target. You eave magic infused with the raw stuff of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]] into the object that, in turn, affects the target. They become obsessed with something: the precise nature of the obsession depends on the individual in question, and your [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Lore]]. For instance, when cast with the [[Lore of Nurgle\|Lore of Nurgle]], *Obsession* may cause a recently wounded target to become obsessed with picking at their scabs. The [[Lore of Slaanesh\|Lore of Slaanesh]] may cause an epicure or gourmand to overeat.

The target must pass an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** Test every hour, or succumb to their obsession. On a failure, future tests become **Challenging (+0)** Subsequent failings make Tests increasingly harder, until they become **Very Hard (-30)**. Should the target ever [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]] one of these [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] (fail hile rolling a double), the target becomes completely obsessed. They can do nothing exceppt indulge the obsession,no matter the costs or implications, for d10-[[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] bonus hours (minimum 1 hour). At the end of the spell’s duration, i addition to any other physical, social, or financial implications, the target must pass a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** Test or gain +1 [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]].

A target may only ever be affected once by this spell once for each Lore with thich it is cast. Any future attempts on the same target with this spell have no effect.

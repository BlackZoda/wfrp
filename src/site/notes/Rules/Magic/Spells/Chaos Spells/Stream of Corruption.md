Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Nurgle Spells\|Lore of Nurgle Spells]]

**CN:** 9
**Range:** Special
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Instant

Your maw distends horrifically before you vomit forth a foul stream of diseased filth and horror. Immediately make a [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Breath (trait)\|Breath (trait)]] attack as if you had spent 2 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]s to activate the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Breath (trait)\|Breath (trait)]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]]. It counts as a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus, and it ignores [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]]. The attack also has the [[Corruption (trait)\|Corruption (trait)]] and [[Poison (trait)\|Poison (trait)]] listed under the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Breath (trait)\|Breath (trait)]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]]. further, should any targets tae more than their [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus in [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], the most pass a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or contract [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Blood Rot\|Blood Rot]]. For every +2 SL achieved, you may also increase [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] by +2.

The Chaos Arcane Spells are available to all Chaos Sorcerers. How these spells manifest ill vary based on the specific [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Lore]] knon. For instance, if a spellcaster has [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]] ([[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Nurgle\|Nurgle]]) and casts [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Joyous Aspect\|Joyous Aspect]], the target blooms ith an unnaturally healthy life, whereas if the spellcaster as [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]] ([[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Slaanesh\|Slaanesh]]) instead, the target would appear so attractive that no one would notice any flaws.

**Note:** Any spell marged ith a ‘+’ at the end of the [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Duration\|Duration]] gains the following extra effect: When the spell should end, you may make a [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Test to extend the Duration for +1 [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]].

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] is the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Master of Magic, Treachery, and Lies]]. The [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch]] epitomises this, as it boast a multitude of powerful spells focusing upon change, all drawing heaviy from the [[Grey Wind\|Grey]], [[Amethyst Wind\|Amethyst]], and [[Bright Wind\|Bright Wind]]s.

[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] is the Changer of ays, the Great Architect and the Spinner of Fates. He standsfor command over fate and destiny, and the capacity to alter preordained paths. He is the patron of many unitting revolutionaries and agitators, secretly worshipped wherever souls crave change to the status quo, or where ideas and intellects gather to ponder the nature of existence. As the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|God]] of [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] favours wiards and sorcerers, especially those frustrated by the limitations place upon them by mortal authorities.

## Lore Attribute
Targets of [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spells\|Spells]] from the Lore of Tzeentch are riven by transformative magic of [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]]. Targets affected by a [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]] from the Lore of Tzeentch must pass a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** Test, or gain +1 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] Point. Should they pass their [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], they instead gain +1 [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune Point]]

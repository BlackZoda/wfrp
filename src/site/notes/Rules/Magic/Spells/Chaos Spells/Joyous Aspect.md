Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Chaos Arcane Spells\|Chaos Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 4
**Range:** Touch 
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] Bonus Rounds

The caster infuses the target (perhaps themselves) with a corrupted caul of [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]]-infused [[Ulgu\|Ulgu]], sheathing them in an aura of concealment. While the spell is in force, the target appears without fla, or blemish Scars, deformities and, most crucially, mutations are undetectable, save through [[Divine\|Divine]] means. While obvious deformities are hidden, the target can appear strange, or outlandish, infused with too much life, or unnaturally alluring. Passing a **Hard (-20) [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]]** Test reveals something is awry, though not precisely hat. For characters with the [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight\|Second Sight]] the Test is **Challenging (+0)**, and should they succeed they will become aware that a spell is in place, but not what it hides.

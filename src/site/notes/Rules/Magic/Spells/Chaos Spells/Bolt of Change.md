Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]]

**CN:** 9
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

A pulsating bolt of scintillating [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaotic]] energy rends the air. *Bolt of Change* is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]], and targets suffer a +7 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] hit. Further, targets must make a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** Test. A target that fails gains +1 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption Point]]. On a [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumble]], the target immediately rolls for a [[Rules/Mutations/Mutation\|Mutation]], and gains the [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]] ([[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]]) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]. A [[0_GM/Fleeting/Character Creation\|Character]] affected by this spell may resist a [[Rules/Mutations/Mutation\|Mutation]] by spending a point of [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]].

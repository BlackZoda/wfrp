Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Lore of Tzeentch Spells\|Lore of Tzeentch Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

The air warps around your fingertips, forming a bolt of shimmering, hazy magical energy that can be hurled at your target. Anyone affected by *Mindfire* must make a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]], or gain 1 [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption Point]]. For every +2 SL, yo may make one target gain an additional [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption Point]] if the [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]] is failed. Should a [[Rules/Mutations/Mutation\|Mutation]] occur as a result of this [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell]], the target automatically rolls on the [[Rules/Tables/Mental Corruption Table\|Mental Mutation]] table, and gains +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] as unnatural fire spills from every orifice in their head.

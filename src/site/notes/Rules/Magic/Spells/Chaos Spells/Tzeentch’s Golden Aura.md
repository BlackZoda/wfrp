**CN:** 7
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

This is one of the most straigtforward and practical spells in a [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Tzeentch\|Tzeentch]] sorcerer’s arsenal. A swirling vortex of yellow fire is summoned out of the [[World/Magic/The Aethyr\|Aethyr]] and wraps about the caster’s body, shielding them from incoming blows and missiles. Whilst this spell is in effect, the caster benefits from the [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Trait]] [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Ward\|Ward]] 9+.

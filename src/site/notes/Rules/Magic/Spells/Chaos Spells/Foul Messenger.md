Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Chaos Spells/Chaos Arcane Spells\|Chaos Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] miles
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Instant

You conjure forth a swarm of [[Minor Daemon\|Minor Daemon]]s shacled to your will. The form of the daemons will vary depending on your deity The daemons will bear a short message (approximately 25 words) to your target. They travel almost instantaneously, and are invisible (and inaudible) to anyone without the [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight (Talent)\|Second Sight (Talent)]] even while delivering their message. Anyone with the [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] may make a **Hard (-20) [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]]** Test to notice the swarm, ahd hear their message. For every +2 SLs, you may double the length of the message.

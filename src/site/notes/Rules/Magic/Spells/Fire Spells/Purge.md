Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Fire Spells/Lore of Fire Spells\|Lore of Fire Spells]]

**CN:** 10
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yeards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You funnel intense flame to burn away the taint and corruption in an area. Anything flammable is set alight, and any creatures in the area takes +SL [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] conditions. If the location contains a [[Rules/Corruption/Corrupting Influences\|Corrupting Influence]], such as [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]], [[World/Magic/Warpstone\|Warpstone]], or a [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]]-tainted object, it too will smoulder and blacken, beginning to burn. This spell may be maintained in subsequent [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]] by passing a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. The precise time needed to eliminate the [[Rules/Corruption/Corrupting Influences\|Corrupting Influence]] will be determined by your GM. As a rough guideline, a small quantity (smaler than an acorn) of [[World/Magic/Warpstone\|Warpstone]], or a minor [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]]-tainted object may require 10-[[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus Rounds (mnimum of 1 [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]]). A larger quality of [[World/Magic/Warpstone\|Warpstone]] – fist-sized – or a more potent [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]]-tained object may require double this. A powerful [[Chaos Artefact\|Chaos Artefact]] may take hours, or even longer.

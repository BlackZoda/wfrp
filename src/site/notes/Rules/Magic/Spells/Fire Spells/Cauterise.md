Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Fire Spells/Lore of Fire Spells\|Lore of Fire Spells]]

**CN:** 4
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

Channelling [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]] through your hands you lay them on an ally’s [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]]. Immediately heal 1d10 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] and remove all [[Rules/Conditions/Bleeding\|Bleeding]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]]. Further, the wounds willl not become infected.

Targets without the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Fire) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], must bass a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or scream in agony. If failed by -6 or more SL, the target gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and is permanently scarred, waking up 1d10 hours later.

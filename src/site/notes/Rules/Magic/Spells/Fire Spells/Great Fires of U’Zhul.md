**CN:** 10
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You hurl a great, explosive blast of [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]] into an enemy, which erupts into a furious blaze, burning with the heat of a forge. This is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] with [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] +10 that ignores [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] and inflict +2 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] and the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] on a target. Everyone within the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]] of that target suffers a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] + 5 hit ignoring [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]], and must pass a [[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or also gain +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. The spell stops behaving like a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] as the fire continues to burn in the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]] for the duration. Anyone within the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]] at the start of a round suffers 1d10+6 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]], ignoring APs, and gains +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

**CN:** 5
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You wrap yourself in a firey cloak of [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]], which channels flame into the [[Aegis\|Aegis]]. You are completely immune to damage from non-magical fire, including the breath attacks of monsters, and ignore any [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] you receive. You receive the [[Ward (trait)\|Ward (trait)]] (9+) [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] against magical fire attacks including spells from [[World/Magic/The Lore of Fire\|The Lore of Fire]].

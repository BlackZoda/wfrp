The [[Lore of Fire\|Lore of Fire]], and the Bright wind of [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]], is anthing but subtle. Its spells are bellowed with fervour and manifest themselves in bombastic fashion, with bright flame and searing heat. You may inflict +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] on anyone targeted by spells from the [[Lore of Fire\|Lore of Fire]], unless they also possess the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Fire) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]. Every [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] condition within [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards adds +10 to attempts to [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] or [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Cast]] with [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]].

**Ingredients:** Pyromancers use a wide selection of flammable materials as ingredients, which are often immolated as the spell is cast, including coal, oils, fats, and ruddy woods. [[Rules/Trappings/Trappings\|Trappings]] immune to fire are also common, such as iron keys, carved sections of fire-grate, and small oven stones.

**CN:** 8
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]] into a majestic crown of inspiring fire about your brow. Gaine the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Feared\|Feared]] (1) and +1 [[Rules/Talents/War Leader\|War Leader]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] while the spell is active.

For every +2 SL, you may increase your [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] value by +1, or take [[Rules/Talents/War Leader\|War Leader]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] again. Furthermore, gain a bonus of +10 on all attempts to [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] and [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Cast]] with [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]] while the spell is in effect.

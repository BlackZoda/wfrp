**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] (Special)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] a fiery streak of [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]], creating a wall of flame. The Firewall is [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards wide, and 1 yard deep. For every +2 SL you may extend the length of the Firewall by +[[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards. Anyone crossing the firewall gains 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and suffers a hit with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus, handled like a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missiles]].

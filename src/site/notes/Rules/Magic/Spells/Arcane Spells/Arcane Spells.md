Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spell List]]

The Arcane spells represent common formulations of the [[Lingua Praestantia\|Lingua Praestantia]]. How these spells manifest in practice will depend on your [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]. For instance, a spelcaster with *Arcane Magic (Fire)* casting [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Drop\|Drop]] may cause the object to overheat, while one with *Arcane Magic (Shadow)* may make it slightly insubstantial, causing the object to literally slip between the target’s fingers.

Treat Arcane spells as extra options for every [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lore of Magic]], including [[World/Magic/Witchcraft\|Witchcraft]], [[World/Magic/Dark Magic\|Dark Magic]], and [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]]. They are counted as [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Lore Spells]] in all ways, meaning they get all the benfits of Lore spells, and can only be learned from and thought to those sharing the same [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]].

**Note:** Any spell marked with a ‘+’ at the end of the [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Duration\|Duration]] gains the following extra text: *When the spell should end, you may make a [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to extend the duration for +1 round.*

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 7
**Range:** You
**Target:** AoE ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You create a dome of magical energy overhead, blocking incoming attacks. Anyone within the Area of Effect gains the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Ward (spell)\|Ward (spell)]] (+6) [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Trait]] against magical or ranged attacks originating outside the dome. Those within may attack out of the dome as normal, and the dome does not impede movement.

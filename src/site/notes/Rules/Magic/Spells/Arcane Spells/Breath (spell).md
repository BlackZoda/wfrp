Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** 1 yard
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Instant

You immediately make a Breath  attack, as if you had spent 2 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] to activete the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Breath (trait)\|Breath (trait)]]. *Breath* is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus. The GM decides which type of Breath attack best suits your [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]].

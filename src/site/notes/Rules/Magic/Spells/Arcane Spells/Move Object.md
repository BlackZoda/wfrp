Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 4
**Range:** WP yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** 1 [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]]

Using magic, you grab hold of an non-sentient object no larger than you, moving it with the sheer force of your will, which is considered to have a [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]]. You may move the object up to [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards. If anyone attempts to impede the object’s movement, make a **Contested [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]]/[[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. For every +2 SL you may increase the distance the object is moved by [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards.

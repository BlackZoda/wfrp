Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Instant

You channel a twisting spur of rupturing magic into your target. This is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] of +4. If *Chain Attack* reduces a target to 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], it leaps to another target within the spell’s initial range, and within [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards of the previous target, inflicting the same [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] again. It may leap a maximum number of times equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus. For every +2 SL achieved, it may chain to an additional target.

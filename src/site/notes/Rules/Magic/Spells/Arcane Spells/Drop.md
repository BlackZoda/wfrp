Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 1
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

You [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] magic into an object being held by an opponent. This could be a weapon, a rope, or someone’s hand. Unless a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is passed, the item is dropped. For every +2 SL you may impose an additional -10 on the [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

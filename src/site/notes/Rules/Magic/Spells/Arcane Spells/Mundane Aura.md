Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 4
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] minutes

You drain all the [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] from within your body and your possessions, removing any magical aura. For the duration of the spell you appear mundane to the [[Rules/Talents/Magical Sense\|Magical Sense]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] and similar. You effectively have no magical ability and your magical nature cannot be detected by any means. While this spell is in effect, you cannot cast any other spells. *Mundane Aura* is immediately dispelled if you make a [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

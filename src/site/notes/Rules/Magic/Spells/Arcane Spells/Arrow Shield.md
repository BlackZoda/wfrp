Any missiles containing organic matter, such as arrows with wooden shafts, are automatically destroyed if they pass within the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]], causing no damage to their target. Missiles comprisong only inorganic matter, such as throwing knives or pistol shots, are unaffected.

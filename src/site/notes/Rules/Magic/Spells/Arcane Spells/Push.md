Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** Instant

All living creatures within [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards are pushed back your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus in yards and gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. If this brings them into contact with a wall or other large obstacle, they take [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to the distance travelled in yards. For every +2 SL, you may push creatures back another [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus in yards.

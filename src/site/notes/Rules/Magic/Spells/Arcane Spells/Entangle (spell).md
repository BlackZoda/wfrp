Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]]

**CN:** 3
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Special

Using magic, you entrap your target, wrapping them in whatever suits your [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lore]]: vines, shadows, their own clothing… Your target gains one [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] with a [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] equl to your [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]]. For every +2 SL, you may give the target +1 additional [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. The spell lasts until all [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] are removed.

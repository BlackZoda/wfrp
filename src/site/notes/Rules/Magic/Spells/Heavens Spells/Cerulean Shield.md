**CN:** 7
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You encase yourself in a crackling cage of sparking electricity and [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]]. For the spell’s duration, gain +SL [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] to all locations against [[Melee Attacks\|Melee Attacks]]. If attacked by metal weapons – such as daggers, swords, and spears with metal tips – your attacker takes +[[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]].

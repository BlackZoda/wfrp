[[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]] cast from [[World/Magic/The Lore of Heavens\|The Lore of Heavens]] are accompanied by the crackling of lightning and the smell of ozone. [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spells\|Spells]] causing [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] ignore [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] from metal armour, and will arc to all other targets within 2 yards, except those with the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] ([[Heavens\|Heavens]]) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], inflicting hits with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus, handled like a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]].

**Ingredients:** Astronomical instruments, charts, lenses, and symbols dominate Celestial magic, as do ingredients associated with augury such as animal innards, mirrors, glass balls, and birt tongues. Some wind-based spells use wings and feathers, where those involving electricity prefer silvers of carved metal.

## Seers
The Seer Career can gain access to the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Heavens) Talent. Seers who take this [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] can only take these spells (and none other, even Arcane Spells): [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Heavens Spells/Fate’s Fickle Fingers\|Fate’s Fickle Fingers]], [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Heavens Spells/Starcrossed\|Starcrossed]], [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Heavens Spells/The First Portent of Amul\|The First Portent of Amul]],[[ The Second Portent of Amul\| The Second Portent of Amul]], [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Heavens Spells/The Third Portent of Amul\|The Third Portent of Amul]]. To gain access to the rest of the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Heavens) spells, they would need to enter the [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizard]] career.

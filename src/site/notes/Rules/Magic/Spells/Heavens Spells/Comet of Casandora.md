Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Heavens Spells/Lore of Heavens Spells\|Lore of Heavens Spells]]

**CN:** 10
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** Special

You [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] all the [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]] you can muster and reach out to the skies, calling down a comet to wreak havoc amongst your foes. Select a target point within range. At the end of the next round, make an **Average (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. For every +SL you achieve, you may move your point of impact by [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus yards.

For every -SL, the GM will move the point of impacts by [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus yards in a random direction. *Comet of Cassandora* then acts as a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magical Missile]] with [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] +12 that hits all targets in the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]], who also gain +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Ablaze\|Ablaze]] and the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

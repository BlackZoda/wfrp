Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Metal Spells/Lore of Metal Spells\|Lore of Metal Spells]]

**CN:** 5
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You touch a non-magical object made of metal, which instantly becomes warm to the touch as you squeeze [[Chamon\|Chamon]] into it. You may bend and mangle the object with an **Average (+20) [[Strenght\|Strenght]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you wish to make more complex alteration, you may attempt an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] (Smith, or similar)** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] instead.

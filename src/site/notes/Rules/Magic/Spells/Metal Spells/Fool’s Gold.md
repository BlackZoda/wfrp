Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Metal Spells/Lore of Metal Spells\|Lore of Metal Spells]]

**CN:** 4
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] minutes

You weave [[Chamon\|Chamon]] into a non-magical object made of metal, fundamentally altering its alchemical nature. For the duration of the spell, all metal in the object becomes gold. This is not an illusion: it has actually transformed into gold. When the spell ends, the item reverts to its original metal. This spell can ruin good weapons, make armour too heavy to wear, and turn lead coins into something much more appealing. Spot effects arising from this spell are left in the hands of the GM.

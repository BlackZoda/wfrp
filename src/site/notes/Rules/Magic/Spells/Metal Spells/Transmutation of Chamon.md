Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Metal Spells/Lore of Metal Spells\|Lore of Metal Spells]]

**CN:** 12
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** AoE ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You wrench [[Chamon\|Chamon]] from the metals worn by your foes, and eath itself, briefly transforming the flesh of your enemies into metal. This is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] affecting all in the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]], with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus; the spell ignores [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus and inflicts +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded\|Blinded]], [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened\|Deafened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], all of which last for the duration of the spell. All affected targets gain +1 [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] from the gold wrapped about their bodies, but also suffer from [[Rules/Injuries/Drowning and Suffocation\|Suffocation]]. If targets die while the spell is in effect, they are permanently encased in a shell of base metals, a macabre reminder of the risks of sorcery.

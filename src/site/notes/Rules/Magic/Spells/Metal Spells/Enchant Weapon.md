Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Metal Spells/Lore of Metal Spells\|Lore of Metal Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** special
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You encase a single non-magical weapon with heavy hands of [[Chamon\|Chamon]], enhancint its potency. For the duration of the spell it counts as magical, gains a bonus to [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus, and gains the [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Quaity/Unbreakable\|Unbreakable]] [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Quality/Weapon Quality\|Weapon Quality]]. For every +3 SL you may also add 1 [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Quality/Weapon Quality\|Weapon Quality]] or remove 1 [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Flaw/Weapon Flaw\|Weapon Flaw]] from the weapon, while the spell is in effect.

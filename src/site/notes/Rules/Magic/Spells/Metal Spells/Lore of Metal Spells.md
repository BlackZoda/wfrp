The wind of [[Chamon\|Chamon]] is very dense, and quickly attaches itself to metallic substances. Spells from this lore are accompanied with golden light and heat, and are especially effective against foes foolish enough to encase themselves in metal. Spells inflicting [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] ignore [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] from metal armour, and inflict bonus [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to the number of [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] of metal armour being worn on any [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location]] stuck. So, if you spell hit an Arm location protected by 2 Armour Points of metal armour, it would cause an additional +2 Damage and ignore the Armour Points.

**Ingredients:** Heavy metals of all types, esotercally inlaid or carved, comprise the majority of Gold ingredients, though many spells also use trappings associated with a forge, including sections of a belows marked with mathematical formula, inscribed chunks of an anvil, or gragments of a furnace.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Beast Spells/Lore of Beast Spells\|Lore of Beast Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Instant

You hurl a great spear of pure [[Ghur\|Ghur]] in a straight line. This is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] of +12. It strikes the first creature in its path, ignoring APs from armour made of leather and furs. If the target suffers any [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], also inflict +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Bleeding\|Bleeding]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], after which the speat continues on its path, striking each target in the same manner, but as -1 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] each time. If the spear fails to inflict any [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], its progress is stopped and the spell comes to an end. *The Amber Spear* only inflicts the minimum 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]] on the first target it strikes.

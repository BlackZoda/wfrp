**CN:** 5
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] minutes

You infuse your very bones and flesh with [[Ghur\|Ghur]], warping your body into that of a creature. When cast, select a new form from any of [[World/Bestiary/The Beasts of the Reikland\|The Beasts of the Reikland]] section of the [[Beastiary\|Beastiary]]. Use the standard [[Trait\|Trait]]s for the creature. For every +2 SL, you may include 1 of the included [[Optional Creature Trait\|Optional Creature Trait]].

While in *Beast Form*, you look like a normal version of the creature, with amber and brown colouring. ou may not speak, which means you cannot cast spells, or attempt to dispel.

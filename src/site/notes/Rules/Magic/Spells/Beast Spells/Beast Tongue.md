Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Beast Spells/Lore of Beast Spells\|Lore of Beast Spells]]

**CN:** 3
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] minutes

You can commune with all creatures possessing the [[Beastial\|Beastial]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]]. [[Ghur\|Ghur]] clogs your throat, and your language comes out as snarls, hisses, and roars as benefit the beasts to whom you talk. While the creatures are not compelled to answer you, or do as you bid, most wil be curious enough to hear your out.

You gain +20 on all [[Rules/Skills/Charm Animal\|Charm Animal]] and [[Rules/Skills/Animal Training\|Animal Training]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. While this spell is active, you may only speak with beasts – you may not speak any civilised tongues, and can only communicate with your party using gestures or [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[Battle\|Battle]]). Note, this also means you cannot cast any spells, or dispel, while Beast Tongue is active.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Beast Spells/Lore of Beast Spells\|Lore of Beast Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus rounds

You call down a murder of crows or similar local bird to assail your foes.

The flock attacks everyone in the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]] who does does  not posses the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Beast) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] ferociously, inflicting a +7 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] hit at the end of the [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]]. The flock remains in play for the duration of the spell. For your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] you may make an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Charm Animal\|Charm Animal]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to move the flock to another target within range. While within the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]], all creatures gain +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded\|Blinded]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

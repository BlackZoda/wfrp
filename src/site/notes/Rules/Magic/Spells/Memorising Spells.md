Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spells\|Spells]]

Holding the complex linguistic structures of [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) in mind is a challenging task, so simply transcribing a spell into your [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Grimoire\|Grimoire]] does not allow you learn a spell.

To memorise a spell – and therefore be able to cast it without access to your grimoire – you typically need to spend the amount of XP noted in your spellcasting [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]. Once a spell has been memorised, a spellcaster knows it permenently, barring special circumstances.

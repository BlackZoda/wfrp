**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Target’s [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You cast a delicate net woven from strands of [[Hysh\|Hysh]] over your targets, whose minds are overcome with conundrums and puzzles, leaving them paralysied with indecision. Targets gain +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], which they cannot lose while the spell is in effect. When recovering from the [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], targets test their [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]. Targets with the [[Beastial\|Beastial]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] are immune to this spell.

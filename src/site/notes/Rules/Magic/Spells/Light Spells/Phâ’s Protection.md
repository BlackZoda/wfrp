**CN:** 10
**Range:** You
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You sumon a protective aura of pure, holy light. Profane creatures – ghose with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] or [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Daemonic\|Daemonic]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]], those with [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Mutations]], and those with more [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] than their [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus and [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus combined – cannot enter the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]]. Any already within the Area gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] until they leave. Creatures within the Area cannot gain any [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]] points while the spell is active.

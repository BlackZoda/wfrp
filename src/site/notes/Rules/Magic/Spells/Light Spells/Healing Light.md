**CN:** 9
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

Your target glows with a bright, cleansing light (equivalent light to a compfire), healing [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus + [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]]. If the [[Target\|Target]] passes a **Very Hard (-20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], 1 [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]] is gained in the last hour is also lost.

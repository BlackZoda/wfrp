**CN:** 6
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] minutes

You calm your target’s mind, allowing them to think clearly. All negative modifiers on their thinking processes – from [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]], [[Rules/Tables/Mental Corruption Table\|Mental Mutations]], [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Pshycologies]], or any other source – are ignored while the spell is in effect.

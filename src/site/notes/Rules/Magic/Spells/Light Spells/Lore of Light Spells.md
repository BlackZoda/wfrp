[[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]] sung from the [[World/Magic/The Lore of Light\|Lore of Light]] tend to emit dazzling rays of blinding white light, or shroud the caster in shimmering waves of radiant purity. You may inflict one [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded\|Blinded]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] on those targeted by [[World/Magic/The Lore of Light\|Lore of Light]] spells, unless they possess the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Light) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]].

If a target has the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Daemonic\|Daemonic]] or [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]], spells also inflict an additional hit with [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus that ignores [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus and [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]].

**Ingredients:** [[Hierophant\|Hierophant]]s of the [[World/Magic/The Lore of Light\|Lore of Light]] use many artefacts associated with holiness and holy places, supplemented by crystals, glass, pyramidions, and small statues, all carved with sacred symbols, twisting snakes, and moral tales. White candles, silver carvings, and bleached paper are also common.

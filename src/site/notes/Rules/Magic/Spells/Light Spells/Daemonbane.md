Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Light Spells/Lore of Light Spells\|Lore of Light Spells]]

**CN:** 10
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

You summon a blast of [[Hysh\|Hysh]] that passes through the border between [[World/Religion and Belief/Realms of Chaos/The Realms of Chaos\|The Realms of Chaos]] and the material world. The [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Test]] is [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] by the target making a [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you win, you obliterate a target with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Daemonic\|Daemonic]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] with a binding white light, sending it back whence it came. If the spell successfully banishes a [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]], everyone looking at your target, unless they possess the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Light) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], receives +SL [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded\|Blinded]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]].

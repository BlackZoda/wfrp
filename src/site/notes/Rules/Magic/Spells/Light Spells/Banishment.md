Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Light Spells/Lore of Light Spells\|Lore of Light Spells]]

**CN:** 12
**Range:** You
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** Instant

You send a cleansing halo of [[Hysh\|Hysh]] out from hands, affecting all creatures with the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]] whose [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] is lower than your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]]. Targets with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] and [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] gain the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Unstable\|Unstable]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]]. If they already have the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Unstable\|Unstable]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]], they are reduced to 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]].

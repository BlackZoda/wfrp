**CN:** 6
**Range:** You
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

To cast this spell, you must be in direct contact with the earth. Standing barefoot counts. For the duration of the spell, any creatures in direct contact with the earth within [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] heal [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus at the start of every [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]].

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Special

You cause [[Ghyran\|Ghyran]] to flood an area that is blighted or desolate. You may target either a dry riverbed, well, field, or a domestic animal. If you successfully cast the spell, the target bursts to life:
* A dry river begins to flow once again
* A dry or polluted well becomes clean and fresh
* A planted field, vineyard or orchard bursts into life, with all crops immediately reaching full ripeness
* A sick or unproductive animal becomes healthy. The affected beast is now healthy productive (cows produce milk, hens lay eggs, coast and hides of sheep and cows are healthy and lustrous) and any disease are cured.

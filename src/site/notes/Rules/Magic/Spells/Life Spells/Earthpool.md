Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Life Spells/Lore of Life Spells\|Lore of Life Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** Instant

On casting the spell, you immediately disappear into the ground in a wild torrent of [[Ghyran\|Ghyran]]. You appear at the start of the next [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] at any point within your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] in yards, erupting from the earth violently. For every +2 SL you may increase the distance travelled by your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] in yards. Any enemies engaged by you on your appearance gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Surprised\|Surprised]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. This spell will not allow you to move through stone but will allw you to move through water.

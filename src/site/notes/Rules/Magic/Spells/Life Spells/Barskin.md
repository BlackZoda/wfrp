Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Life Spells/Lore of Life Spells\|Lore of Life Spells]]

**CN:** 3
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You cause the target’s skin to become hard and rough like the bark of a tree. While affected by the spell, add +2 to the target’s [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus, but suffer a penalty of -10 to [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] and [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]].

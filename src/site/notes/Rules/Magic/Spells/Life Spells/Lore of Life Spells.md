Spells cast with [[Ghyran\|Ghyran]], the [[Jade Wind\|Jade Wind]], are suffuse with life, tending to manifest with a vibrant green light, and are often accompanied by natural pehnomena given supernatural qualities. Vines, undergrowth, trees, and rivers all bend to the [[Druid\|Druid]]’s wills.

Receive a +10 bonus to [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting]] and [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] rolls when in a rural or wilderness environment. Living creatures – e.g. those without the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Daemonic\|Daemonic]] or [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] – targeted by [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]] from the [[Lore of Life\|Lore of Life]] have all [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Bleeding\|Bleeding]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] removed after any other effects have been applied as life magic floods through them. Creatures with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]], on the other hand, suffer additional [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus, ignoring [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus and [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]], if affected by any spell cast with the [[Lore of Life\|Lore of Life]].

**Ingredients:** Druids use a wide variety of naturally occuring ingredients, ranging from rare seeds and nuts, humours gathered from sentient creatures in the flush of life, uncommon tree saps, fertile loam, spring waters, and a variety of living ingredients, including plants and smaller animals.

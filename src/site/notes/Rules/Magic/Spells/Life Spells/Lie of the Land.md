Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Life Spells/Lore of Life Spells\|Lore of Life Spells]]

**CN:** 5
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus miles
**Target:** You
**Duration:** Special

Touching the earth, your senses flow through the [[Ghyran\|Ghyran]] tracing the nearby area. After communing for 1 minute, you receive a detailed mental map of all-natural features – land, forests, rivers, but not settlements – within range. Settlements may be alluded to – areas of clear terrain, or dug trenches, for example. Each time you increase the range with SL increases the time taken communing with the land by +1 minute.

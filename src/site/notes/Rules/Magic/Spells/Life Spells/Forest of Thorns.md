**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

This spell may only target a patch of earth (though the patch can be very small). You cause a dense knot of wikedly spiked brambles and tangled vines to burst upwards, covering the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]].

While the spell is active, anyone attempting to traverse the area on foot without the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Life) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] must mae a **Hard (-20) [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. Failure means they gain 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Bleeding\|Bleeding]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], and 1 [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], with your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] used for its [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]]. After the spell concludes, the growth remains, but loses its preternatural properties.

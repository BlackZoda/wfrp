## Chaos Magic
Three of the four [[Great Powers of Chaos\|Great Powers of Chaos]] have unique [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] of [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]] – only [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Khorne\|Khorne]], [[The Blood God\|The Blood God]], who adhors treacherous magic and all it stands for, has no Lore of his own. One spell is provided here for each othe primary [[Chaos Lores\|Chaos Lores]], which can be added to provide flavour to your [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Chaos Cults\|Chaos Cults]] in addition to the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]] and the [[Lore of Deamonology\|Lore of Deamonology]].

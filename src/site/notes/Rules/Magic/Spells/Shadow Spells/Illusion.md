Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Shadow Spells/Lore of Shadows Spells\|Lore of Shadows Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] minutes

You spin a web of intricate strands of [[Ulgu\|Ulgu]], obfuscating the [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]] with an illusory image of your choosing. You will automatically fool anyone without the [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight\|Second Sight]]. Those with that [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] must pass a **Difficult (-10) [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to notice the illusion. This does not let them see through the spell. They must dispel it to do so.

The illusion is, by default, static. For your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]], you may make a **Hard (-20) [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to make the illusion move for that [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]].

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Shadow Spells/Lore of Shadows Spells\|Lore of Shadows Spells]]

**CN:** 10
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus minutes

You weave a mask and cloak of [[Ulgu\|Ulgu]] around your form, assuming the likeness of another humanoid creature with whom you are familiar (as determined by the GM). Your appearance will automatically fool anyone without the [[Rules/Talents/Second Sight (Talent)\|Second Sight (Talent)]], though some may note if any of your mannerisms are incorrect. Those with that [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] must pass a **Difficult (-10) [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to notice you are disguising your form. This does not let them see through the spell. They must dispel it to do so.

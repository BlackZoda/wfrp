Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Shadow Spells/Lore of Shadows Spells\|Lore of Shadows Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Until the next sunrise

You summon forth a shadowy steed. The creature’s unnatural flesh is black as midnight, and at times it appears to be both solid and insubstantial. Use the rules for a riding horse. When the Shadowsteed is out of sunlight, it also gains the following [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]]: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Dark Vision\|Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Dark Vision]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Ethereal\|Ethereal]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Magical\|Magical]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Painless\|Painless]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Stealthy\|Stealthy]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Stride\|Stride]], [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Feared\|Feared]] (1) and [[Ward (trait)\|Ward (trait)]] (+9).

Even when insubstantial, Shadowsteeds may be ridden. Riders with the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Shadow) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] do so with a bonus of +20 to [[Rules/Skills/Ride\|Ride]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. Those without suffer a penalty of -20 to [[Rules/Skills/Ride\|Ride]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. Shadowsteeds are tireless, so need no rest (though their unsettled riders might!). As the first rays of dawn break over the horison the steeds melt into insubstantial mist. If still being ridden when the spell ends, or when dispelled, the rider will suffe [[Rules/Combat/Falling\|Falling]] [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] (page 166 WFRP).

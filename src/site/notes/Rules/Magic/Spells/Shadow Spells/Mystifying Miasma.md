**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You conjure forth a swirling mass of mist shot through with roiling shadow that flits and confounds the senses. Anyone within the mist who does not possess the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Shadows) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] is affected by the Miasma, gaining +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded\|Blinded]], [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened\|Deafened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], which remain for the spell’s duration. Anyone affected attempting to move must pass a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], or gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. If the spell is dispelled once in play, anyone affected by the spell must mae a **Routine (+40) [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], or gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

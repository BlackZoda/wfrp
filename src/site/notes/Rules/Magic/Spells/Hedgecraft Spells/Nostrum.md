Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Hedgecraft Spells/Lore of Hedgecraft Spells\|Lore of Hedgecraft Spells]]

**CN:** 0
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You incant a spell over an already prepared draught, imbuing it with magical power. If drunk while the spell is in effect, the target immediately heals your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus in [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] and is cured of one disease. For every +2 SL you may cure an extra disease.

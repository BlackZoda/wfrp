The Hedgefolk believe their Lore is a gift from the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]], referring to their spellcasters as the [[Blessed Few\|Blessed Few]]. Due to their ancient traditions and ingrained belief, their spells cannot be cast without ingredients, which are an integral part of their spellcasting process.

Fortunately, the ingredients they use are easily found on the fringes of settlements and are usually herbs or plants. You receive 1 + SL ingredients on a successful foraging roll, using [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lore]] ([[Herbalism\|Herbalism]]), as described under [[Rules/Skills/Gathering Food and Herbs\|Gathering Food and Herbs]], or you can by them for 5 brass pennies each.

**Ingredients:** Hedgefolk use easily sourced local materials prepared to extracting standards using special tools. So, ingredients may include things such as the wings of a dragonfly killed with a silver pin, rods of poplar polished  beewax on [[Sonnstill\|Sonnstill]], or bones buried beneath a hederow for a winter’s month.

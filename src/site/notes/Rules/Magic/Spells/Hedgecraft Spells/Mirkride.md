**CN:** 0
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus minutes

Speaking ancient words of magic, your spirit leaves your body, stepping into the [[Hedge\|Hedge]], the dark spece between the material world and the spirit realm. For the duration, you stand apart from the world, abe to witness it invisibly, but not affect it in any way. Physical barriers are no impediment to you, and you may walk through non-magical obstacles at will. Your body remains in place, immobile and insensate.

At the end of the spell you will be pulled suddenly back to your body. If your body is killed while you are walking the [[Hedge\|Hedge]], your spirit will wanter aimlessly for eternity.

**CN:** 7
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Instant

As you reach out your hand, it appears withered, even skeletal, drawing [[Shyish\|Shyish]] from your your target’s corpus. This counts as a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] of +6 that ignores [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus and [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]]. For every 2 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] inflected, you may recover 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]].

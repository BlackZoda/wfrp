**CN:** 6
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You conjure a magical scythe, which can be wielded in combat, using the [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] ([[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Polearm Weapon\|Polearm]]) [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]. It acts like a normal schyte with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus+3. Enemies with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] do not receive [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] when [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] in [[Rules/Combat/Combat\|Combat]] with you.

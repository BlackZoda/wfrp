Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Death Spells/Lore of Death Spells\|Lore of Death Spells]]

**CN:** 7
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

Thin strands of purple mist connect you briefly to your target, who wastes away before your very eyes. This counts as a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] with a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] of +6 that ignores [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] and inflicts +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. Further, you remove all [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] you currently suffer, and ma heal yourself up to half the [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] the target suffers, rounding up.

The purple wind of [[Shyish\|Shyish]] carries with it dry, dusty winds and the insistent rustling of sand passing through Time’s hourglass. Targets afflicted by spels from [[World/Magic/The Lore of Death\|The Lore of Death]] are drained of life, enervated, and listless. You may assign +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] to any living target affected by a spell from this lore. A target may only ever have a single [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigue]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] gained in this manner at any one time.

**Ingredients:** The bones of sentient creatures feature havily in Amethyst magic, as do the trappings of death, including woods or nails from coffins, embalming fluids, hourglasses, silver coins, and grave dirt, all carefully presented or engraved. Purple gemstones, materials, and flowers (particularly roses) are also common.

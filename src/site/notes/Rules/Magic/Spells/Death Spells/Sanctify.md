**CN:** 10
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)

Inscribing a magical circle, you ward it with [[Shyish\|Shyish]], forming an impenetrable barrier to the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]]. Creatures with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] cannot enter or leave the circle.

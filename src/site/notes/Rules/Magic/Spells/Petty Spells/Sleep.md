Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Petty Spells/Petty Spells\|Petty Spells]]

**CN:** 0
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You touch your opponent, sending them into a deep sleep. If the target has the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], they gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] as they fall asleep. The remain unconscious for the duration, although loud noises or being moved or jostled will awaken them instantly. If your targets are standing or sitting when affected, they start themselves awake as they hit the ground, gaining the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], but remaining conscious.

If your targets are not resisting, and are suitably tired, they will, at the spell’s end, pass into a deep end restful sleep.

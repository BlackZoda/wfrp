Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Petty Spells/Petty Spells\|Petty Spells]]

**CN:** 0
**Range:** You
**Target:** You
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] minutes

The magic flowing through your feet ensures any organic matter you tread upon remains undamaged: twigs do not break, grass springs back to its original position, and even delicate flowers are unharmed. Those seeking to use the [[Rules/Skills/Track\|Track]] skill to pursue you through rural terrain suffer a -30 penalty to their [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].

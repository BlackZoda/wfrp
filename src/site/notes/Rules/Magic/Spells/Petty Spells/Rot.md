Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Petty Spells/Petty Spells\|Petty Spells]]

**CN:** 0
**Range:** 1 yard
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Instant

You cause a roughly fist-sized volume of organic material to immediately rot. Food stuffs perish, clothes crumble, learhers shrivel (losing 1 [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] on hit location), and similar, as dictated by the GM.

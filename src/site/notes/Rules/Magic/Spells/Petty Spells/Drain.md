**CN:** 0
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** Instant

You touch your targets, draining their life. This counts as a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] with [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] +0 that ignores [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]]. You then Heal 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]].

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Petty Spells/Petty Spells\|Petty Spells]]

**CN:** 0
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] minutes

You create a number of flickering magical lights up to your [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] Bonus. They resemble torches or hooded lanterns. Providing they remain within line of sight, for your action you may contro the lights by passing a **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]; a success allows you to send the lights moving in any direction. They will move at walking pace in a straight line, passing through any object (or witnesses) in their path, unless you test again to change their direction.

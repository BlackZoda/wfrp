For the few [[World/Races/Human\|Humans]] blessed – or cursed, depending upon your point of view – with the spark of magic, it generally manifests around puberty, and almost always before 25 summers have passed. The first indications of impending witchery are often little tricks, knacks, cantrips, or similar, showing the wizard-to-be should probably be trained for everyone else’s safety.

For [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]], this is just part of growing up, and those with interest in magic are schooled to develop thheir bugeoning talents. For [[World/Races/Human\|Humans]], assuming they avoid being lynched, it likely means years of training as an apprentice to a [[Floating/Magister\|Magister]] from one of the [[Eight Colleges of Magic\|Eight Colleges of Magic]], after which they should never use the little tricks they learned when young again. But most do.

As Petty spells are not formally codified, they have many different names. Players are encouraged to devise their own, more characterful names, reflecting their personality.

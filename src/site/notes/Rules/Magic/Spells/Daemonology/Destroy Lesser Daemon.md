**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

Your spell disrupts the [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]] holding a target [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]] together, and draws it back to you. A target with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Daemonic\|Daemonic]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] and lower [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] than you loses [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus, ignoring [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus or [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]]. In turn, you may increase one of your [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]] by +10 for the duration as you siphon profane energies.

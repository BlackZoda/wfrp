**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] a sickening flow of [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]], briefly forcing a rent into the fabric of reality. A [[Lesser Daemon\|Lesser Daemon]] immediately manifest through the rent (see [[Bloodletter\|Bloodletter]] and [[Daemonette\|Daemonette]] for examples). Perform an **Opposed [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] ([[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]])/[[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] with the [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]]. If successful, the Daemon will respond to one command issued by you, quite literally, then vanish (assuming the command is completed before the [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Duration\|Duration]] ends). If failed, the [[World/Bestiary/Daemons\|Daemons]] immediately attacks.

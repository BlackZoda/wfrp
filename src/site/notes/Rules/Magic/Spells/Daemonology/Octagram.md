Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Daemonology/Lore of Daemonology Spells\|Lore of Daemonology Spells]]

**CN:** 10
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] (maximim, [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards across)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] minutes

Daubing an octagram to the floor, and marking it with unholy symbols, you ward against all daemonic influence. Those with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Daemonic\|Daemonic]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]] cannot enter or leave the octagram unless their [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] is more than twice yours.

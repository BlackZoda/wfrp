The forbidden Lore of Daemonology is concerned with summoning, binding, and controling Daemons, typically to empower the spellcaster. It is horrifically dangerous, usually leading to the [[Daemonologist\|Daemonologist]]’s downfall as their soul is taken by the [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Ruinous Powers]] and a new [[Chaos Sourcerer\|Chaos Sourcerer]] is born.

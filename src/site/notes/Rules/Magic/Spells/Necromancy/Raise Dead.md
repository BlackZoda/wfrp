Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Necromancy/Lore of Necromancy Spells\|Lore of Necromancy Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** Until sunrise

You [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] a heavy flow of [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]] into bare earth, causing old bones to gather and rise. SL+1 [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Skeleton\|Skeleton]]s will claw upwards into the affected area at the end of the [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], which are organised as you prefer within the area of effect. They start with the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. The summoned undead are entirely under your control and can perform simple orders as you command. If you are killed or gain an [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], the spell comes to an end and the sumoned [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] collapse. For each +2 SL you score, you may summon an extra SL [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Skeleton\|Skeleton]]s.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Necromancy/Lore of Necromancy Spells\|Lore of Necromancy Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] ([[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards)
**Duration:** Until sunrise

You channel worming strands of thick [[Dhar\|Dhar]] before you, sinkng it into corpses, reanimating that which was once dead. Reanimate [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus + SL dead bodies (as [[Zombie\|Zombie]]s) or skeletons (as [[Skeleton\|Skeleton]]s) within range. They start with the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. the summoned [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] are entirely under your control and can perform simple orders as you command. If you die or gain an [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], the spell comes to an end and the reanimated corpses fall dead again. For each +2 SL you score, you may reanimage an extra [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] + SL [[Skeleton\|Skeleton]]s or [[Zombie\|Zombie]]s.

**CN:** 5
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

Your magic pierces deepwithin your target, flooding the soul with an acute awareness of their broken dreams. The target’s [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristic]] drops to 10, if it was not already lower, as the mind turns inwards. On the target’s [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]], all movement is randomised as determined by the GM as the character bemoans life, lewdly telling all what should have been as hands are flung in the air. Further, the target can only perform an [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] if a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is first passed; if failed te Target is far too busy bemoaning what could have been to do anything else.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Necromancy/Lore of Necromancy Spells\|Lore of Necromancy Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Instant

You shriek the high-pithed words of the spell and a large, black skull wreathed with greenish-purpe fire forms before you, then flies forwards, screaming and cackling as it goes. The skull moves in a straight line for the spell range, following the contours of the land, passing through any obstacles in its way. *Screaming Skull* is a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Magic Missiles\|Magic Missile]] that only afects targets without the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Undead\|Undead]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]], and has a [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] bonus. Any suffering [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] from the spell must pass a **Chalenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or also take a [[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].

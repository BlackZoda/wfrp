Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Witchcraft Spells/Lore of Witchcraft Spells\|Lore of Witchcraft Spells]]

**CN:** 10
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

Stabbing a crude representation of your target – a doll or puppet – you inflict crippling pain. When successfully cast, choose which hit location to stab:
* **Leg** – Leg becomes useless, as if it was [[Rules/Injuries/Amputated Parts\|Amputated]]. If running, the target also gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and takes falling [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]].
* **Arm** – Arm becomes useless, as if it was [[Rules/Injuries/Amputated Parts\|Amputated]]. If target was holding anything in that hand, it is automatically dropped.
* **Body** – Target doubles up in agony, gaining +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], and must pass a **Hard (-20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].
* **Head** – Target gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], and must pass an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] for the [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Duration\|Duration]].

While the spell is in effect, for your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] you may make a [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], stabbing the doll again, to affect a different location.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Witchcraft Spells/Lore of Witchcraft Spells\|Lore of Witchcraft Spells]]

**CN:**  8
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus miles
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus days

The ingredient for this spell is something belonging to the target, either a personal possession or a strand of hair. For the duration, you target suffers bad luck. Lace snap, chairs break, and other minor narrative effects inconvenience them. The target suffers a penalty of -10 to all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], in addition to any other modifiers, and may not spend [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] points.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Witchcraft Spells/Lore of Witchcraft Spells\|Lore of Witchcraft Spells]]

**CN:** 14
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus yards
**Target:** Special
**Duration:** Special

You may target either a well, a field, or a domestic animal. If you successfully cast the spell, the target suffer from a [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Blight\|Blight]]:
* A blighted well becomes instantly brackish and stagnant
* Any crops currently planted in a Blighted field rot overnight
* A blighted animal sickens. The afflicted beast no longer produce anything (cows produce no milk, hens no eggs, coats and hides gro mangy and unusable) and will die in 10-SL days.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Witchcraft Spells/Lore of Witchcraft Spells\|Lore of Witchcraft Spells]]

**CN:** 6
**Range:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards
**Target:** 1
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]]

You summon a swarm of creeping, slithering creatures to harass your foes. Each target affected is immediately engaged by a swarm of [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Giant Rat\|Giant Rat]]s, [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Giant Spider\|Giant Spider]]s, or [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Snake\|Snake]]s. Use the standard profiles for the relevant creature type, adding the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Swarm\|Swarm]] [[World/Bestiary/Creature Traits\|Creature Traits]]. For your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] you may make a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Charm Animal\|Charm Animal]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to direct 1 or more swarms to attack a different target.

When the spell ends, any remaining swarms disappear into the shadows.

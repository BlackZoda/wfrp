Home: [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Witchcraft Spells/Lore of Witchcraft Spells\|Lore of Witchcraft Spells]]

**CN:** 8
**Range:** Touch
**Target:** [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|AoE]] (a single location)
**Duration:** [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] days

Your target a single location, such as a house or clearing, and inflict haunting dreams and waking nightmares on any who enter thre. Those entering while the spell is in effect are unnerved by eerie sensations, flitting shadows, and whispered voices luring on the treshold of hearing. Unless they possess the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] ([[World/Magic/Witchcraft\|Witchcraft]]) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]], they gain +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. Then, unless they pass an **Average (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], they gain another +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and the [[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], both of which are only removed upon leaving the location.

Spells from the [[Lore of Witchcraft\|Lore of Witchcraft]] draw on whichever winds of magic are available, without care or concern for mingling the winds and the potentially horrific results. Each time practitioners of [[World/Magic/Witchcraft\|Witchcraft]] roll on a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Fumbled Casting\|Miscast]] table, they also gain 1 [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]]. Further, you may inflict one [[Rules/Conditions/Bleeding\|Bleeding]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] on anyone targeted by spells from the [[World/Magic/Witchcraft\|Lore of Witchcraft]]. Lastly, [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] or [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting]] spells from this Lore automatically require a roll on the [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]] table unless cast with an ingredient, where the ingredient provides no further protection should you roll a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Fumbled Casting\|Miscast]]. Fortunately, ingredients for the [[World/Magic/Witchcraft\|Lore of Witchcraft]] are cheap and readily available: body parts of small animals for the most part. Ingredients cost a spell’s CN in brass pennies, instead of silver shillings, to purchase. Alternatively, a [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witch]] may forage for parts, using the [[Rules/Skills/Outdoor Survival\|Outdoor Survival]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]: a successful foraging roll receives 1 + SL ingredients, as described under [[Rules/Skills/Gathering Food and Herbs\|Gathering Food and Herbs]].

**Ingredients:** [[Rules/Career/Witch\|Witches]] use a horrific mixture of animal body parts, often harvested when the creature is still alive. It is not uncommon to find them clutching lizard eyes, dog toes, dony grizzard, or much, much worse in their bloody hands as they cast their fould magics.

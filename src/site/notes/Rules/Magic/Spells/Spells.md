There are four type of spell: *Petty*, *Arcane*, *Lore*, and *Chaos* spells. [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Petty Spells/Petty Spells\|Petty Spells]] are simple tricks that use negligible amounts of magic. [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Arcane Spells/Arcane Spells\|Arcane Spells]] are generic spells open to those studying any [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] of [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]] or [[Rules/Talents/Chaos Magic\|Chaos Magic]]. [[Lore Spells\|Lore Spells]] are those you can only learn if you know that [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]]; i.e. to learn spells from [[Lore of Fire\|Lore of Fire]], you need the [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] (Fire) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]. [[Chaos Spells\|Chaos Spells]] are those practiced by those who’ve lost their souls to [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]].

For every +2 SL ou achieve in a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Test]], you may add additional [[Range\|Range]], [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Area of Effect\|Area of Effect]], [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Duration\|Duration]], or [[Targets\|Targets]] equal to the initial value listed in the spell. [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spells]] with a Range and Target of “You” may only ever target the spellcaster making the [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Test]]. [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spells]] with a Range of “Touch” may not be extended. If the Spell has no Duration, you cannot extend it.

You may choose the same option more than once. For instance, if you achieved + 4 SL above your [[Casting Number\|Casting Number]] on a spell with a [[Target\|Target]] of 1, you may now Target 3 individuals. Certain [[Rules/Magic/Spells/Spell List\|Spells]] may have additional, optional benefits for additional SL noted in their description.

## Winds of Magic Fixes
* Instead of flat increases to targets, area of effect, range and duration as well as stacking with increased damage, you now decide to spend overcasting SLs on these things with diminishing returns and additional damage is one of these options rather than stacking on top of them.

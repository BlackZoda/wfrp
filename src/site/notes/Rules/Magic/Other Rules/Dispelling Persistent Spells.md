Home: [[Rules/Magic/Other Rules/Dispelling\|Dispelling]]

If a spell has a lasting effect, you may attempt to dispell that spell for your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]]. This is done by making an **Extended [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]])** [[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Test]]. When your SL reaches the CN of the ongoing spell, you successfully dispell it.

Multiple spellcasters attempting to dispell the same spell each roll separately. If they cast using the same [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Lore]], the may decide to make an [[Rules/Tests/Assistance\|Assisted Test]] instead.

If a [[Spell\|Spell]] targets you, or a point you can see within [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] yards, you may oppose the [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Test]] with [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] as you chant a counterspell. Make an **Opposed [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]])** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you win the [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]], you dispel the incoming spell; if you ose, the spell uses the SL of the Opposed Test to determine whether the casting was successful as normal. You may only attempt to dispel a single spell every [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]].

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Magic Rules\|Magic Rules]]

if you’re an [[World/Races/Elf\|Elf]], you can learn a number of [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Arcane Lores]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus. If you have [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] 65, you can learn 6 of the 8 [[World/Magic/The Eight Lores\|Arcane Lores]]. However, doing this takes time and effort. ou can’t purchase a new [[Rules/Talents/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] until you have mastered your previous one by taking at least 20 [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] in the [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channel]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] of, and learning 8 spells from, your [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]]. Alternatively, any [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizard]] can learn a single [[Dark Lore\|Dark Lore]] in addition to other [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]], assuming they are foolish enough and can find a teacher or forbidden [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Grimoire\|Grimoire]] to study.

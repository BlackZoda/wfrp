Home: [[Rules/Magic/Channelling/Channelling Test\|Channelling Test]]

[[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] the [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] in a large flow is dangerous. You count any double or any roll ending in a 0 over your [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] as a *Fumble*, so 00, 99, 90, 88, and so on. If you fumble a [[Rules/Magic/Channelling/Channelling Test\|Channelling Test]], you suffer a [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Fumbled Casting\|Miscast]]. Roll 1d100 and consult the [[Rules/Tables/Major Miscast Table\|Major Miscast Table]].

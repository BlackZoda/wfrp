Some magical spells require far more magic than can normally be found ambiently flowing through the world. To power such spells, it is possible to draw the [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] and concentrate them into a more powerful form by using the [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]. Channelling the [[World/Magic/The Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]] can be a dangerous procedure, but it is the only sure way to cast some powerful spells. To channel magic for a spell, make an [[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Channelling Test]].

When your SL reaches the CN of your selected [[Spell\|Spell]] you have channelled enough magic to cast it. On the next [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], you can cast your spell using the normal [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting]] rules, but count the chosen spell’s [[Casting Number\|Casting Number]] as 0. If the casting [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] fails, you also lose all your channelled magical energy, and suffer a [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]] as it all writhes free from your [[World/Magic/The Aethyr\|Aethyric]] grasp.

## Winds of Magic Fixes
* You don’t have to pick a spell first when you start channelling.
* You don’t need to channel all the power for a spel – you can part-channel and then take the [[Rules/Magic/Casting/Casting Test\|Casting Test]] in the hope you get the rest of the SLs you need.
* You can vent the power as an action.
* You can [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]] Bonus extra channelling SLs, but also take [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]] (unless you have [[Rules/Talents/Aethyric Attunement\|Aethyric Attunement]]).
* If you fumble a miscast roll you only take a minor miscast (ignored if you used ingredients with the spell).

## Notes
* Check *Archives of the Empire 3* for “cants” which can use small parts of the channelled energy for smaller effects.

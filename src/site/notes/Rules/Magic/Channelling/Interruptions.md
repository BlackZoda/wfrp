Home: [[Rules/Magic/Channelling/Channelling Test\|Channelling Test]]

Concentration is vital when channelling. If you are distracted by anything – loud noises, suffering damage, flashing lights, or similar – you must pass a **Hard (-20) [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], or suffer a [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast]] and lose all SL you have accrued in the [[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Channelling Test]] so far.

Home: [[Rules/Magic/Channelling/Channelling Test\|Channelling Test]]

If you roll a [[Rules/Combat/Criticals\|Critical]] when [[Rules/Skills/Channelling\|Channelling]], you have channelled a mighty flow of magic, and can cast your spell next [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] regardless of the SL you’ve scored in the [[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Test]] so far; however, so much magic so quickly concentrated in one place results in some magical backlash: roll 1d100 on the [[Rules/Tables/Minor Miscast Table\|Minor Miscast Table]] unless you have the [[Rules/Talents/Aethyric Attunement\|Aethyric Attunement]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]].

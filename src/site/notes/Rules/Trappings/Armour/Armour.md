Armour is listed in the following format:

* **[[Armour Type\|Armour Type]]:** Each armour is listed by the material its constructed from, in order of protective effectiveness.
* **Price:** The price to buy an average piece of the armour.
* **[[Rules/Trappings/Encumberance/Encumberance\|Enc]]:** The Encumbrance for the armour.
* **Penalty:** Any penalty for wearing that specific piece of Armour in addition to Encumbrance (Page 293). Note: Some armour brings a penalty if you wear any of it, such as wearing any chain brings a penalty of –10 Stealth.
* **[[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Locations]]:** The Hit Locations the Armour protects.
* **[[World/Bestiary/Traits/Armour Point\|AP]]s:** The number of Armour Points the armour provides to the protected Hit Locations
* **[[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour Quality\|Armour Quality]] and [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour Flaw\|Flaws]]:** Any Armour Qualities or Flaws the armour may possess.

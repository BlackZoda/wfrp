Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour Flaw\|Armour Flaw]]

The armour has small weakpoints where a blade can slip in if your opponent is sufficiently skilled or lucky. If your opponent has a weapon with the Impale Quality and scores a Critical, the APs of your armour are ignored.

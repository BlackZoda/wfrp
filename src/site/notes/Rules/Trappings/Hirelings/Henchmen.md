Henchmen are Hirelings, companions, friends, pets, or other NPCs that are effectively permanent members of your party. Unlike other NPCs, Henchmen should have full character sheets just like a PC, and are typically attached to one player, either as an employee, friend, owner, or similar. The Henchmen earns hal the XP of that player, rounding down, which the player may spend on the Henchman’s behalf. Henchmen not only make memorable allies, but should your character die, they can also make marvellous PC replacements instead of creating a new character.

If you need extramuscle or brains whilst adventuring, you may pay Hireling NPCs to accompany ou. You can hire any [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] with GM permission, and they are paid an amount of coin equal to their [[Social Status\|Social Status]] for a quick job, or paid triple for a full day’s work. The GM may increase or decrease this amount accoring to individual personality and local circumstnces.

If the job at hand is unexpectedly dangerous, Hirelings will expect double the normal pay unless a [[Rules/Skills/Leadership\|Leadership]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is passed. Leadership Tests may also be required when Hirelings are assigned complex, independent tasks, or if they have good reason to flee from an encounter. QuickHireling profiles are created by adding 5 [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] to every [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristic]] and [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] per level of the [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] hired, and 1 [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] per level. Hirelings with more experienced than this usully cost more.

Wrap weapons typically have long chains with weights at the end, making it very difficult to parry them effectively. *[[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] Tests opposing an attack from a Wrap weapon suffer a penalty of –1 SL*, as parried strikes wrap over the top of shields, or around blades.

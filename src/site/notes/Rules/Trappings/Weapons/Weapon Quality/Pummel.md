Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Quality/Weapon Quality\|Weapon Quality]]

Pummel weapons are especially good at battering foes into submission. If you score a [[Head\|Head]] hit with a Pummel weapon, attempt an Opposed [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]]/ [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]] test against the struck opponent. If you win the test, your opponent gains a [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] Condition.

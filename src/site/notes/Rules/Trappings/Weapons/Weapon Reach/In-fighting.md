Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/Weapon Reach\|Weapon Reach]]

As your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]], you can perform an **Opposed [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to attempt to step inside your opponent’s weapon length. The winner chooses if combat continues as normal or as ‘infighting’. During in-fighting, any weapon longer than [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/Short\|Short]] counts as an [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Improvised Weapon\|Improvised Weapon]].

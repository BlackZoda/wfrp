The lengths of the weapons are progressively bigger as follows.
* **[[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/Personal\|Personal]]:** Your legs and fists, perhaps your head, and anything attached to those.
* **[[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/Very Short\|Very Short]]:** Less than a foot in length.
* **[[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/Short\|Short]]:** Up to 2 feet in length.
* **[[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/Average (Weapon)\|Average (Weapon)]]:** Up to 3 feet long.
* **[[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/Very Long\|Very Long]]:** Up to 10 feet in length; can Engage enemies up to 4 yards away, rather than just 2.
* **[[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Reach/Massive\|Massive]]:** Anything over 10 feet long; can Engage enemies up to 6 yards away, rather than just 2.

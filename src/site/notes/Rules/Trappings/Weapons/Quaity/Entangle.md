Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Quality/Weapon Quality\|Weapon Quality]]

Your weapon wraps around your opponents, entangling them. Any opponent successfully hit by your weapon gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] with a Strength value equal to your Strength Characteristic. When Entangling an opponent, you cannot otherwise use the weapon to hit. You can end the Entangling whenever you wish.

Defensive weapons are designed to parry incoming attacks. If you are wielding such a weapon, gain a bonus of ***+1 SL** to any **[[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]]** when you oppose an incoming attack*.

Impale weapons can kill with a single clean blow. *Impale* weapons cause a *[[Rules/Combat/Criticals\|Critical Hit]] on any number divisible by 10* (i.e.: 10, 20, 30, etc.) as well as on doubles (i.e.: 11, 22, 33) rolled equal or under an appropriate [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]] in combat.

If the impale comes from a ranged weapon, the ammunition used has firmly lodged itself in the target’s body. *Arrows and bolts require a successful **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]]** Test to remove — bullets require a surgeon* (see the [[Rules/Talents/Surgery\|Surgery]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] in *Chapter 4: Skills and Talents*). *You cannot heal 1 of your [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] for each unremoved arrow or bullet.*

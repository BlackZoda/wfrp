Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Quality/Weapon Quality\|Weapon Quality]]

The crack of gunfire followed by gouts of smoke and confusion can be terrifying. If you are targeted by a **Blackpowder** weapon, you must pass an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or take a **[[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] Condition**, even if the shot misses.

On a *Fumble that also is an even number*, your weapon [[Misfires\|Misfires]], exploding in your hand. You take full [[Rules/Combat/Apply Damage\|Damage]] to your [[Right Arm\|Primary Arm]] location using the *unit die as an effective SL for the hit*, and your weapon is destroyed.

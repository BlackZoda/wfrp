Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Quality/Weapon Quality\|Weapon Quality]]

Some weapons are designed to trap other weapons, and sometimes even break them. *If you score a Critical when defending against an attack from a bladed weapon you can choose to trap it instead of causing a Critical Hit.*

If you choose to do this, enact an **Opposed Strength [[Test\|Test]]**, adding your **SL** from the previous [[Melee \|Melee ]][[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]]. *If you succeed, your opponent drops the blade* as it is yanked free. If you score an [[Astounding Success\|Astounding Success]], you *not only disarm your opponent, but the force of your maneuver breaks their blade unless it has the [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Quaity/Unbreakable\|Unbreakable]] quality*. If you fail the [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]], your opponent frees the blade and may fight on as normal.

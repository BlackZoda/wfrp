Distracting weapons can be used to drive an opponent back due to their dangerous or whip-like natures. *Instead of causing Damage*, a successful attack with a *Distracting* weapon can *force an opponent back 1 yard per SL* by which you win the [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]].

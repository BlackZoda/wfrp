Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Quality/Weapon Quality\|Weapon Quality]]

Fast weapons are designed to strike out with such speed that parrying is not an option, leaving an opponent skewered before they can react. A wielder of a *Fast* weapon can choose to attack with the *Fast* weapon outside of the normal Initiative sequence, either striking first, last, or somewhere in between as desired.

Further, *all [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to defend against Fast weapons suffer a penalty of –10* if your opponent is using a weapon without the *Fast Quality*; other [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skills]] defend as normal. Two opponents with Fast weapons fight in Initiative order (relative to each other) as normal. A Fast weapon may never also be [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Flaw/Slow\|Slow]] (Slow takes precedent).

Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Flaw/Weapon Flaw\|Weapon Flaw]]

The weapon is slow to reload. An unloaded weapon with this flaw requires an **Extended [[Rules/Skills/Ranged\|Ranged]] Test** for the appropriate *Weapon Group* scoring *5 SL* to reload. If you are interrupted while reloading, you must start again from scratch.

A *Damaging weapon* can use the higher score from either the *units die or the SL to determine the [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]]* caused from a successful hit. For example, if you roll 34 in your attack [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] and the target number was 52 you can choose to use the SL, which in this case is 2, or the units die result, which is 4. An [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Flaw/Undamaging\|Undamaging]] weapon can never also be *Damaging* (*Undamaging* takes precedent).

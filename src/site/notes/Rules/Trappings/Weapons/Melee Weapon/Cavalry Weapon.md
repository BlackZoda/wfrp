Cavalry weapons are assumed to be used when mounted. When not used from horse-back, all two-handed weapons in the Cavalry Weapon Group also count as [[Two-Handed\|Two-Handed]] weapons. Singlehanded Cavalry weapons are not normally used when unmounted.

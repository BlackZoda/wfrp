All melee weapons are assigned to a **Weapon Group**. Each [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Group\|Weapon Group]] requires a separate skill to master its use. So, *Melee (Flail)* allows you to use [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Flail Weapon\|Flail Weapon]]s and is a separate skill from [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] ([[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Polearm Weapon\|Polearm Weapon]]), which allows you to use [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Polearm Weapon\|Polearms]]. If you use a weapon from a Group where you have no Advances, you Test your Weapon Skill to hit with the weapon. While you still suffer all the weapon’s Flaws, you cannot use any of its Qualities. Some Melee Weapon Groups also have special rules.

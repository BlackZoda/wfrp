Ranged weapons are difficult to master. You cannot attempt a Ranged [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] for a weapon you do not have the correct speciality for. So, if you know **[[Rules/Skills/Ranged\|Ranged]] (Blackpowder)**, you cannot attempt a **Ranged (Bow) Test**. However, there are a few exceptions.

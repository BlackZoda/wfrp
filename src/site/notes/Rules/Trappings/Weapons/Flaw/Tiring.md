The weapon is fatiguing to use or difficult to bring to bear. You *only gain the benefit of the [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Quaity/Impact\|Impact]] and [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Quaity/Damaging\|Damaging]] Weapon Traits on a [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]] you [[Rules/Combat/Charging\|Charge]]*.

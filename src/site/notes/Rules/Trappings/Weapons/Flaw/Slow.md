Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Flaw/Weapon Flaw\|Weapon Flaw]]

Slow weapons are unwieldy and heavy, making them difficult to use properly. Characters using Slow weapons *always strike last in a [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]]*, regardless of Initiative order. Further, *opponents gain a bonus of +1 SL to any [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Test]] to defend* against your attacks.

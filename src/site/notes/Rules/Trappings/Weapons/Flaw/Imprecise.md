Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Flaw/Weapon Flaw\|Weapon Flaw]]

Imprecise weapons are difficult to bring to bear as they are unwieldy or hard to aim. Suffer a penalty of *–1 SL when using the weapon to attack*. An *Imprecise Weapon* can never be [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Quaity/Precise\|Precise]] (Imprecise takes precedent).

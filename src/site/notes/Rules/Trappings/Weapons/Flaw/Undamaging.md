Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Flaw/Weapon Flaw\|Weapon Flaw]]

Some weapons are not very good at penetrating armour. *All APs are doubled* against Undamaging weapons. Further, *you do not automatically inflict a minimum of 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]]* on a successful hit in combat.

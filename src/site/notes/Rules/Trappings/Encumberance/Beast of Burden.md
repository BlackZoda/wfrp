Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Encumberance/Encumberance\|Encumberance]]

Draft animals ignore the standard *Strength Bonus + Toughness Bonus* formula for [[Encumbrance\|Encumbrance]] points. Encumbrance points for [[Mule\|Mule]]s, [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Horse\|Horse]]s, [[Cart\|Cart]]s and [[Wagon\|Wagon]]s are listed in their descriptions. Each Human-sized passenger is assumed to weigh around 10 Encumbrance points, modified by the GM as necessary.

Even the doughtiest Dwarf cannot carry a limitless number of trappings. To help you track how much you are carrying, all items are marked as weighing a number of Encumbrance points (sometimes abbreviated to ‘Enc’), typically from 0–3, where 0 shows a trifling item that’s easily carried, and 3 represents something that’s a struggle to heft. The number of Encumbrance points you can carry without penalty is determined by your *Strength Bonus + Toughness Bonus*. Thus, average humans begin play able to carry around 6 Encumbrance points.

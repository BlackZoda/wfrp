Home: [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Diseases\|Diseases]]

Your very blood is diseased, and your heart now pumps sickness through your body. Leeching the rot is the accepted cure, but some doctors instead cut careful incisions near the neck to free the contaminated blood, demanding the patient ingest vast quantities of healthy blood to replace what is lost. Whether the patient accept such remedies or not, without treatment, Blood Rot is deadly and will likely end in a visit from the [[Mournes’ Guild\|Mournes’ Guild]] and the [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Morr\|Cult of Morr]].

**Contraction:** As a development of another disease or a [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]].
**Incubation:** Instant
**Duration:** 1d10 days
**Symptoms:** [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Blight\|Blight]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Fever\|Fever]] (Severe), [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Malaise\|Malaise]]

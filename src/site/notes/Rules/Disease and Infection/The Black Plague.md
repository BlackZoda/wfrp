The [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Cult of Shallya\|Cult of Shallya]] has sworn to do everything in their power to eradicate this foul disease. Using ancient laws and rights granted to them, white-roped *cordon sanitaires* are erected wherever they encounter the Plague, ensuring none can leave or enter the quarantined area until the outbreak i contained, and all bodies are disposed.

**Contration:** Enact an **Average (+20) Endurance** Test for each hour, or part thereof, spent in an area infested with infected fleas, or when exposed to infected fluids.
**Incubation:** 1d10 minutes
**Duration:* 3d10 days
**Symptoms:** [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Buboes\|Buboes]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Blight\|Blight]] (Moderate), [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Fever\|Fever]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Gangrene\|Gangrene]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Malaise\|Malaise]]

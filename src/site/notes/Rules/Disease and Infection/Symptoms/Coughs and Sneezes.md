Home: [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Symptoms\|Symptoms]]

You intermittently cough or sneeze, spreading your disease to all around you. Any characters in your environment are exposed to the disease you carry, and need Test for Contraction once per hour, or part thereof, of exposure.

**Treatment:** None that work.

You are seriously ill and perhaps close to [[Morr’s Portal\|Morr’s Portal]] as deadly poisons flood your body.

Pass a **Very Easy (+60) Endurance** Test dail (normally when you sleep) r die, passing away, perhaps in your sleep, perhaps lost in a fever, perhaps in agony. If Blight is marked as *(Moderate)*, this test is **Easy (+40)**; if marked as *(Severe)*, this Test is **Average (+20)**.

**Treatment:** None that work.

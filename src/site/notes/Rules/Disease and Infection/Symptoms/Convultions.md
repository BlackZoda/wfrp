Home: [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Symptoms\|Symptoms]]

Your body periodically spasms or shakes as the infection seemingly uses you like a puppet.

Suffer a penalty of *-10* to all physical Tests as your body convulses beyond your control. If this sumptom is marked as *(Moderate)*, the penalty increases to *-20*. If it is marked as *(Severe)*, you need to be tied down in order not to hurt yourself, leaving you effectively incapacitated.

**Treatment:** Rare herbs and alchemical mixes can lessen this symptom for a day, bringing *Severe* down to *Moderate*, and *Moderate* down to standard convultions. These can be created by any with the **[[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] (Apothecary)** skill and access to the appropriate ingredients (which can cost upwards of 10 shillings or more per dose). The final medicine is [[Rules/Economy/Rare\|Rare]] and usually genuine (80%), and can be bought for around *1 GC* per dose from Apothecaries, putting it beyond the reach of most citizens of the Empire.

Home: [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Symptoms\|Symptoms]]

You don’t feel at all well. You are tired, find it hard to concentrate, and just generally ill. Take a [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] Condition that you can only remove when you have recovered from your illness.

**Treatment:** Medicine treating [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Malaise\|Malaise]], costing anything from a few pence to a handful of silver, is usually genuine (75% chance); and, if so, pass a **Challenging (+0) Endurance** Test to ignore the syptom for the day.

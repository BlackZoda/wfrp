Home: [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Symptoms\|Symptoms]]

You have a wound or open sore that does not heal properly because of an infection. For each Wounded sypmtom you have, you cannot heal one of your Wounds, which stays open and sore, possibly seeping foul-smelling pus. Every day, take an **Easy (+20) Endurance** Test or gain a [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Festering Wound\|Festering Wound]] if you do not already have one.

**Treatment:** A daily successful [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] Test ensures the Wound is clean and does not require an Endurance Test to be further infected.

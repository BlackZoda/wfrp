You feel very sick and are prone to vomiting if you move around too quickly. henever you fail a Test involving physical movement, your nausea overhelms you and you vomit. You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] condition, which reppresents you being sick repeatedly or dry heaving, depending upon your current curcomstances.

**Treatment:** Remedies for *Nausea* are Common and usuall genuine (60% Chance), and typical cost around thirty pennies – if genuine, pass a **Chalanging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** Test, and the Nausea can be ignored for a number of hours equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness (T)]] Bonus.

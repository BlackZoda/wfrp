Home: [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Symptoms\|Symptoms]]

You are covered in pustules, inflamed swellings, disgucting rashes, or itchy spots, which are unlikely to be your most attractive feature. *Pox* are largely a cosmetic issue, resulting in a penalty of *-10 to Fellowship Tests*. Adiitionally, remember to roleplay and describe all the scratching; if you want to withhold from this for a shile, pass an **Average (+20) Cool** Test. When the Pox ends, attempt an **Average (+20) Cool** Test. If this fails reverse the Test dice and apply permanent scarring to that [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location Table]], showing an area where you scratched and the pox there healed badly. If the Hit Location is the head, permanently lose 1 in Fellowship.

**Treatment:** Poxes are exceedingly common, and so are remedies, which are usually creams or oils. Larger temples of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]] often keep a stock of the cream for free (though donations are expected) and apothecaries and herbalists of all kinds sell similar products for relatively low prices (rarely more than six or seven pence for a week’s worth of treatment, with a 90% chance of being genuine medicine). Using a cream increases all [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]] Tests to resist scratching to Very Easy (+60), but is usually unsightly.

Home: [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Symptoms\|Symptoms]]

A part of the body swells up to several times its normal size, turning an angry red or purple color and becoming almost unusable. The affected body part is normay the location of a wound or bite causing the Selling, or the point of contact here a disease or infection entered the body.

**Treatment:** Most treatments involve plunging the affected part, or sometimes the hole body, into an ice-water bath to reduce the heat that accompanies the swellings. An **Exteded (-20) [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]]** Test requiring **+3 SL** reduces theswelling by 2d10 hours. Each Test takes an hour. The patient is left with +1 [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] for every Test made in the process.

Some physicians instead bleed the patient ith a blade or leeches. A successful **[[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended]] [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]]** Test requiring **+4 SL** and [[Trade Tools\|Trade Tools]] ([[Rules/Career/Physician\|Physician]]) reduces the swelling by (1d10 + the patient’s [[Toughness Bonus\|Toughness Bonus]]) hours. Each Test has a base [[Rules/Tests/Difficulty\|Difficulty]] of **Impossible (-50)** and takes half an hour.

| Location | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Head     | The eyes and tongue bulge, the cheeks become livid, the jaw is forced open. Eating is impossible, but clear liquids may be sipped in small quantities. Any Test requiring speech are 3 steps more Difficult. |
| Arm      | The arm and hand swell, the shoulder and elbow joints cannot move. and the hand becomes useless. For the duration, the arm counts as [[Rules/Injuries/Amputated Parts\|Amputated]].                                         |
| Body     | The entire body swells until the sufferer can no longer ear clothes. All Tests invoving movement become 3 steps more Difficult.                                                                              |
| Leg         | The leg sells grotesquely, becoming as wide as the widest part of the thigh for its entire length. The foot is almost indistinguishabe. For the duration, the leg counts as [[Rules/Injuries/Amputated Parts\|Amputated]].                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

This [[Rules/Tests/Difficulty\|Difficulty]] is reduced by 1 step for each [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]] the patient suffers before the Test is taken. So, if a patient is bled of 5 Wounds of blood, the Test is **Challenging (+0).**

A few healers use healing poultices for swelling reduction, especially if there is a visible wound to which poultices can be applied. A successful **[[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Test]] Hard (−10) [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]]** requiring **+5 SL** and a Healing Poultice per Test reduces the swelling by (2d10 + the patient’s Toughness Bonus) hours. Each Test takes 10 minutes.

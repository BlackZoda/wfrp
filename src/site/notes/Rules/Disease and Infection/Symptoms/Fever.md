Home: [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Symptoms\|Symptoms]]

Your temperature is high, you’re likely sweating, and you really don’t look at all well. Suffer a penalty of *-10 to all Pyhsical and Fellowship Tests*. If your Fever is marked as *(Severe)*, it has completely incapacitated you, leaving you bed-ridden. Take the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] Condition, though expenditure of a Resolve point can bring consciousness for a few minutes.

**Treatment:** There are many common, often disgusting, remedies for a fever, most of which don’t work (only 10% of commercial cures are genuine). A successful [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] Test does little more than inform you how long the fever will likely last. The cures range from a few pannies to many shillings in cost. If genuine, they will remove the sumptoms of a Fever (not a severe one) if you pass a **Challenging (+0) Endurance** Test.

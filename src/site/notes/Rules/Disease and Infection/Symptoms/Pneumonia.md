This is a build-up of fluid in the lungs from other illnesses that cause inflammation, fever, and sometimes even death. Pneumonia is known collequially as *Morr’s Rattle* ([[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]]’s Skrangle), named after the chilling rattle noise made when the infected attempt to breathe deeply.

**Contraction:** See [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Common Cold\|Common Cold]]
**Incubation:** Instantaneous
**Duration:** 1d10 days
**Symptoms:** [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Coughs and Sneezes\|Coughs and Sneezes]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Fever\|Fever]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Malaise\|Malaise]]

A Character with *Pneumonia* must succeed on a **Challanging (+0) Endurance** test every day that they exert themselves throughtravel, combat, or any other physical activity. Failure causes the Fever smptoms to become Severe. Whilst the *Fever (Severe)* symptom is in effect, the same Test must be passed every day, otherwise the disease develops the [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Blight\|Blight]] smptom as well.

Purple Brain Fever is a feared disease. It causes the head to swell to appalling size, and is accompanied by flusing fevers and delirium. The face turns a frightful plum color, the eyes bulge from their sockets, and the tongue swells until the mouth can no longer contain it. Death usuall occurs within a week.

**Contraction:** If you fail an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]] Test** when physical contact is made with an infected individual (at a rate of 1 Test per hour of exposure).
**Incubation:** 1d10 hours.
**Duration:** 1d10 days.

It is said Halfling food won’t give you *Galloping Trots*, a messy affliction all too common in the Empire given just how little care is fiven to the proper preparation of food, but those suffering “Rumster’s Revenge” from the cheap, Halfling pies on sale across Altdorf would disagree, assuming they could escape the privy for long enough…

**Contraction:** If you fail an **Easy (+40) Thoughness** Test after an infected wource enters the mouth.
**Incubation:** 1d10 hours
**Duration:** 1d10 days
**Symptoms:** [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Flux\|Flux]] (Moderate), [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Malaise\|Malaise]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Nausea\|Nausea]]

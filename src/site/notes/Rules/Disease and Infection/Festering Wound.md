Infected cuts and abrasions are commonplace, meaning many superstitions exist explaining best how to treat them. Poutices fashioned from wrapped leaves and dung, the skin of toads, and dove-feathers are common, as is rubbing the wound in [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmar]]’s Good Soil. Most physicians rubbish such talk, and prefer more scientific curatives, such as mixing the gall of a black ox with three spoonfuls of the patient’s urine and half a spoon of sea salt, which is then rubbed into the festering, pus-filled wound. The screams this elicits prove just how effective the remedy is.

**Contraction:** If you fail an **Easy (+40) Endurance Test** after a combat with a creature with the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Infected\|Infected]] trait (see [[Beastiary\|Beastiary]]). You can also develop a *Festering Wound* from a [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Minor Infection\|Minor Infection]].
**Incubation:** 1d10 days, or instantly if developed from another symptom.
**Duration:** 1d10 days
**Symptoms:** [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Fever\|Fever]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Lingering\|Lingering]] (Challenging), [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Malaise\|Malaise]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Wounded\|Wounded]]

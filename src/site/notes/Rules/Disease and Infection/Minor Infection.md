Minor infections – slow-healing wounds with a modicum of swelling and heat – are extremely common. Most heal of their own accord in time, so few worry about them until it’s too late, and Morr’s Portal opens.

**Contraction:** If you fail a **Very Easy (60) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** Test after a combat where ou suffered a Critical Wound.
**Incubination:** 1d10 days
**Duration:** 1d10 days
**Symptoms:** [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Lingering\|Lingering]] (Easy), [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Malaise\|Malaise]], [[Rules/Disease and Infection/Symptoms/Wounded\|Wounded]]

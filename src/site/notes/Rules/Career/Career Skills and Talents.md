Home: [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]]

You begin at the first [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] level listed in your [[Career Path\|Career Path]]. There are 8 [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s and 4 [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s listed with that level, and you can choose wich of these you are most proficient at. Allocate 40 [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] to your eight starting [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s, with no more than 10 [[Advance\|Advance]]s allocated to any single [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] at this stage. This is enough for you to add 5 [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] to every Career Skill if you wish, which is one of the required steps to complete your [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]]if you wish to [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Changing Career\|move to a new one]].

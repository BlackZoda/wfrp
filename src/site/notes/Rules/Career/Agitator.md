Agitators lobby for political causes using print, protest, and [[Rules/Skills/Public Speaking\|Public Speaking]]. They muster the down-trodden populace’s sympathy and support but must be wary of drawing the attention of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Sigmar\|Sigmarites]] or [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]]s interested in maintaining established traditions. The most dangerous Agitators have destabilised the rule of nobles, towns, and even entire provinces. Pamphleteers nail signs to billboards, or distribute them in market squares, though often those they seek to reach cannot read.

Religious Agitators can make a good living as street preachers, receiving donations from pious devotees and attracting flagellants and zealots as followers. Agitators who survive long enough to become Demagogues are often supported by powerful, hidden allies pursuing change for their own motives.

> *‘ALTDORF FOR ALTDORFERS! MIDDENLANDERS OUT!’* – Pamphlet, Street of a Hundred Taverns, Altdorf

> *‘Mark my words, if you’re looking to root out the agents of the Spinner of Fate, follow the clamouring in the streets. They can’t resist it. They’ll surface, sooner or later.’* – Adrian Hoven, Cleric-Captain, Knights of the Fiery Heart

Agitators often move around, in search of larger crowds or fleeing the authorities. They may become leaders of motley groups of the restless and disaffected, exhorting — or even extorting — them to fight for a greater cause.

[[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] has a legendary reputation for deep fogs and riots. Perhaps this happens because Altdorf is the Imperial Capital, or maybe its because the [[Grey College\|Grey College]] draws swathes of treacherous [[Ulgu\|Ulgu]], the [[Grey Wind of Magic\|Grey Wind of Magic]], to the city. Whatever the case, it seems that whenever the fog draws in from the [[World/The Empire/Marshes/Altdorf Flats\|Altdorf Flats]], mobs rise in the streets, and woe betide any watchmen who try to silence their right to be heard.

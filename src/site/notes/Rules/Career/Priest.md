Priests tend to congregations of the faithful throughout [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. While many are assigned to a specific temple, others choose a wandering life to reach worshippers who cannot, or will not, attend temple. They are expected to exemplify the beliefs of their religion – which vary greatly depending on which deity they serve. [[Rules/Career/High Priest (level 3)\|High Priests]] are responsible for a temple and all its [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|Cult]] and lay members. Alongside [[Rules/Career/Lector (level 4)\|Lectors]], they will often be called upon to advise the ruling classes, with many active in local politics. [[Rules/Career/Priest (level 2)\|Priests]] have many duties connected to their God, such as Priests of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]]’s responsibility to consecrate new ships, or a [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]’s duty to tend to the sick and wounded, so they touch on most aspects of life in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. For more on religion and the different [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Orders\|Orders]], see [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|The Gods]].

> *For sound advice, I seek a Priest of Verena. For everything else, I seek a Priest of Ranald* – Wermer Losch, [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchant]]

> *The Shallyan, just a girl she was, stroked my little Anton’s forehead and whispered, and the screaming stopped. He smiled at me for the first time in days. I will never forget it. Oh, aye, he died not long after, but not in pain. Not in pain.* – Sabine Schmidt, Fishmonger

> *Listen, there is nothing to fear. Hexensnacht comes every year. We need only call on the Lord of Death to watch over us. So, come the midnight hour, we cry MORR! MORR! MORR!* – Father Wilhelm Abgott, Priest of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]]

Some temple-bound Priests seek distractions to justify sojourns away. Disturbing matters heard from their congregations can send them on a quest for answers. Some High Priests find their administrative duties so far removed from the life they envisaged when they joined the Cult that they take extended pilgramages away from their temple.

Home: [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]]

* **Name:** The name of the [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]].
* **Limitations:** A list of the different [[Rules/Species/Species\|Species]] typically using the [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]].
* **Summary:** A one-sentence introduction to the [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]].
* **Description:** A paragraph or two describing the career, and alternative ways it may be used.
* **Advance Scheme:** The Career’s Advance Scheme.
* **Career Path:** Name, Status, Skills, Talents, and Trappings for each of the Career’s four levels. *Note:* if a Talent or Skill is marked ‘(Any)’ it means you can choose one of the options for that [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] or [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] when in that [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]]; So, [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] ([[Rules/Keywords/Any\|Any]]), could be *Lore (Geography)*, *Lore (Folklore)*, *Lore (Magick)*, or any other similar example. Likewise, if one is marked ([[Rules/Keywords/Local\|Local]]), it means you can choose one of the options for the [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] or [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] drawn from the local area; So, [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] ([[Rules/Keywords/Local\|Local]]), could be *[[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (Altdorf)*, *[[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (Riverways)*, *[[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (The Wasteland)*, or any other similar example.
* **Quotations:** A few in-game quotations about the [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]].
* **Adventuring:** Some hints and tips about how those in the [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] might find themselves adventuring.

Home: [[Rules/Trappings/Trappings\|Trappings]]

Every [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] has a list of [[Rules/Trappings/Trappings\|Trappings]] for each of its four evels. You begin play with all the Trappings listed in the first level of your [[Career Path\|Career Path]].

You character’s [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]] which is listed beside your [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] level’s name [[Rules/Tables/Starting Wealth Table\|determine your initial wealth]].

This money can be used to purchase more [[Rules/Trappings/Trappings\|Trappings]] or saved for spending during play. The relative worth of the coins is covered in the [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]].

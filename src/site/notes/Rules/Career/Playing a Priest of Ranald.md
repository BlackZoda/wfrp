# Playing a Priest of Ranald
If a Player wants to play a [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]] of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]], they can select an aspect of the god that best reflects the character they want to play. A player who generates a Priest as a starting career could choose to be a [[Rules/Career/Thief-Priest\|Thief-Priest]] (worshipping [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald/Ranald the Night Prowler\|Ranald the Night Prowler]]), a [[Rules/Career/Gambler-Priest\|Gambler-Priest]] (worshipping [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald/Ranald the Gamster\|Ranald the Gamster]]), a [[Trickster-Priest\|Trickster-Priest]] (worshipping [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald/Ranald the Deceiver\|Ranald the Deceiver]]), or a [[Liberator-Priest\|Liberator-Priest]] (worshipping [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald/Ranald the Protector\|Ranald the Protector]]).

The [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]] [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] in **Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay**, page 58, could represent a priest of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] who embodies all aspects of the god. If they prefer this route, give thim the following choice of [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skills]] at Initiate level:

**Skills:** [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]], [[Rules/Skills/Bribery\|Bribery]], [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]], [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]], [[Rules/Skills/Gamble\|Gamble]], [[Rules/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]], [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] ([[Theology\|Theology]]), [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]], *[[Rules/Skills/Pray\|Pray]]*, [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]] ([[Urban\|Urban]])

All priest variants can be shaped to suit the wandering life of an adventurer or a compaign set in a fixed location. Some of these priests reach a point in their spiritual journey where they feel drawn to a specific aspect. This can only happen at the [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]] level or higher, and they can only move to the [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]] level int heir specific aspect career, dropping down in seniority if necessary. They retain any [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]] they know, even if their new aspect cannot usually learn them.
## Blessings
Regardless of which aspect a priest of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] follows, they all have access to the same [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessings\|Blessings]] as general priests of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] (**WFRP**, page 220):
* [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessing of Charisma\|Blessing of Charisma]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessing of Conscience\|Blessing of Conscience]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessing of Finesse\|Blessing of Finesse]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessing of Fortune\|Blessing of Fortune]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessing of Protection\|Blessing of Protection]], [[World/Religion and Belief/Blessings/Blessing of Wit\|Blessing of Wit]]
## Miracles
[[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priests]] of a specific aspect of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] have their own selections of [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]]. If a Character is using the [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]] [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] form the **WFRP Core Rulebook**, they can use XP to buy the following [[World/Religion and Belief/Miracles/Miracles\|Miracles]] from this book.

* [[Talk Your Way Out\|Talk Your Way Out]]

## Ranald’s Priests in an Adventuring Party
In certain tales of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]], the god is accompanied by one or more acquaintances, each a colorful character in their own right. These companions act as foils or accomplices to the trickster god’s cunning and a reminder that even a god must band together with others to achieve great things.

* **[[Vulmar\|Vulmar]]:** A drunken noble and carouser. Useful to have around but always tricked by [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] by the end of the story. Vylmar was a minor god of debauchery ho is now largely forgotten.
* **[[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Katya\|Katya]]:** Minor [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] goddess of beauty. In [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]]’s tales, Katya is a bawdy sparring partner for the trickster god.
* **[[Mummit\|Mummit]]:** A miserable acrobat and clown who can climb or hide in the tightest places. In one memorabe tale, Mummit cimbs up to the sky and gets stuck there.
* **[[Zanni\|Zanni]]:** A cunning servant who toils in the halls of the nobility and helps [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] get the better of them.
* **[[Stanley\|Stanley]]:** A [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] who is able to pick any lock but talks far too much.

Several other followers come in and out of stories as the teller needs. [[World/Other Nations/Tilea/Tilea\|Tilean]] bands of [[Rules/Career/Entertainer\|Entertainer]]s draw on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] and hisentourage for their performances, using them as stock characters familiar to all audiences. The example of these motley characters may inspire a priest of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] to join an adventuring party.

Remember a priest of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Ranald\|Ranald]] should never be *entirely* turstworthy – the other players should be kept guessing about what they’re really up to, even though they can be loyal, compassionate, and supportive to those around them. It’s important ot to overplay the untrustworthiness of a Ranaldan priest, for the enjoyment of everyone in the game. Try not to steal from other Characters and keep the annying riddles in check…

By the command of [[Emperor Magnus the Pious\|Emperor Magnus the Pious]] after the [[World/The Empire/History/Great War Against Chaos\|Great War Against Chaos]], all provinces of [[World/The Empire/Imperiet\|Imperiet]] had to maintain a standing [[State Army\|State Army]]. Soldiers are the mainstay of these armies, trained to fight as part of a larger group with individual skill supplemented by strength in numbers. Rarely encouraged to think for themselves, Soldiers are famous for their stoic fatalism as they are ordered from pillar to post in the service of their betters. Soldiers could be [[Archer\|Archer]]s, [[Crossbowman\|Crossbowman]], [[Halberdier\|Halberdier]]s, [[Handgunner\|Handgunner]]s, [[Swordsman\|Swordsman]], or [[Spearman\|Spearman]], and that’s just in a typical [[State Regiment\|State Regiment]]. [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]]s employ soldiers like [[Hammerer\|Hammerer]]s and [[Thunderer\|Thunderer]]s, while [[World/Races/Elf\|Elven]] rank and file are usually archers and spearmen. There are many other Soldiers, such as [[Mercenaries\|Mercenaries]], local [[Militias\|Militias]] (which are rarely better than Recruits), [[Private Armies\|Private Armies]], cult forces, and more.

> *‘Go down to the bottom of the hill, the captain told us. So we did, and the general told us to go up to the top of the hill and await further orders. Then the captain told us we were wanted at the bottom.’* – Holger Kass, 1st Bögenhafen Halberdiers

> *‘Though Lords and Ladies come and go, A soldier’s life is all I know, Karl-Franz commands, and we obey, O’er the hills and far away.’* – Marching Song, Reikland 118th Regiment of Foot, ‘The Greenbacks’

Soldiers have little free time, but they still have adventuring opportunities. Outside campaigning season, many receive extended periods of leave. Officers of the State Armies can also command small bands of Soldiers to investigate unusual happenings in their regiment’s ‘territory’ and some officers view this kind of adventuring as excellent training to keep their Soldiers sharp. Non-human Soldiers will often be on missions in the Empire that are adventures by their very nature.

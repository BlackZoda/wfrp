Seamen sail the high seas in the [[Imperial Navy\|Imperial Navy]] or for one of the [[Merchant House\|Merchant House]]s. The [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] may have no coast, but the River [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] from the [[World/Other Nations/Wasteland/Wasteland\|Wasteland]] to [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] is miles wide and full of ocean-going vessels. The [[Imperial First Fleet\|Imperial First Fleet]] that patrols these waters rarely sees the open sea, because the taxes to pass warships through [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]] are exorbitantly high.

Seamen can always find work, whether as cabin staff on an [[Imperial Battleship\|Imperial Battleship]] or on the crew of a small trading sloop. Some Seamen travel the world by working to pay their passage. The [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]] also houses many ‘Missions’, buildings provided by the [[Imperial Navy\|Imperial Navy]] for their staff, many of which employ Seamen.

> *‘I can see the Sea!’* – Marian Zelman, Optimistic Reiklander Sailor

> *’Yeah, I’ve sailed through Marienburg. Bloody Wastelanders made it as difficult as possible for us to get out to sea. I swear the huffer took us three times round the islands just for fun before we even smelled salt water. And they taxed us twice for everything. Even the ship’s cat got taxed. Glad to be home, to be honest.’* – Thom Wesserbrug, Boatswain

Beyond [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]], the whole world awaits a Seaman: the perilous [[Sea of Claws\|Sea of Claws]], the unfathomable [[Great Ocean\|Great Ocean]], the exotic [[Thousand Islands\|Thousand Islands]], the [[Southern Sea\|Southern Sea]], the [[Black Gulf\|Black Gulf]]. And at home, a Seaman can spend a whole life plying the [[World/The Empire/Rivers and Canals/Reik\|Reik]]’s waters and never have the same day twice.

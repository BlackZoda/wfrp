The best way to keep something safe is to post a guard. Guarding looks easy enough, it’s usually standing around doing very little. Guards live and die, quite literally, by how they behave during that one moment when they are required to spring into action. The best can command high wages and are entrusted with the lives of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]’s finest personages and most valuable items. Guards are everywhere, from the [[Imperial Palace\|Imperial Palace]] down to bouncers who stand outside taverns all night, ready to throw drunkards into the street. They also include [[Grave Wardens\|Grave Wardens]] — those who defend [[Morr’s Garden\|Morr’s Garden]]s in the dead of night, watchful for [[Rules/Career/Grave Robber\|Grave Robber]]s — and [[Temple Guard\|Temple Guard]]s who defend holy sites and important [[Rules/Career/Priest\|Priest]]s. [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchant]]s often employ many Guards to defend their valuable stock. Some claim bodyguards have it best because they stay close to their esteemed employers, and often partake of a life far beyond that which their station would normally allow.

> *‘I stood outside the shrine for thirty days and thirty nights, without fail. No-one got in and no-one got out. Of course, it turned out I was stood outside the wrong door.’* – Ernst Bluchard, Ex-Temple Guard of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Manann\|Manann]]

> *‘If you’re not on the list, you’re not coming in!’* – Anonymous [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] guard to the [[World/Religion and Belief/Titles/Grand Theogonist\|Grand Theogonist]] at the coronation of [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]], apocryphal

Guards can find adventure when their guardianship is compromised. Any guard worth his salt would wish to follow up and investigate those who have thwarted them, and get whatever they were guarding back to its true owner. This could easily turn into an exciting adventure. Many of those going on expeditions will require guards to accompany them, to adventure, profit or an untimely demise.

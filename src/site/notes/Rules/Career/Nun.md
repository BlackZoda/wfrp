Nuns are members of religious orders, normally cloistered within an abbey, convent, or monestary. Most rise before the sun for morning prayer before toiling in fields, tending the sick, or preserving important manuscripts. Vows of pilgrimage case some to travel [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], while others take oaths to serve the community, moving amongst the people, tending to their spiritual needs. Devoted hermits and tenders to shrines are also thought of as “Nuns” or “Monks” by the folk of the Empire. Man Nuns learn valuable trades such as vintners, brewers or calligraphers. [[Abbesses (level 3)\|Abbesses (level 3)]] use thse activities to attract donations and patronage from the local nobility. Leaders of particularly large or martial [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Orders\|Orders]] can gather significant influence both within their own cut and with the ruling classes of a province. For more on [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|The Gods]], [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|The Cults]] and the different [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Orders\|Orders]] see [[World/Religion and Belief/Religion and Belief\|Religion and Belief]].

When a religious order discovers terrible secrets or fragments of prophecy, its leaders may feel they must act, sending brothers and sisters abroad. [[Abbey\|Abbey]]s along pilgrimage routes will often also send their members abroad to guard the many pilgrims traversing the lengthy holy [[Road\|Road]]s across [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. And there are always itinerant [[Friar\|Friar]]s wantering the world, risking adventure with every new land they enter.

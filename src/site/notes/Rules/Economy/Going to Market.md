Once you start play, the availability of rarer goods is restricted. After all, it’s hard to find a [[harpsichord\|harpsichord]] in a five-hose hamlet, bt not so hard in the shops and markets of imperial capital, [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]].

The following rules for buying and selling are all optional. The GM may prefer to roleplay every visit to each market, hawker, or shop, meaning Tests for Abailability are unrequired as the GM simply states what is or isn’t purchasable. Equally, the GM may prefer you to simply check the trapping list, roll for Availability and if the Test succeeds, spend the coin on whatever you want. Possibly without even [[Rules/Skills/Haggle\|Haggle]]ing!

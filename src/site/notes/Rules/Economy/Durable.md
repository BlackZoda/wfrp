Home: [[Rules/Economy/Item Quality\|Item Quality]]

Laboriously crafted using strong materials, the item can take +Durable Damage points before it suffers any negatives (see Weapon Damage and Armour Damage on pages 296 and 299) and gains a saving throw of 9+ on a 1d10 roll against instant breakage from sources like Trap Blade (see page 298). This Quality can be taken multiple times. Each time it is taken, the saving throw improves by 1 (e.g. From 9+ to 8+).

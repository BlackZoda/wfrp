Home: [[Rules/Economy/Item Flaw\|Item Flaw]]

An awkward design crafted clumsily. Increase Encumbrance by +1 (small trinkets cannot normally have this flaw). Bulky clothing and armour are Enc 1 even when worn, and Fatigue penalties for armour are doubled.

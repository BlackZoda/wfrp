The items available in the shops, stalls, and merchant halls of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] vary significantly in quality. Not everything is made equal, and the Craftmanship rules reflect this using [[Rules/Economy/Item Quality\|Item Quality]], and [[Rules/Economy/Item Flaw\|Item Flaw]].

An [[Rules/Economy/Item Quality\|Item Quality]] makes the trapping a little better, but harder to find. An [[Rules/Economy/Item Flaw\|Item Flaw]] makes the trapping a little worse, but easier to find.

The diversity of coinage circulating [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] means counterfeiting is rife. Reiklanders may be accustomed to seeing coins from other provinces, but are always wary of being scammed. A successful [[Rules/Skills/Evaluate\|Evaluate]] Test will detect counterfeit coins, typically by inspecting coins for weight and hardness.

Actually producing couterfeit coins is much harder. It requires an **[[Rules/Skills/Art\|Art]] (Engraving)** Test to carve convincing stamping die, then a **[[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] (Blacksmith)** Test to strike the coins, usually with the aid of an assistant. Including a higher precious metal content makes [[Rules/Skills/Evaluate\|Evaluate]] Tests to detect counterfeits more difficult; using less than a fifth maes it much easier to spot the fake coins.

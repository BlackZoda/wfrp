All [[Rules/Talents/Trapping\|Trapping]]s have an Availability, either *Common*, *Scarce*, *Rare*, or *Exotic*.

*Common* items are found in almost every corner of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] and are always assumed to be readily available.

*Scarce* and *Rare* items are less common, and you need to pass an *Availability Test* to find any locally in stock; the chance of passing the Test depends upon the settlement size.

*Exotic* items are super rare, and only available if the GM says so, or if ou comission the item from an appropriate artisan (perhaps with a [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Commission\|Commission]] Endeavour, page 197 WFRP), or make it yourself (perhaps [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Crafting\|Crafting]] Endeavour).

If you fail an Availability Test for Scarce or Rare items, you can either reroll when you arrive at t new settlement or you can try again next week if the settlement is Town-size or larger.

If an item is in stock, it’s up to the GM to determine how many are available. As a general rule-of-thumb, villages have a single item in stock, towns have 1d10 items, and cities has as many as the GM deems appropriate. These quantities are generally doubled or Common items, and halved for rare ones.

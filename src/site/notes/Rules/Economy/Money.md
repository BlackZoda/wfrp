Before you can buy new trappings, you first need to understand how money works in [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]].

The Empire’s cons are most commony minted in three denominations: *Brass Pennies (d)* (*Kopperpenny*), *Silver Shillings (/)* (*Søvshilling*), and *Gold Crowns (GC)* (*Gullkroner*). Coins usually weigh around an ounce and, as their inherent value is determined by weight, even foreign coins can be easily valued with a set of [[Scale\|Scale]]s, though will likely draw a suspicious eye.

> [!Info] Coin Values
> 1 Gold Crown (GC) = 20 Silver Shillings (20/–) = 240 Brass Pennies (240d)
> 
> 1 Silver Shilling (1/–) = 12 Brass Pennies (12d)
> 
> This is usually abbreviated to:
> **1GC = 20/– = 240d**
> 
> **6/8** is equal to **80d** or one third of a GC, and is commonly refered to as a *noble*.
> 
> **1d = 15 NOK**
> **1/– = 180 NOK**
> **1GC = 3600 NOK**

>[!Info] Money Slang
>**Gold Crown:** Marks, Karls, Gelt
>**Silver Shillings:** Bob, Shimmies, Mucks
>**Brass Pennies:** Pfennig, Clanks, Shrapnel

>[!Info] The [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Wissenland/Nuln\|Nuln]] Standard
>[[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] may be the current capital of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] and [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], but coinage standards are established in the city-state of [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Wissenland/Nuln\|Nuln]] to the south. Historically, Nuln was the Empire’s capital until [[House Holswig-Schliestein\|House Holswig-Schliestein]] restored the throne to Altdorf a century ago, and many Imperial institutions still call that city home. The Nuln Standard only governs coin weight and metallurgy, not stamp imagery, which varies signigicantly accross the [[World/The Empire/Politics/Entities/Province\|Province]]s.

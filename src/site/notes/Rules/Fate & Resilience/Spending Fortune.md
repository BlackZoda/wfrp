Home: [[Rules/Fate & Resilience/Fate & Fortune\|Fate & Fortune]]

You may spend a [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] point from your pool to turn luck to your advantage: hitting with that difficult crossbowshot when you would have otherwise missed; or perhaps an opponent slips, granting you a chance to land a blow. Your three options are:
* Reroll a failed [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]
* Add +1 SL to a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] after it is rolled.
* At the start of the [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], choose when to act in that [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] disregarding [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] order.

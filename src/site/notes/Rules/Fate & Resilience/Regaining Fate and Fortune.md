Home: [[Rules/Fate & Resilience/Regaining Fate and Fortune\|Regaining Fate and Fortune]]

You regain all [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] points at the start of every gaming session, up to the maximum of your current [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]]. In addition, certain in-game encounters may also replenish (or remove!) Fortune points.

You GM may grant ou a [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] point for an act of extreme heroism, bravery, or significance. Normally this only happen at the successful end of an important adventure, so make sure to spend them carefully as they rarely replenish.

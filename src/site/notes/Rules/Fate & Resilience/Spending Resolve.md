Home: [[Rules/Fate & Resilience/Resilience & Resolve\|Resilience & Resolve]]

You may spend [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]] point to draw upon your inner reserves: maybe confronting a terrifying [[World/Races/Ogre\|Ogre]] without flinching; or ignoring the effects of even the most powerful blows. Your choices are:
* Become immune to [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]] until the end of the next round.
* Ignore all modifiers from all [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]] until the beginning of next round.
* Remove one [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]; if you removed the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] Condition, regain 1 Wound as you surge to your feet.

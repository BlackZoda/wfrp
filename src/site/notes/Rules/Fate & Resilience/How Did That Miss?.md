Home: [[Rules/Fate & Resilience/Spending Fate\|Spending Fate]]

Your character comletely avoids the incoming damage by some extraordinary fluke, such as slipping just as a blow is about to connect, a weapon mysteriously jamming, or an unexpected source of light blinding an opponent; your character can continue on without penalty, but has no guarantee of survival in later rounds.

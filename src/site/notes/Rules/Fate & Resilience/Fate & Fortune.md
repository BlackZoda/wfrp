Player characters have destiny. While their ultimate future is a mstery and there is no guarantee it will be glorious, heroic, or even pleasant, they are seemingly fated for something important.

To represent this, you begin play with a number of [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] Points. Fate points mark you out from the rest of the denizens of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. They allow you to survive against impossible odds and prevail where ordinary folk fail.

[[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] is diretly related to your [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] points. Fortune points are spent to receive minor bonuses, includeing the baility to reroll failed [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or gain an edge as luck favours ou, and the number you have will likely fluctuate widly during play. [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] determines how many [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]] points you can have, and can be spent in the direst of circumstances to avoid death.

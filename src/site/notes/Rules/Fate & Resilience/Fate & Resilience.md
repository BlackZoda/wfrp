Whether it’s guts, luck or the favour of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]], there is something special about you. [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] and [[Rules/Attributes/Resilience\|Resilience]] represent the different ways you stand out from the masses.

Characters gain their starting Fate and Resilience in [[0_GM/Fleeting/Character Creation\|Character Creation]]. Both are related to pools of points: [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] to [[Rules/Attributes/Fortune\|Fortune]], [[Rules/Attributes/Resilience\|Resilience]] to [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]]. You may spend your points to achieve small benefits, and these pools will refill over the course of play. You spend your Fate or Resilience points for a significant benefit during play, but this permanently reduces them and also reduces the associated pool of Fortune or Resolve points. While Fate and Resilience points may be regained, this occurs only rarely, so spend them carefully.

Although they are very rare and normally reserved for PCs, the GM may also want to apply [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] and [[Rules/Attributes/Resilience\|Resilience]] to noteworthy NPCs, like a necromantic nemesis, a local luminary of significance, or a recurring [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/The Cults\|Cult]] leader.

[[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]] is regained whenever you act according to your [[Rules/Attributes/Motivation\|Motivation]]. During play, whenever you feel you have done this, you may ask the GM if ou can recover one or more [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]] points.

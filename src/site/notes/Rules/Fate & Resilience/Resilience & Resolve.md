While [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] points represent your destiny, perhaps chosen by some distant uncaring deity. [[Rules/Attributes/Resilience\|Resilience]] is an indication of your personal drive and determination to endure, and overcome, no matter the obstacles you face.

Like [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]], [[Rules/Attributes/Resilience\|Resilience]] is directly linked to a pool of points, this time called [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]]. Resolve points are spent to push through minor obstacles, such as ignoring the negative effects of critical wounds for a [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] or removing [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]].

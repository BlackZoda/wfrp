Home: [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]]

A military “language” spoken by warriors of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]]. It is very common amongst soldiers and mercenaries and is used for giving orders swiftly during battle. It was supposedly developed by the goddess [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Myrmidia\|Myrmidia]] when she walked [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] as a mortal.

Battle Tongue represents a series of simple commands and gestures that may be made in the heat of combat. Players with **[[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] (Battle Tongue)** may communicate briefly with one another during combat without penalty. Those without the Skill cannot quickly coordinate their attacks or discuss strategy once combat begins.

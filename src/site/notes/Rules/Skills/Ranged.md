Home: [[Rules/Skills/Skill List\|Skill List]], [[Rules/Combat/Ranged Combat\|Ranged Combat]]
**(BS) advanced, grouped**

Whilst anone can throw a rock simply using their Ballistic Skill, it takes training and practice to use weapons like [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Ranged Weapon/Bow\|Bows]] and [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Ranged Weapon/Pistol (Weapon)\|Pistols]]. Each Ranged Specialisation is specific to a group of ranged weapons. If you don’t have the Ranged Specialisation for a weapon you wish to use, refer to [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]] to see what penalties you will suffer when using the weapon.

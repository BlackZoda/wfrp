**(Int) basic**

Allows you to measure the likelihood that a bet will pay off, as well as successfully engage in various games of chance.

To represnt a gambling match, all players make a Gamble Test – applying appropriate modifiers for familiarity with the game – and the player with the highest SL wins. On a tie, any lower scoring players drop out, and those remaining enact another Gamble Test, repeat this process until you have a winner.

If you wish to influence the game through less honest mechanics, see [[Rules/Skills/Sleight of Hand\|Sleight of Hand]].

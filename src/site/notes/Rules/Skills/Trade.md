**(Dex) advanced, grouped**

Most folk of the [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Reikland\|Reikland]] follow a trade; even adventurers often have a more reliable or respectable career to fall back on, between bouts of hair-raising, bowel-loosening excitement.

The Trade Skill represents your ability to create something or provide a service, as well as your knowledge of the relevant [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] surrounding your trade.

Having the Skill is enough to automatically perform the tasks associated with your trade, assuming you have the correct resources and [[Trade Tools\|Trade Tools]]. ([[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]]). You need only [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] your Trade [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] if you are seeking to create something quickly, conditions are adverse, or you are seeking to invent or create a high-quality item.

Often Trade Tests of this type are [[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Test]], with the SL and time required depending upon the scope or scale of what is being produced; a quick meal with [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] ([[Cook\|Cook]]) to impress a local lord will take far les time than constructing a warship with [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] ([[Shipwright\|Shipwright]]).

You may also make a Trade Test as a [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] Skill, to determine information relevant to the trade in question. In such circumstances, the GM may prefer to use **Int** over **Dex** as the base Characteristic, though often this is ignored to keep play simple.

While most Trade Skills have little function in [[Rules/Combat/Combat\|Combat]], there are as many Trade Skills as there are trades, and some may be of use depending upon the circumstances. For example, a successful [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] ([[Rules/Career/Apothecary\|Apothecary]]) Test may be useful if fighting in an [[Apothecary’s Shop\|Apothecary’s Shop]] as you identify some astringent chemicals to hurl at your foes.

The Trade Skill is also used for enacting a [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Crafting\|Crafting]] Endeavour.

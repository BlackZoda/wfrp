**(WP) basic**

You aptitude for befriending, quickly calming, or subjugating animals.

Passing a Charm Animal Test allows you to influence the behaviour of one or more animals, to a maximum of *Willpower Bonus + SL*. If the target animals are naturally docile, this Test may be uncontested, but it will generally be [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed]] by the target’s [[Willpower\|Willpower]].

In combat, you may use Charm Animal when facing animals. If you succeed, any affected targets will not attack you this [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] and you gain +1 Advantage. You may do this in successive rounds until you choose to stop or fail a Charm Test, after which the creature’s instincts take over and you have no further influence.

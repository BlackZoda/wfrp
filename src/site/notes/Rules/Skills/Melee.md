The Melee Skill represents specific training with a single type of close [[Rules/Combat/Combat\|Combat]] weaponery. Each Melee Specialisation indicates training in using a specific class of weapon. If you don’t have the correct Specialisation for a weapon you wish to use, refer to [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]] for the correct weapon entry, and what penalties you will suffer.

**(Ag) basic**

Dodge is your ability to avoid things, through ducking, iving, and moving quickly, and is used extensively to sidestep falling rocks, incoming weapons, unexpected [[Trap\|Traps]], and the like.

In [[Rules/Combat/Combat\|Combat]], Dodge is generally used to resist attacks or to avoid damage.

Home: [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]]

Shadowing is following someone secretly. To do this requires a Combined [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]] and [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] (see [[Rules/Tests/Combining Skills\|Combining Skills]] page 155 WFRP). If the target is actively trying to lose a tail, it is Opposed with the target’s [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]].

If you pass the [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], and fail the [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] you follow your target, but are spotted. If you fail the [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], yet pass the [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], you lose your target, but go unnoticed.

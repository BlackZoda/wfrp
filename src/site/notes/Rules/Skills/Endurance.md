**(T) basic**

The Endurance [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] is called upon when you must endure hardship, withstand deprivation, sit without moving for long periods of time, or survive harsh environemts. In particuar, Endurance is [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tested]] to resist or recover from various [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Conditions]] and helps you recover lost [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds (W)]].

**(Dex) advanced**

Lets you pick pockets, palm objects, and perform minor tricks of prestigination, as well as cheating with games of chance. This [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] is typically opposed by the [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]] Skill of your target; success means you have palmed the object, picked the pocket, or swapped the cards, while a *Marginal Success (+0 to +1)* may suggest that your nefarious misdeeds have left your opponent suspicious.

You can also use Sleight of Hand to ‘help’ your [[Rules/Skills/Gamble\|Gamble]] Skill when playing appropriate games of chance. Before any round (or similar, depending upon the game at hand) you can attempt a Sleight of Hand [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] (which will be opposed if others suspect). If successful, you may reverse your Gamble Test for the round if this will score a Success. If failed, your opponents may not be best pleased…

Sleight of Hand and [[Rules/Combat/Combat\|Combat]] rarely mix, though inventive players may be able to conjure an impressive distraction with GM approval, perhaps even gaining [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] by making a Dagger seemingly appear from nowhere, surprising a superstitious foe.

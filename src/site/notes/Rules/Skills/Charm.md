**(Fel) basic**

Charm makes people think favourably of you, your opinions, and proposed actions. Passing an **Opposed Charm/[[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]]** Test allows you to influence the behaviour of one or more targets, up to a maximum number equal to your [[Fellowship\|Fellowship]] Bonus + SL, affecting those with the lowest [[Willpower\|Willpower]] first. If a target is amenable to your Charm, the [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] will be uncontested.

Your GM may permit you to use Charm in Combat if they think your foes might be susceptible to you pleading for your life or making persuasive arguments to stop the voilence (although good luck charming a [[Goblin\|Goblin]])!

If you use Charm as your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]], calculate the number of targets affected as normal. If you use it to defent, you only affect your ttacker. If you succeed, any affected targets will not attack you this round and you gain +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] as normal. ou may do this in sccessive rounds until you choose to stop or fail a Charm Test, after which your words carry no more weight.

**(Int) advanced**

Lets you determine the value of rare artefacts, unusual trade goods, and works of art. Everybody is assumed to know the relative worth of general items, but a successful use of the Evaluate allows you to identify the value of curious and unique items. A successful Evaluate Test may also alert you if the goods (or coins) you are studying are [[Counterfeit\|Counterfeit]] — this [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] will usually be Opposed by the forger’s SL on their Art or Trade Test. Your GM may apply modifiers based on just how rare or obscure the item is, or on your character’s particular expertise or background.

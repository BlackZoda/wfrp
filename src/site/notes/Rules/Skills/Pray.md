**(Fel) advanced**

Your ability to invoke, or otherwise commune with, a deity. For more information on using the Pra Skill to seek divine intervention, see [[World/Religion and Belief/Religion and Belief\|Religion and Belief]].

In combat, if your GM deems it appropriate considering your circumstances and faith, you may use Pray to meditate and focus your mind. Each round you spend praying in combat – and making a successful Pray Test – gives you +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]. You can gain additional Advantage this way, up to a maximum of your [[Fellowship\|Fellowship]] Bonus.

Further, if your enemies understand your language and recognise (and fear) your deity, the GM may allow you to use Pray in lieu of the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidate\|Intimidate]] Skill.

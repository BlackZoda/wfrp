**(S) advanced**

Your ability to swim in water without drowning. If you have the Swim [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]], you are automatically presumed to be able to swim freely without a Test. But if ou find yourself in difficult currents, racing, or fleeing from an oversized shark sent by [[Stromfels\|Stromfels]], the [[God of Drowning\|God of Drowning]], a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] will be required. This may be modified by the condition of the water, or how encumbered you are by clothes, armour, and other trappings.

Swim is onl used in combat if you happen to be fighting in the water, where it replaces [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skills]] like [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] to govern [[Rules/Attributes/Movement (M)\|Movement (M)]]. If exact speeds are required, you swim at half your [[Rules/Tables/Movement Table\|Movement rate]], using the normal rules for [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]].

**(WP) advanced, grouped**

The Channeling Skill measures your ability to call upon and control the various [[Winds of Magic\|Winds of Magic]], and is solely used by the [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]] rules. Channeling is a special skill in that it is both Grouped, allowing for [[Rules/Skills/Grouped Skills and Specialisation\|Specialisations]], and also ungrouped, for those not properly trained to channel magic.

**Specialisations:**
* [[Aqshy\|Aqshy]]
* [[World/Magic/Azyr\|Azyr]]
* [[Chamon\|Chamon]]
* [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]]
* [[Ghur\|Ghur]]
* [[Ghyran\|Ghyran]]
* [[Hysh\|Hysh]]
* [[Shyish\|Shyish]]
* [[Ulgu\|Ulgu]]

**(T) basic**

Your ability to handle alcohol without letting it cloud your judgement or reder you senseless.

After each alcoholic drink make a Consume Alcohol Test, modified by the strength of the dring. For each Test you fail you suffer a -10 penalty to WS, BS, Ag, Dex, and Int, to a maximum of -30 per Characteristic. After you fail a number of Tests equal to your Thoughness Bonus, you are [[Rules/Tables/Stinking Drunk Table\|Stinking Drunk Table]].

After not drinking for an hour enact a **Challenging (+0) Consume Alcohol** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. The effects of being drunk will wear off after 10-SL hours, with any Characteristic modifiers for being drunk lost over that time. After all effects wear off, enact another **Chalending (+0) Consume Alcohol** Test. You now gain a hangover, which is a [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] Condition that cannot be removed for 5-SL hours.

You may expand 1 [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]] point to ignore negative modifiers of being drunk until the end of net round.

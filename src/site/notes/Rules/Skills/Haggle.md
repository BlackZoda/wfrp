**(Fel) basic**

Haggle allows you to secure better deals when negotiating with others. In general, *Haggle* is used to see whether you do, or do not, make a good deal, most commonly with an **Opposed Haggle** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. Specifically, it can be used when shopping to secure better prices – see [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]].

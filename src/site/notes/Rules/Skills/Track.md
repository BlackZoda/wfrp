**(I) advanced**

Your ability to follow subtle trails left by others. Track is used to follow difficult trails accross the wilderness. This is not a skill for following a set of footprints in the snow – a simple [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]] test covers that – Track involves deeper nowledge and awareness used to recognise the subtle signs of a quarry’s passage. You can also attempt to hide your trail, in shich case use your Track [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] to oppose your pursuer’s [[Rules/Skills/Track\|Track]] Test.

Often an [[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Track Test]] is required to follow a trail, with the [[Rules/Tests/Difficulty\|Difficulty]] modified by how fres the tracks are, and how suitable the ground is : damp earth betrays passage better than stony ground. The GM may also use the Pursuit rules to determine if you manage to track down a fleeing quarry – see [[Rules/Combat/Pursuit\|Pursuit]].

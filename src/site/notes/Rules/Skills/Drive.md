**(Ag) basic**

Drive lets you guide vehicles – most commonly simple carts and lumbering coaches, not to mention the more “experimental” creations of the [[Imperial Engieer\|Imperial Engieer]]s – along the roads of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]] with as little incident as possible.

Under normal circumstances, if you possess the Drive [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]], there is no need to [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If conditions are less than ideal – perhaps the road is in poor condition, or the weather is terrible – a Drive Test will be required. If you do not possess the Drive Skill, you may be required to make a Test to carry out even basic manouvers. An [[Astounding Failure\|Astounding Failure]] (-6) on a Drive Test  means something bad has happened. Roll on the the [[Rules/Tables/Failed Drive Table\|Failed Drive Table]].

## Crashing
Occupants of Crashing vehicle usually suffer 2d10 Wounds modified by Thoughness Bonus and Armour Points unless the vehicle was moving slowly (as determined by the GM). Crashed vehicle must be required by someone with an appropriate Trade Skill, such as [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] ([[Carpenter\|Carpenter]]) or [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] ([[Cartwright\|Cartwright]]). Spare wheels can be installed by annyone with a Drive Test or with an appropriate Trade Test.

In combat, Drive may be used if circumstaces allow – for instance, if the party is in a coach being raided by outlaws, and you wish to ram an enemy, or outrun them ([[Rules/Combat/Pursuit\|Pursuit]]).

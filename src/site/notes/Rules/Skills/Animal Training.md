**(Int) advanced, grouped**

Animal Training represents your understanding of a particular type of [[Animal\|Animal]], and your ability to train them. A successful use of the Skill allows you to identify the Trained abilities possessed b an animal belonging to you [[Rules/Skills/Grouped Skills and Specialisation\|Specialisation]].

In combat, a successful **Opposed Animal Training/Willpower** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] allows you to intimidate a single animal belonging to your Specialisation; you cause [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] in the animal targeted until the end of your next turn. When causing Fear in this way, you may subsequently use your Animal Training Skill instead of [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] when defending against your target; with your GM’s approval you may also use Animal Training to attack your target, issuing specific commands.

**Example:** *Facing a slavering war-hound, Ilse passes an **Opposed Animal Training (Dog) roll**, in the first round of combat, causing Fear in the beast, and gaining +1 Advantage. In the next round she ‘attacks’ her target with her Animal Training Skill, beating the dog’s defence. Her GM allows her to instruct the beast to flee, which it does, its tail between its legs.*

**Specialisations:** [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Demigryph\|Demigryph]], [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Dog\|Dog]], [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Horse\|Horse]], [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Pegasus\|Pegasus]], [[World/Bestiary/Creatures/Pigeon\|Pigeon]]

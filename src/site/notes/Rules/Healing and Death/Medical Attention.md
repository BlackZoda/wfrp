Several [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]] results and conditions cannot be resolved until you receive medical attention. This includes, but is not limited to, the following:

* Successful use of the [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] skill.
* Application of a [[Bandages\|Bandages]], [[Healing Pultice\|Healing Pultice]], or similar.
* Successful use of a [[Spell\|Spell]] or [[Prayer\|Prayer]] that heals [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]].

## Surgery
If you suffer a Cirtical Wound where surgery is required to attend to it, you suffer the marked penalties until you visit an appropriately trained doctor or barber-surgeon, or somehow source a [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magical]] or divine surgery equivalent.

Further, if you suffer an amputated body part, you cannot heal 1 of the suffered Wounds until a surgeon successfully treat it with a [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] Test. For more information refer to the [[Rules/Talents/Surgery\|Surgery]] talent.

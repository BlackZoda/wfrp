If a [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] or [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] is marked *(Local)*, it means you can choose one of the options for the [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] or [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] drawn from the local area; So, *[[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (Local)*, could be *[[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (Altdorf)*, *[[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (Riverways)*, *[[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] (The Wasteland)*, or any other similar example.

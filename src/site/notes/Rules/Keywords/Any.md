If a [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] or [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] is marked “(*Any*)” it means you can choose one of the options for that [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] or [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] when in that [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]]; So, [[Rules/Skills/Lore\|Lore]] ([[Rules/Keywords/Any\|Any]]), could be *Lore (Geography)*, *Lore (Folklore)*, *Lore (Magick)*, or any other similar example.

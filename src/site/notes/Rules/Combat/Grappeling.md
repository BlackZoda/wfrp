Home: [[Rules/Combat/Unarmed Combat\|Unarmed Combat]]

Instead of inflicting damage with an unarmed attack, you can attempt to [[Grapple\|Grapple]] and  immobilise your opponent. You must declare this is your intent before rolling to hit your opponent. If you win the [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]], you and your opponent are *Grappling*, and your opponent gains the **[[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] Condition**. If you begin your turn *Grappling*, you may break the Grapple if you have a higher [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] than your opponent, and do not count as being [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] for your [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]]; otherwise, you must make an **Opposed Strength** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] for your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]]. If you win, you can do one of the following:

* Deal **SB + SL Damage** using your *Strength roll* to determine the [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location Table]] affected. You ignore any [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] as you wrench arms and pull muscles.
* Either: **1)** *Give* your opponent an **[[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] Condition**, or **2)** *Remove* an **[[Rules/Conditions/Entangled\|Entangled]] Condition** from yourself, plus *lose an extra one for each SL* by which you win.

If you lose the [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]], you can do nothing but struggle as your opponent gains **+1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]**.

Home: [[Rules/Combat/Moving During Combat\|Moving During Combat]]

If you are not [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] in combat already, you can use your [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]] to [[Charge\|Charge]]. If you Charge, your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] must be a [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to attack an opponent. If your opponent is at least your Move characterstic in yards away before you Charge, but within your Run range ([[Rules/Tables/Movement Table\|Movement Table]]), you will also gain +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] as you barrel into your opponent.

During the cut and thrust of combat, accidents, mistakes, and moments of extraordinary skill can happen in quick succession. To represent the unpredictable extremes of a life-or-death skirmish, there are [[Rules/Combat/Criticals\|Criticals]] — extraordinarily good hits — and [[Rules/Combat/Fumbles\|Fumbles]] — egregious errors.

In most circumstances, exact distances don’t matter too much. Use your Movement Characteristic as a guide to the ground you can cover, and the GM will let you know if the move will take more than one [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]]. Reaching difficult places, perhaps by climbing or jumping, will also use your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] if you need to [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] a [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] to get there (perhaps with a [[Rules/Skills/Climb\|Climb]] or [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] Test).

If you are not already fighting someone, you can also use your Move to [[Charge\|Charge]] into close combat, gaining +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] (see page 164). For more on this, and for a detailed breakdown of movement speeds, leaping distances, and more, see [[Moving\|Moving]] on page 164.

Home: [[Rules/Combat/Moving During Combat\|Moving During Combat]]

Most climb moves won’t need [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]s. Climbing rules are only required when the [[Rules/Skills/Climb\|Climb]] is difficult or if knowing exactly how long you take to ascend is important.

Climbing a ladder or similarly easy-to-climb surface does not require a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] but does slow you down. You move at half rate up or down ladders or other easily climbed surfaces. So, it would cost 4 yards of your Movement to climb a 2-yard ladder. If you want to climb more quickly, spend your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] to make an **Average (+20) Climb** Test. You will Climb an etra [[Rules/Tables/Movement Table\|Movement]] + SL yards. So a character with Movement 4 who rolled +2 SL will climb an additional 6 yards (4+2=6).

If both hands are free, you can blimb a surface with suitable handholds with a [[Rules/Skills/Climb\|Climb]] Test using your Action for the turn. You ascend or descend at a rate of 1/2 Movement + SL in yards.

Climbing difficulty is set by the GM and varies with the nature of the climbed surface. Some climbs will be beyond the ability of most characters without the [[Rules/Talents/Scale Sheer Surface\|Scale Sheer Surface]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]].

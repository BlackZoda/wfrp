There are some extra considerations that apply to ranged attacks:
* You cannot make ranged attacks whilst [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]], unless you are armed with a ranged weapon that has the [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Quaity/Pistol (Quality)\|Pistol (Quality)]] (see Chapter 11: [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]])
* If you use your [[Rules/Skills/Ranged\|Ranged]] [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] when you are [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]] with your target, the target may *Oppose* your attack with any *Melee Skill*.

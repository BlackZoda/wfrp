Home: [[Rules/Combat/Moving During Combat\|Moving During Combat]]

If you are [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]], and no lonter wish to be trading blows with your opponent, you have the following two options for leaving combat safely.

## Use Advantage
If you have more [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] than your opponents, you are in a superior position and can easily manouver yourself beyond reach. If you choose to drop your Advantage to 0, you can move away forom your opponents without penalty, perhaps choosing to [[Rules/Combat/Charging\|Charge]] a new target, run away as fast as possible, or backstep a little and fire a pistol in an opponent’s face!

## Use Dodge
If you have lower or equal [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] to your opponents or do not wish to spend you Advantage, you are pinned in place. If you wish to escape, you will need to use your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] to make an *Oppsed [[Rules/Skills/Dodge\|Dodge]]/[[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]]* [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you succeed, you gain +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], and can use your Move to go anywhere ou wish using the normal rules. If you fail, each opponent defeating you gains +1 Advantage and makes it impossible for you to escape without a blow to your back.

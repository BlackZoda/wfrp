Using the [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] and the [[Hit Location\|Hit Location]] you struck, you now see how many Wounds your opponent loses from your attack. Subtract your opponent’s [[Toughness Bonus\|Toughness Bonus]] and any [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] protecting the *Hit Location* from your *Damage*. The remaining *Damage* is suffered as [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds (W)]] by your opponent. If this is 1 or less, your opponent has shrugged off the worst of the attack and only loses *1 Wound*. Should the Wounds lost exceed your opponent’s remaining Wounds total, your opponent takes a [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]] (see page 172) and gains the [[Prone Condition\|Prone Condition]] (see page 169).

Home: [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]

If you lose an [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]] during combat, suffer any [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] (see page 167), or lose any **Wounds**, you automatically lose all *Advantage*. Further, should the combat end, all *Advantage* you’ve gained is lost. Lastly, if you have accrued no [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] for the [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], or end the *Round* outnumbered, you lose 1 point. *Advantage* can also be used to [[Disengage\|Disengage]] from combat (see page 165). There are also [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s and [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s that cause you to lose *Advantage*, or transfer *Advantage* to another character. For more on these, read **Chapter 4: Skills and Talents**.

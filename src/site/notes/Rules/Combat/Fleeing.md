Home: [[Rules/Combat/Moving During Combat\|Moving During Combat]]

If you cannot escap otherwise, you can turn your back and flee as you [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]]. Often, Fleeing is involuntary, caused by [[Rules/Psychology/Terror\|Terror]] or [[World/Magic/Magic\|Magic]].

If ou flee, your opponent immediately gains 1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]] and may attempt 1 free [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Attack]]. The free attack is an unopposed [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] using whatever weapon is currently held, using the SL scored to [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] as you normal. As you are throwing caution to the wind, your opponent gains +20 to hit you. If you are hit, your opponent gainst +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]], and you must enact a **Challenging (+0) Cool** Test; if failed, gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] condition per SL below 0. Once the free attack is concluded, you may move up to your [[Rules/Tables/Movement Table\|Run Movement]] directly away from your opponent, assuming you still can.

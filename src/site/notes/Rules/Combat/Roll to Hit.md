Home: [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Attacking]]

**Melee:** To attack, perform an **Opposed Melee** Test with your Opponent. Whoever scores the *highest SL wins*. If you win the Test, you hit your opponent and gain *+1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]*. If you lose the [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]], your opponent gains *+1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]* and your Action is finished.

**Ranged:** Roll a **Ranged Test** for the [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon\|Weapon]] you’re using. If you are successful, you hit your opponent and gain +1 [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]. If you fail, your Action is over. Your opponent doesn’t gain *Advantage* in ranged combat.

In both melee and ranged [[Rules/Combat/Combat\|Combat]], it’s possible to score an impressive hit called a [[Critical\|Critical]], or to make a mistake, called a [[Rules/Magic/Channelling/Fumbled Channeling\|Fumbled Channeling]]. See Criticals and Fumbles in the section below.

Home: [[Rules/Combat/Criticals and Fumbles\|Criticals and Fumbles]]

Some players like a heroic approach to combat, with characters able to wade throug lesser foes. If this suits your style of play, use the following rule:

If you kill a melee opponent in a single blow, you may move into the space the character occupied and attack another opponent if there is one available. You may keep doing this a number of times equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristic Bonus\|Bonus]], and may not attack the same [[Character\|Character]] more than once on the same [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]]. Some creatures are so big they can activate this rule without killing any opponents. See [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Sized\|Sized]] and [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Swarm\|Swarm]].

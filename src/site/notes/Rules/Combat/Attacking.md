One of the most common [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]]s is to attack an opponent. To make a ranged attack, your weapon must be in range (see Chapter 11: [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]] for weapon ranges) and your target visible in line-of-sight. For melee attacks, you must be adjacent your target with a weapon ready (see [[Rules/Combat/Engaged\|Engaged]]).

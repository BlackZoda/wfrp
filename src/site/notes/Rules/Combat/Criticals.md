Home: [[Rules/Combat/Criticals and Fumbles\|Criticals and Fumbles]]

Any successful [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] or [[Rules/Skills/Ranged\|Ranged]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] that also rolls a double causes a *Critical*. This means you have dealt a significant blow, and it even happens when you are the defender in an [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]].

If you score a *Critical*, your opponent receives an immediate [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]] as your weapon strikes true. See [[Rules/Tables/Critical Tables\|Critical Tables]] (page 172) for more on what this means. Beyond that, SL is calculated as normal, as is who wins any [[Opposed Tests\|Opposed Tests]].

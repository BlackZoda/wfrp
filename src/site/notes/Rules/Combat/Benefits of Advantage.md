Home: [[Rules/Combat/Advantage\|Advantage]]

Each Advantage you secure adds +10 to any appropriate combat [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] (see Psychology on page 190). Thus, if you have 5 Advantage tokens, you have an impressive +50 bonus to hit, defend, and resist the influence of others.

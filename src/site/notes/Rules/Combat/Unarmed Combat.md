Not every fight in  WFRP  involves  guns or swords. Many  conflicts  are  settled  with good,  old-fashioned  fisticuffs. A successful **[[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] (Brawling)** Test for unarmed combat is handled in the same way as any other combat Test, but you have one extra option: you may enter a Grapple!

Home: [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]]

You can leap your Movement in feet without having to make a Test. If you want to jump farther, this takes an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]]** Test if you have a run up of at least your Movement in yards; if you do not, the Test is **Challenging (+0)**. On a success, each SL adds an extra foot to the leap. If you succeeded with +0 SL, you manage an extra 6 inches with your jump.

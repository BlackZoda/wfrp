*Reverse your ‘Roll to hit’ Test result to determine a Hit Location.*

If you successfully hit, find out where — reverse the roll to hit and compare this number to the [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location Table]]. So, a roll of 23 to hit would become 32 on the table, a hit on the [[Right Arm\|Right Arm]].

Creatures with different body shapes may use different hit location tables. Refer to **Chapter 12: [[World/Bestiary/Bestiary\|Bestiary]]** for more on the different Hit Location tables.

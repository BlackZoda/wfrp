When falling, you suffer 1d10, plus 3 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] for every yard you fall. Any suffered Damage is reduced by your [[Thougness\|Thougness]] Bonus, but not your [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] you may be wearing.

If you are puposefully falling – or, if you prefer, jumping downwards – you can attempt an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to lessen the damage you may sustain. If successful, you count as having fallen 1 less yard, plus an extra yard less per SL scored.

If you reduce the distance you count as having fallen to 0 or less, you will suffer no [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] from the fall.

If more [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds (W)]] are suffered from the fall than your Thoughness Bonus, you also gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] Condition.

Home: [[Rules/Combat/Initiative Order\|Initiative Order]]

Som groups prefer to randomise [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]]. There are several ways to do this, choose your favourite:

* Each character rolls an [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to determine SL.
* Each character rolls 1d10 and adds it to their [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]]
* Each character rolls 1d10 and adds it to their [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] Bonus + [[Rules/Attributes/Initiative (I)\|Initiative]] Bonus

The GM notes the results in decreasing numerical order and uses this as the Initiative Order. You could use this order for every Round (quickest option) or roll each Round (gives some variety to the order; slow characters have the chance to not always go last).

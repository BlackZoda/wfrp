*Damage = Weapon Damage + SL*

Once you have determined the hit location, it is time to work out how much [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] you deal. Each weapon has a [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Damage\|Weapon Damage]] characteristic (see page 293). This is usually your modified [[Strength Bonus\|Strength Bonus]] for [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Melee Weapon\|Melee Weapon]]s, or a fixed number for [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Ranged Weapon/Ranged Weapon\|Ranged Weapon]]s. Take the SL of your [[Rules/Tests/Opposed Test\|Opposed Test]] and add it to the [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon Damage\|Weapon Damage]] of the weapon you’re using. This final number is your [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]].

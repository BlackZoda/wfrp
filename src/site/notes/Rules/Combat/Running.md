Home: [[Rules/Combat/Moving During Combat\|Moving During Combat]]

On your turn, you can use your Action to sprint. This requires an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], and the distance covered is in addition to your [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]] this round. You sprint your [[Rules/Tables/Movement Table\|Run Movement]] + SL in yards. So, a character with Move 4 who rolled -2 SL would sprint an additional 14 yards (16-2=14).

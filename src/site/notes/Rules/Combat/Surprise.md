Taking your enemy by surprise gives you a big advantage. If one side is planning an attack, they can attempt to harness the element of surprise by:

* [[Rules/Combat/Hiding\|Hiding]]
* [[Rules/Combat/Sneaky Tactics\|Sneaky Tactics]]
* [[Rules/Combat/Distractions\|Distractions]]
* [[Rules/Combat/Unprepared Defenders\|Unprepared Defenders]]

Or other sneaky and ingenious plans concocted by cunning players — the GM has the final call on the possibility of surprise.

If there is a chance the ambushers will be spotted, the GM will typically call for **Opposed [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]] vs Perception Test**, usually with the character with the worst Stealth opposing all potential spotters. If the ambusher wins, each defeated character gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Surprised\|Surprised]] Condition.

If anyone is surprised, they can use a [[Rules/Attributes/Resolve\|Resolve]] point to remove the Surprised Condition.

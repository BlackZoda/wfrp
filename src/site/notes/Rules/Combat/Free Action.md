Home: [[Rules/Combat/Characters Take Turns\|Characters Take Turns]]

Some things you’ll want your [[Character\|Character]] to do won’t count as your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] for the round – such as shouting a warning, drawing your weapon, or drinking a potion. It’s the GM’s decision on what takes up your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]], and what you can do in a [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]]. A good general guide is that if an act requires you to make a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], it is an [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] rather than a *Free Action*.

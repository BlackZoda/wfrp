Home: [[Rules/Combat/Criticals and Fumbles\|Criticals and Fumbles]]

The converse of [[Rules/Combat/Criticals\|Criticals]], any failed combat [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] that also rolls a double is a *Fumble*, which means something very unfortunate has occurred. To determine what happens, roll on the [[Rules/Tables/Fumbles Table\|Fumbles Table]].

Fumbles that also is an **even number** for a [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Ranged Weapon/Blackpowder Weapon\|Blackpowder Weapon]], [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Ranged Weapon/Explosive Weapon\|Explosive Weapon]], or [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Ranged Weapon/Engineering Weapon\|Engineering Weapon]] result in a misfire – your weapon Misfires, exploding in your hand. You take **full Damage to your Primary Arm location** using the units die as an effective SL for the hit, and your *weapon is destroyed*.

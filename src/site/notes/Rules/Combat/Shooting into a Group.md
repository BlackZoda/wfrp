Ranged Tests made to hit a group of targets are *Average (+20) if there is 3-6* of them, *Easy (+40) if there are 7-12* of them, and *Very Easy (+60) if there is 13 or more* of them. Any successful *hits are randomised* between all likely targets as the GM prefers.

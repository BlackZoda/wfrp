Home: [[Rules/Combat/Attacking\|Attacking]]

Whenever you attack an opponent, or are attacked, in melee combat, you count as *Engaged*. This means you are tussling with each other in a fight, and others rules (due to [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s, [[Spell\|Spell]]s, etc.) for being *Engaged* might apply. If you don’t attack each other for a full [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]], you are no longer *Engaged*.

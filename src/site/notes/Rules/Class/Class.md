Your Class determines your general place in society. You Career describes your current job and determines your [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]], which also influence how much money you earn. Your choice of Class determines from which [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]]s you can choose. The Careers under each Class are of a broadly equivalent social level that offer similar types of opportunities between adventures.

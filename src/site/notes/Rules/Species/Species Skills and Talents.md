Home: [[Rules/Species/Species\|Species]]

Each Species has a variety of [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s and [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s to choose from. You may choose 3 [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s to gain 5 [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] in each, and 3 [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s to gain 3 [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] in each. If a [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] listing presents a choice, you select one [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] fromt the choices given. Any Random Talents are determined by the [[Rules/Tables/Random Talent Table\|Random Talent Table]]. If you roll a Talent you already have, you may reroll.

**Note:** All Characters are assumed to be fluent in [[World/Other Nations/Languages/Reikspiel\|Reikspiel]], the language of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], and do not need to take it as a [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]].

It burns beneath your flesh as blisters from then burst, each peeling away as if long vurnt by the sun. Rather than raw reddened flesh, your new skin is revealed as hard scales, which are greatly reduced in sensitivity and flexibility, but much tougher.

Lose 10 [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]] and [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]]. Gain +1 AP on all locations. This AP may not be used for [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Critical Deflection\|Critical Deflection]].

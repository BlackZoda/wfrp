It started with a tingle. Then an irresistible itch. Unable to stop yourself, you tore and tore and tore as your flesh corroded beneath your touch.

Now your skn constantly rots and regenerates, falling away in long strips revealing patches of oozing blood and pus in place of skin and hair.

Gain the [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] 3 [[Trait\|Trait]]. Further, lose 20 [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]].

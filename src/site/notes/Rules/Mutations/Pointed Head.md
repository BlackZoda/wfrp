Home: [[Rules/Mutations/Pointed Head\|Pointed Head]]

Now bone gathers beneath your scalp and erupts from the crown of your head in a wet splash of blood, skin, and hair. Your new, pointed sull, which has the color of an infected toenail, is permanently on display where it breaks free from your torn scalp.

Lose 5 [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] and 10 [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]]. Gain +1 [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour\|Armour]] point to your [[Head Hit Location\|Head Hit Location]]. Headwear only fits if specially crafted for you, though you do squeeze into pointed hats relatively well.

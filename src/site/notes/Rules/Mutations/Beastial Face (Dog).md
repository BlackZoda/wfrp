Home: [[Rules/Mutations/Mutation\|Mutation]]

YOu feel an irresistible urge to fall to your knees and howl. As your jaw is forced to its maximum extension, you begin to choke uncontollably as a thickly furred snout forces its way through yourface. Your [[World/Races/Human\|Human]] nose, lips, and teeth slough off, nothing more than relics of your former existence, replaced by the snarling face of a dog.

Lose 20 [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]] and 10 [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]]. Gain the [[Rules/Talents/Acute Sense\|Acute Sense]] (Smell) [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]].

You remember little of the event. Blinding pain. Tears. Fear. But, worst of al, the ooze – there just wasn’t space in your head for who you once were. Now your head is no larger than an apple, not that you really understand what a head is. Or an apple.

You lose 40 [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]] to a minimum of 10. Gain the [[World/Bestiary/Traits/Stupid\|Stupid]] [[Creature Trait\|Creature Trait]].

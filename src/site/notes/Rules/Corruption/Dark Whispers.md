Home: [[Rules/Corruption/Losing Corruption Points\|Losing Corruption Points]]

The GM may ask to spend one of your [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]]s to use the darkness building in your soul to twist your actions. If you agree you lose 1 [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]]. Examples of what may be asked include:
* Letting an enemy escape
* “Accidentally” shooting an ally
* Falling asleep on watch

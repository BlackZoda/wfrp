Home: [[Rules/Corruption/Losing Corruption Points\|Losing Corruption Points]]

Asking forgiveness from a local preacher won’t be enough. Consider the following:
* Cleanse a profane temple to the [[Dark Gods\|Dark Gods]]. Though the very act may expose you to more corruption…
* Complete a holy pilgrimage and receive a blessing from a high priest at the end of the long, dangerous journey.
* Destroy an unholy [[Artefact\|Artefact]], or otherwise render it safe.
* Join a holy [[World/Religion and Belief/The Cults/Orders\|Order]] and dedicate your life to one of the [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/The Gods\|Gods]] opposed to [[World/Religion and Belief/Chaos\|Chaos]].

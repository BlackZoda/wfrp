Home: [[Rules/Corruption/Corruption\|Corruption]]

Should you ever gain more [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]]s than your **Willposer Bonus + Thoughness Bonus**, make a **Challenging (+0) Endurance** Test.
* **Success:** No effect. Test again next time you gain [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]]
* **Failure:** Lose **[[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]]s = Willpower Bonus** and then roll on the following table:

| –    | Elf    | Halfling | Human  | Dwarf  |
| ---- | ------ | -------- | ------ | ------ |
| Body | –      | 01–10    | 01-50  | 01–05  |
| Mind | 01–100 | 11–100   | 51–100 | 06–100 |
Now roll on either the [[Rules/Tables/Physical Corruption Table\|Physical Corruption Table]] or [[Rules/Tables/Mental Corruption Table\|Mental Corruption Table]]

Should you survive to gain more mutations than your Thoughness Bonus, or more mental corruptions than your Willpower Bonus, your soul is completely lost to the uncaring [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Chaos Gods\|Chaos Gods]]. Roll a new character, your current one is now damned, becoming an NPC.

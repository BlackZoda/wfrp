Home: [[Rules/Corruption/Corrupting Influences\|Corrupting Influences]]

Witness multiple [[Deamon\|Deamons]], Contact with a Deamon, [[World/Magic/Warpstone\|Warpstone]], profane [[Artefact\|Artefact]], brief exposure to environments steeped with [[World/Magic/Dhar\|Dhar]], embracing despair, rage, excess, or the desire to become someone new.

* **Success (2+ SL):** No [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]]
* **Marginal Success (0–1 SL):** Gain 1 [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Point]]
* **Failure:** Gain 2 [[Corruption Point\|Corruption Points]]

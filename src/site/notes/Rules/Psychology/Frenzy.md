Home: [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]]

With a Willpower [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], you can work yourself into a state of frenzy by psyching yourself up, howling, biting your shield, or similar. If you succeed, you become subject to *Frenzy*.

While subject to *Frenzy* you are immune to all other psychology, and will not flee or retreat for any reason; indeed you must always move at full rate towards the closest enemy you can see in order to attack. Generally, the only [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]] you may take is a Weapon Skill [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] or an [[Rules/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to reach an enemy more quickly. Further, you may take a [[Rules/Combat/Free Action\|Free Action]] [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] each [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] as you are throwing everything you have into your attacks. Lastly, you gain a bonus of +1 Strength Bonus, such is your ferocity. You remain in *Frenzy* until all enemies in your line of sight are pacified, or you receive the [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition. After your **Frenzy** is over you immediately receive a [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued\|Fatigued]] condition.

Home: [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]]

When having *Terror (Rating)* some creatures are so profoundly unsettling as to provoke dire, bone-chilling terror in their foes. When you first encounter a creature causing *Terror*, make a [[Rules/Skills/Cool\|Cool]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you pass, you suffer no further effects of *Terror*, if you fail, you receive a number of [[Rules/Conditions/Broken\|Broken]] Conditions equal to the creature’s *Terror Rating*, plus the number of SL below 0.

After resolving the [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], the creature causes [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]], with a *[[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] Rating* equal to its *Terror Rating*.

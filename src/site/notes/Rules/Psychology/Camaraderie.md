Camaraderie reflects positive feelings towards a group of individuals. You must come to their aid if the group is threatened physically or socially, and you gain a bonus of +1 SL to any Tests to defend or support the group.

> **Example:** Amhold had a difficult childhood, growing up on the streets without a family. Despite his gruff demeanour, he has Camaraderie (Orphans).

Home: [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]]

With *Animosity (Target)* you harbour an enmity for the *Target*, which will normally be a group of people or creatures, such as “Nordlanders”, “Beastmen”, or “Nobles”. You must attempt a Pshycoogy Test whenever you encounter the group. If you pass, you may grumble and spit, but only suffer a *-20* penalty to all *Fellowship [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]s* towards that group. Should you fail you are subject to *Animosity*.

At the end of every subsequent Round, you may attempt another Psychology test to bring the *Animosity* to an end. If you do not, the effects of *Animosity* naturally come to an end when all members of the specified group in your line of sight are utterly pacified or gone, or you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] Condition, or you become subject to another [[Rules/Psychology/Psychology\|Psychology]].

When subject to *Animosity*, you must immediately attack the disagreeable creatures, either socially (insults, jobes, and similar) or physically (most commonly with fists), as appropriate. You also gain a bonus of *+1 SL* on any attempts to socially or mentally attack the group. *Animosity* is over-ridden by [[Rules/Psychology/Fear\|Fear]] and [[Rules/Psychology/Terror\|Terror]].

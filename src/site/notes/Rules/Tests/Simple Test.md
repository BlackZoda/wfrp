Home: [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]

Roll 1d100 and compare the result rolled to the [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] or [[Characteristic\|Characteristic]]. 

If you roll *lower* or equal to the Skill or Characteristic, the Test’s outcome is a *success*, and our Character performs the task.

If you roll *higher* than the Skill or Characteristic, the Test’s outcome is a *failure*, and your Character does not perform the task.

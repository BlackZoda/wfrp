What if you want to prepare to dodge or parry incoming blows, to hold a defensive position in a doorway, or use [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] ([[World/Other Nations/Languages/Magick\|Magick]]) to deply an array of dispels? As your [[Rules/Combat/Action\|Action]], choose a [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] to use defensively and you will get a +20 to defensive [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] using the [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]] until the start of your next [[Rules/Combat/Turn\|Turn]].

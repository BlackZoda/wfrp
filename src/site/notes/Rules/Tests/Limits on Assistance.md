Home: [[Rules/Tests/Assistance\|Assistance]]

Characters can assist each other in most tasks; however, there are limits.

* To assist you require at least 1 Advance in the Skill being tested.
* Assisting Characters must normally be adjacent to the Character taking the Test.
* You may not assist on Tests made to resist disease, poison, fear, hazards, or anything else the GM deems inappropriate.
* You may not be assisted by more Characters than you have in the appropriate Characteristic Bonus.

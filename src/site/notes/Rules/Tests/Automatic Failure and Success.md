Home: [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]

No matter how skilled or talentet a character is, there is always a chance of failure. Equally there is always a chance of success.

If you roll *96–00* on any [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], it is *always* a filure. A failure occurs even if your modified characteristic or skill is 96 or higher.

Similarly, if you roll *01–05* on a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], it is always a success. A *success* occurs even if your modified characteristic or skill is less than 01–05.

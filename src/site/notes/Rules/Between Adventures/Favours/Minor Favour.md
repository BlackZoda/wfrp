Represents a simple task that can be completed in no more than a few hours. Perhaps you need volunteer at a [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]n mercy-house or put in a good word for someone. With the GM’s permission, you may undertake an [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] to pay off this favour.

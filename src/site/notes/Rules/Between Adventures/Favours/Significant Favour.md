A *Significant Favour* is relatively rare and will almost certainly involve risking life and limb. Months of travel may be involved, and major voilence is likely. Perhaps you will be asked to elminate a rival gang boss, wipe out a Goblin encampment, or instigate a riot. Significant Favours cannot be paid off via Endeavours; they are roleplayed out as full adventures.

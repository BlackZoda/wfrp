This Endeavour covers the many ways you can make money between bouts of adventuring. The majority will most likely work in recognized professions – bunty-hounters hount bounties, riverwardens ward rivers, troll slayers slay Trolls – others may rely on a lucreative skill. Wealthier characters, such as [[Rules/Career/Noble\|Noble]]s and [[Rules/Career/Merchant\|Merchant]]s, might collect a stipend by (briefly) indulging their family, or collecting interest on their investments and business ventures. Roughish types have any number of means to acquire funds, though it’s best not to ask for any details.

You should briefly arrate exactly how your character makes money. This is intended more for fun background detail than to have any direct consequences, though GMs may well gather useful or sinister ideas for future use depending on what you describe. You then aquire the income you’ll start your next adventure with, which is your standard income tetermine by your [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]]. Certain results on the [[Rules/Tables/Events Table\|Events Table]] may directly affect your income. The money you get from an *Income* Endeavour is given to you after your money from the last adventure is spent (see [[Rules/Between Adventures/Money to Burn\|Money to Burn]]).

In addition to acquiring funds for later use, if ou are in the third or fourth level of a Career and undertake this Endeavour, you automatically maintain your standing in you career. See [[Rules/Between Adventures/Duties & Responsibilities\|Duties & Responsibilities]].

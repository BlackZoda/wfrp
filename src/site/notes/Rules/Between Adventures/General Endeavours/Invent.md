Invent allows you to invent new trappings. This is done in two stages: Plan and Build. To Plan your new trapping, you first decide the trappings you wish to combine to create your invention. As examples, [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Ranged Weapon/Pistol (Weapon)\|Pistol (Weapon)]] have been combined with other Pistols to create [[Repeating Pistols\|Repeating Pistols]], and [[Pigeons\|Pigeons]] have been combined with [[Bombs\|Bombs]] to create [[Pigeon Bombs\|Pigeon Bombs]]. Let our imagination go wild. Once you have your plan, it’s time to make the blueprints, which cost 1 Endeavour to attempt. Take a **[[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] (Engineer)** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], with a Difficulty set by how outlandish your propsed trapping combination is as determined by the GM. If you succeed, you are ready to start Building. Each SL scored adds +1 SL to the attempt to build your invention, as the plans are well laid out and easy to follow.

To Build the invention, you must either undertake a [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Crafting\|Crafting]] of [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Commission\|Commission]] Endeavour. If it’s [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Crafting\|Crafting]], you are building the item yourself, and you must have the appropriate Skills to do so for the trappings being combined; so, if you were combining Pigesons with Bombs, ou would need **[[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Animal Training (Endeavour)\|Animal Training (Endeavour)]]** and **[[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] (Explosives)**. The Difficulty to craft the invention is always **Very Hard (-30)**, and the SL is set as normal. The raw materials needed to build the invention cost twice the listed price for the trappings being combined, and have an Availability rating equal to the most scarce of the trappings used in your invention. If you [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Commission\|Commission]] the invention, you will likely need to undertake a [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Consult an Expert\|Consult an Expert]] Endeavour first to find someone with the required Skills. That done, follow the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Commission\|Commission]] Endeavour as normal; it cost six times the listed price for the trappings to be combinedto sommission the work. The exact rules for your new invention are left to your FM to finalise.

Rather than browse shops or commissioning someone else to do the hard work, you can fashion our own trappings. [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Crafting\|Crafting]] allows you to make any trappings from [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]] (or anything else the GM allows) if you have the correct [[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]] Skills. To do this, you require appropriate [[Trade Tools\|Trade Tools]], raw materials, and access to an appropriate workshop. In general, the raw materials to create the trappings of your choice will cost a quater of the trapping’s list price, and must be purchased before *Crafting* begins (with an [[Rules/Economy/Availability\|Availability]] as determined by the GM); the GM may rule the raw materials are cheaper or more expensive according to the trapping to be created, and the nature of the raw materials required.

To craft the trapping, attempt an [[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Test]] ([[Rules/Skills/Trade\|Trade]]), with a [[Rules/Tables/Difficulty Table\|Difficulty Table]] set by the GM, typically using the Trapping’s Availability as a loose guide.

| Availability | Difficulty       |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| Common       | Average (+20)    |
| Scarce       | Challenging (+0) |
| Rare         | Difficult (-10)  |
| Exotic       | Very Hard (-30)                 |

The number of SL required to succeed at the test is also set by the GM, using the listed price for the trapping in the [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Consumers’ Guide\|Consumers’ Guide]] as a reference.

The SL required is futher modified by the [[Qualities\|Qualities]] or [[Flaws\|Flaws]] you work into the trapping. Each Flaw halves the SL required, and each Quality adds +5 (worked out after halving for Flaws). Each Endeavour you spend *Crafting* allows you to make one roll towards your [[Rules/Tests/Extended Test\|Extended Test]]. Unfinished work can be kept in whatever you have that amounts to lodgings, or carried around with you as you adventure, should that be possible.

| List Price | SL  |
| ---------- | --- |
| Brass      | 5   |
| Silver     | 10  |
| Gold       | 15+    |

It is one thing to pick up a [[Skills\|Skills]] that can be readily practised, quite another to learn something for which one may have no aptitude at all. This Endeavour allows you to attempt to learn a [[Talents\|Talents]] outside of your [[Careers\|Careers]]. There is no guarantee this attempt will be successful, meaning there is a good chance you will fail to learn the Talent you hope to acquire, expending XP and money to no avail.

You can only learn a Talent if you have an appropriate tutor. In most big cities, this will not be a problem, but if the Talent is particularly obscure, or you are far from a large city, the GM may require you complete a [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Consult an Expert\|Consult an Expert]] Endeavour first.

Once you have acquired a tutor, the fee to train the Talent is 2d10 shillings per 100 XP it costs to purchase the Talent. If you pay this fee, attempt a **Hard (-20)** Test using the Charactersitic or Skill most relevant to the Talent as determined by the GM. If you succeed, you have learned the Talent. If not, you have failed this time but can try again with a future Endeavour and gain a +10 modifier for each failed attempt.

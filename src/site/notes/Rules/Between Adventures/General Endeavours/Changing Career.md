Home: [[Endeavour\|Endeavour]]

In a society as rigidly hierarchical as [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], changing [[Careers\|Careers]] may require grit and perseverance. Taking time to forge the right connections, and learn what is expected of you, can help, significantly. Assuming your GM agrees, and you have completed your current Career, you can move to any Career Level that fits the story of your Character for free. If you have not completed your current Career, the change costs 100 XP. The time spent on this Endeavour represents making the appropriate introductions, doling out bribes, aquiring permits and licences, advertising, and similar activities.

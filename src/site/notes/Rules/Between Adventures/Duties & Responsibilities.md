## With Great Power…
As you climb the ranks of your career you accrue expectations and responsibilities that cannot be avoided. [[Noble Lord\|Noble Lord]]s must see to the affairs of their estates, [[Guildmaster\|Guildmaster]]s must oversee their guild’s work, and [[Watch Captain\|Watch Captain]]s must watch their watchmen. If you neglect your responsivilities, you will soon lose status amongst your peers.

If you have attained one of the top two tires of your Career Path and do not undertake the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]], which reflects taking care of your obligations, you drop one level within your Career.

With this step backwards in your Career Path you will now have lower [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]], and will earn less money should you perform future [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]]s. The loss in status does not remove any [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] aquired while you were in your previous Career.

## Elf IMprovements
Elves, even those resident within the borders of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], do not consider themselves citizens of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]]. There are [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elf]] with diplomatic positions in [[World/The Empire/Politics/People/Emperor Karl-Franz I\|Emperor Karl-Franz I]]’s dourt, as well as traders and merchant princes in many of the great cities of [[World/The Empire/The Empire\|The Empire]], but the majority of the Asur avoid entanglements with [[World/Races/Human\|Human]]s (not to mention [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarf]]s) and fewwould call a [[Reiklander\|Reiklander]] “friend”.

The [[World/Races/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] are even more removed; nearly mythical, rarely seen, and when they do appear they always have their own concerns that they rarely share with others.

Between adventures, all Elven characters must undertake one [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] maintaining contact with their own kind seeing to their responsibilities to their people. [[World/Races/High Elf\|High Elf]] commonly give reports on interesting things they’ve learned to agens of [[Ulthuan\|Ulthuan]] in either [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Altdorf\|Altdorf]] or [[World/Other Nations/Marienburg/Marienburg\|Marienburg]], whereas [[Asrai\|Asrai]] do the same with the spies from their aboreal homes. This [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] offers no advantage beyond the inherent advantage of being an Elf.

**Note:** Elves only lose an [[Rules/Between Adventures/Endeavours\|Endeavours]] if the [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Between Adventures\|Between Adventures]] section is at least 3 weeks long.

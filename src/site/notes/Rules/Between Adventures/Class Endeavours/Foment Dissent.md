**[[Rules/Class/Burgher\|Burgher]], [[Rules/Class/Peasant\|Peasant]]**

If you are looking to sow civil unrest, undertake the *Foment Dissent* Endeavour. By talking with local citizens, leading meetings, and involving yourself, you can direct conversations to sowly feed anger and resentment towards a specific individual, group, or institution. But this takes time, so *Fomenting Dissent* takes two Endeavours to attempt (and will also count as an [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Income]] Endeavour if you are in the [[Rules/Career/Agitator\|Agitator]] career).

To undertake the Endeacour, you must first attempt an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Gossip\|Gossip]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to understand the local movers and shakers. If you succeed attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] with the Difficulty determined by how unpopular the target may be. Rousing anger against an oppressive noble may be **Easy (+40)**, while raising a mob to protest the good work of a [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]]n hospice may be **Hard (-20)**. If you fail either of these tests, the Endeavour fails.

If successful, you will find it easier to invoke the wrath of crowds upon your chosen target. During your next adventure, you can attempt a **Charm** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] to gather a rioting mob to accost the target, with the difficulty determined by the GM according to how well-planned the mob-to-be is.

A single success means you gather enough angered townsfolk to confront the target, shouting insults, demanding justice, throwing rotting vegetables, and generally accosting the focus of their ire. An Impressive or Astounding Success may lead to uncontrollable lynch mobs, or even attempted burnings!

Failure means folk have no stomach for wrath; filing by several SL may mean the target, or their sympathisers, become aware of your actions…

Once you have the ear of a mob, you can attempt to rally the mob against a different target during an adventrue, although this is more difficult, and the Difficulty of the [[Rules/Skills/Charm\|Charm]] Test is two levels higher.

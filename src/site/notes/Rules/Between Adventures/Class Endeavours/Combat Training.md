**[[Rules/Class/Ranger\|Ranger]], [[Rules/Class/Warrior\|Warrior]]**

Warriors too long from the field can find their fighting skills loose in their edge. Hard regular training not only mitigates this, it can add an extra advantage when when the time comes. If you undertake this Endeavour, you spend your das training with the weapons you know, honing your skills for the day when it matters most.

After undertaking this Endeavour, attempt a **Challenging (+0)** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] using either a [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] or [[Rules/Skills/Ranged\|Ranged]] [[Skills\|Skills]]. If you succeed, you may reverse a Test with the associated Skill once during your next adventure. Note this on your character sheet. You can enact this Endeavour multipe times if you wish.

**Rouge**

You use this Endeavour to observe a potential target and gain advantage during future criminal enterprises. A [[Rules/Career/Charlatan\|Charlatan]] may study a merchant in order to better impersonate them during a future scam, or a [[Rules/Career/Grave Robber\|Grave Robber]] may linger about a Temple of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Morr\|Morr]], watching the coming and going of the priests to get an idea of their practices and their [[Garden of Morr\|Garden of Morr]]’s layout.

Attempt a **Challenging (+0) Perception** Test, though the difficulty may be modified for especially easy or difficult marks. If you succeed, you may reverse a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] concerning your mark once during your next adventure. Note this on you character sheet. YOu can enact this Endeavour multiple times if you wish. Further, the GM should provide information (or disinformation!) concerning your mark according to the SL you scored.

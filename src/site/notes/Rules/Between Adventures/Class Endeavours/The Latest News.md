**[[Rules/Class/Ranger\|Ranger]], [[Rules/Class/Riverfolk\|Riverfolk]]**

This Endeavour allows you to learn the latest news from afar. Attempt a **Challenging (+0) [[Rules/Skills/Gossip\|Gossip]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you succeed, you learn in interesting rumour; each SL adds another rumour, and they may even be connected to your upcoming adventure. If you fail particularly badly (an Impressive Failure) you learn something false you are convinced is true; to allow this, the GM ma make this roll in secret on your behalf.

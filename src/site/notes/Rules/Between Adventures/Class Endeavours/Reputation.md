**[[Rules/Class/Academic\|Academic]], [[Rules/Class/Burgher\|Burgher]], [[Rules/Class/Courtier\|Courtier]]**

Money talks, as they say. If you are looking to exert your influence and elevate your [[Rules/Status/Status\|Status]], you had best be willing to spread your wealth around. Any boost to your reputation will fade once the coins stop flowing, so you should exploit this advantage while it lasts.

Characters can spend money to increase their standing by +1 for the next adventure, after which the bonus fades. This costs your maximum [[Earning\|Earning]] income – so, if you would normally earn 4d100 pennies, it cost 40d – and requires a **Chalenging (+0) Career Skill** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]]. If you succeed, your Standing increases by +1. If you score an Astounding Success (+6), it inceases by +2. If you fail, you have just wasted your money. if you score an Astounding Filure (-6), your doadying has so infuriated your peers, your standing decreases by -1 for the entire duration of your next adventure.

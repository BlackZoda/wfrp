Home: [[Rules/Injuries/Wounds, Critical Wounds, and Death\|Wounds, Critical Wounds, and Death]]

Wounds are lost by sustaining *Damage*. Each time you suffer a point of Damage, you lose 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]]. So, if you suffer 8 points of Damage, you lose 8 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]]. Often, especially in combat, your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus or [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour\|Armour]] Points may reduce suffered *Damage*.

So, if you were hit in the arm of 10 *Damage* and your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus was 3, and you had leather armour on the arm for 1 [[Rules/Trappings/Armour/Armour\|Armour]] Point, you woud suffer 6 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] (10-3-1=6). The rules will tell you when you can or cannot reduce [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] with [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] and [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]].

If you lose all of your [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], your collected injuries overwhelm you, and you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]. Until you heal at least 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]], you cannot lose the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]]; worse, if you’re not [[Rules/Healing and Death/Healing\|Healed]] within a number of [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Rounds]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus, you will pass out, gaining the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] Condition. You will not regain exonsiousness until you [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] back at least 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]].

If you take enough [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] that ou would be taken to negative [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] – say you suffer 5 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] when you only had 2 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] left – you suffer a [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]]. If you suffer fewer negative [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] than your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus, you subtract -20 from your [[Rules/Tables/Critical Tables\|Critical Table]] result, with a minimum result of 01.

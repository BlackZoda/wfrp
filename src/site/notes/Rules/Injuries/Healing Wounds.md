Home: [[Rules/Healing and Death/Healing\|Healing]]

You are considered wounded if you have lost any of your [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]].

Without medical attention, you can attempt an **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** Test after a good sleep once a day. You Heal wounds equal to the *SL + Thoughness Bonus*. For each day you spend taking it easy, you also heal an extra number of [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] equal to your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus.

To [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] more than this, you need to be attended by someone with the [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] skill, or you’ll need bandages, a healing poultice, or similar.

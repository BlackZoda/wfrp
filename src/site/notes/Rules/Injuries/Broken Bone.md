## Minor
The [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location]] is unusable until the breakage heals:
* For arms and legs (see [[Rules/Injuries/Amputated Parts\|Amputated Parts]]).
* For [[Rules/Tables/Head Critical Wounds\|Head]] hits your on a liquid diet and suffer -30 to all [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] Tests.
* For [[Rules/Tables/Body Critical Wounds\|Body]] hits suffer a -30 loss to your [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] and [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]], and half your Move.
### Healing
A fractured bone wil take *30+1d10* days to heal. At the end of this, a successful **Average (+20) [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]]** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] means the bone has set well, and no long-term effects wil be suffered. If the [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] is failed, you suffer a permanent loss of -5 to all [[Agility\|Agility]] Tests when using the damaged arm, a permanent loss of -5 to your [[Agility\|Agility]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristic]] for a badly healed *Body* or *Leg* hit, or a permanent -5 to spoken [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] for a badly healed *Head* hit.

A successful **Average (+20) Heal** [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] within a week will negate the need for the [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]], but the affecte area must remain bound and held in place for the duration of the healing. If the binding is undone, another **Average (+20) Heal** Test is needed within a day to avoid the Endurance test.
## Major
One of your bones is badly broken, and is either resting at an odd angle, or has spintered at the point of breakage. It is unlikely to heal properly without medical attention.

**Healing:** Healing takes 10 days longer. All associated [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] are **Challenging (+0)**. Any penalties for failed [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] increase to -10.

Home: [[Rules/Injuries/Exposure\|Exposure]]

* First failure case *-10* to [[Rules/Attributes/Ballistic Skill (BS)\|Ballistic Skill]], [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]], and [[Rules/Attributes/Dexterity (Dex)\|Dexterity]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].
* Second failure reduce **all other [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]]** by *-10*.
* Third and subsequent failures causes **1d10 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]]**, not reduced by [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] Points with a minimum of 1 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wound]] lost.
* At zero Wounds, you immediately take an [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]].
* Certain [[Rules/Trappings/Trappings\|Trappings]] provide bonuses to these tests.

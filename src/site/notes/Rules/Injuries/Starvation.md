Home: [[Rules/Injuries/Thirst and Starvation\|Thirst and Starvation]]

* Every 2 days without [[Campaign/Items/Food\|Food]] require an [[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].
* First failed [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] causes a penalty of *-10* to [[Rules/Attributes/Strength (S)\|Strength]] and [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]].
* Second and subsequent failures *reduce all other [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]] by -10* and cause *1d10 Damage* not modified by [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] and with a minimum of 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]] lost.

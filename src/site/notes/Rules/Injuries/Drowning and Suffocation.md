* If prepared you can hold our breath for *[[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus x 10* seconds without a [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]].
* If unprepared you will start to suffocate immediately.

Each Round you are bing suffocated you lose 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]]. You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] if you reach 0 Wounds. After that, you will die of suffocation or drowning in *[[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus Rounds*.

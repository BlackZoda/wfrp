Home: [[Injuries\|Injuries]]
## Minor
* -10 to all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] involving the [[Rules/Tables/Hit Location Table\|Hit Location]].
* If a Leg is hit, halve your [[Rules/Attributes/Movement (M)\|Movement]].
### Healing
Partially heal in *30 - Thoughness Bonus* days. Use of the Healing skill will reduce the time by 1 day, plus a further day ber SL (gain this benefit once).
## Major
Same effect as *Minor*, but the penalties are -20 to all Tests.
### Healing
Partially heal in *30 - Thoughness Bonus* days. After this the penalties are reduced to -10 to all relevant Tests. The muscle will completely heal after another *30 - Thoughness Bonus* days.

Home: [[Rules/Injuries/Thirst and Starvation\|Thirst and Starvation]]

* Every day without water requires you to make an **[[Rules/Skills/Endurance\|Endurance]] Test**.
* First failed test causes a *-10* penalty to [[Rules/Attributes/Intelligence (Int)\|Intelligence]], [[Rules/Attributes/Willpower (WP)\|Willpower]], and [[Rules/Attributes/Fellowship (Fel)\|Fellowship]].
* Second and subsequent failures *reduce all [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]] by -10* and cause *1d10 [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]]* not modified by [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] and with a minimum of 1 [[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]] lost.

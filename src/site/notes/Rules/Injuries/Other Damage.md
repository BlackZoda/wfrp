## Thirst and Starvation
Endurance Tests to withstand thirst and hunger become increasingly difficult – a cumulative -10 for every test. Characters without food and water cannot heal Wounds or recover Fatigue naturally.
### Water
* Every day without water requires you to make an **Endurance Test**.
* First failed test causes a *-10* penalty to **Intelligence**, **Willpower**, and **Fellowship**.
* Second and subsequent failures *reduce all Characteristics by -10* and cause *1d10 Damage* not modified by Armor Points and with a minimum of 1 Wound lost.
### Food
* Every 2 days without food require an Endurance Test.
* First failed Tests causes a penalty of *-10* to **Streangth** and **Thoughness**.
* Second and subsequent failures *reduce all other Characteristics by -10* and cause *1d10 Damage* not modified by Armor points and with a minimum of 1 Wound lost.

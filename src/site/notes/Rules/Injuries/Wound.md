Home: [[Rules/Injuries/Wounds, Critical Wounds, and Death\|Wounds, Critical Wounds, and Death]]

* Lose [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]] equal to [[Rules/Injuries/Damage\|Damage]] recived.
* Often, especially in combat, your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus or [[Armour Points\|Armour Points]] may reduce Damage.
* Gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] Condition if you lose all of your Wounds.
* You cannot remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] Condition until you [[Rules/Skills/Heal\|Heal]] at least 1 Wound.
* If you don’t heal within **Rounds = Thoughness Bonus**, you will pass out, gaining the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] Condition.
* You cannot regain consciousness until you heal back to at least 1 Wound.
* If suffer more Damage than you have Wounds left, you suffer a [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]]. If **Negative Wounds < Thoughness Bonus** you subtract *-20* from the [[Rules/Tables/Critical Tables\|Critical Tables]] result, with a minimum of 1.
* You can never go into negative Wounds.

Home: [[Rules/Injuries/Injury\|Injury]]

When suffering a Critical Wound marked **Amputation (difficulty)** you must pass an **Endurance** Test or gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] Condition. On a Failure (-2 or worse), ou also gain a [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned\|Stunned]] Condition; on an Impressive Failure (-4 or worse), gain an [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] Condition.

All amputations require [[Rules/Talents/Surgery\|Surgery]] to heal properly, meaning 1 Wound cannot be healed until you visit a surgeon.
## Arm
Use the rules for lost hand, but you cannot strap a [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Melee Weapon/Shield\|Shield]] to the arm since you don’t have one.
## Ear
* Suffer a -5 penalty to all [[Fellowship\|Fellowship]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] per ear, when visible.
* -20 to all hearing-based [[Rules/Skills/Perception\|Perception]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] if you have lost both ears.
## Eye
* -5 penalty to all [[Fellowship\|Fellowship]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] per scarred socket visible.
* -30 to all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] influenced by sight, if you have lost both eyes.
## Fingers
* [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] with the number of lost fingers or less on the unit die counts as a fumble.
* -5 to all Tests using the hand in question per finger lost. Uses rules for severed hand for 4 or more fingers lost.
## Foot
* Reduce [[Rules/Combat/Move\|Move]] by half
* -20 penalty to all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] that rely on mobility.
* Losing both feet makes it hard to walk…
## Hand
* -20 on all [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] that rely on the use of two hands
* Cannot wield two-handed weapons (but a shield can be strapped to the injured arm).
* Normal -20 penelty to [[Rules/Skills/Melee\|Melee]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] made with your secondary hand. For every 100XP you spend, you can reduce this penalty by 5.
## Leg
Treat as lost foot, but cannot use the [Dodge] skill at all.
## Nose
* -20 to your [[Fellowship\|Fellowship]].
* -30 to all Tests involving the sense of smell.
## Teeth
* 1 from your [[Fellowship\|Fellowship]] characteristic per second tooth.
* Eating becomes difficult if you lose more than half your teeth ([[World/Races/Human\|Humans]]: 16, [[World/Races/Elf\|Elves]]: 18; [[World/Races/Halfling\|Halflings]] and [[World/Races/Dwarf\|Dwarfs]]: 20).
## Toes
* -1 to [[Rules/Attributes/Agility (Ag)\|Agility]] and [[Rules/Attributes/Weapon Skill (WS)\|Weapon Skill]] [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]] per lost toe.
## Tongue
* Leaves you grunting and humming, or relying on make-shift sign [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]].
* Automatically fail all [[Rules/Skills/Language\|Language]] [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] involving speaking.

Home: [[Rules/Injuries/Injury\|Injury]]

[[Rules/Injuries/Wound\|Wound]] loss represent minor cuts and abrasions, bruises and bashes, and even the depletion of spiritual and mental energy reserves. By comparison, [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]] are much more severe, expressing serious injuries, [[Rules/Injuries/Broken Bone\|Broken Bones]], [[Rules/Injuries/Torn Muscle\|Torn Muscles]], and [[Rules/Injuries/Amputated Parts\|Amputated Parts]]. Gain too many [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]], and you risk death.

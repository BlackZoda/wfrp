Home: [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Wounds, Healing and Death\|Wounds, Healing and Death]]

If you take the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]] and have 0 [[Rules/Attributes/Wounds (W)\|Wounds]], compare the total number of [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]] you currently suffer with your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus. If you have more [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]] than your [[Rules/Attributes/Toughness (T)\|Toughness]] Bonus, you succumb to your horrific wounds and will die at the end of next [[Rules/Combat/Round\|Round]] unless, by some miracle, someone heals one of your [[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wounds]].

Also, if you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] [[0_GM/GM Screen/Sections/Condition\|Condition]], anyone attacking you with suitable [[Rules/Trappings/Weapons/Weapon\|Weapons]] can kill you, should they wish.

Though, in the thick of [[Rules/Combat/Combat\|Combat]], those lying apparently dead on the floor are almost always ignored in favour of combatants still standing.

Lastly, a few results sourced from the [[Rules/Tables/Critical Tables\|Critical Tables]] can result in *Death*. Should any these occur, it is time to permanently spend a [[Rules/Attributes/Fate\|Fate]] point if you have one.

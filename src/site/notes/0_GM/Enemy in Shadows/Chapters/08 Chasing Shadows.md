## Rewards
Rewards as well as your usual Experience point awards for good roleplaying and having fun, XP should be awarded for the following:
* 5 points for discovering the Hidden Temple i beneath the Steinhäger Offices
* 10 points for befriending the Crooked Fingers (if not previously completed)
* 5–10 points for each location where useful information is gained
* 5 points for receiving one or more holy visions
* 30 points for going to the Golden Trout with Magirius. 
If you wish to grant extra Experience points for other events, use the listed XP amounts above as a guideline to build your own awards.

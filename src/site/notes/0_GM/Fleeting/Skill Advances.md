Home: [[0_GM/Fleeting/Advancing Through Your Career\|Advancing Through Your Career]]

You can advance all the [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s listed for your [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] level and lower. So, if you were an [[Apothecary Apprentice\|Apothecary Apprentice]] (level 1), you could only access the Skills listed unde Apothecary’s Apprentice (level 1).

**The cost in XP of a Skill Advance is shown in the [[Rules/Tables/Characteristic and Skill Improvement XP Costs Table\|Characteristic and Skill Improvement XP Costs Table]], and depends on the number of Skill Advances you have already taken in that [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]**

Each Skill Advance adds +1 to your Skill level. Therefore, if you had purchased 9 Advances in [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]] and your [[Agility\|Agility]] was 31, your [[Rules/Skills/Stealth\|Stealth]] would be 40. Thefirst 5 Advances would cost 10 XP each, and the 4 remaining Advances would cost 15 XP each.

**Note:** One of the Skills in the first level of your Career will be marked in italics. This is the Skill you [[Rules/Tests/Tests\|Tests]] when [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Income\|Earning Money]].

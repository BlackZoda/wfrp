Home: [[Rules/Attributes/Advancement\|Advancement]], [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]]

Your Character’s [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] affects how they will grow through experience. Every Career offers three forms of Advance:
* [[0_GM/Fleeting/Characteristic Advances\|Characteristic Advances]]
* [[0_GM/Fleeting/Skill Advances\|Skill Advances]]
* [[0_GM/Fleeting/Talent Advances\|Talent Advances]]

These are purchased with [[Experience Points (XP)\|Experience Points (XP)]]. You can also use XP to Change Career. Your [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]] determines what [[Rules/Attributes/Characteristics\|Characteristics]], [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s, and [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s are available to you.
## Non-career Advances
Non-Career Characteristic and Skill Advances cost double the amount of XP listed in the [[Rules/Tables/Characteristic and Skill Improvement XP Costs Table\|Characteristic and Skill Improvement XP Costs Table]]. Normally non-Career [[Talents\|Talents]] may not be purchased with XP, although [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Training\|Training]] and [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Unusual Learning\|Unusual Learning]] provide an opportunity to purchase non-Career Advances as if they were Career Advances, and give the possibility of learning non-[[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s.
## Changing Career
When you have taken all the [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] you want in your current [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]], it’s time to change Career. Changing Career means one of two things:
1. Moving to a different level within your current Career (from [[Rules/Career/Apothecary (level 2)\|Apothecary (level 2)]] to [[Rules/Career/Master Apothecary (level 3)\|Master Apothecary (level 3)]]); or
2. Moving to an entirely new Career (from [[Rules/Career/Apothecary\|Apothecary]] to [[Rules/Career/Scholar\|Scholar]])

In each case, you will have to first determine if you have completed your current Career level. If you have, changing Career costs 100 XP. If you have not, it costs 200 XP.
## Completing a Career
Completing a [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] represents mastering your current vocation and being ready to move on to something new. To complete a Career, you must have the number of [[Rules/Attributes/Advances\|Advances]] listed below in all your Career level’s Characteristics and in eight of your Career level’s available Skills. You must also have at least 1 [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] from your current Career level. [[Rules/Skills/Skill\|Skill]]s and [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s you have gained from advancement prior to entering your current [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] count towards this.

* [[Rules/Status/Career Level Advances Table\|Career Level Advances Table]]

**Note:** Just because you have completed a [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] level does not mean you have to move to a new one. Moving Career is always entirely up to you. If you wish to stay in the first level of [[Pauper\|Pauper]] forever, that’s completely fine.

## Changing to a New Level
If you have completed your current [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] level, you can enter the next [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]], or any lower Career level, for 100XP. So, if you completed [[Rules/Career/Hunter (level 2)\|Hunter (level 2)]] , you could exit to [[Rules/Career/Tracker (level 3)\|Tracker (level 3)]] or [[Rules/Career/Trapper (level 1)\|Trapper (level 1)]] for 100 XP, but not [[Rules/Career/Huntmaster (level 4)\|Huntmaster (level 4)]].

With GM permission, you can also skip [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]]s. This is normally driven by in-game events. For exampe: A [[Rules/Career/Scion (level 1)\|Scion (level 1)]] inherits a significant estate during play, so the GM offers [[Rules/Career/Noble Lord (level 4)\|Noble Lord (level 4)]] as a potential change of [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]]. Like normal, changing to this [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]] costs 100 XP if you have completed your current [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]], or 200 XP if you have not.

## Changing to a New Career
If you have completed your current [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]], you can enter the first level of any [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] from your [[Rules/Class/Class\|Class]] for 100 XP, or for 200 XP if you have not completed your current [[0_GM/Fleeting/Career Level\|Career Level]]. If you want to enter the first level of a [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] from a different [[Rules/Class/Class\|Class]], this cost an extra 100 XP.

**Note:** Your GM may require in-game justification for such Career changes; after all, not just anyone can enter [[Rules/Career/Noble\|Noble]], even if you are in the [[Rules/Class/Courtier\|Courtier]] Class.

Further, if you have completed your current Career Level, with GM permission, and solid justification, you can enter the same Career level in any Career within your Class. So if you completed [[Rules/Career/Master Apothecary (level 3)\|Master Apothecary (level 3)]], you could move to [[Rules/Career/Scholar\|Scholar]] – [[Rules/Career/Fellow (level 3)\|Fellow (level 3)]], to show you were taking a teaching position at [[World/The Empire/Electorial Provinces/Reikland/Altdorf/Locations/University of Altdorf\|University of Altdorf]]. This cost 100 XP. However, be aware there are obvious limitations here. Some Careers, such as [[Rules/Career/Wizard\|Wizard]], require the basic to be learned before the more advanced lore can be understood and the [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s of earlier Career evels will not be open to you at a higher level.

Lastly between adventures, you can also change Career using the [[Rules/Between Adventures/General Endeavours/Changing Career\|Changing Career]] [[Endeavour\|Endeavour]].

Every Career has four levels, each progressively better than the last. When choosing a [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] for a new character, you always start at the first level of the career – so, of you were an Apothecary, you would start as an [[Rules/Career/Apothecary Apprentice (level 1)\|Apothecary Apprentice (level 1)]].

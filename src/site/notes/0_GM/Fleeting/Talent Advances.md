Talents are only available when you are in the level of the [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]] that lists them. So, if you are an [[Rules/Career/Apothecary-General (level 4)\|Apothecary-General (level 4)]], you can only buy [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s listed under [[Rules/Career/Apothecary-General (level 4)\|Apothecary-General (level 4)]], not those listed under [[Apprentice Apothecary\|Apprentice Apothecary]], [[Rules/Career/Apothecary\|Apothecary]], or [[Master Apothecary\|Master Apothecary]].

**Talents Advances cost 100 XP +100 XP per Advance already taken in that Talent.**

The rules for every [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]] can be found in [[Rules/Talents/Talent\|Talent]]s. The first time you purchase a new talent (for 100 XP) it grants you access to the Talent’s special rues. If you purchase a Talent multiple times (second time cost 200 XP, third 300 XP) any additional abilities bestowed are listed in the Talent description.

**Note:** Not all Talents canbe purchased multiple times. Check the Talent listing for any limitations.

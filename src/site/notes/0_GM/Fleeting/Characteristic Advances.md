Home: [[0_GM/Fleeting/Advancing Through Your Career\|Advancing Through Your Career]]

Every Career has an Advance Scheme governing its Characteristic Advances. The Advance Scheme shows your character’s 10 Characteristics, 3 marked with a black cross :RaHospitalCross:, 1 marked with hammers on a brass background ⚒, 1 marked with a skull on silver :RaSkull:, and the last veing a shield marked on gold :RaShield:.

The three marked :RaHospitalCross: are Characteristics you can advance in the first level of the Career. When you enter the second levelof a career, the ⚒ Characteristics also become available. When ou enter the third level, the :RaSkull: Characteristic also becomes available. And when you enter the fourth and last level of a [[Rules/Career/Career\|Career]], the :RaShield: Characteristics finally become available.

**The cost in XP of a Characteristic Advance is shown in the [[Rules/Tables/Characteristic and Skill Improvement XP Costs Table\|Characteristic and Skill Improvement XP Costs Table]], and depends on the number of Characteristic Advances you have already takin in that Characteristic.**

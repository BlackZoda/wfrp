## Wounds
**Wounds = Damage - Thoughness Bonus - Armour Points**
Some damage does not allow *Armor* or *Thoughness* mitigation.

* If *Wounds* is reduced to **0**, gaine the [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] Condition.
* You cannot remove [[Rules/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] Condition until *1 Wound is healed*.
* Gain [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] Condition if not healied in **Rounds = Thoughness Bonus**.
* You cannot remove [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] Condition until *1 Wound is healed*.
* if *Wounds* is reduced below 0, suffer a **Critical Wound** in addition to the above ([[Rules/Injuries/Critical Wound\|Critical Wound]]).

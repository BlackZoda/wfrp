Plague and pestilence — the purview of [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Nurgle\|Nurgle]], the Chaos God of Disease and Despair — are an everyday fact of [[World/Other Nations/Den Gamle Verden\|Den Gamle Verden]] life. Yearly outbreaks of disease plague all levels of society, although the common folk usually fare the worst. To ward against these terrible infections, most turn to the welcoming arms of Nurgle’s sworn enemy, [[World/Religion and Belief/Gods/Shallya\|Shallya]], the Goddess of Mercy and Healing, but those actually suffering a disease will often turn to any possible cure, ensuring wise women, herbalists, and travelling doktors are never out of business, even when most peddle quackery rather than true remedies.
